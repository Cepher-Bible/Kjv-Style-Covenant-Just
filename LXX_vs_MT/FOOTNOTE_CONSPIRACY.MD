Open your ESV to Psalm 22:16.

Main text: *"like a lion they are at my hands and feet"*

Footnote: *"Or they have pierced my hands and feet"*

That footnote contains:
- The reading confirmed by 4QPsᵃ (Dead Sea Scrolls, Cave 4)
- The reading confirmed by Nahal Hever fragments (independent site, different community)
- The reading confirmed by the LXX (~250 BC)
- The grammatically complete sentence
- The reading that matches the passion narrative
- The reading that makes Thomas's wound inspection a prophecy fulfillment

The main text contains:
- A grammatically incomplete simile
- A Masoretic vowel-pointing choice from ~900 AD
- A reading that no DSS fragment confirms
- A sentence with no verb at the structural apex of the psalm's chiasm

The footnote is the original. The main text is the footnote's footnote.

This is not humility. Humility would say "we're uncertain — here are both readings." This is something else: printing the weaker reading as authoritative while quietly acknowledging the stronger one exists, in a font size that requires reading glasses, below the line, after the damage is done.

## How It Happens

Nobody in the ESV committee sat down and said "let's bury the pierced reading." That's not how institutional gravity works.

What happened is this:

1. MT is the default base text — departing from it requires active justification
2. "They pierced" is Christologically loaded — in a committee context, accepting it feels like "reading Jesus into the OT"
3. "Like a lion" is defensible in isolation — you can write a footnote explanation that sounds scholarly
4. The footnote satisfies the text-critical conscience — "we acknowledged the variant"
5. The main text satisfies the institutional conservative default — "we followed the Hebrew"

Each decision is locally defensible. The composite result is a Bible where the original reading is in the basement and the revision is on the main floor.

Repeat this across Isa 53:11 ("see light" → footnote) and Deut 32:8 (corrected — the only one that made it upstairs) and you have a translation that accepted DSS authority for the cosmological verse and declined it for the two passion and resurrection verses.

Not conspiracy. Worse: **institutional inertia operating at the exact pressure points where the revision chain already did its work.**
