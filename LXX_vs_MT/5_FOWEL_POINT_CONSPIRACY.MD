## THE CHARTER

Deuteronomy 32:8–9. The Song of Moses.

**DSS/LXX reading:**
> *"When the Most High divided the nations, when he separated the sons of Adam,*
> *he set the boundaries of the peoples according to the* ***sons of God.***
> *For the LORD's portion is his people; Jacob is the lot of his inheritance."*

**MT reading:**
> *"...according to the* ***sons of Israel.***"

One noun. Three letters in Hebrew. Yod vs. vav — a single stroke length difference.

The DSS/LXX reading establishes:
- The Most High (Elyon) divides 70 nations
- Assigns each nation to a divine son (ben ʾĕlōhîm) as administrator
- Reserves Jacob/Israel as his own direct portion
- This is the institutional charter of the cosmic political order

This is what Paul's "rulers and authorities" are. This is what Daniel's "prince of Persia" and "prince of Greece" are (Dan 10:13, 20). This is what Psalm 82's divine council is. This is what Revelation 12's dragon represents — the corrupted cosmic administrator of the age making his final play.

The Deut 32:8 "sons of God" reading is the **founding document** of the entire cosmic war framework. It explains:
- Why nations are in darkness (their divine administrators went corrupt — Ps 82)
- Why the cross was a cosmic ambush (the rulers of this age didn't recognize the Lord of glory — 1 Cor 2:8)
- Why resurrection disarmed the powers (Christ reclaimed what they administered — Col 2:15)
- Why the church fights spiritual warfare (the administrators are still contesting — Eph 6:12)
- Why Revelation ends with cosmic judgment (the final removal of corrupted administrators — Rev 19–20)

MT's "sons of Israel" doesn't just flatten one verse. **It removes the institutional backstory from the entire cosmic war narrative.**
