## WHAT THIS DOESN'T CLAIM

- This is not a claim that the MT is worthless or unreliable across the board. The MT is ~95% consistent with DSS on orthography. Most of the Bible is unaffected by the revision chain.
- This is not a claim that MT-based translations can't convey saving truth. They can and do. The core gospel survives.
- This is not a claim that every LXX divergence from MT reflects a revision. Some LXX divergences are translator interpretation, not Vorlage difference. The diagnostic engine distinguishes these.
- This is not a conspiracy theory. There is no central conspiracy. The revision chain operated through documented institutional pressures, each with identifiable actors and stated motives. No secret coordination required — institutional gravity and polemical pressure are sufficient explanation.
