**FORENSIC COMPARISON:**

| Source | Elements Listed |
|--------|-----------------|

- MT: 3 elements (לֵבָב, נֶפֶשׁ, מְאֹד)

  - ἰσχύς (ischys) = strength, power, might

**Jesus is exegeting the LXX**, not the Hebrew.

**LIGHTNING STRIKE #2:**

**Jesus defines the greatest commandment using LXX terminology.**

- Hebrew gives Him 3 terms
- LXX gives Him 3 terms (with δύναμις ambiguity)
- Jesus **clarifies LXX** by splitting δύναμις into mind + strength
- Result: **4-fold love command** (heart, soul, mind, strength)

**This becomes Christian doctrine:**
- Medieval scholastics: 4-fold love (intellectus, affectus, opus)
- Reformers: Total love with all faculties
- Modern: Holistic discipleship

**All based on Jesus' LXX-derived formulation.**

**VERDICT:** Challenge acknowledged but doesn't undermine case. NT form derives from LXX.
