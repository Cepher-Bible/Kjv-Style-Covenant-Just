## **METHODOLOGY**

**Evaluation Criteria (100 points total):**
- **Age** (20 pts) - Earlier = better
- **Textual Quality** (25 pts) - Fewer corruptions, better readings
- **Early Church Usage** (20 pts) - Patristic citations match
- **Scholarly Consensus** (15 pts) - Critical editions use it
- **Completeness** (10 pts) - How much of NT preserved
- **Independence** (10 pts) - Free from later harmonization
