## Executive Summary

The Dead Sea Scrolls, New Testament apostolic quotations, and early Church testimony converge on one conclusion: **the Septuagint (LXX) frequently preserves older, more original Hebrew readings than the Masoretic Text (MT). Where the two traditions diverge, the inspired New Testament witness gives canonical priority to the LXX for Christian use.**

This is **not** “LXX is always superior.”  
This is **not** “MT is corrupt.”  

This is: **Multiple Hebrew text traditions existed side-by-side in the Second Temple period. The LXX witnesses one ancient Hebrew tradition. The MT witnesses another. The DSS prove both are pre-Christian. In theologically decisive passages, the LXX + DSS + NT align against the MT. Therefore, for Christian theology and translation, LXX readings validated by the apostles carry canonical weight.**

## Flagship Cases (The Undisputed Core)

### 1. Deuteronomy 32:43 — Christ’s Deity & Angelic Worship
**MT:** “Rejoice, O nations, with his people”  
**LXX:** “…let all the angels of God worship him”  
**DSS (4QDeut^q):** Matches LXX exactly  
**NT (Heb 1:6):** Quotes LXX verbatim  

**Verdict:** MT lost text that existed in pre-Christian Hebrew. NT canonizes the LXX reading as proof of Christ’s deity. **LXX PRIMARY.**

### 2. Psalm 145:13 — The Missing Nun Verse
**MT:** Skips נ (nun) — acrostic broken  
**LXX:** Full acrostic with nun verse  
**DSS (11QPsa):** Confirms LXX  

**Verdict:** MT suffered scribal loss. Modern translations (ESV, NIV, NASB) now restore from DSS/LXX. **LXX PRIMARY.**

### 3. Amos 9:12 — Basis for Gentile Inclusion
**MT:** “that they may possess the remnant of Edom”  
**LXX:** “that the remnant of men may seek the Lord”  
**NT (Acts 15:16-17 — James at Jerusalem Council):** Quotes LXX verbatim to settle the Gentile question  

**Verdict:** The doctrinal foundation of the Gentile mission rests on the LXX reading. **LXX CANONICAL.**

### 4. Psalm 40:6 → Hebrews 10:5 — Incarnation Theology
**MT:** “ears you have dug for me”  
**LXX:** “a body you have prepared for me”  
**NT (Heb 10:5):** Quotes LXX  

**Verdict:** The entire argument for the incarnation and superior sacrifice of Christ is built on the LXX wording. **LXX CANONICAL.**
