| Text | Reading |
|------|---------|
| **MT** | "Rejoice, O nations, with his people" |
| **LXX** | "Rejoice, O heavens... let all God's **angels worship him**" |
| **DSS (4QDeut^q)** | ✅ **Matches LXX** |
| **NT (Heb 1:6)** | ✅ **Quotes LXX** |

**Verdict:** MT missing text. LXX preserves original. NT canonizes LXX.

## 🏛️ Pre-Christian Jewish Witness

### Philo of Alexandria (20 BC - 50 AD)

- Jewish philosopher, contemporary with Jesus
- **NOT Christian** (wrote before Jesus' ministry)
- Quoted LXX **~400 times** as "Scripture"
- Treated LXX as **divinely inspired**
- Never questioned LXX vs Hebrew

**Forensic Value:** If pre-Christian Jews treated LXX as Scripture, and NT apostles did the same, then LXX had Jewish + Christian authority **before MT was standardized**.
