## QUARTER 3: THE 430 YEARS DISPUTE (Exodus 12:40)

### **3rd & Long at the Egypt Border**

**MT's offense takes over:**
"The Israelites were **in Egypt** 430 years." (Ex 12:40 MT)

**LXX's defense:**
"Hold up! They were **in Egypt AND Canaan** 430 years." (Ex 12:40 LXX)

**Key difference:**
- **MT:** 430 years = Jacob to Egypt (1876 BC) → Exodus (1446 BC) = **Egypt only**
- **LXX:** 430 years = Abraham's call (2008 BC) → Exodus = **Egypt + Canaan sojourn**

**🚨 BOOTH REVIEW — CHECKING PAUL'S COMMENTARY 🚨**

**Galatians 3:17:**
> "The Law, which came **430 years afterward**, does not annul a covenant previously ratified by God."

**Paul's clock starts at:** Abraham's covenant (Gen 12/15)  
**Paul's clock ends at:** The Law (Sinai)  
**That's 430 years** = Abraham to Exodus

**RULING:**
"**LXX gains 15 yards!** Paul's game notes support LXX's interpretation. He's measuring from Abraham's covenant to the Law, not just the Egypt portion. Advantage LXX."

**MT's coach:**
"But Paul could be using rounded numbers or a different calculation!"

**Referee:**
"Possible, but the plain reading supports LXX's play. First down, LXX."
