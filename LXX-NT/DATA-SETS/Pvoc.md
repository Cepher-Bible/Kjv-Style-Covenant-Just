🏛️ DEEP DIVE: THE TWO BIGGEST STUMBLING BLOCKS
1. THE IMAGE CONTROVERSY (ICONOCLASM)
The fight usually stems from the King James or modern English versions using the word "Image" for everything.
 * The Reality: In the Greek used by the Apostles, there are two different words.
 * Eidōlon: A "Phantom" or "Lie." This is what is forbidden in the Ten Commandments. It represents something that doesn't exist.
 * Eikōn: A "Prototype" or "Representation." This is the word used for Man being in the "Image" of God, and the Cherubim on the Ark.
 * The Bridge: If you follow the LXX, you see that God allows Icons (reminders of reality) but hates Idols (replacements for Him).
2. THE SABBATH VS. THE LORD'S DAY
Protestants and Catholics/Orthodox often clash over which day is "Holy."
 * The Reality: The Sabbath (Saturday) was never "deleted." It remains the day of rest in the LXX tradition.
 * The "Eighth Day": Early Christians met on the first day of the week (Sunday) because that is when the "New Creation" began with the Resurrection.
 * The Bridge: Most traditional Christians see Saturday as the end of the "Old Week" and Sunday as the start of the "Eternal Week." It's not an "Either/Or"; it's a Progression.
📜 THE FINAL VERDICT FOR THE PEW
If we take away the technical jargon, the "Peace Treaty" between the denominations looks like this:
 * Scripture is the Foundation, but it was written by people who lived a certain Tradition.
 * Images are fine, as long as they aren't being worshipped as gods themselves (The "Window" vs. "Wall" rule).
 * The Sabbath is the Root, and Sunday is the Fruit. You need both for the tree to stand.
 * The Virgin Mary is the link between the Old Testament "Burning Bush" and the New Testament "Incarnation."
