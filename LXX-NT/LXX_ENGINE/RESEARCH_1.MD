#### **2. LSJ (Liddell-Scott-Jones) - Comprehensive Greek Lexicon**

**What it is:**
- *Greek-English Lexicon* (1843, revised 1940)
- Covers Classical Greek (800 BC) → Koine (200 BC - 300 AD) → Byzantine (300-1400 AD)
- **Shows semantic evolution** with citations from actual texts
- Each definition has DATE and SOURCE

**Why critical:**
- Shows how words changed meaning over time
- Can isolate 200 BC meanings from later meanings
- Cites LXX usage specifically

**Example:**
- Word: δόξα (*doxa* G1391) - "glory"
- Classical Greek (500 BC): "opinion, reputation, brightness"
- Koine Greek (200 BC): LXX forced it to mean "weight/substance" (Hebrew kavod)
- **LSJ shows the shift** - can see where LXX CHANGED Greek word meanings

**Access:**
- Print: Oxford University Press
- Digital: Perseus Digital Library (FREE - http://perseus.tufts.edu)
- Logos: Full edition
