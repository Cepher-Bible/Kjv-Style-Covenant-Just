# THE NAME & IMAGE CORD: FROM GENESIS TO REVELATION
## The Golden Thread of Identity, Authority, and Representation

**Repository Status:** Systematically Validated | Scripturally Grounded | Repo-Ready  
**Theme:** Name (Internal Character/Reputation) and Image (External Representation) as Two Sides of One Coin  
**Scope:** Genesis 1 → Revelation 22 | Ancient Near East Context | Christological Fulfillment

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

**The Golden Thread:**
Throughout Scripture, **Name** (Hebrew: *Shem* - character, reputation, authority) and **Image** (Hebrew: *Tselem* - representative figure, shadow, reflection) function as an inseparable cord connecting God's identity to human vocation.

**Ancient Near East Context:**
In the ancient world, a king would set up an **Image** (statue) of himself in distant lands to represent his **Name** (authority and reputation) where he wasn't physically present. This practice provides the framework for understanding Genesis 1:26-28.

**Biblical Arc:**
1. **Creation:** Humans made as God's Image to represent His Name on earth (Gen 1:26-28)
2. **Vocation:** Adam names creation, exercising God's authority as Image-bearer (Gen 2:19-20)
3. **Fall:** Image marred/cracked, can no longer reflect Name accurately (Gen 3)
4. **Prohibition:** Idols forbidden because they're dead images falsely bearing God's Name (Exod 20:4-5)
5. **Restoration:** Jesus as perfect Image and highest Name (Heb 1:3, Phil 2:9)
6. **Renaming:** Jesus renames His followers, restoring their Image/Name alignment (Matt 16:18, Rev 2:17)
7. **Prayer:** Lord's Prayer as daily Name/Image calibration (Matt 6:9-13)
8. **Consummation:** New name on white stone, perfect Image/Name alignment forever (Rev 2:17, 22:4)

**Key Insight:**
> "Image is how we look; Name is who we are. We are Image-bearers tasked with protecting the Reputation (Name) of the Creator. When the Image is restored, the Name is glorified."

## PART 1: FOUNDATIONAL DEFINITIONS

### 1.1 Name (Hebrew: שֵׁם - *Shem*)

**More Than a Label:**
In ancient Hebrew culture, a **name** was not merely a linguistic identifier. It was a legal and moral "brand" encompassing:

- **Reputation:** What people say about you when you're not present
- **Authority:** The power to act on behalf of someone ("in the name of the king")
- **Character:** The essence of who you are (not what you do, but what you *are*)
- **History:** Your track record, your proven reliability

**Biblical Examples:**

| Name | Meaning | Character Revealed |
|------|---------|-------------------|
| **Abram → Abraham** (Gen 17:5) | "Exalted Father" → "Father of Many" | God changes destiny by changing name |
| **Jacob → Israel** (Gen 32:28) | "Deceiver" → "He Struggles with God" | Name reflects transformed character |
| **Simon → Peter** (Matt 16:18) | "Hearing/Reed" → "Rock" | Jesus renames based on future identity |

**Key Principle:**
To act "in someone's name" is to act as their **authorized agent** with their full backing. Your actions reflect on their reputation.

---

### 1.2 Image (Hebrew: צֶלֶם - *Tselem*)

**More Than Appearance:**
The Hebrew word *tselem* literally means "shadow" or "representative figure." It carries these nuances:

- **Shadow:** Not the thing itself, but its projected presence
- **Representative:** Stands in for someone in their absence
- **Reflection:** Mirrors the original (like looking in water, Gen 1:27)
- **Statue/Icon:** Physical manifestation of authority (ancient royal practice)

**Genesis 1:26-27:**
> "Then God said, 'Let us make mankind in our **image** (צֶלֶם, *tselem*), in our likeness...' So God created mankind in his own **image**, in the **image** of God he created them."

**Ancient Near East Context:**
When a king conquered distant lands, he would erect a **statue** (image) of himself there. This statue:
- Represented his authority where he wasn't physically present
- Bore his name (inscription on base)
- Reminded subjects of his power and character

**Theological Application:**
God doesn't need stone statues—He placed **living images** (humans) on earth to represent His authority, character, and rule.

---

### 1.3 The Connection: "The Mirror and the Model"

**The Image is the Vessel; the Name is the Content.**

Think of it this way:
- **Mirror (Image):** The physical form that reflects
- **Model (Name):** The character being reflected

**When the Mirror is Clear:**
- Image accurately reflects the Name
- God's character (mercy, justice, love) is seen through human actions
- The Name is "hallowed" (treated as holy) because the Image represents it well

**When the Mirror is Cracked (The Fall):**
- Image is "marred" (Gen 6:5, Rom 3:23)
- Cannot accurately reflect God's character
- The Name is "dragged through the mud" (taken in vain)

**Genesis 1:26 Unpacked:**
> "Let us make mankind in our **image** (צֶלֶם), in our **likeness** (דְּמוּת, *demut*)..."

- **Image** (tselem) = The form/structure (physical, rational, relational)
- **Likeness** (demut) = The function/character (moral capacity, reflects God's nature)

**Romans 8:29 (Restoration):**
> "For those God foreknew he also predestined to be **conformed to the image** of his Son..."

The Image is being restored in Christ.

## PART 2: ADAM'S MANDATE — NAMING AS SOVEREIGN PROXY

### 2.1 The Delegation of Sovereignty

**Genesis 2:19-20:**
> "Now the LORD God had formed out of the ground all the wild animals and all the birds in the sky. He brought them to the man **to see what he would name them**; and whatever the man called each living creature, **that was its name**."

**Ancient Near East Principle:**
In the ancient world, **the right to name = the right to rule**. This is why:
- Kings named conquered territories
- Parents named children (authority over them)
- God named the cosmos (sovereign Creator)

**The Delegation:**
When God brought animals to Adam, He wasn't asking for help because He "forgot" their names. He was **inviting the Image to exercise the Authority of the Name.**

> "I am the High King, but I am making you the Governor. You speak for Me here."

**What God Named:**
- The big categories: Day, Night, Sky, Land, Sea (Gen 1:5, 8, 10)
- The foundational structure of creation

**What Humanity Named:**
- The specific "details" of earth under their care
- Individual creatures in their stewardship

**The Cord:**
- The **Image** (Adam) uses the **Authority** (The Name/Word) to bring **Order** (The Kingdom)

---

### 2.2 Naming = Defining Essence

**The Hebrew Mindset:**
You couldn't name something until you **understood its nature**. To name an "ox" an "ox," Adam had to:
1. Observe its strength and behavior
2. Understand its place in creation
3. Discern its essence/function

**The Reflection:**
Just as God defined the universe's essence by **speaking it into existence** (Gen 1: "Let there be..."), the Image-bearer defines the "flavor" of the world by **naming it**.

**Genesis 2:19 (Critical Detail):**
> "He brought them to the man **to see what he would name them**."

God was **testing** the Image. Would Adam name things according to God's character (truth, order, goodness), or would he impose his own agenda?

**The Responsibility:**
If the Image-bearer is corrupt, they **name things falsely**:
- Calling "evil" good (Isaiah 5:20)
- Calling "bitter" sweet
- Naming injustice "justice"
- This "drags the Name through the mud"

---

### 2.3 The Co-Creative Partnership

**Genesis 2:19 (Key Phrase):**
> "And **whatever the man called each living creature, that was its name**."

**The Weight of This:**
God **legally bound Himself** to the human's decision. He respected Adam's naming authority so completely that the names *stuck*.

**Why This Matters:**
This shows the incredible **weight of being an Image-bearer**:
- We don't just "suggest" to God how reality should be
- We are **co-creators** in defining the earthly realm
- God respects the names we give to things under our care

**Psalm 8:4-6:**
> "What is mankind that you are mindful of them? ...You made them a little lower than the angels and **crowned them with glory and honor**. You made them **rulers over the works of your hands**; you put **everything under their feet**."

The "glory and honor" is the **crown of naming authority**—to define reality according to God's character.

---

### 2.4 Summary: The Naming Mandate

> **"To name is to claim."**

**When We Name Correctly:**
- We function as a clear mirror (Image)
- God's character (Name) is reflected accurately
- The world is ordered according to truth

**When We Misname:**
- We "take the Name in vain" (carry it emptily)
- We distort reality
- We fail in our Image-bearing vocation

**Why God Acts "For His Name's Sake":**
When God intervenes "for His name's sake," He is often **restoring the right names to things**:
- Calling "justice" justice when we've called it "vengeance"
- Calling "sin" sin when we've called it "freedom"
- Calling "mercy" mercy when we've called it "weakness"

His Image-bearers have stopped naming correctly, so He steps in to defend His reputation.

## PART 3: THE FALL — THE BROKEN MIRROR

### 3.1 What Happened in Genesis 3

**The Lie:**
The serpent didn't just tempt Adam and Eve to disobey—he **renamed reality**:
- Named death "life" ("You will not surely die," Gen 3:4)
- Named disobedience "wisdom" ("You will be like God, knowing good and evil," Gen 3:5)
- Named God's good command "restriction"

**The Result:**
When Adam and Eve ate, they became **corrupted namers**. Their Image was marred.

**Genesis 6:5:**
> "The LORD saw how great the wickedness of the human race had become on the earth, and that **every inclination of the thoughts of the human heart was only evil all the time**."

The mirror cracked. The Image could no longer reflect the Name accurately.

---

### 3.2 The Broken Mirror Cannot Reflect

**Romans 3:23:**
> "For all have sinned and **fall short of the glory of God**."

**"Glory" (δόξα, *doxa*) = The Weight of God's Reputation:**
- We were supposed to carry (bear) God's glory (reputation)
- We **fall short**—the Image doesn't reach the standard
- The mirror is too cracked to show God's true character

**2 Corinthians 3:18 (Restoration):**
> "And we all, who with unveiled faces contemplate the Lord's glory, are being **transformed into his image** with ever-increasing glory."

The mirror is being polished again.

---

### 3.3 "Taking the Name in Vain" — Carrying It Poorly

**Exodus 20:7:**
> "You shall not take the name of the LORD your God in vain (שָׁוְא, *shav*), for the LORD will not hold anyone guiltless who takes his name in vain."

**Common Misunderstanding:**
Most think this just means "don't use 'God' as a swear word."

**Deeper Meaning:**
- **Vain** (*shav*) = Empty, useless, false, worthless
- **"Take"** (*nasa*) = Carry, bear, lift up
- **Literal:** "Do not **carry** the name of the LORD in a way that is **empty**."

**In the Name/Image Context:**
This means: **Do not represent God's Name falsely while claiming to be His Image.**

**Practical Examples:**
- A Christian who lies: Claims God's name (Christian = "little Christ") but acts contrary to His character
- A church that abuses power: Bears God's name ("House of God") but behaves opposite to His mercy
- Anyone who says "I'm a believer" but lives in unrepentant sin: Carries the name emptily

**The Commandment's Logic:**
If you are an **Image-bearer** (representing God externally), but you **misrepresent His character** (the Name), you have made His reputation empty/false.

## PART 4: THE IDOL BREACH — FALSE IMAGES VS. TRUE IMAGE

### 4.1 The Second Commandment

**Exodus 20:4-5:**
> "You shall not make for yourself an **image** (פֶּסֶל, *pesel* - carved/graven thing) in the form of anything in heaven above or on the earth beneath or in the waters below. You shall not bow down to them or worship them."

**The Logic Through Name/Image Lens:**
If God already placed **living images** (humans) on earth to represent His **Name**, why would He want **dead statues**?

---

### 4.2 Living Images vs. Dead Statues

**The True Image (Humans):**
- ✅ **Living:** Can speak, act, love, show mercy
- ✅ **Dynamic:** Move, adapt, respond to God
- ✅ **Relational:** Can have genuine relationship with God
- ✅ **Capable:** Can accurately reflect God's character
- ✅ **The only "technology" capable of reflecting the Name**

**The False Image (Idols):**
- ❌ **Dead:** Wood, stone, gold—lifeless
- ❌ **Static:** Cannot move, speak, or act
- ❌ **Impersonal:** Just an object
- ❌ **Incapable:** Cannot show mercy, justice, or love

**Psalm 115:4-8 (Mocking Idols):**
> "But their idols are silver and gold, made by human hands. They have mouths, but cannot speak, eyes, but cannot see. They have ears, but cannot hear, noses, but cannot smell. They have hands, but cannot feel, feet, but cannot walk, nor can they utter a sound with their throats."

**The Irony:**
The idol has **eyes** but cannot see—yet God's name is **El Roi** (The God Who Sees).
The idol has **ears** but cannot hear—yet God's name is tied to **Ishmael** (God Hears).
The idol has **hands** but cannot provide—yet God's name is **Yahweh Yireh** (The LORD Provides).

**The Logic:**
To use a **dead statue** to represent the **Living God** is to insult His Name. It suggests God is as **silent and powerless** as the gold it's made of.

---

### 4.3 The "Name Hijack" (Identity Theft)

**Ancient Near East Practice:**
People performed a ritual called **"Opening of the Mouth"** on statues to "invite the name" of a god into it. They believed they could **capture** a god's power in a specific location.

**The Cord Break:**
By making an idol, humans try to **control the Name**:
- "If I have the statue in my house, I have God's authority in my pocket"
- "I can manipulate God by controlling His image"
- **This is identity theft**—hijacking God's reputation

**God's Response:**
God refuses to be "caged." He acts **"for His name's sake"** by:
- Destroying idols (Exod 32, Judges 6:25-27, 1 Kings 18)
- Proving His reputation is not tied to a physical object
- Showing He is the Living God, not bound to locations

**Jeremiah 10:5:**
> "Like a scarecrow in a cucumber field, their idols cannot speak; they must be carried because they cannot walk."

---

### 4.4 Taking the Name "In Vain" (Idol Edition)

**The Connection:**
An **idol is an empty vessel**. If you attach God's Holy Name to a piece of wood, you have made His Name "vain" (empty).

**Analogy:**
You've put a **"Designer Label" on a "Knock-off Product"**.
- The label says "Authentic"
- The product is counterfeit
- You've devalued the brand (Name) by associating it with cheap imitation (false Image)

---

### 4.5 Summary: The Image Mandate

> **"God does not want a statue of Himself in the temple because He already put a 'statue' of Himself in the world: YOU."**

**Every time we look to an idol for help:**
- We are resigning from our job as True Image-bearers
- We are saying "I don't trust God's way (living representatives)"
- We are trying to control God instead of representing Him

**1 Corinthians 6:19-20:**
> "Do you not know that your bodies are **temples of the Holy Spirit**? ...You are not your own; you were bought at a price. Therefore **honor God with your bodies**."

You *are* the temple. You *are* the image. Live like it.

- Jesus **IS** that Word made flesh

**As "The Word," Jesus:**
1. **Names reality correctly** (calls sin "sin," love "love")
2. **Restores order** (calms storms, heals diseases, casts out demons)
3. **Functions as Last Adam** (re-naming creation under God's authority)

**1 Corinthians 15:45:**

Since the first "Namers" (Adam and Eve) failed, **the Word came to rename and restore everything**.

### 6.3 The "Peter" Protocol: Name Meets Image

**Matthew 16:18:**
> "And I tell you that you are **Peter** (Πέτρος), and on this **rock** (πέτρα) I will build my church, and the gates of Hades will not overcome it."

**What Jesus Did:**
1. **Observed** the essence of the man (Simon's potential faithfulness despite failures)
2. **Named** that essence ("Rock")
3. **Authorized** the man to act in His Name ("keys of the kingdom," Matt 16:19)

**This Is Exactly What Adam Did With Animals:**
- Observe → Name → Authorize under God's rule

**By Giving Simon a New Name:**
Jesus **"re-images"** him. Simon is no longer just a fisherman; he is now a **representative (Image)** of the King's **Authority (Name)**.

---

### 6.4 The "White Stone" Promise

**Revelation 2:17:**
> "Whoever has ears, let them hear what the Spirit says to the churches. To the one who is victorious, I will give some of the hidden manna. I will also give that person **a white stone with a new name written on it, known only to the one who receives it**."

**The Final Name:**
This is the **completion of the cord**:
- The **Name** (your true identity) and the **Image** (your resurrected self) are finally **perfectly aligned**
- No one else knows this name because it represents the **unique, intimate way you reflect God's glory**

**Why "White Stone"?**
In Roman culture, white stones were used for:
- Acquittal in court (black stone = guilty, white stone = innocent)
- Admission tickets to special events
- Tokens of favor from nobility

**The Symbolism:**
- **Acquitted:** Your sins are forgiven
- **Admitted:** You belong in God's kingdom
- **Favored:** God knows you intimately and calls you by name

**Isaiah 62:2:**
> "The nations will see your vindication, and all kings your glory; you will be called by **a new name** that the mouth of the LORD will bestow."

---

### 6.5 Summary: The Repair of the Reputation

> **"For His Name's Sake, God cannot leave us with 'names' (identities) defined by our failures. He re-names us—calling us 'Saints,' 'Children,' and 'Friends'—so that our lives (The Image) finally bring honor to His Reputation (The Name)."**

**Romans 8:29:**
> "For those God foreknew he also predestined to be **conformed to the image of his Son**, that he might be the firstborn among many brothers and sisters."

The Image is being restored. The Name is being hallowed.

## PART 7: GOD'S NAMES IN THE OLD TESTAMENT

### 7.1 The Foundational Names

**1. Yahweh (יהוה - YHWH): The Self-Existent One**

**Exodus 3:14:**
> "God said to Moses, '**I AM WHO I AM** (אֶהְיֶה אֲשֶׁר אֶהְיֶה, *Ehyeh Asher Ehyeh*). This is what you are to say to the Israelites: I AM has sent me to you.'"

**Meaning:**
- Translated as **LORD** (all caps) in most English Bibles
- The **personal, covenant name** of God
- Signifies: Eternal, self-sufficient, keeper of covenants
- The name so holy Jews wouldn't speak it aloud

**Significance:**
He is the **absolute, uncreated reality**. All other existence depends on Him.

---

**2. Elohim (אֱלֹהִים): The Creator and Supreme God**

**Genesis 1:1:**
> "In the beginning **Elohim** created the heavens and the earth."

**Meaning:**
- Translated simply as "God"
- Grammatically **plural** in Hebrew, but used with **singular verbs** (plural of majesty)

**Significance:**
Supreme, infinite power. The first name of God in Scripture.

---

**3. Adonai (אֲדֹנָי): The Master and Sovereign Ruler**

**Meaning:**
- Translated as "Lord" (capital L, lowercase ord)
- Means: Master, Owner, Sovereign Ruler

**Significance:**
- Because *Yahweh* was too sacred to speak, Jews would substitute **Adonai** when reading Scripture
- Acknowledges God's ultimate authority and ownership

---

### 7.2 The "El" Titles (Traits of God)

**El** (אֵל) is the singular form of Elohim, often attached to another word to describe a specific attribute.

| Name | Meaning | Key Reference |
|------|---------|---------------|
| **El Shaddai** (אֵל שַׁדַּי) | God Almighty / All-Sufficient One | Genesis 17:1 (covenant with Abraham) |
| **El Elyon** (אֵל עֶלְיוֹן) | God Most High | Genesis 14:19 (Melchizedek blesses Abram) |
| **El Roi** (אֵל רֳאִי) | The God Who Sees | Genesis 16:13 (Hagar names God) |
| **El Olam** (אֵל עוֹלָם) | The Everlasting God | Genesis 21:33 |

---

### 7.3 Compound Names of Yahweh

**When God revealed a new aspect of His character, Israelites combined His personal name (Yahweh) with a description of what He'd done:**

| Name | Meaning | Biblical Context | Reference |
|------|---------|-----------------|-----------|
| **Yahweh Yireh** (יְהוָה יִרְאֶה) | The LORD Will Provide | Abraham names the place where God provided a ram instead of Isaac | Genesis 22:14 |
| **Yahweh Rapha** (יְהוָה רָפָא) | The LORD Who Heals | God promises to be Israel's healer if they obey | Exodus 15:26 |
| **Yahweh Nissi** (יְהוָה נִסִּי) | The LORD is My Banner | Moses builds altar after victory over Amalekites | Exodus 17:15 |
| **Yahweh Shalom** (יְהוָה שָׁלוֹם) | The LORD is Peace | Gideon builds altar after encountering God | Judges 6:24 |
| **Yahweh Roi** (יְהוָה רֹעִי) | The LORD is My Shepherd | David's psalm of trust | Psalm 23:1 |
| **Yahweh Tsidkenu** (יְהוָה צִדְקֵנוּ) | The LORD Our Righteousness | Messianic prophecy | Jeremiah 23:6 |
| **Yahweh Shammah** (יְהוָה שָׁמָּה) | The LORD is There | Vision of restored temple | Ezekiel 48:35 |

**Why This Matters for "His Name's Sake":**
When God acts "for His name's sake," He is defending His identity as:
- The Ultimate Provider (Yahweh Yireh)
- The Healer (Yahweh Rapha)
- The Shepherd (Yahweh Roi)

His actions prove His character/reputation (Name) is true.

---

### 7.4 Special Case: Hagar Names God (El Roi)

**Genesis 16:13:**
> "She gave this name to the LORD who spoke to her: '**You are El Roi**' (אֵל רֳאִי), for she said, 'I have now seen the One who sees me.'"

**Why This Is Remarkable:**
- **Only time a human names God** (and it sticks)
- Hagar was a **marginalized, runaway slave woman**
- God didn't rebuke her—He **accepted the name**

**The Context:**
Hagar fled Sarah's abuse, pregnant and alone in the desert. God found her and promised her son (Ishmael) would become a great nation.

**The Meaning:**
- **El Roi** = "The God Who Sees (Me)"
- Personal, intimate: Not just "The All-Seeing One" but "The God who sees **me**"

**Why It Matters:**
This name reveals God's character:
- He sees the suffering and outcast
- He is not distant—He intimately cares
- His reputation (Name) is built on attention to the marginalized

**Connection to Ishmael:**
God told Hagar to name her son **Ishmael** (יִשְׁמָעֵאל) = "God Hears"

Between **El Roi** (The God Who Sees) and **Ishmael** (God Hears), Hagar's story establishes: **God's very identity is tied to His senses being tuned toward human pain.**

## PART 8: GOD'S NAMES IN THE NEW TESTAMENT

### 8.1 Jesus (Yeshua) — "Yahweh Saves"

**Matthew 1:21:**
> "She will give birth to a son, and you are to give him the name **Jesus** (Ἰησοῦς, *Iesous* from Hebrew יֵשׁוּעַ, *Yeshua*), because he will **save** his people from their sins."

**The Name "Jesus" Is a Compound:**
- **Yah** (יָהּ) = Short form of Yahweh
- **Shua** (שׁוּעַ) = Salvation/Saves
- **Yeshua** = "Yahweh Saves" or "Yahweh is Salvation"

**By Giving Him This Name:**
The NT declares that the God of the OT (Yahweh) is now **acting personally** to rescue humanity.

---

### 8.2 Immanuel — "God With Us"

**Matthew 1:23 (quoting Isaiah 7:14):**
> "The virgin will conceive and give birth to a son, and they will call him **Immanuel**" (Ἐμμανουήλ, from Hebrew עִמָּנוּ אֵל, *Immanu El*) —which means, "God with us."

**The Shift:**
- OT: God's presence in pillar of fire, behind thick curtain in Temple
- NT: God is "**Right Here**" in flesh

From **El Elyon** (God Most High, distant) to **Immanuel** (God With Us, intimate).

---

### 8.3 The Seven "I AM" Statements (Claiming Yahweh's Identity)

**John's Gospel:** Jesus uses "**I AM**" (ἐγώ εἰμι, *egō eimi*) seven times, echoing Exodus 3:14 ("I AM WHO I AM").

He takes the **compound names of Yahweh** and applies them to His own ministry:

| Jesus' "I AM" Statement | OT Yahweh Name Connection | Reference |
|-------------------------|--------------------------|-----------|
| **"I am the bread of life"** | Yahweh Yireh (The LORD Provides) | John 6:35 |
| **"I am the light of the world"** | Yahweh Nissi (The LORD My Banner/Light) | John 8:12 |
| **"I am the door"** | Yahweh Shalom (The LORD is Peace/Safety) | John 10:9 |
| **"I am the good shepherd"** | Yahweh Roi (The LORD My Shepherd) | John 10:11 |
| **"I am the resurrection and the life"** | Yahweh (I AM - Eternal Life) | John 11:25 |
| **"I am the way, truth, and life"** | Yahweh Tsidkenu (The LORD Our Righteousness/Truth) | John 14:6 |
| **"I am the true vine"** | Yahweh (Sustainer, cf. Isaiah 5) | John 15:1 |

**John 8:58 (Most Explicit):**
> "Very truly I tell you," Jesus answered, "before Abraham was born, **I AM**!"

The Jews picked up stones to stone Him (John 8:59) because they understood He was claiming to be **Yahweh**.

---

### 8.4 The Shift to "Abba"

**Romans 8:15:**
> "The Spirit you received does not make you slaves... Rather, the Spirit you received brought about your adoption to sonship. And by him we cry, **'Abba, Father'**."

**Galatians 4:6:**
> "Because you are his sons, God sent the Spirit of his Son into our hearts, the Spirit who calls out, **'Abba, Father'**."

**What "Abba" Means:**
- Aramaic: אַבָּא (*Abba*)
- Term of endearment, similar to "Father" or "Papa"
- **Not** formal—it's intimate, familial

**The Radical Shift:**
- OT: The Name *Yahweh* was too holy to even speak aloud
- NT: Because of Jesus, that same Name has become one **children use to call out to their Father**

---

### 8.5 "The Name" — Authority in the NT

**Acts 4:12:**
> "Salvation is found in no one else, for there is no other **name** under heaven given to mankind by which we must be saved."

**John 14:13:**
> "And I will do whatever you ask **in my name**, so that the Father may be glorified in the Son."

**Philippians 2:9-11:**
> "Therefore God exalted him to the highest place and gave him the **name that is above every name**, that at the name of Jesus every knee should bow."

**The Focus:**
In the NT, "the Name's sake" theme centers on the **authority of Jesus**. Everything done "in His name" is done by His authority and for His reputation.

## PART 9: THE LORD'S PRAYER — DAILY NAME/IMAGE CALIBRATION

### 9.1 Introduction: The Prayer as Name/Image Manual

**Matthew 6:9-13:**
The Lord's Prayer is the **"User Manual"** for keeping the Name and Image cord in perfect tension. Jesus wasn't just giving a script—He was giving a way to **realign our identity (Image) with God's reputation (Name)**.

---

### 9.2 Line-by-Line Analysis

**1. "Our Father" — Establishing the Relationship**

**The Relation:**
- Before asking for anything, we establish relationship
- We don't start with "Master" or "Judge," but with **Abba** (Father)

**The Image Connection:**
- This confirms we are made in His likeness—**children reflecting the Father**
- We bear the family resemblance (Image) and family name (Name)

---

**2. "Hallowed be Thy Name" — The Name's Sake Clause**

**The Logic:**
- **"Hallowed"** (ἁγιασθήτω, *hagiasthētō*) = Treated as holy, set apart, unique
- This is the **"For His Name's Sake"** clause

**The Goal:**
We are asking that God's **Reputation be cleared** of all the "smudges" put on it by:
- Human failure
- False idols
- Misrepresentation by broken images

**We're saying:** "Let the world see who You **really are**."

---

**3. "Thy Kingdom Come, Thy Will Be Done" — The Image at Work**

**The Image-Bearer's Job Description:**
- Adam was tasked with bringing the **order of the Garden** to the rest of the wild earth
- We are asking for **"Heavenly Order"** to be mirrored on **"Earthly Soil"**

**The Cord:**
You cannot have the **Kingdom** (The Image's authority) without the **Hallowed Name** (The Source's character).

**Matthew 6:10:**
> "Your kingdom come, your will be done, **on earth as it is in heaven**."

**Translation:** "Let the Image (us on earth) reflect the Name (You in heaven) perfectly."

---

**4. "Give Us This Day Our Daily Bread" — Yahweh Yireh**

| The Petition | The "Name" Attribute | The "Image" Responsibility |
|--------------|---------------------|---------------------------|
| "Daily Bread" | **Yahweh Yireh** (The Provider) | Relying on the Source, not our own greed or anxiety |

**Matthew 6:25-34 Context:**
Right after the Lord's Prayer, Jesus teaches: "Do not worry about what you will eat... But seek first his kingdom and his righteousness, and all these things will be given to you."

**The Image Reflects:**
When we trust God for provision instead of hoarding/stealing/worrying, we show the world that **Yahweh Yireh** (The LORD Provides) is trustworthy.

---

**5. "Forgive Us Our Debts" — God of Mercy**

| The Petition | The "Name" Attribute | The "Image" Responsibility |
|--------------|---------------------|---------------------------|
| "Forgive us our debts, **as we also have forgiven our debtors**" | **God of Mercy** (Exodus 34:6-7) | Reflecting that mercy to others (Image-mirroring) |

**Exodus 34:6-7:**
> "The LORD, the LORD, the compassionate and gracious God, slow to anger, abounding in love and faithfulness, maintaining love to thousands, and **forgiving** wickedness, rebellion and sin."

**The Image Reflects:**
When we forgive others, we **show** (Image) that God is merciful (Name). If we don't forgive, we misrepresent Him.

**Matthew 6:14-15:**
> "For if you forgive other people when they sin against you, your heavenly Father will also forgive you. But if you do not forgive others their sins, your Father will not forgive your sins."

---

**6. "Lead Us Not Into Temptation, But Deliver Us From Evil" — Yahweh Nissi**

| The Petition | The "Name" Attribute | The "Image" Responsibility |
|--------------|---------------------|---------------------------|
| "Deliver us from the evil one" | **Yahweh Nissi** (Our Banner/Protection) | Walking in the King's authority over evil, resisting temptation |

**Exodus 17:15:**
> "Moses built an altar and called it **The LORD is my Banner** (Yahweh Nissi)."

**The Image Reflects:**
When we resist temptation and trust God's protection, we demonstrate His power over evil.

---

**7. The Doxology: "For Thine is the Kingdom, the Power, and the Glory"**

*Note: This doxology appears in some manuscripts (Byzantine text) but not in the earliest manuscripts (Alexandrian text). However, it's widely used in liturgy.*

| Element | Meaning |
|---------|---------|
| **Kingdom** | The territory of the Image (where God's rule is manifested) |
| **Power** | The authority of the Name (God's ability to act) |
| **Glory** | The visible weight of a Reputation well-kept |

**Summary:**
> "When we live this prayer, we become a **'Living Cord.'** Our actions on earth (Image) prove that the God we serve (Name) is good, powerful, and real. We don't just **say** His name is holy; we **show** it."

## PART 10: PRACTICAL APPLICATION

### 10.1 Daily Name/Image Check-In

**Morning Prayer:**
- "God, let my life today reflect Your character accurately."
- "May my words and actions 'hallow' Your name."
- "Help me name things correctly today (calling sin 'sin,' love 'love')."

**Evening Reflection:**
- "Did I misrepresent You today? (Take the Name in vain?)"
- "Did I name things falsely? (Call evil good, or good evil?)"
- "Where did the Image crack today? How do I repair it tomorrow?"

---

### 10.2 Recognizing When We've "Taken the Name in Vain"

**Self-Assessment Questions:**
1. **Did I claim to be a Christian today but act contrary to Christ's character?**
   - If yes: I carried the Name emptily (vain)
2. **Did I judge others harshly while claiming to represent the God of mercy?**
   - If yes: I misrepresented the Name through my Image
3. **Did I name something falsely today?**
   - Called my anger "righteousness"
   - Called my fear "wisdom"
   - Called my greed "security"
   - If yes: I'm naming things contrary to God's character

**Repentance:**
- Confess: "I carried Your name poorly today."
- Request: "Polish the mirror again. Restore the Image."

---

### 10.3 The "Image Check" in Relationships

**Before Speaking/Acting:**
- "Will this action reflect God's character accurately?"
- "Am I about to 'drag the Name through the mud' with this behavior?"
- "If someone saw me now, would they see a clear or cracked mirror?"

**James 3:9-10:**
> "With the tongue we praise our Lord and Father, and with it we curse human beings, who have been made in God's likeness. Out of the same mouth come praise and cursing. My brothers and sisters, this should not be."

**Application:**
If you curse someone made in God's Image, you're cursing God's craftsmanship. You're saying, "God, You made this person poorly."

---

### 10.4 Connection to Tefillin → Mark Pattern

**Cross-Reference:** This Name/Image theology connects to the Tefillin → Spirit Seal → Beast Mark analysis in the repository.

**The Pattern:**
- **Forehead Mark (Tefillin/Spirit Seal/Beast Mark):** External sign of **whose NAME you bear**
- **Hand Mark (Actions):** External display of **whose IMAGE you reflect**

**The Alignment:**
- **God's Mark:** Forehead (Name/Identity: "I belong to Yahweh") + Hand (Image/Actions: "I act like Him")
- **Beast's Mark:** Forehead (Name/Identity: "I belong to the Beast") + Hand (Image/Actions: "I serve his system")

## PART 11: CONCLUSION

### 11.1 The Golden Thread Summary

**From Genesis to Revelation, the Bible tells one coherent story:**

1. **Creation:** God makes humans as His Image to represent His Name (Gen 1:26-28)
2. **Vocation:** Humans are given naming authority to rule as God's proxies (Gen 2:19-20)
3. **Fall:** The mirror cracks; the Image can't reflect the Name accurately (Gen 3, Rom 3:23)
4. **Prohibition:** Idols are forbidden because they're dead images falsely bearing God's Name (Exod 20:4-5)
5. **Revelation:** God reveals His Name through compound names showing His character (Exodus - Judges)
6. **Incarnation:** Jesus comes as perfect Image and highest Name (Heb 1:3, Phil 2:9)
7. **Renaming:** Jesus renames His followers, restoring their Image/Name alignment (Matt 16:18)
8. **Calibration:** The Lord's Prayer teaches daily Name/Image realignment (Matt 6:9-13)
9. **Consummation:** Perfect Image/Name alignment forever—new name on white stone (Rev 2:17, 22:4)

---

### 11.2 Key Takeaway

> **"Image is how we look; Name is who we are. We are Image-bearers tasked with protecting the Reputation (Name) of the Creator. When the Image is restored, the Name is glorified."**
