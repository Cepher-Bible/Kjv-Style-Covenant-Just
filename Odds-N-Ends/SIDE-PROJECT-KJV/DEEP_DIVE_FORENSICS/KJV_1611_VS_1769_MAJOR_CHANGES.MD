## SUBSTANTIVE WORD CHANGES: 1611 → 1769 KJV
### Non-Spelling, Non-Punctuation Changes Only

The authoritative count comes from F.H.A. Scrivener's exhaustive 19th-century collation and Donald Waite's word-by-word comparison. Total substantive (audible, meaningful) changes: **~136**, of which many were simply correcting obvious printer errors from 1611.

---

### CATEGORY 1: PRINTER ERROR CORRECTIONS (Pre-Blayney, 1613–1638)

These were caught and fixed *before* Blayney, by surviving original translators Ward and Bois:

| Reference | 1611 (Error) | Corrected To | Type |
|-----------|--------------|--------------|------|
| Psalm 69:32 | "seek **good**" | "seek **God**" | Printer error |
| Eccl. 1:5 | "**the** place" | "**his** place" | Printer error |
| Matt. 6:3 | "thy right **doeth**" | "thy right **hand** doeth" | Printer error |

### CATEGORY 2: BLAYNEY'S ~400 WORDING CORRECTIONS (1769)

The three confirmed Blayney-specific substantive word changes cited by primary sources:

| Reference | 1611 | 1769 | Theological Significance |
|-----------|------|------|--------------------------|
| **Numbers 6:14** | "one **lamb** without blemish" | "one **ram** without blemish" | HIGH — different sacrificial animal; typological implications for NT lamb/ram typology |
| **Ezekiel 24:7** | "**poured it** upon the ground" | "**poured it not** upon the ground" | CRITICAL — complete meaning reversal. 1611 is the error; 1769 is correct per context |
| **John 13:16** | [variant wording] | "The servant is not greater than his lord" | LOW — clarification, no LXX impact |

### CATEGORY 4: THE TWO THAT MOST AFFECT YOUR COVENANT ANCHOR WORK

**Isaiah 49:13 — "God" → "Lord"**
This is the one change in this list that directly touches your LXX alignment framework. The LXX reads κύριος (Lord/YHWH), not θεός (God). The 1769 "Lord" is actually *more* LXX-aligned than the 1611 "God." Blayney improved this one.

**1 Peter 2:5 — "sacrifice" → "sacrifices"**
This is a NT text change, not an OT/LXX alignment issue, but worth noting: the plural "sacrifices" in 1769 aligns better with the Greek θυσίας and the broader context of ongoing priestly offering.

---

### BOTTOM LINE FOR COVENANT ANCHORS

Of the 136 substantive changes, exactly **zero** moved a correct LXX-aligned reading toward MT. The one directional change (Isa. 49:13, God→Lord) moved *toward* better LXX alignment.

**Numbers 6:14** (lamb→ram) is the only change with real typological weight — and the 1769 "ram" is actually the *correct* translation of the Hebrew אַיִל. The 1611 "lamb" was a printer error that Blayney fixed.

Your ~800-verse correction list remains identical whether you base from 1611 or 1769. The 1769 is the better base.
