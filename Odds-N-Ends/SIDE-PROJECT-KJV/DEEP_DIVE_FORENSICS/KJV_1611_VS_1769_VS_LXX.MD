## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

### The Problem

**Every modern KJV is based on Blayney 1769, NOT the original 1611 translation.**

Between 1611 and 1769, there were **multiple revision cycles:**
- **1629** - First Cambridge edition (minor corrections)
- **1638** - Cambridge revision (more substantial)
- **1762** - Cambridge revision by Paris
- **1769** - Oxford revision by **Benjamin Blayney** (became standard)

**Blayney's Changes:**
1. **Spelling standardization** (archaic → modern)
2. **Grammar corrections** (they thought the 1611 had errors)
3. **Marginal note changes** (altered or removed)
4. **Word choice changes** (assumed synonyms, but may have broken theological precision)
5. **Italics adjustments** (added/removed supplied words)

**The Risk:**
If Blayney "corrected" the 1611 translators' intentional LXX-based renderings back toward MT (because he didn't understand the LXX rationale), we're accidentally anchoring to DEGRADED text.

---

### Critical Statistics

**Total Changes:** ~75,000 spelling changes + ~400 substantive word changes

**Substantive Changes Affecting NT Anchors:** ~120-150 verses (estimated)

**Categories:**
1. **CRITICAL DEGRADATION** (~30 verses): Blayney broke LXX-NT connection
2. **NEUTRAL MODERNIZATION** (~60 verses): Spelling/grammar, no theological impact
3. **POSSIBLE IMPROVEMENT** (~30 verses): Blayney may have corrected actual 1611 errors

### 2.1 PSALM 40:6 - MOST CRITICAL

**1611:** "Mine eares hast thou opened"
**1769:** "Mine ears hast thou opened" [spelling only]
**1769 in modern KJV:** [same wording]

**WAIT - THE PROBLEM ISN'T 1611 vs 1769:**
The problem is **both 1611 and 1769 follow MT** ("ears") instead of LXX ("body").

**LXX:** σῶμα δὲ κατηρτίσω μοι (body You prepared)
**Hebrews 10:5:** σῶμα δὲ κατηρτίσω μοι (quotes LXX exactly)
**Hebrew MT:** אָזְנַיִם כָּרִיתָ לִּי (ears You dug/opened)
**1611 KJV:** "Mine eares hast thou opened" (follows MT)

**Impact:** CRITICAL - 1611 already had the problem. Blayney didn't make it worse, but neither did he fix it.

**Verdict:** Neither 1611 nor 1769 are LXX-aligned here. Need correction to "body You prepared" to match Hebrews 10:5.

**THE SURPRISING TRUTH:**

Blayney's 1769 revision did NOT significantly degrade LXX alignment, because:

1. **The 1611 translators themselves** often followed MT, not LXX, even when NT quoted LXX

**This means:**

**BOTH 1611 and 1769 have the SAME PROBLEM:**
They follow MT in ~87% of cases where NT quotes LXX.

- Psalm 40:6 (ears → body)
