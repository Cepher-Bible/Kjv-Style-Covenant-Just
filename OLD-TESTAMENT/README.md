**Covenant Language Solution:**
- Translate as "Turning" or "Covenant-Return"
- Shows repentance is MOVEMENT back to God
- Not just emotional regret - it's DIRECTION change

**Does this impose Torah?**
- ❌ NO - Prodigal son "came to himself and RETURNED" (Luke 15:17-20)

- ✅ Shows repentance is relational, not just emotional
- ❌ Does NOT mean "return to Torah observance"

**Result:** We understand CHRISTIAN repentance better, not Torah obligation.
