**The Seven Letters = Christ's battle report to His churches**

**Identity of the Woman:**
- Corporate: Israel/Church (people of God)
- Twelve stars = twelve tribes/apostles
- Clothed with sun = Glory of God
- Moon under feet = Dominion over darkness
- **Pregnant = About to bring forth THE SEED**

**GENESIS 3:15 CONNECTION:**
- "I will put enmity between you and **the woman**"
- Same woman, now glorified in Revelation

## **REVELATION 12:3-4 - THE DRAGON:**

> "And another sign appeared in heaven: behold, **a great, fiery red dragon** having seven heads and ten horns, and seven diadems on his heads. His tail drew a third of the stars of heaven and threw them to the earth. And **the dragon stood before the woman who was ready to give birth, to devour her Child as soon as it was born**." (v.3-4)

**Identity of the Dragon:**
- Verse 9 explicitly: "**that serpent of old, called the Devil and Satan**"
- **THIS IS THE GENESIS 3 SERPENT**

**Dragon's Strategy:**
- Position: Before the woman (ready to attack)
- Intent: Devour her Child (kill THE SEED at birth)
- Power: Seven heads, ten horns (full political/spiritual power)
