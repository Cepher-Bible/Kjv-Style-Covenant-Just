\ms BOOK ONE: THE BOOK OF THE WATCHERS

\c 1
\p
\v 1 The words of the blessing of Enoch — by which he blessed the elect and the righteous who will be present on the day of tribulation — when all the wicked and ungodly are to be removed.
\v 2 And Enoch — a righteous man whose eyes were opened by God — saw the vision of the Holy One in the heavens — which the angels showed me. From them I heard all things and from them I understood what I saw — but not for this generation but for a distant generation that will come.
\v 3 Concerning the elect I spoke and took up my parable concerning them:
The Holy Great One will come out from his dwelling —
\v 4 and the everlasting (*aiōnios*) God will tread upon the earth — even upon Mount Sinai — and appear from his camp — and appear in the strength of his might from the heaven of heavens.
\v 5 And all shall be afraid and the Watchers shall shake — and great fear and trembling shall seize them to the ends of the earth.
\v 6 And the high mountains shall be shaken and the high hills shall be laid low and shall melt like wax in the flame.
\v 7 And the earth shall be wholly rent in sunder and all that is upon it shall perish — and there shall be a judgment upon all.
\v 8 But with the righteous he will make peace and will protect the elect — and mercy (*eleos*) shall be upon them — and they shall all belong to God — and they shall be prosperous and blessed — and the light of God shall shine upon them.
\v 9 And behold — he comes with ten thousands of his holy ones to execute judgment upon all — and to destroy all the ungodly — and to convict all flesh of all the works of their ungodliness which they have ungodly committed — and of all the hard things which ungodly sinners have spoken against him.

\c 2
\p
\v 1 Observe all that happens in the heavens — how they do not change their paths — and the luminaries which are in the heaven — how they all rise and set in order each in its season and do not transgress against their appointed order.
\v 2 See the earth and give heed to the things which happen from winter to summer — how the whole earth is filled with water and clouds and dew and rain rest upon it.

\c 3
\p
\v 1 Observe and see how in the winter all the trees seem as though they had withered and shed all their leaves — except fourteen trees which do not lose their foliage but retain the old foliage from two to three years until the new comes.

\c 4
\p
\v 1 And again observe the days of summer — how the sun is above the earth over against it — and you seek shade and shelter because of the heat of the sun — and the earth also burns with growing heat — and you cannot tread on the earth or on a rock because of its heat.

\c 5
\p
\v 1 Observe how the trees cover themselves with green leaves and bear fruit — understand and know with regard to all his works and recognize how he that lives forever made them so.
\v 2 And all his works go on thus from year to year forever — and all the tasks which they accomplish for him and their tasks change not — but according as God has ordained so is it done.
\v 3 And see how the seas and the rivers in like manner accomplish and change not their tasks from his commandments.
\v 4 But you have not been steadfast nor done the commandments of the Lord — but you have turned away and spoken proud and hard words with your impure mouths against his greatness. O you hard-hearted — you shall find no peace.
\v 5 Therefore shall you execrate your days and the years of your life shall perish — and the years of your destruction shall be multiplied in eternal execration — and you shall find no mercy (*eleos*).
\v 6 In those days you shall make your names an eternal execration to all the righteous — and by you shall all who curse swear — and all the sinners and ungodly shall imprecate by you — and for you the ungodly there shall be a curse.
\v 7 And for the elect there shall be light and joy and peace — and they shall inherit the earth — and then shall wisdom (*sophia*) be given to the elect — and they shall all live and never again sin either through ungodliness or through pride — but they who are wise shall be humble.
\v 8 And they shall not again transgress — nor shall they sin all the days of their life — nor shall they die of the divine anger or wrath — but they shall complete the number of the days of their life and their lives shall be increased in peace — and the years of their joy shall be multiplied in eternal gladness and peace — all the days of their life.

\c 6
\p
\v 1 And it came to pass when the children of men had multiplied that in those days were born unto them beautiful and comely daughters.
\v 2 And the angels — the children of the heaven — saw and lusted after them and said to one another: Come let us choose us wives from among the children of men and beget us children.
\v 3 And Semjâzâ — who was their leader — said unto them: I fear you will not indeed agree to do this deed — and I alone shall have to pay the penalty of this great sin.
\v 4 And they all answered him and said: Let us all swear an oath and all bind ourselves by mutual imprecations not to abandon this plan but to do this thing.
\v 5 Then swore they all together and bound themselves by mutual imprecations upon it.
\v 6 And they were in all two hundred who descended in the days of Jared on the summit of Mount Hermon — and they called it Mount Hermon because they had sworn and bound themselves by mutual imprecations upon it.
\v 7 And these are the names of their leaders: Sêmîazâz their leader — Arâkîba — Râmêêl — Kôkabîêl — Tâmîêl — Râmîêl — Dânêl — Êzêqêêl — Barâqîjâl — Asâêl — Armârôs — Batârêl — Anânêl — Zaqîêl — Samsâpêêl — Satarêl — Tûrêl — Jômjâêl — Sariêl.
\v 8 These are their chiefs of tens.

\c 7
\p
\v 1 And all the others together with them took unto themselves wives — and each chose for himself one — and they began to go in unto them and to defile themselves with them — and they taught them charms and enchantments and the cutting of roots — and made them acquainted with plants.
\v 2 And they became pregnant and bore great giants whose height was three thousand ells —
\v 3 who consumed all the acquisitions of men. And when men could no longer sustain them —
\v 4 the giants turned against them and devoured mankind.
\v 5 And they began to sin against birds and beasts and reptiles and fish — and to devour one another's flesh and drink the blood.
\v 6 Then the earth laid accusation against the lawless ones.

\c 8
\p
\v 1 And Azâzêl taught men to make swords and knives and shields and breastplates — and made known to them the metals of the earth and the art of working them — and bracelets and ornaments and the use of antimony and the beautifying of the eyelids and all kinds of costly stones and all colouring tinctures.
\v 2 And there arose much godlessness and they committed fornication and they were led astray and became corrupt in all their ways.
\v 3 Semjâzâ taught enchantments and root-cuttings — Armârôs the resolving of enchantments — Barâqîjâl taught astrology — Kôkabêl the constellations — Ezêqêêl the knowledge of the clouds — Araqiêl the signs of the earth — Shamsiêl the signs of the sun — and Sariêl the course of the moon. And as men perished they cried out and their cry went up to heaven.

\c 9
\p
\v 1 And then Michael and Uriel and Raphael and Gabriel looked down from heaven and saw much blood being shed upon the earth — and all lawlessness being wrought upon the earth.
\v 2 And they said one to another: The earth made without inhabitant cries the voice of their crying up to the gates of heaven.
\v 3 And now to you the holy ones of heaven the souls of men make their suit saying: Bring our cause before the Most High.
\v 4 And they said to the Lord of the ages: Lord of lords — God of gods — King of kings — and God of the ages — the throne of your glory stands unto all the generations of the ages and your name is holy and glorious and blessed unto all the ages.
\v 5 You have made all things and you have power over all things — and all things are naked and open in your sight — and you see all things — and nothing can hide itself from you.
\v 6 See then what Azâzêl has done — who has taught all unrighteousness on earth and revealed the eternal secrets which were preserved in heaven — which men were striving to learn —
\v 7 and Semjâzâ — to whom you have given authority to bear rule over his associates.
\v 8 And they have gone to the daughters of men upon the earth and have slept with the women and have defiled themselves and revealed to them all kinds of sins.
\v 9 And the women have borne giants and the whole earth has thereby been filled with blood and unrighteousness.
\v 10 And now behold the souls of those who have died are crying and making their suit to the gates of heaven — and their lamentations have ascended — and cannot cease because of the lawless deeds which are wrought on the earth.
\v 11 And you know all things before they come to pass and you see these things and you have permitted them and say nothing to us about these things. What are we to do with them about these things?

\c 10
\p
\v 1 Then said the Most High — the Holy and Great One — and he sent Uriel to the son of Lamech and said to him:
\v 2 Go to Noah and tell him in my name: Hide yourself — and reveal to him the end that is approaching — that the whole earth will be destroyed and a deluge is about to come upon the whole earth — and will destroy all that is on it.
\v 3 And now instruct him that he may escape and his seed may be preserved for all the generations of the world.
\v 4 And again the Lord said to Raphael: Bind Azâzêl hand and foot and cast him into the darkness — and make an opening in the desert which is in Dûdâêl — and cast him therein.
\v 5 And place upon him rough and jagged rocks and cover him with darkness — and let him abide there forever — and cover his face that he may not see light.
\v 6 And on the day of the great judgment he shall be cast into the fire.
\v 7 And heal the earth which the angels have corrupted — and proclaim the healing of the earth that I will heal its plague and that all the children of men will not perish through all the secret things that the Watchers have disclosed and have taught their sons.
\v 8 And the whole earth has been corrupted through the works that were taught by Azâzêl — to him ascribe all sin.
\v 9 And to Gabriel said the Lord: Proceed against the bastards and the reprobates and against the children of fornication — and destroy the children of the Watchers from among men — and cause them to go forth and send them one against the other that they may destroy each other in battle.
\v 10 For length of days shall they not have — and no request that you make for them shall be granted to their fathers on their behalf — for they hope to live an eternal life and that each one of them will live five hundred years.
\v 11 And the Lord said to Michael: Go and bind Semjâzâ and his associates who have associated themselves with women so as to have defiled themselves with them in all their uncleanness.
\v 12 And when their sons have slain one another and they have seen the destruction of their beloved ones — bind them fast for seventy generations in the valleys of the earth until the day of their judgment and of their consummation — until the eternal judgment is accomplished.
\v 13 In those days they shall be led off to the abyss of fire and to the torment and the prison in which they shall be confined forever.
\v 14 And whosoever shall be condemned and destroyed will from thenceforth be bound together with them to the end of all generations.
\v 15 And destroy all the spirits of the reprobate and the children of the Watchers — because they have wronged mankind.
\v 16 Destroy all wrong from the face of the earth and let every evil work come to an end — and let the plant of righteousness and truth appear — and it shall prove a blessing — the works of righteousness and truth shall be planted in truth and joy forevermore.
\v 17 And then shall all the righteous escape and shall live until they beget thousands of children and all the days of their youth and their old age shall they complete in peace.
\v 18 And then shall the whole earth be tilled in righteousness and shall all be planted with trees and be full of blessing.
\v 19 And all desirable trees shall be planted on it and they shall plant vines on it — and the vine which they plant thereon shall yield wine in abundance — and as for all the seed which is sown thereon each measure shall bear a thousand — and each measure of olives shall yield ten presses of oil.
\v 20 And cleanse the earth from all oppression and from all unrighteousness and from all sin and from all godlessness — and all the uncleanness that is wrought upon the earth destroy from off the earth.
\v 21 And all the children of men shall become righteous and all nations shall offer adoration and shall praise me and all shall worship me.
\v 22 And the earth shall be cleansed from all defilement and from all sin and from all punishment and from all torment — and I will never again send a flood upon it from generation to generation and forever.

\c 11
\p
\v 1 And in those days I will open the store chambers of blessing which are in the heaven so as to send them down upon the earth over the work and labour of the children of men.
\v 2 And truth and peace shall be associated together throughout all the days of the world and throughout all the generations of men.

\c 12
\p
\v 1 Before these things Enoch was hidden — and no one of the children of men knew where he was hidden and where he abode — and what had become of him.
\v 2 And his activities had to do with the Watchers and his days were with the holy ones.
\v 3 And I Enoch was blessing the Lord of majesty and the King of the ages — and lo — the Watchers called me Enoch the scribe and said to me:
\v 4 Enoch you scribe of righteousness — go declare to the Watchers of the heaven who have left the high heaven the holy eternal place and have defiled themselves with women and have done as the children of earth do and have taken unto themselves wives: You have wrought great destruction on the earth —
\v 5 and you shall have no peace nor forgiveness of sin — and inasmuch as they delight themselves in their children —
\v 6 the murder of their beloved ones shall they see — and over the destruction of their children shall they lament — and shall make supplication unto eternity but mercy (*eleos*) and peace shall you not attain.

\c 13
\p
\v 1 And Enoch went and said: Azâzêl — you shall have no peace — a severe sentence has gone out against you to put you in bonds —
\v 2 and you shall not have peace and respite and entreaty — because of the unrighteousness which you have taught and because of all the works of godlessness and unrighteousness and sin which you have shown to men.
\v 3 Then I went and spoke to them all together — and they were all afraid and fear and trembling seized them.
\v 4 And they besought me to draw up a petition for them that they might find forgiveness and to read their petition in the presence of the Lord of heaven.
\v 5 For from thenceforward they could not speak with him nor lift up their eyes to heaven for shame of their sins for which they had been condemned.
\v 6 Then I wrote out their petition and the prayer in regard to their spirits and their deeds individually and in regard to their requests that they should have forgiveness and length of days.
\v 7 And I went off and sat down at the waters of Dan in the land of Dan — to the south of the west of Hermon — I read their petition till I fell asleep.
\v 8 And behold a dream came to me and visions fell down upon me and I saw visions of chastisement — and a voice came bidding me to tell it to the sons of heaven and reprimand them.
\v 9 And when I awaked I came to them and they were all sitting gathered together weeping in Abelsjâîl which is between Lebanon and Sênêsêr with their faces covered.
\v 10 And I recounted before them all the visions which I had seen in sleep and I began to speak the words of righteousness and to reprimand the heavenly Watchers.

\c 14
\p
\v 1 The book of the words of righteousness and of the reprimand of the eternal Watchers in accordance with the command of the Holy Great One in that vision.
\v 2 I saw in my sleep what I will now say with a tongue of flesh and with the breath (*pneuma*) of my mouth which the Great One has given to men to converse therewith and to understand with the heart.
\v 3 As he has created and given to man the power of understanding the word of wisdom (*sophia*) — so has he created me also and given me the power of reprimanding the Watchers — the children of heaven.
\v 4 I wrote out your petition and in my vision it appeared thus — that your petition will not be granted unto you throughout all the days of eternity — and that judgment has been finally passed upon you — yea your petition will not be granted unto you.
\v 5 And from henceforth you shall not ascend into heaven unto all eternity — and in bonds of the earth the decree has gone forth to bind you for all the days of the world.
\v 6 And that previously you shall have seen the destruction of your beloved sons and you shall have no pleasure in them — but they shall fall before you by the sword.
\v 7 And your petition on their behalf shall not be granted — nor yet on your own even though you weep and pray and speak all the words contained in the writing which I have written.
\v 8 And the vision was shown to me thus: Behold in the vision clouds invited me and a mist summoned me — and the course of the stars and the lightnings sped and hastened me — and the winds in the vision caused me to fly and lifted me upward and bore me into heaven.
\v 9 And I went in until I drew nigh to a wall which was built of crystals and surrounded by tongues of fire — and it began to affright me.
\v 10 And I went into the tongues of fire and drew nigh to a large house which was built of crystals — and the walls of the house were like a tessellated floor made of crystals — and its groundwork was of crystal.
\v 11 Its ceiling was like the path of the stars and the lightnings and between them were fiery cherubim and their heaven was clear as water.
\v 12 A flaming fire surrounded the walls and its portals blazed with fire.
\v 13 And I entered into that house and it was hot as fire and cold as ice — there were no delights of life therein. Fear covered me and trembling took hold upon me.
\v 14 And as I quaked and trembled I fell upon my face.
\v 15 And I beheld a vision and lo — there was a second house greater than the former — and the entire portal stood open before me and it was built of flames of fire.
\v 16 And in every respect it so excelled in splendour and magnificence and extent that I cannot describe to you its splendour and its extent.
\v 17 And its floor was of fire and above it were lightnings and the path of the stars and its ceiling also was flaming fire.
\v 18 And I looked and saw therein a lofty throne — its appearance was as crystal and the wheels thereof as the shining sun and there was the vision of cherubim.
\v 19 And from underneath the throne came streams of flaming fire so that I could not look thereon.
\v 20 And the Great Glory sat thereon and his raiment shone more brightly than the sun and was whiter than any snow.
\v 21 None of the angels could enter and could behold his face by reason of the magnificence and glory — and no flesh could behold him.
\v 22 The flaming fire was around and about him and a great fire stood before him — and none around could draw nigh him — ten thousand times ten thousand stood before him — yet he needed no counsellor.
\v 23 And the most holy ones who were nigh to him did not leave by night nor depart from him.
\v 24 And until then I had been prostrate on my face trembling — and the Lord called me with his own mouth and said to me: Come hither Enoch and hear my word.
\v 25 And one of the holy ones came to me and waked me — and he made me rise up and approach the door — and I bowed my face downwards.

\c 15
\p
\v 1 And he answered and said to me and I heard his voice: Fear not Enoch you righteous man and scribe of righteousness — approach hither and hear my voice.
\v 2 And go say to the Watchers of heaven who have sent you to intercede for them: You should intercede for men and not men for you —
\v 3 wherefore have you left the high holy and eternal heaven and lain with women and defiled yourselves with the daughters of men and taken to yourselves wives — done like the children of earth and begotten giants as your sons?
\v 4 And though you were holy spiritual (*pneumatikoi*) — living the eternal life — you have defiled yourselves with the blood of women and have begotten children with the blood of flesh and as the children of men have lusted after flesh and blood as those also do who die and perish.
\v 5 Therefore have I given them wives also that they might impregnate them and beget children by them that thus nothing might be wanting to them on earth.
\v 6 But you were formerly spiritual (*pneumatikoi*) — living the eternal life and immortal for all generations of the world.
\v 7 And therefore I have not appointed wives for you — for as for the spiritual ones of the heaven in heaven is their dwelling.
\v 8 And now the giants who are produced from the spirits and flesh shall be called evil spirits (*pneumata ponēra*) upon the earth — and on the earth shall be their dwelling.
\v 9 Evil spirits (*pneumata ponēra*) have proceeded from their bodies because they are born from men and from the holy Watchers — their beginning is of primal origin — they shall be evil spirits (*pneumata ponēra*) on earth and evil spirits shall they be called.
\v 10 As for the spirits of heaven in heaven shall be their dwelling but as for the spirits of the earth which were born upon the earth on the earth shall be their dwelling.
\v 11 And the spirits of the giants afflict oppress destroy attack do battle and work destruction on the earth and cause trouble — they take no food but nevertheless hunger and thirst and cause offences.
\v 12 And these spirits shall rise up against the children of men and against the women because they have proceeded from them.

\c 16
\p
\v 1 From the days of the slaughter and destruction and death of the giants — from the souls of whose flesh the spirits having gone forth shall destroy without incurring judgment — thus shall they destroy until the day of the consummation — the great judgment in which the age (*aiōn*) shall be consummated over the Watchers and the godless — yea it shall be wholly consummated.
\v 2 And now as to the Watchers who have sent you to intercede for them — who had been aforetime in heaven —
\v 3 say to them: You have been in heaven but all the mysteries had not yet been revealed to you and you knew worthless ones — and these in the hardness of your hearts you have made known to the women and through these mysteries women and men work much evil on earth.
\v 4 Say to them therefore: You have no peace.

\c 17
\p
\v 1 And they took and brought me to a place in which those who were there were like flaming fire — and when they wished they appeared as men.
\v 2 And they brought me to the place of darkness and to a mountain the point of whose summit reached to heaven.
\v 3 And I saw the places of the luminaries and the treasuries of the stars and of the thunder and in the uttermost depths where were a fiery bow and arrows and their quiver and a fiery sword and all the lightnings.
\v 4 And they took me to the living waters and to the fire of the west which receives every setting of the sun.
\v 5 And I came to a river of fire in which the fire flows like water and discharges itself into the great sea towards the west.
\v 6 I saw the great rivers and came to the great river and to the great darkness and went to the place where no flesh walks.
\v 7 I saw the mountains of the darkness of winter and the place whence all the waters of the deep flow.
\v 8 I saw the mouths of all the rivers of the earth and the mouth of the deep.

\c 18
\p
\v 1 I saw the treasuries of all the winds — I saw how he had furnished with them the whole creation and the firm foundations of the earth.
\v 2 And I saw the cornerstone of the earth — I saw the four winds which bear the earth and the firmament of the heaven.
\v 3 And I saw how the winds stretch out the vaults of heaven and have their station between heaven and earth — these are the pillars of the heaven.
\v 4 I saw the winds of heaven which turn and bring the circumference of the sun and all the stars to their setting.
\v 5 I saw the winds on the earth carrying the clouds — I saw the paths of the angels.
\v 6 I saw at the end of the earth the firmament of the heaven above and I proceeded and saw a place which burns day and night where there are seven mountains of magnificent stones — three towards the east and three towards the south.
\v 7 And as for those towards the east — one was of coloured stone and one of pearl and one of jacinth — and those towards the south of red stone.
\v 8 But the middle one reached to heaven like the throne of God — of alabaster and the summit of the throne was of sapphire.
\v 9 And I saw a flaming fire. And beyond these mountains —
\v 10 is a region the end of the great earth — there the heavens were completed.
\v 11 And I saw a deep abyss with columns of heavenly fire and among them I saw columns of fire fall — which were beyond measure alike towards the height and towards the depth.
\v 12 And beyond that abyss I saw a place which had no firmament of the heaven above and no firmly founded earth beneath it — there was no water upon it and no birds — but it was a waste and horrible place.
\v 13 I saw there seven stars like great burning mountains and to me when I inquired regarding them —
\v 14 the angel said: This place is the end of heaven and earth — this has become a prison for the stars and the host of heaven.
\v 15 And the stars which roll over the fire are they which have transgressed the commandment of the Lord in the beginning of their rising because they did not come forth at their appointed times.
\v 16 And he was wroth with them and bound them till the time when their guilt should be consummated even for ten thousand years.

\c 19
\p
\v 1 And Uriel said to me: Here shall stand the angels who have connected themselves with women — and their spirits assuming many different forms are defiling mankind and shall lead them astray into sacrificing to demons as gods — here shall they stand till the day of the great judgment in which they shall be judged till they are made an end of.
\v 2 And the women also of the angels who went astray shall become sirens.
\v 3 And I Enoch alone saw the vision — the ends of all things — and no man shall see as I have seen.

\c 20
\p
\v 1 And these are the names of the holy angels who watch.
\v 2 Uriel — one of the holy angels who is over the world and over Tartarus.
\v 3 Raphael — one of the holy angels who is over the spirits (*pneumata*) of men.
\v 4 Raguel — one of the holy angels who takes vengeance on the world of the luminaries.
\v 5 Michael — one of the holy angels to wit he that is set over the best part of mankind and over chaos.
\v 6 Saraqâêl — one of the holy angels who is set over the spirits (*pneumata*) of men who sin in the spirit (*pneuma*).
\v 7 Gabriel — one of the holy angels who is over Paradise and the serpents and the Cherubim.
\v 8 Remiel — one of the holy angels whom God set over those who rise.

\c 21
\p
\v 1 And I proceeded to where things were chaotic.
\v 2 And I saw there something horrible — I saw neither a heaven above nor a firmly founded earth but a place chaotic and horrible.
\v 3 And there I saw seven stars of the heaven bound together in it like great mountains and burning with fire.
\v 4 Then I said: For what sin are they bound and on what account have they been cast in hither?
\v 5 Then said Uriel one of the holy angels who was with me and was chief over them: Enoch why do you ask and why are you eager for the truth?
\v 6 These are of the number of the stars of heaven which have transgressed the commandment of the Lord and are bound here till ten thousand years — the time entailed by their sins — are consummated.
\v 7 And from thence I went to another place which was still more horrible than the former and I saw a horrible thing — a great fire there which burnt and blazed and the place was cleft as far as the abyss — being full of great descending columns of fire — neither its extent or magnitude could I see nor could I conjecture.
\v 8 Then I said: How fearful is the place and how terrible to look upon.
\v 9 Then Uriel answered me — one of the holy angels who was with me — and said unto me: Enoch why have you such fear and affright? And I answered: Because of this fearful place and because of the spectacle of the pain.
\v 10 And he said unto me: This place is the prison of the angels and here they will be imprisoned forever.

\c 22
\p
\v 1 And thence I went to another place and he mountain and of hard rock.
\v 2 And there was in it four hollow places — deep and wide and very smooth. How smooth are the hollow places and deep and dark to look at.
\v 3 Then Raphael answered one of the holy angels who was with me and said unto me: These hollow places have been created for this very purpose — that the spirits (*pneumata*) of the souls of the dead should assemble therein — yea that all the souls of the children of men should assemble here.
\v 4 And these places have been made to receive them till the day of their judgment and till their appointed period — till the period appointed — till the great judgment comes upon them.
\v 5 I saw the spirit (*pneuma*) of a dead man — and his voice went forth to heaven and made suit.
\v 6 And I asked Raphael the angel who was with me and I said unto him: This spirit which makes suit — whose is it — whose voice goes forth and makes suit to heaven?
\v 7 And he answered me saying: This is the spirit (*pneuma*) which went forth from Abel whom his brother Cain slew — and he makes his suit against him till his seed is destroyed from the face of the earth and his seed is annihilated from among the seed of men.
\v 8 Then I asked regarding it and regarding all the hollow places: Why is one separated from the other?
\v 9 And he answered me and said unto me: These three have been made that the spirits (*pneumata*) of the dead might be separated. And such a division has been made for the spirits (*pneumata*) of the righteous in which there is the bright spring of water.
\v 10 And such has been made for sinners when they die and are buried in the earth and judgment has not been executed upon them in their lifetime.
\v 11 Here their spirits (*pneumata*) shall be set apart in this great pain until the great day of judgment and punishment and torment of those who curse forever and retribution for their spirits (*pneumata*).
\v 12 There he shall bind them forever. And such a division has been made for the spirits (*pneumata*) of those who make their suit — who make disclosures concerning their destruction when they were slain in the days of the sinners.
\v 13 Such has been made for the spirits (*pneumata*) of men who were not righteous but sinners and were complete in transgression — and of the transgressors they shall be companions — but their spirits (*pneumata*) shall not be slain in the day of judgment nor shall they be raised from thence.
\v 14 Then I blessed the Lord of glory and said: Blessed be my Lord the Lord of righteousness who rules forever.

\c 23
\p
\v 1 From thence I went to another place to the west of the ends of the earth.
\v 2 And I saw a burning fire which ran without resting and paused not from its course by day or by night but ran regularly.
\v 3 And I asked saying: What is this which rests not?
\v 4 Then Raguel one of the holy angels who was with me answered me and said unto me: This course of fire which you have seen is the fire in the west which persecutes all the luminaries of heaven.

**1 ENOCH IN THE CAE ARCHITECTURE**

1 Enoch occupies a structurally unique position in the Cepher Hakam project: it is not a canonical book in the standard sense but the single most cited non-Protocanonical source in the NT — directly quoted by Jude (1:9 = 1 Enoch 1:9) and extensively echoed across Revelation / 2 Peter / Matthew / Romans / 1 Corinthians / Hebrews. More critically for the CAE, 1 Enoch is the primary Second Temple document that mediates between the LXX's Genesis-through-Daniel narrative and the NT's apocalyptic / Christological / pneumatological framework. Without 1 Enoch, large sections of the NT's theological vocabulary have no recoverable background — particularly: the *Son of Man* enthronement tradition / the *Lord of Spirits* divine-name theology / the *Elect One* pre-existence and cosmic-judicial function / the *pneumata ponēra* (evil spirits) demonology / the *zōē aiōnios* (eternal life) inheritance language for the righteous / and the *parousia*-with-ten-thousands judgment-scene.

The CAE's engagement with 1 Enoch serves three distinct functions:

(1) **Canonical-bridge function:** 1 Enoch demonstrates the continuous theological tradition flowing from the LXX's Genesis and Isaiah through the Second Temple period into the NT — the *Logos / Sophia / Pneuma* theology of the NT is not a Hellenistic innovation but the culmination of a trajectory visible in the LXX, developed in 1 Enoch and the Wisdom tradition, and completed in the NT's Christological application.

(2) **Vocabulary-source function:** Key NT theological terms receive their pre-NT definition in 1 Enoch — the *Son of Man* as a pre-existent heavenly figure with judicial authority / the *Elect One* chosen before creation / *zōē aiōnios* as the inheritance of the righteous / *pneumata ponēra* as the disembodied spirits of the Nephilim giants. These definitions are presupposed by NT usage — the NT authors do not define these terms because their communities already knew them through 1 Enoch.

(3) **Prophetic-authority function:** Jude's *Enoch prophēteusen* — "Enoch prophesied" — establishes that the Holy Breath-of-life operating through the NT authors recognized authentic prophetic content in 1 Enoch. This does not canonize all of 1 Enoch but it establishes that the prophetic tradition within 1 Enoch (particularly the parousia-judgment of Book One and the Son of Man enthronement of the Parables) carries genuine prophetic authority recognized by the NT canon itself.

**THE FIVE BOOKS — CAE ANALYSIS**

**BOOK ONE: THE BOOK OF THE WATCHERS (cc. 1–36)**

The foundational book — the oldest stratum of 1 Enoch and the most directly influential on the NT. Five primary CAE nodes:

**1. The *parousia*-judgment prologue (cc. 1–5):**

The opening *parousia* oracle of 1:3–9 is the single most important pre-NT judgment-theophany text in the Second Temple tradition. The structure:

*1:3–4:* The Holy Great One comes from his dwelling / the everlasting (*aiōnios*) God treads the earth / mountains shaken / the creation trembles before the divine approach
*1:5:* The Watchers shake / fear seizes all to the ends of the earth — the cosmic scope of the theophany
*1:8:* With the righteous — peace / mercy (*eleos*) / belonging to God / prosperity / the light of God shines — the Wisdom-register eschatological state
*1:9:* The Lord comes with ten thousands of his holy ones — to execute judgment on all — to convict all the ungodly of all their deeds of ungodliness

**1:9 as the Jude 14–15 citation base:** The NT's only direct 1 Enoch citation reproduces this verse nearly verbatim. The CAE function: the *parousia*-with-holy-ones judgment-structure of 1 Enoch 1:9 is the source-tradition for the NT *parousia* theology. The convergence chain:

**1 Enoch 1:9 → Zechariah 14:5 LXX (*hēxei kyrios ho theos mou kai pantes hoi hagioi met' autou*) → 1 Thessalonians 3:13 (*en tē parousia tou kyriou Iēsou meta pantōn tōn hagiōn autou*) → Jude 14–15 → Matthew 25:31 (*hotan de elthē ho huios tou anthrōpou en tē doxē autou kai pantes hoi angeloi met' autou*) → Revelation 19:14 (*ta strateumata ta en tō ouranō*)**

Six-witness *parousia*-with-holy-ones chain complete. **LXX-primacy argument:** Zechariah 14:5 LXX is the canonical bridge between 1 Enoch 1:9 and the NT *parousia* tradition — the same theophany structure (Lord comes / holy ones accompany / judgment enacted) appears in all six witnesses, confirming a continuous tradition from pre-exilic prophecy through the Second Temple period into the NT.

**The cc. 2–5 creation-witness section:** The observation of the natural order's obedience to divine command contrasted with humanity's disobedience (5:4 — *you have not been steadfast nor done the commandments of the Lord*) is the Wisdom register's creation-conscience argument — the same argument Paul deploys in Romans 1:20 (*ta gar aorata autou apo ktiseōs kosmou tois poiēmasin nooumena kathoratai* — his invisible attributes from the creation of the world are perceived in the things made). **Cross-link: Romans 1:19–20 = 1 Enoch 2–5** — the creation's order as the ground of human accountability. The *sophia* given to the elect in 5:7–8 (*wisdom shall be given to the elect / they shall all live and never again sin*) is the Wisdom-register eschatological telos — the *sophia* that was withheld from the Watchers (16:3 — *all the mysteries had not yet been revealed to you*) is given in fullness to the righteous elect.

**2. The Watcher narrative (cc. 6–16):**

The foundational Second Temple angelology-and-demonology text. Five CAE nodes:

*The *archē* abandonment (6:1–6 / Jude 6):* The Watchers' descent is defined by their abandonment of their proper *archē* (domain / station) and *oikētērion* (dwelling). **CAE Folly-register structural analysis:** the Folly register's cosmic origin is the refusal of proper station — Semjâzâ and his companions chose *thelēma* (their own desire — the daughters of men) over *taxis* (their appointed order in heaven). This is the cosmic form of Genesis 3's Eden-transgression: desire overriding appointed order / the *epithymia* (desire) that 2 Peter 1:4 identifies as the mechanism of *phthora* (corruption). The Watcher-narrative is the cosmic scale of the same Folly-register pattern visible in Adam and Eve — the *will toward what is not mine to take* — enacted by beings of cosmic power with cosmic consequences.

*Azâzêl as the sin-bearer (10:8):* *Whole earth corrupted through the works taught by Azâzêl — to him ascribe all sin.* The scapegoat tradition (Leviticus 16:8/10/26 LXX — *Azazel* as the name associated with the wilderness-sent goat) receives its Second Temple elaboration here: Azâzêl taught the corrupting arts / to him is ascribed all sin / he is bound and cast into the desert pit until the day of the great judgment. **CAE Chain 1 (*hilastērion*) pre-figuration note:** the LXX Leviticus scapegoat ritual (the goat bearing the sins into the wilderness — *aphorismenon eis aphesian* — set apart for release / sent to Azâzêl) is the atonement-ritual whose anti-type is the 1 Enoch judgment on Azâzêl — the ritual enacts proleptically what the eschatological judgment accomplishes finally. The *hilastērion* tradition's background includes not only the *kapporet* / mercy seat of Exodus 25 LXX but the Azâzêl / scapegoat ritual of Leviticus 16 LXX as its complementary pole.

*The Michael / Raphael / Gabriel / Uriel quadrarchy (cc. 9–10 / 20):* The four archangels as the divine agents of cosmic administration — Michael (*merciful and long-suffering* — 40:9) / Raphael (*over all the diseases and wounds of men* — 40:9) / Gabriel (*over all the powers* — 40:9) / Phanuel (*over repentance unto hope of those who inherit eternal life* — 40:9) / Uriel (*over the world and Tartarus* — 20:2). **NT angel-tradition background:** the named archangels of 1 Enoch are the background for: Michael's dispute with the devil over Moses's body (Jude 9 = Testament of Moses / 1 Enoch tradition) / Gabriel's annunciation role (Luke 1:19/26) / the *heavenly host* of Luke 2:13 / the *archangel's voice* of 1 Thessalonians 4:16 / the four living creatures of Revelation 4–5 (structurally parallel to the four archangels of 1 Enoch 40).

*The *pneumata ponēra* (evil spirits) demonology (15:8–12):* The origin of evil spirits in 1 Enoch is the most detailed pre-NT demonology available: the Nephilim giants died / their disembodied spirits became *pneumata ponēra* upon the earth / they afflict / oppress / destroy / attack / cause trouble / hunger and thirst without eating. **NT demonology background:** the *pneumata ponēra* (evil/unclean spirits) of the Synoptics (Matthew 10:1 / 12:43–45 / Mark 1:23–27 / Luke 8:29 etc.) presuppose exactly this 1 Enoch anthropology — the unclean spirits are disembodied entities seeking *oikia* (dwelling) in human bodies because they are *sarkos* (flesh) born but *pneuma* without body. The Synoptic exorcism tradition is intelligible against the 1 Enoch *pneumata ponēra* background in a way it is not against any other available tradition.

*The eternal chains / darkness prison (10:4–6 / 18:14 / 21:10):* The fallen Watchers bound in chains under darkness until the judgment of the great day. **Two-witness lock: 1 Enoch 10:4–6 / 18:14 / 21:10 = Jude 6 (*desmois aidiois hypo zophon tetērēken*) = 2 Peter 2:4 (*seirais zophou tartarōsas paredōken eis krisin tēroumenous*)**. The *zophos* (darkness) / *desmoi* (chains) / *tēreō* (kept/guarded) vocabulary of both Jude and 2 Peter is taken directly from the 1 Enoch Watcher-imprisonment tradition. The *Tartarus* of 2 Peter 2:4 (*tartarōsas*) is the Greek mythological term used in 1 Enoch 20:2 (Uriel *over Tartarus*) — the only NT use of *tartarōsas* — confirming 2 Peter's direct dependence on the Greek 1 Enoch tradition.

**3. The cosmic geography (cc. 17–36):**

The throne-vision of c.14 is the most important single chapter in the pre-NT heavenly-throne tradition. **CAE throne-vision chain:**

**Isaiah 6 LXX (the *kadosh kadosh kadosh* / seraphim) + Ezekiel 1 LXX (the *chariot-throne* / living creatures / wheels) + 1 Enoch 14 (the crystal throne / fiery cherubim / the Great Glory / falling on face before the divine presence) → Daniel 7:9–14 LXX (*Ancient of Days / Son of Man / throne of glory*) → Revelation 4–5 (the heavenly throne / four living creatures / the Lamb / the *axios* — worthy) + Hebrews 12:18–29 (the *phlogos pyros* — flame of fire / the unapproachable light)**

The 1 Enoch 14 throne vision is the structural bridge between the LXX prophetic throne-visions (Isaiah 6 / Ezekiel 1) and the NT heavenly-throne tradition (Revelation 4–5 / Hebrews 12). The specific vocabulary: *crystals / tongues of fire / fiery cherubim / the Great Glory / falling prostrate / called by name* — all appear in 1 Enoch 14 before they appear in their NT forms. The Revelation 4 throne-room is the NT *apokalypsis* (unveiling) of what 1 Enoch 14 saw in vision.

The *sophia* / tree of wisdom of cc. 24–32 — the tree whose fragrance penetrates afar / identified as the Eden wisdom-tree — is the 1 Enoch form of the Genesis 2–3 LXX wisdom-tree tradition. **CAE Eden-to-new-creation arc:** the wisdom-tree seen by Enoch in the Garden of Righteousness (32:3–6) is the same tree whose fruit Adam and Eve ate — Raphael identifies it (*this is the tree of wisdom of which your father and mother ate*) — but now it is in a restored garden, its fragrance accessible to the righteous Enoch without prohibition. The Eden-tree's restoration in the *Garden of Righteousness* is the 1 Enoch form of the Revelation 22:2 tree-of-life (*xylon zōēs*) in the New Jerusalem — **Cross-link: Genesis 2:9 LXX → 1 Enoch 24–32 → Revelation 22:2 — the tree-of-wisdom / tree-of-life arc across the canon.**

**BOOK TWO: THE BOOK OF PARABLES (cc. 37–71)**

The most theologically dense section of 1 Enoch — and the most directly Christologically significant. The Parables constitute the primary Second Temple witness to a pre-existent, named, heavenly *Son of Man* figure with judicial and soteriological functions. Five primary CAE nodes:

**1. The *Lord of Spirits* (*Kyrios tōn Pneumatōn*) divine title:**

The title *Lord of Spirits* (*Kyrios tōn Pneumatōn*) appears over 100 times in the Parables — the most concentrated divine-name usage in any Second Temple text. The title is unique to 1 Enoch in the pre-NT tradition. **CAE pneumatological significance:** *Lord of Spirits* as the divine name presupposes that the *pneumata* (spirits / breaths-of-life) are under divine sovereignty — the same *pneuma* that animates the Wisdom register / that the *psychikoi pneuma mē echontes* (Jude 19) lack / that is given as the *aparabaton* (permanent — Hebrews 7:24) guarantee of the new covenant inheritance. The *Lord of Spirits* is the one from whom the Wisdom-register *pneuma* derives and to whom it returns.

**LXX-primacy argument:** *Kyrios tōn Pneumatōn* echoes Numbers 16:22 LXX and 27:16 LXX (*ho theos tōn pneumatōn kai pasēs sarkos* — the God of the spirits of all flesh) — Moses's address to God when pleading for the community at the Korah-rebellion and the succession-appointment of Joshua. The 1 Enoch *Lord of Spirits* title is an elaboration of the LXX *theos tōn pneumatōn* — God's sovereignty over all *pneumata* as the ground of his authority to judge and to give life. **NT cross-link: Hebrews 12:9 (*tō patri tōn pneumatōn* — the Father of spirits)** — the NT's closest equivalent to the 1 Enoch *Lord of Spirits* title, confirming the continuity of the tradition.

**2. The pre-existent *Son of Man* / *Elect One* (cc. 46–49 / 62–63 / 69–71):**

The Parables' central theological contribution is the identification of a heavenly figure — called variously *Son of Man* / *Elect One* / *Righteous One* / *Anointed* (*Christos autou* — his Anointed / 48:10 / 52:4) — who:

- Existed before the creation of the sun and stars (48:3 — *before the sun and the signs were created his name was named before the Lord of Spirits*)
- Is hidden before God until his revelation (48:6 — *he has been chosen and hidden before him before the creation of the world*)
- Sits on the throne of glory to judge all (45:3 / 51:3 / 62:2–5 / 69:27–29)
- Is the light of the Gentiles and the hope of the troubled of heart (48:4 — *he shall be the light of the Gentiles*)
- Has *pneuma sophias* (breath-of-life of wisdom) dwelling in him (49:3 — *in him dwells the breath-of-life of wisdom and the breath-of-life of him who gives insight*)
- Is called *Christos autou* — his Anointed (48:10 / 52:4)

**CAE Christological chain — the *Son of Man* pre-existence and judicial function:**

**Daniel 7:13–14 LXX (*hōs huios anthrōpou ērcheto* — one like a son of man coming / given authority / glory / kingdom) → 1 Enoch 46:1–4 (Son of Man with the Head of Days / revealed as the Righteous One / raises kings from their thrones) → 1 Enoch 48:2–6 (Son of Man named before creation / light of Gentiles / hidden before God) → 1 Enoch 62:2–14 (Son of Man on throne of glory / kings bow / righteous eat and rest with him forever) → Matthew 26:64 (*opsesthe ton huion tou anthrōpou kathēmenon ek dexiōn tēs dynameōs* — you will see the Son of Man seated at the right hand of Power) → John 5:27 (*exousian edōken autō krisin poiein hoti huios anthrōpou estin* — he gave him authority to execute judgment because he is the Son of Man) → Revelation 1:13–16 (one like a Son of Man / white hair / eyes of fire / voice of many waters / holding seven stars)**

**The most significant CAE node in the entire Parables:** 1 Enoch 48:10 uses the exact Greek equivalent of *Christos* — *Christou autou* / *his Anointed* — for the Son of Man / Elect One. This is the pre-NT tradition's identification of the *Son of Man* with the *Anointed* — the confluence that the NT presents as realized in Jesus the Anointed. The title-convergence in 1 Enoch (Son of Man = Elect One = Righteous One = his Anointed) is the background for the NT's presentation of Jesus as simultaneously the *Son of Man* (Synoptic self-designation) / the *Anointed* (confessional title) / the *Elect One* (*eklektos* — Luke 23:35 / 1 Peter 2:4/6 / Isaiah 42:1 LXX) / the *Righteous One* (*dikaios* — Acts 3:14 / 7:52 / 22:14 / 1 John 2:1).

**LXX-primacy argument:** Isaiah 42:1 LXX (*Iakōb ho pais mou — Israēl ho eklektos mou* — Jacob my servant / Israel my Elect One) / Isaiah 49:6 LXX (*dōsō se eis phōs ethnōn* — I will give you as a light to the Gentiles) — the two LXX Servant Songs that directly parallel 1 Enoch 48:4 (*light of the Gentiles*) and 48:6 (*chosen / hidden*). The 1 Enoch Parables are an extended midrash on the LXX Isaiah Servant Songs, identifying the Servant / Elect One as the pre-existent heavenly Son of Man — the same identification the NT makes in applying these texts to Jesus the Anointed.

**3. The *pneuma* septenary of 49:3 and 61:11:**

*49:3:* In the Elect One dwells — *pneuma sophias* (breath-of-life of wisdom) / *pneuma tou didonton synesin* (breath-of-life of understanding) / *pneuma dynameos* (breath-of-life of might) / *pneuma* of the sleeping righteous.

*61:11:* The heavenly beings bless in — *pneuma pisteōs* (breath-of-life of faith) / *pneuma sophias* (breath-of-life of wisdom) / *pneuma hypomonēs* (breath-of-life of patience) / *pneuma eleous* (breath-of-life of mercy) / *pneuma kriseōs* (breath-of-life of judgment) / *pneuma eirēnēs* (breath-of-life of peace) / *pneuma agathōsynēs* (breath-of-life of goodness).

**CAE LXX-primacy chain — the Isaiah 11 *pneuma* septenary:**

**Isaiah 11:2 LXX (*anapausetai ep' auton pneuma tou theou — pneuma sophias kai syneseōs — pneuma boulēs kai ischyos — pneuma gnōseōs kai eusebeias* — the breath-of-life of God shall rest on him / breath-of-life of wisdom and understanding / of counsel and might / of knowledge and piety) → 1 Enoch 49:3 (*pneuma sophias / syneseōs / dynameos*) → Revelation 1:4 (*hepta pneumata ta enōpion tou thronou autou* — the seven spirits before his throne) → Revelation 5:6 (*hepta pneumata tou theou* — the seven spirits of God sent into all the earth)**

The 1 Enoch 49:3 *pneuma* list is a direct elaboration of Isaiah 11:2 LXX — the *ruach* attributes of the Messianic Branch developed into the Elect One's pneumatological profile. The Revelation *hepta pneumata* (seven spirits) is the final canonical form of the same tradition — the fullness of the divine *pneuma* as seven attributes, resting on and operating through the Anointed. **CAE pneumatological continuity confirmed across four witnesses:** Isaiah 11:2 LXX → 1 Enoch 49:3 → 1 Enoch 61:11 → Revelation 1:4/5:6.

**4. The *sophia* who found no dwelling (42:1–3):**

*Wisdom found no place where she might dwell — then a dwelling-place was assigned to her in the heavens. Wisdom went forth to make her dwelling among the children of men and found no dwelling-place — wisdom returned to her place and took her seat among the angels. And unrighteousness went forth from her chambers — whom she sought not she found and dwelt with them.*

**CAE Wisdom-Christology chain — the rejected Sophia:**

**Proverbs 8:1–36 LXX (*hē sophia kēryssei* — Wisdom cries out / present at creation / delighting in the sons of men) → Wisdom of Solomon 7:22–8:1 LXX (Sophia as *atmos tēs tou theou dynameōs* — vapor of divine power / reaches from one end to another) → 1 Enoch 42:1–3 (Sophia seeking dwelling among men / finding none / returning to heaven / unrighteousness filling the vacant space) → Sirach 24:8–12 LXX (*en Iakōb kataskenōson* — dwell in Jacob / the Creator commanded her) → John 1:11 (*eis ta idia ēlthen kai hoi idioi auton ou parelabon* — he came to his own and his own received him not)**

The 1 Enoch 42 Sophia-who-finds-no-dwelling is the structural pre-figuration of John 1:11 — the *Logos / Sophia* coming to dwell among men / finding no reception / the contrast with Sirach 24:8 LXX where Sophia is commanded to dwell in Israel. The Johannine Prologue's *en tois idiois ēlthen* (he came to his own) is the historical realization of what 1 Enoch 42 describes as Sophia's failed mission — except that in John the *Logos* does find a dwelling: *kai ho Logos sarx egeneto kai eskēnōsen en hēmin* (John 1:14 — the Word became flesh and tabernacled among us). **CAE Chain 9 (*skēnē / naos*) Sophia-dwelling node:** the *eskēnōsen* (tabernacled) of John 1:14 is the answer to the 1 Enoch 42 Sophia who *found no dwelling-place* — the Incarnation is the resolution of the rejected-Sophia trajectory.

**5. The eschatological banquet of 62:13–15:**

*The Lord of Spirits will abide over them and with that Son of Man shall they eat and lie down and rise up forever and ever. And the righteous and elect shall have risen from the earth — they shall have been clothed with garments of glory — and these shall be the garments of life from the Lord of Spirits.*

**CAE eschatological-banquet chain:**

**Isaiah 25:6–8 LXX (*kai poiēsei kyrios Sabaōth pasi tois ethnesin — piesontai euphrosynēn — piesontai oinon — chrisontai myron* — the Lord of Hosts will make for all peoples a feast) → 1 Enoch 62:13–15 (eating / lying down / rising up with the Son of Man forever) → Matthew 8:11 (*polloi apo anatolōn kai dysmōn hēxousin kai anaklithēsontai meta Abraam kai Isaak kai Iakōb* — many from east and west shall recline with Abraham Isaac and Jacob) → Luke 22:30 (*hina esthiēte kai pinēte epi tēs trapezēs mou en tē basileia mou* — that you may eat and drink at my table in my kingdom) → Revelation 19:9 (*makarioi hoi eis to deipnon tou gamou tou arniou keklēmenoi* — blessed are those called to the marriage supper of the Lamb)**

The *eating and lying down and rising* with the Son of Man in 1 Enoch 62:13 is the precise background for the NT's eschatological-banquet language — the community of the righteous permanently dwelling with the enthroned Son of Man in the new creation. The *garments of life* (*chiton zōēs* — 62:15) are the background for the Revelation white robes (*stolē leukē* — Revelation 3:5 / 6:11 / 7:9) and the Jude v.23 *espiloménon chitōna* (garment stained by the flesh) as the inverse — the corrupted garment contrasted with the *chiton zōēs* of the righteous.

**BOOK THREE: THE ASTRONOMICAL BOOK (cc. 72–82)**

The most technically detailed section of 1 Enoch — 364-day solar calendar / lunar cycle calculations / wind and dew chambers / star portals. CAE function: primarily the establishment of the *Logos* as the governing principle of cosmic order — the same *Logos* that governs the creation's physical order is the *Logos* that governs the Wisdom-register's moral order.

**Key CAE node — the oath-sustained creation (69:16–25):**

The *oath* (*horkos*) that sustains the creation — *through it the earth was founded upon the water / through that oath the sea was created / the depths made fast / the sun and moon complete their course / the stars complete their course*. The creation is held in order by an *oath* — a sworn word — the divine *davar* enacted as cosmic covenant. **CAE *Logos*-as-creation-ground confirmation:**

**Genesis 1:1–3 LXX (*en archē epoiēsen ho theos — kai eipen ho theos*— in the beginning God made / and God said) → Psalm 32:6 LXX (*tō logō tou kyriou hoi ouranoi estereōthēsan* — by the word of the Lord the heavens were made firm) → 1 Enoch 69:16–25 (the oath sustaining all creation / heaven suspended / earth founded / sea bounded / stars ordered) → 2 Peter 3:5–7 (*tō logō tou theou ouranoi ēsan* — by the word of God the heavens existed) → John 1:3 (*panta di' autou egeneto* — all things came into being through him) → Hebrews 1:3 (*pherōn ta panta tō rhēmati tēs dynameōs autou* — sustaining all things by the word of his power)**

The 1 Enoch oath-sustained creation is the Second Temple elaboration of the LXX *Logos*-creation tradition — the creation is not self-sustaining but held in existence by the divine sworn word. This is the background for 2 Peter 3:5–7's argument that the same *Logos* that created can judge (the creation's stability is dependent on the *Logos* / the *Logos* can withdraw that sustaining word in judgment). **CAE Chain 15 (Eden-to-new-creation) cosmic-order node:** the *Logos* as the sustaining oath of creation is the cosmic form of the same *Logos* that is the *sperma* (seed — 1 Peter 1:23) / *logos tēs alētheias* (word of truth — James 1:18) in the Wisdom-register community — the same word that creates and sustains the cosmos creates and sustains the new creation community.

**BOOK FOUR: THE BOOK OF DREAM VISIONS (cc. 83–90)**

The Animal Apocalypse — history from Adam to the Maccabean period encoded in animal symbolism. CAE function: primarily the demonstration of the *poimēn* (shepherd) tradition and the false-shepherd indictment as a continuous covenant-history theme.

**Key CAE node — the seventy shepherds and the Lord of the sheep (cc. 89–90):**

The seventy shepherds appointed over Israel who exceed their mandate and destroy more than commanded — judged and cast into the fiery abyss. **CAE Chain 11 (*poimēn*) false-shepherd history-of-Israel node:**

**Ezekiel 34:1–10 LXX (*ouai hoi poimenes tou Israēl* — woe to the shepherds of Israel) → Zechariah 11:4–17 LXX (*poimaine ta probata tēs sphagēs* — shepherd the flock marked for slaughter / the worthless shepherd) → 1 Enoch 89:59–90:25 (the seventy shepherds / exceeding their mandate / judgment) → John 10:1–18 (*ho poimēn ho kalos* — the Good Shepherd who lays down his life / the *kleptēs kai lēstēs* — thief and robber) → Matthew 23:37 (*Ierousalēm — apokteinousa tous prophētas* — Jerusalem who kills the prophets) → Revelation 7:17 (*to Arnion...poimanei autous* — the Lamb will shepherd them)**

The seventy-shepherds judgment-sequence of 1 Enoch 89–90 is the most elaborate pre-NT false-shepherd typology available — the covenant-history narrated as a sequence of unfaithful shepherds whose judgment is held until the great day. The Good Shepherd of John 10 is presented against exactly this background — he is the one true shepherd in a history of false shepherds / he lays down his life rather than exploiting the flock / his sheep hear his voice (*kat' onoma* — by name / John 10:3 = 3 John v.15 = 1 Enoch 43:1 — *he called them all by their names*).

**The new house / white bull / transformation (89:65–90:38):**

The eschatological vision: the old house folded up / a new greater house set up / all the animals transformed into white bulls / the first becoming a lamb / the lamb becoming a great animal. **CAE Chain 9 (*skēnē / naos*) eschatological-temple node:**

The *new house greater than the first* of 1 Enoch 89:65 is the Second Temple tradition's eschatological-temple expectation — the same expectation underlying: Haggai 2:9 LXX (*hē doxa tou oikou tou eschatou hyper tēn tou prōtou* — the glory of the latter house greater than the former) / Ezekiel 40–48 LXX (the visionary temple) / John 2:19–21 (*lysate ton naon touton* — the temple-body of the Anointed as the new temple) / Revelation 21:22 (*naon ouk eidon en autē* — I saw no temple in it — the Lord God Almighty and the Lamb are its temple). The transformation of all animals into white bulls (*all generations transformed / all became white bulls*) is the 1 Enoch form of the new creation's universal transformation — the *ouranous kainous kai gēn kainēn* (new heavens and new earth — 2 Peter 3:13 / Isaiah 65:17 LXX) as the restoration of the original white-bull / Adam humanity.

**BOOK FIVE: THE EPISTLE OF ENOCH (cc. 91–108)**

The most directly paraenetic section — ethical instruction / woe-oracles / the Apocalypse of Weeks / the eschatology of the righteous dead. Four primary CAE nodes:

**1. The *euangelion tēs dikaiosynēs* (91:5):**

*It shall be given to the righteous to announce good tidings (*euangelion*) of righteousness.*

**CAE note:** This is the pre-NT use of *euangelion* in the eschatological-righteousness context — the announcement of righteousness as *good news* to be proclaimed by the righteous. **NT cross-link: Romans 1:17 (*dikaiosynē gar theou en autō apokalyptetai ek pisteōs eis pistin* — for in it the righteousness of God is revealed from faith to faith) + Isaiah 52:7 LXX (*hōs hōraioi hoi podes euangelizomenou akoēn eirēnēs euangelizomenou agatha* — how beautiful the feet of those who announce good news of peace)** — the *euangelion dikaiosynēs* of 1 Enoch 91:5 is the pre-NT form of the Pauline *euangelion* whose content is *dikaiosynē theou* (righteousness of God). The CAE's *euangelion* vocabulary lock receives its Second Temple validation: the good news is fundamentally news of righteousness / salvation / the eschatological vindication of the righteous.

**2. The Wisdom / *sophia* as transmitted book (82:1–3 / 92:1 / 104:12–13):**

The recursive self-referential structure of 1 Enoch: Enoch writes the books of wisdom / transmits them to Methuselah / for all future generations. The books are the vehicle of the *sophia* — whoever reads and understands them receives the *sophia* that makes them wise. **CAE Chain 10 (*alētheia* / *sophia* transmission) note:** the book-as-wisdom-transmission is the 1 Enoch form of the *logos tēs alētheias* (word of truth — James 1:18) / the *theopneustos graphē* (God-breathed Scripture — 2 Timothy 3:16) as the transmitted *sophia* deposited for all generations. The *hapax paradotheisē pistis* (faith once for all delivered — Jude 3) is the NT form of the 1 Enoch wisdom-book's once-delivered and preserved transmission.

*104:10–13* is the most significant passage in the entire Epistle for the CAE's textual-transmission concern: *sinners will alter and pervert the words of righteousness in many ways / but when they write down truthfully all my words in their languages and do not change or minish ought from my words — then books will be given to the righteous and the wise.* The 1 Enoch tradition's own internal textual-integrity concern — the preservation of the transmitted words against alteration — is structurally identical to the CAE's core methodology: the multi-witness triangulation system designed to detect and resist the same kind of *alteration and perversion* that 1 Enoch itself warns against. The MT compression events (six forensic events identified in the CAE's red-team attack key) are precisely the kind of *altering and diminishing* that 1 Enoch 104:10 identifies as the Folly register's textual strategy.

**3. The *psychai / pneumata* of the righteous dead (cc. 102–104):**

The most developed pre-NT eschatology of the righteous dead outside Daniel 12 LXX:

*102:4–5:* Fear not ye souls (*psychai*) of the righteous / grieve not if your soul (*psychē*) has descended into Sheol in grief
*103:3–4:* All goodness and joy and glory are prepared for them / written down for the breath-of-life (*pneumata*) of those who have died in righteousness / the breath-of-life of you who have died in righteousness shall live (*zēsousin*)
*103:7–8:* Into darkness and chains and burning flame shall your breath-of-life (*pneumata*) enter — the great judgment for sinners
*108:7–11:* Those who loved God and gave their bodies to torture / their breath-of-life (*pneumata*) were found pure / the Lord tried them much

**CAE anthropological note:** 1 Enoch maintains the precise *psychē / pneuma* distinction that GLE v2.2 preserves throughout the NT rendering:

*psychē* — the volitional/affective faculty that can be in Sheol in grief — the personal identity that persists in the intermediate state
*pneuma* — the animating life-force that is tried / found pure / shall live — the register-orientation capacity that is the ground of eschatological destiny

The intermediate state in 1 Enoch 22 (four hollow places for the spirits of the dead — separated: the righteous with a spring of water / sinners in pain awaiting judgment / murdered righteous making their suit / unrepentant sinners) is the background for the NT's intermediate-state theology. **Cross-link: Luke 16:22–26 (*kolpos Abraam* — Abraham's bosom / the great chasm / the righteous and wicked separated in the intermediate state) = 1 Enoch 22:9–13** — the four-category separation of 1 Enoch 22 is the structural background for the Lucan parable's two-category presentation (the righteous comforted / the wicked in torment).

**4. The Apocalypse of Weeks (93:1–10 / 91:12–17):**

The ten-week history of the world — each week representing an era of covenant history:

Week 1: Enoch himself / righteousness still endured
Week 2: Great wickedness / first end / a man saved (Noah)
Week 3: A man elected as plant of righteous judgment (Abraham)
Week 4: Visions of the holy / law given (Moses / Sinai)
Week 5: House of glory built (Solomon's Temple)
Week 6: Hearts forsake wisdom / house burned / chosen root dispersed (Babylonian exile)
Week 7: Apostate generation / elect righteous chosen to receive sevenfold instruction
Week 8: Sword given to the righteous / sinners delivered into their hands / temple built
Week 9: Righteous judgment revealed / wicked deeds of sinners written
Week 10: Eternal judgment / Great Judgment / new heaven

**CAE eschatological-structure note:** The Apocalypse of Weeks is the most compressed pre-NT salvation-history periodization available — ten eras from creation to the new heaven. The NT's *eschatos kairos* (last time — 1 Peter 1:5) / *eschate hēmera* (last day — John 6:39/40/44/54 / 11:24 / 12:48) / *eschatai hēmerai* (last days — Acts 2:17 / 2 Timothy 3:1 / Hebrews 1:2 / 2 Peter 3:3 / James 5:3) presuppose a periodized salvation-history in which the current era is the final era before the eschatological judgment — exactly the periodization the Apocalypse of Weeks provides. The NT community understood itself to be living in Week 7–8 of the Enochic schema — the apostate generation / the elect righteous receiving sevenfold instruction / the sword given to the righteous in spiritual combat.
