**JUBILEES — GLE v2.2 CAE NOTES (COMPLETE)**

---

**TEXT BASE:** Ethiopic Ge'ez (*Kufale*) as primary — Hebrew fragments from Qumran (4Q216–228 / 4Q482–483 / 1Q17–18 / 2Q19–20 / 3Q5 / 11Q12) as the primary textual-critical control. Jubilees is one of the most extensively attested DSS texts — fifteen or more manuscript copies at Qumran, confirming its authoritative status in the Qumran community and its wide circulation in Second Temple Judaism.

**CHAPTER COUNT:** 50 chapters — structured as 49 jubilees (49 × 49-year periods) from creation to the giving of the Law at Sinai, with the 50th jubilee as the eschatological telos implied throughout.

---

**JUBILEES IN THE CAE ARCHITECTURE**

Jubilees occupies a position in the CAE second only to 1 Enoch in its significance for the NT's theological vocabulary and narrative framework. Where 1 Enoch provides the apocalyptic / Christological / pneumatological background, Jubilees provides the **covenantal-calendar / halakhic / sabbath-theology / angelic-mediation** background that is presupposed but rarely explained in the NT. The letter to the Hebrews / the Pauline covenant-theology / the Synoptic sabbath-controversies / the Johannine Prologue's *en archē* framing / and the entire NT Passover-typology tradition are all more fully intelligible against the Jubilees background than against any other available Second Temple source.

Four primary architectural functions:

(1) **Calendar-theology function:** Jubilees establishes the 364-day solar calendar as the divinely ordained festival-structure — every feast / sabbath / new moon / jubilee occurring on a fixed day of the week, never variable. The NT's theological use of festival-timing (the Passover timing of the crucifixion / the Pentecost timing of the Spirit's descent / the sabbath-controversy tradition) presupposes a community aware of calendar-conflict between the solar (Jubilees / DSS) and lunar (Pharisaic / Temple) calendars.

(2) **Angelic-mediation function:** Jubilees frames the entire revelation as dictated by the *Angel of the Presence* to Moses on Sinai — the Law was not given directly but mediated through the angelic hierarchy. **Cross-link: Galatians 3:19 (*diatageis di' angelōn* — the Law ordained through angels) / Acts 7:53 (*ton nomon eis diatagās angelōn* — the Law received through the disposition of angels) / Hebrews 2:2 (*ho di' angelōn lalētheis logos* — the word spoken through angels)** — the NT's angelic-mediation-of-the-Law tradition is the Jubilees framework assumed and then theologically deployed to argue the superiority of the new covenant's direct mediation through the Son (Hebrews 1:1–2 / 2:1–4).

(3) **Covenantal-recapitulation function:** Jubilees presents every major covenant (Noahide / Abrahamic / Mosaic) as a renewal of the original creation-covenant — the sabbath / circumcision / festival-calendar / dietary laws all grounded in creation-order rather than Sinai-innovation. **CAE Chain 8 (*diathēkē aiōnios*) primary source:** the *eternal covenant* theology of the NT (Hebrews 13:20 / Revelation 11:19) has its most developed pre-NT elaboration in Jubilees — the covenant is not a historical contingency but a creation-embedded reality renewed across successive generations.

(4) **Patriarchal-halakhah function:** Jubilees presents the patriarchs (Noah / Abraham / Isaac / Jacob) as Torah-observant before Sinai — practicing sabbath / circumcision / Passover / tithing / festival-keeping as divinely revealed obligations. This directly addresses the Pauline *before the Law* problem (Romans 4–5 / Galatians 3) — Jubilees and Paul are in deep conversation about the same texts (Genesis 15:6 / 17:10–14 / 22:1–18) from opposite directions.

**BOOK STRUCTURE — JUBILEE-BY-JUBILEE CAE ANALYSIS**

**cc. 1–2 — THE SINAI FRAME / THE CREATION-SABBATH THEOLOGY**

**c.1 — The Sinai frame and the eschatological return:**

The opening frame is structurally critical: Moses is commanded to *come up to the Lord on the mountain* / the Lord speaks concerning Israel's future apostasy / their scattering among the nations / and their eventual eschatological return. The frame establishes the entire book's theological purpose: Jubilees is not mere historical recounting but a covenantal-warning document addressed to a community the Lord foreknows will apostatize and be restored.

**Key CAE nodes in c.1:**

*1:7 — the apostasy prediction:* *And they will forget all my commandments — even all that I command them — and they will walk after the Gentiles and after their uncleanness and after their shame.* The apostasy-prediction is the *Deuteronomy 28–32 LXX* covenant-curse sequence applied proleptically to Israel's entire history — the same sequence Paul unpacks in Romans 9–11. **LXX-primacy argument:** Deuteronomy 32:15–21 LXX (*Iakōb ephagen kai eneplēsthē... kai apelipan ton theon ton poiēsanta autous* — Jacob ate and was filled / and they forsook the God who made them) is the direct LXX background for Jubilees 1:7's apostasy description. The Jubilees author is reading Deuteronomy 32 LXX and elaborating its covenant-curse trajectory.

*1:15–18 — the eschatological return:* *And after this they will turn to me from among all the nations with all their heart and with all their soul and with all their strength and I will gather them from among all the nations and they will seek me so that I shall be found of them when they seek me with all their heart.* The return-formula is a composite of Deuteronomy 4:29–30 LXX (*zētēseis kyrios ton theon sou kai heurēseis auton — ean zētēs auton ex holēs tēs kardias sou* — you will seek the Lord your God and find him — if you seek him with all your heart) and Jeremiah 29:13 LXX (*kai zētēsete me kai eurēsete hoti zētēsete me en holē kardia hymōn* — you will seek me and find me when you seek me with all your heart). **CAE Chain 8 (*diathēkē aiōnios*) eschatological-return node:** the two LXX texts converge in Jubilees 1:15–18 to form the standard eschatological-return formula — the same formula Paul deploys in Romans 11:26 (*kai houtōs pas Israēl sōthēsetai* — and so all Israel will be saved) as the culmination of the covenant-history argument.

*1:23–25 — the new creation circumcision:* *And I will circumcise the foreskin of their heart and the foreskin of the heart of their seed and I will create in them a holy breath-of-life (*pneuma*) and I will cleanse them so that they shall not turn away from me from that day forever.* This is the most explicit pre-NT citation of Deuteronomy 30:6 LXX (*perikathariei kyrios ho theos sou tēn kardian sou* — the Lord your God will circumcise your heart) combined with Ezekiel 36:26–27 LXX (*dōsō hymin kardian kainēn kai pneuma kainon dōsō en hymin* — I will give you a new heart and a new breath-of-life). **CAE Chain 8 (*diathēkē aiōnios*) heart-circumcision / new-*pneuma* node:** Jubilees 1:23–25 is the Second Temple tradition's most explicit statement of the new-covenant interior transformation — before the NT makes it the centerpiece of Pauline anthropology (Romans 2:29 *peritomē kardias en pneumati* — circumcision of the heart in the Spirit / Colossians 2:11 *peritomē acheiropoiētō* — circumcision not made by hands). The Jubilees text is the missing link between Deuteronomy 30:6 LXX / Ezekiel 36:26–27 LXX and the Pauline *pneumatikē peritomē* (spiritual circumcision) — the tradition was already reading these texts together before Paul.

**c.2 — The creation-week and the sabbath theology:**

The creation account retold with the sabbath as the theological summit — not merely a rest-day but the day the Lord blessed and sanctified *above all days*. Four CAE nodes:

*2:1 — The Angel of the Presence dictation:* *Write the complete history of the creation — how in six days the Lord God finished all his works and all that he created and kept sabbath on the seventh day and hallowed it for all ages and appointed it as a sign for all his works.* The sabbath as *sign for all his works* — the creation-sabbath as the covenant-sign preceding all subsequent covenant-signs (circumcision / Passover / Sinai). **LXX-primacy argument:** Genesis 2:1–3 LXX (*kai sunetelesthēsan ho ouranos kai hē gē kai pas ho kosmos autōn — kai katepausen... tē hēmera tē hebdomē* — and the heavens and earth and all their order were completed / he rested on the seventh day) is the Jubilees base-text — the *suntelesthēsan* (completed / finished) vocabulary of Genesis 2:1 LXX is the same vocabulary used for the eschatological completion (*sunteleias tou aiōnos* — Matthew 24:3 / 28:20) — the creation-sabbath and the eschatological-sabbath are structurally linked by vocabulary in the LXX tradition.

*2:17–33 — The sabbath as Israel's unique covenantal identity-marker:* *And he gave us a great sign — the sabbath day — that we should work six days but keep sabbath on the seventh day from all work... And above all he sanctified Israel that they should be with him and keep sabbath together on the seventh day.* The sabbath as the unique identity-marker distinguishing Israel from the nations — given first to the angels of the Presence and the angels of sanctification before being given to Israel. **CAE Chain 8 (*diathēkē aiōnios*) sabbath-as-covenant-sign node:**

**Genesis 2:1–3 LXX (creation-sabbath) → Exodus 31:12–17 LXX (*dia touto phylaxousin hoi huioi Israēl ta sabbata... kai en tē hēmera tē hebdomē katepauen* — the sons of Israel shall keep the sabbaths / sign between me and you forever) → Jubilees 2:17–33 (sabbath given to Israel as the unique covenantal identity-sign / given first to the heavenly angels) → Hebrews 4:1–11 (*apoleipomenēs epangelias eiselthein eis tēn katapausin autou* — the promise of entering his rest remaining / the creation-sabbath rest as the eschatological telos)**

The Hebrews 4 sabbath-rest argument (*katapausis* / *sabbatismos*) is intelligible against the Jubilees sabbath-theology: the *sabbatismos* (sabbath-rest — Hebrews 4:9 — the only NT occurrence of this word) is not merely the Sinai-sabbath but the creation-sabbath that preceded all covenant-signs and points to the eschatological rest. Jubilees 2 is the tradition that established the sabbath's creation-order grounding before Hebrews deploys it as the eschatological argument.

**cc. 3–4 — EDEN / THE FALL / CAIN AND ABEL / ENOCH**

**c.3 — The Eden narrative and the creation-order theology:**

The Eden account with distinctive Jubilees additions: the animals name-giving (3:1–2) / the creation of Eve (3:4–7) / the forty-day / eighty-day purification periods for male / female births (3:8–14) / the entrance into Eden on the fortieth day (3:9–12) / the Fall (3:17–25) / the garments (3:26–31) / the expulsion (3:32–35).

**Key CAE nodes:**

*3:8–14 — The purification periods and Leviticus 12 LXX:* The Jubilees detail that Adam was brought to Eden on the fortieth day and Eve on the eightieth — corresponding to the Leviticus 12 LXX purification periods for male (40 days) and female (80 days) births — grounds the Levitical law in creation-order: the purification periods are not Sinai-innovations but creation-embedded patterns. **LXX-primacy argument:** Leviticus 12:2–5 LXX (*epi tessarakonta hēmerōn kathizetai epi haimati katharseos autēs* — for forty days she sits in her purification / for female birth eighty days) — the Jubilees correlation of creation-events with Levitical law confirms the author's reading of the LXX Levitical calendar as a mirror of creation-order.

*3:17–25 — The Fall as Folly-register event:* The Jubilees Fall-narrative emphasizes the serpent's *deception* — the same vocabulary as 2 Corinthians 11:3 (*hos ho ophis exēpatēsen Euan en tē panourgią autou* — as the serpent deceived Eve through his cunning) and 1 Timothy 2:14 (*Adam ouk ēpatēthē — hē de gynē exapatētheisa* — Adam was not deceived but the woman was fully deceived). **CAE Chain 15 (Eden-to-new-creation) Folly-register deception node:** Jubilees 3:17–25 confirms the CAE's foundational reading of the Fall as a *Folly event* — the serpent's deception operates through false representation of the divine command / the *alētheia* of God's word (the covenant-order) is subverted by the serpent's *pseudos* (lie). The Jubilees word-choice (*beguiled / deceived*) parallels the LXX *exēpatēsen* — the Fall is not merely a moral failure but an epistemological subversion of the Wisdom register's foundational trust in the divine *davar*.

*3:26–31 — The garments and the theology of shame-covering:* After the Fall the Lord makes garments of skin for Adam and Eve — in Jubilees this becomes: the Lord made for them garments of skin and clothed them / he showed them how to cover their shame. **CAE Chain 15 garment-of-shame / garment-of-glory arc:**

**Genesis 3:21 LXX (*kai epoiēsen kyrios ho theos tō Adam kai tē gynaiki autou chitōnas dermatinous* — the Lord God made for Adam and his wife garments of skin) → Jubilees 3:26–31 (garments as shame-covering / the Lord's own provision for what the Fall exposed) → Isaiah 61:10 LXX (*ependysen me himation sōtēriou* — he clothed me with garments of salvation / robe of righteousness) → 1 Enoch 62:15 (*chitōnes zōēs* — garments of life) → Revelation 3:5 / 7:9 (*stolē leukē* — white robe) → Jude v.23 (*chiton espilōmenos* — garment stained by flesh)**

The garment-arc from Genesis 3 LXX through Jubilees 3 through the prophetic tradition into the NT Revelation-white-robes is one of the CAE's most visually coherent symbolic chains — shame exposed by the Fall / covered by the Lord's own provision / finally replaced by the garment of eternal life in the new creation.

**c.4 — Cain / Abel / Seth / Enoch / the *sophia* transmission:**

*4:15–26 — Enoch the righteous scribe:* Jubilees' account of Enoch is the most important pre-NT Enoch tradition outside 1 Enoch itself — Enoch learned writing from the angels / was the first to write testimony and law among men / wrote the signs of heaven according to the order of their months / wrote all the years of jubilees / made known and could not be altered. **CAE transmission-chain note:** the Jubilees Enoch is the paradigmatic *scribe of righteousness* (1 Enoch 12:4) — the one through whom the divine *sophia* is transmitted in written form to all generations. The CAE's own methodology — written preservation / multi-witness verification / resistance to alteration — is formally parallel to the Jubilees-Enoch transmission model: the righteous scribe who writes accurately / transmits completely / protects against corruption.

*4:26 — The four sacred places:* The Garden of Eden / the Mount of the East / Mount Sinai / Mount Zion as the four sacred places on earth where the Lord is present and worshipped. **CAE Chain 9 (*skēnē / naos*) sacred-geography node:** the four-place theology of Jubilees 4:26 is the narrative background for the NT's temple-theology trajectory — Eden as the original sanctuary / Sinai as the revelation-mountain / Zion as the covenant-throne-city / and the eschatological New Jerusalem as the restoration of Eden-as-sanctuary. **Cross-link: Revelation 21–22 (the New Jerusalem = Eden restored = the *naos* in which the Lord and the Lamb dwell) = the Jubilees four-sacred-place theology completed.**

**cc. 5–10 — NOAH / THE FLOOD / THE COVENANT WITH NOAH**

(Chapter 5 already rendered — notes integrated here.)

**c.5 — The Watchers / the Flood / the heavenly tablets:**

*5:12 — The new and righteous nature:* After the flood the Lord *made for all his works a new and righteous nature so that they should not sin in their whole nature forever.* This is one of the most theologically dense statements in Jubilees — the creation's *nature* itself renewed after the flood's judgment. **CAE Chain 15 (Eden-to-new-creation) creation-renewal node:** the post-flood new nature of 5:12 is the Jubilees form of the *kainos ktisis* (new creation — 2 Corinthians 5:17 / Galatians 6:15) — the divine act of making the creation's nature *righteous* in the wake of corruption-and-judgment. The same pattern: corruption → judgment → new nature → covenant → eschatological fulfillment.

*5:13–16 — The heavenly tablets:* The judgment of all is *ordained and written on the heavenly tablets in righteousness* — nothing in heaven or earth or Sheol is outside the written judgment. **CAE heavenly-tablets tradition:**

**1 Enoch 81:1–3 (*heavenly tablets*) → Jubilees 5:13–16 → Daniel 7:10 LXX (*biblia ēnoichthēsan* — books were opened) → Revelation 20:12 (*bibloi ēnoichthēsan... biblion tēs zōēs* — books were opened / the book of life)**

The heavenly-tablets tradition is the Second Temple background for the NT's eschatological-books-opened judgment-scene. The Jubilees contribution: every creature / every kind / great and small / all are written in the heavenly tablets — the judgment is total / universal / already inscribed before the great day.

*5:17–19 — Annual mercy for Israel / Noah's acceptance:* The Lord will show *mercy* (*eleos*) to all who turn from their guilt *once each year* — the Day of Atonement theology grounded in the flood-narrative. Noah's *person was accepted* (*proswpon* — face / person) — the basis of the flood-survivors' preservation. **CAE Chain 1 (*hilastērion*) annual-mercy / Noah-acceptance node:** the annual *eleos* of 5:18 is the Jubilees form of the Yom Kippur *hilasmos* — the once-yearly covenant-renewal mercy that the NT presents as superseded by the *ephapax* (once-for-all) offering of the Anointed (Hebrews 9:25–28 / 10:1–14). The Jubilees annual-mercy establishes the pattern whose fulfillment the NT announces.

**cc. 6–7 — The Noahide covenant / the feast of Shavuot / the blood prohibition:**

*6:1–4 — The Noahide covenant:* The covenant after the flood — the rainbow sign / the promise of no second flood / the blood-prohibition. **Key CAE detail:** Jubilees 6:4 explicitly grounds the blood-prohibition (*you shall not eat flesh with its life — with its blood*) in the covenant with Noah — the same prohibition cited in Acts 15:20/29 (*apechesthai... haimatos* — abstain from blood) as the Apostolic Decree's requirement for Gentile believers. **LXX-primacy argument:** Genesis 9:4 LXX (*plēn kreas en haimati psychēs — ou phagesthe* — but flesh with the blood of its life you shall not eat) = the LXX base-text / Jubilees 6:4 = the Second Temple elaboration / Acts 15:20/29 = the NT application to Gentile converts. The Apostolic Decree is not an innovation but an application of the Noahide covenant's Jubilees-tradition — the minimum requirements for covenant-participation recognized as binding on all nations, not only Israel.

*6:15–22 — The feast of Shavuot (Weeks) as the covenant-renewal festival:* Jubilees' most distinctive contribution to NT background — the feast of Weeks / Shavuot as the annual covenant-renewal feast, celebrated by Noah / Abraham / Isaac / Jacob / and at Sinai. **This is the single most important Jubilees contribution to NT interpretation:**

**Jubilees 6:17 (*it is ordained and engraved on the heavenly tablets — the feast of Weeks — in the middle of the third month*) + Jubilees 1:1 (*on the new month of the third month — Sinai*) → Acts 2:1 (*en tō symplerousthai tēn hēmeran tēs Pentēkostēs* — when the day of Pentecost was being fulfilled)**

The descent of the Holy Breath-of-life at Pentecost / Shavuot is not a random calendar coincidence but a theologically loaded fulfillment: the feast on which the *covenant* was renewed annually / on which the Sinai theophany occurred / on which Noah offered the first post-flood sacrifice — is the feast on which the new covenant's *pneuma* is poured out. **CAE Chain 8 (*diathēkē aiōnios*) Shavuot-as-covenant-renewal node:** the Jubilees Shavuot tradition is the background that makes Acts 2's Pentecost event a covenant-renewal event — not merely a spiritual phenomenon but the fulfillment of the annual covenant-renewal festival at the eschatological level. The *glossai hōsei pyros* (tongues as of fire — Acts 2:3) echo the Sinai theophany (*pyr katabaînon* — fire descending — Exodus 19:18 LXX) that Jubilees explicitly associates with the Shavuot covenant-renewal.

*6:22 — The calendar warning:* *Beware lest you forget the feast of weeks and the feast of the first fruits of this covenant lest you forget it — but observe it throughout all your jubilees — for it is a feast of Weeks and a feast of First Fruits — this feast is twofold and of a double nature.* **CAE calendar-conflict note:** the calendar-warning in Jubilees 6:22 is directed against the lunar calendar's disruption of the fixed solar calendar — the same calendar-conflict that underlies the DSS community's separation from the Temple establishment and, indirectly, the NT's relativization of sabbath-observance controversies (the calendrical disputes presupposed by Colossians 2:16 / Galatians 4:10 / Romans 14:5–6).

**cc. 7–10 — Noah's sons / the division of the earth / Mastema:**

*7:20–39 — Noah's final testament:* Noah's deathbed instruction to his sons — the most important passage for the Jubilees ethics of creation-order: observe righteousness / cover the shame of the flesh / bless the Creator / honor father and mother / love your neighbor / guard from fornication and uncleanness and all iniquity. **CAE Chain 8 (*diathēkē aiōnios*) creation-embedded ethics node:** Noah's testament establishes that the ethical requirements of the Mosaic Law are not Sinai-innovations but creation-order recoveries — the Law makes explicit what Noah already knew and transmitted to his sons. **Cross-link: Romans 2:14–15 (*hotan gar ethne ta mē nomon echonta physei ta tou nomou poiōsin* — when Gentiles who do not have the Law by nature do what the Law requires) = the Jubilees Noahide ethics as the creation-embedded law that Gentiles know through natural reason.

*10:1–14 — Mastema and the evil spirits:* After the flood Noah prays against the evil spirits afflicting his grandchildren — the Lord commands that all evil spirits be bound. But Mastema (the chief of the evil spirits — the adversary figure) petitions: *Lord Creator do not cause all the spirits of the Mastema to be cast into the place of judgment — leave some of them to me and let them hearken to my voice and do all that I shall say unto them — if some of them are not left to me I shall not be able to execute the power of my will on the sons of men.* The Lord allows one tenth to remain — the rest are bound.

**CAE adversary-theology note:** the Jubilees Mastema is the most developed pre-NT adversary figure in the Second Temple tradition — a named prince (*Mastema* = hostility / adversary) who functions as the:

- Tempter of Abraham (17:16 — Mastema requests the Aqedah as a test)
- Accuser before God (17:16 — parallel to Job 1–2 / Zechariah 3:1)
- Hardener of Pharaoh's heart (48:9–10 — Mastema assists the Egyptians)
- Commander of the remaining evil spirits

**Cross-link: Matthew 4:1–11 (*ho Satanas* — the satan / tempter) + John 12:31 (*ho archōn tou kosmou toutou* — the ruler of this world) + Ephesians 6:11–12 (*pros tas archas — pros tas exousias* — against the rulers / against the powers) + Revelation 12:9 (*ho drakōn ho megas — ho ophis ho archaios — ho kalamenos Diabolos kai ho Satanas* — the great dragon / ancient serpent / called the Devil and Satan)**

Jubilees 10 provides the structural explanation for why the NT assumes an adversary with organized authority over a portion of the spirit-realm: the *one-tenth retained by Mastema* are the spirits that the NT *Satanas / Diabolos / archōn tou kosmou* commands. The Jubilees Mastema-permission-structure (God permits a portion of the adversary's activity within defined limits) is the background for the NT's consistently limited-but-real adversary — the *Satanas* who is powerful but not sovereign / who must be resisted (James 4:7 / 1 Peter 5:8–9) but who is already defeated (John 12:31 / Colossians 2:15 / Revelation 12:7–12).

**cc. 11–23 — ABRAM / ABRAHAM / THE COVENANT / ISAAC / THE AQEDAH**

**cc. 11–12 — Abram's early life / idolatry / the call:**

*11:4–6 — Ur-Mastema and the tower:* The Tower of Babel event associated with Mastema's influence — the nations led into idolatry through the adversary's instruction. **CAE Folly-register idolatry analysis:** the Jubilees connection between Mastema and idolatry is the background for Paul's analysis in Romans 1:18–32 — the suppression of the *alētheia tou theou* (truth of God) leading to *eidōlolatria* (idol-worship) is not merely human stupidity but adversary-driven deception. **Cross-link: 1 Corinthians 10:20 (*alla hoti ha thyousin ta ethnē — daimoniois thyousin* — what the Gentiles sacrifice they sacrifice to demons) = Jubilees 11 — idol-worship as demon-service.**

*12:16–21 — Abram prays against idolatry:* Abram's prayer against the idols — *Lord my God and God of the Most High — you alone are my God / you have created all things / and all things are the work of your hands / and you and your kingdom I have chosen.* The prayer is the purest monotheistic confession in the Jubilees tradition — the personal-covenant-choice of the individual before the formal Abrahamic covenant. **CAE Chain 10 (*alētheia*) personal-monotheistic-confession node:** Abram's prayer is the Second Temple tradition's form of the *Shema* (*Deuteronomy 6:4 LXX — Kyrios ho theos hēmōn Kyrios heis estin* — the Lord our God the Lord is one) as personally enacted covenant-choice rather than merely recited formula.

*12:25–27 — The Hebrew language restored:* The Lord opens Abram's mouth and his heart *in the Hebrew tongue* — the language sealed from the time of Babel. **CAE transmission-chain note:** the Jubilees Hebrew-language restoration is a key element in the tradition's argument for the LXX-tradition priority debate: Hebrew is the original language of revelation / sealed at Babel / restored to Abram / the source from which all subsequent covenant-transmission flows. The triconsonantal root system of biblical Hebrew as the *davar*-encoding medium is the Jubilees tradition's implicit theology — every Hebrew root carries the compressed semantic depth that the CAE's 3:1 expansion ratio attempts to recover.

**cc. 13–16 — The Abrahamic covenant / circumcision / Sodom / Isaac's birth:**

*14:1–20 — The covenant of the pieces (Genesis 15):* The covenant-making ceremony of Genesis 15 retold — with Jubilees' distinctive addition that Abraham drove away the birds from the sacrifice and this act foreshadowed Israel's driving away the nations. The *four hundred years* of affliction (14:13) / the fourth generation returning (14:16) — Jubilees maintains the Genesis 15:13–16 LXX chronology.

*14:20 — The covenant as feast of First Fruits:* Abraham observes the feast of First Fruits (*Shavuot*) on the day of the covenant — **confirming the Jubilees 6 thesis that Shavuot is the covenant-renewal feast across all generations from Noah through Abraham through Sinai.** The covenant of Genesis 15 occurs on Shavuot / the Sinai covenant occurs on Shavuot / the new-covenant *pneuma* descends on Shavuot — the covenant-renewal festival is the structural thread across the entire covenantal history. **CAE Chain 8 (*diathēkē aiōnios*) Shavuot-covenant-thread complete.**

*15:1–34 — Circumcision as the covenant-sign:* The most theologically detailed pre-NT treatment of circumcision — four CAE nodes:

(1) *15:11–14 — Circumcision as creation-embedded:* *This law is for all the generations forever and there is no circumcising of the days and there is no omitting this one day from the eight days.* The eight-day circumcision requirement is creation-embedded — not culturally contingent. **Cross-link: Luke 2:21 (*kai hote eplēsthēsan hēmerai oktō tou peritemein auton* — when eight days were completed for circumcising him) = the Jubilees eight-day requirement applied to the Anointed himself — the Incarnate Son enters the covenant through the covenant-sign, fulfilling rather than abolishing the creation-embedded pattern.**

(2) *15:26 — Uncircumcised = destined for destruction:* *But as for any son who is not circumcised on the eighth day — he belongs not to the children of the covenant which the Lord made with Abraham but to the children of destruction.* The Jubilees binary (covenant-children / destruction-children) is the sharpest possible formulation of the circumcision requirement — and the precise formulation that Paul must address in Galatians 5:2–4 (*ean peritemnesthe — Christos hymas ouden ōphelēsei* — if you receive circumcision Christ will profit you nothing). Paul's Galatian controversy is directly against the Jubilees 15:26 halakhic position — those imposing circumcision on Gentile converts are applying the Jubilees covenant-binary to the new covenant community.

(3) *15:28–32 — The angels of the Presence and sanctification already circumcised:* The angels of the Presence and the angels of sanctification were created *already circumcised from the day of their creation* — circumcision is the creation-order state of the holy beings. **CAE creation-order / new-creation theology:** if the angels are *created circumcised* then the eschatological new creation's *peritomē acheiropoiētos* (circumcision not made by hands — Colossians 2:11) is the recovery of the creation-order state — the Jubilees tradition inadvertently provides the background for Paul's *spiritual circumcision* argument: the creation-intended state is interior (*kardias*) circumcision / the *sarkikē peritomē* (fleshly circumcision) was always the outward sign of an inward reality.

(4) *15:34 — The covenant to be kept through all generations / never abolished:* **Cross-link: Matthew 5:17 (*mē nomisēte hoti ēlthon katalysai ton nomon ē tous prophētas — ouk ēlthon katalysai alla plērōsai* — do not think that I came to abolish the Law or the Prophets — I came not to abolish but to fulfill) = the NT's direct engagement with the Jubilees permanent-covenant claim.** The Jubilees position (the covenant-signs are permanent / never abolished) and the NT's position (they are fulfilled / their inward reality is made fully accessible through the Anointed) are in deep conversation — the NT does not ignore Jubilees 15:34 but claims to fulfill it at the level of inward reality rather than outward sign.

**cc. 17–18 — Mastema / the Aqedah:**

*17:15–18:19 — The Aqedah as Mastema's test:* Jubilees' retelling of Genesis 22 — Mastema petitions the Lord to test Abraham / the Lord permits the test / Abraham passes / the Lord rebukes Mastema. The Jubilees Aqedah adds two elements absent from Genesis 22 LXX:

(1) The adversarial origin of the test — Mastema requests it as a test of Abraham's faithfulness (structurally identical to Job 1:9–12 LXX — *ei mēn dōrean sebetai Iōb ton theon* — does Job fear God for nothing? — the adversary's challenge before the divine throne)

(2) The explicit rebuke of Mastema after the angel's intervention — *Mastema was put to shame* — the test becomes the adversary's defeat rather than merely Abraham's trial

**CAE Chain 1 (*hilastērion*) Aqedah-substitution / Mastema-defeat node:**

**Genesis 22:1–18 LXX (*ho theos peirazei ton Abraam* — God tests Abraham) → Jubilees 17:15–18:19 (*Mastema petitions* / *Mastema put to shame* / the ram substituted) → Hebrews 11:17–19 (*pistei prosenenōchen Abraam ton Isaak* — by faith Abraham offered Isaac / reckoning that God could raise from the dead) → Romans 8:32 (*ho ge tou idiou huiou ouk epheisato* — he who did not spare his own Son)**

The Jubilees Mastema-defeat dimension of the Aqedah is the background for Paul's *ho ge tou idiou huiou ouk epheisato* (Romans 8:32) — the Father does not spare his own Son / just as Abraham did not spare Isaac — but the Jubilees Mastema element adds the dimension of the adversary's defeat through the very act of substitutionary offering. The Aqedah in Jubilees is simultaneously Abraham's test / the substitution-pattern / and the adversary's shame — all three dimensions converge in the NT's Christological deployment.

*18:12–13 — The Aqedah site as Passover institution:* Jubilees explicitly connects the Aqedah with Passover: *For this reason it is ordained and written on the heavenly tablets — to celebrate the feast of Passover on this day.* The Passover is grounded in the Aqedah — the ram-substitution of Isaac is the prototype of the Passover lamb. **CAE Chain 13 (*arnion / amnos*) Aqedah-Passover-Lamb arc:**

**Genesis 22:13 LXX (*ton kriou... antì tou paidos autou* — the ram instead of his son) → Jubilees 18:12–13 (Aqedah as Passover prototype) → Exodus 12:3–13 LXX (*amnos... tamios* — a lamb / spotless) → Isaiah 53:7 LXX (*hōs probaton epi sphagēn ēchthē* — as a sheep led to slaughter) → John 1:29 (*ho amnos tou theou ho airōn tēn hamartian tou kosmou* — the Lamb of God who takes away the sin of the world) → 1 Peter 1:19 (*amnou amōmou kai aspilou* — blameless and spotless lamb) → Revelation 5:6 (*arnion hestēkos hōs esphagmenon* — a Lamb standing as though slain)**

The Jubilees connection of the Aqedah-ram with Passover establishes the theological continuity that makes John 1:29 intelligible — the Baptist's identification of Jesus as *the Lamb of God* presupposes the Aqedah-Passover-Lamb typological chain that Jubilees makes explicit. The five-witness Chain 13 is grounded in the Jubilees tradition.

**cc. 19–36 — ISAAC / REBEKAH / JACOB / ESAU / LABAN**

**cc. 19–22 — The patriarchal blessings and the covenant transmission:**

*22:10–30 — Abraham's final blessing of Jacob:* The most theologically rich patriarchal blessing in Jubilees — eight elements:

(1) *22:11 — God of all:* *May God Most High — the Lord of all ages — bless you.*
(2) *22:13 — Election:* *May he choose you and your seed above all flesh that you may be a holy people to him as he has said.*
(3) *22:15 — Land promise:* The land to Jacob and his seed forever.
(4) *22:19 — Separation from nations:* *Separate yourself from the nations and eat not with them.*
(5) *22:20 — Abomination of their works:* Their works are unclean / all their ways are a pollution / an abomination.
(6) *22:24 — Covenant of peace:* May peace be with you.
(7) *22:27 — Nations will serve Jacob's seed.*
(8) *22:30 — Covenant sealed:* The blessing sealed on the heavenly tablets.

**CAE Chain 8 (*diathēkē aiōnios*) patriarchal-covenant-transmission node:** Abraham's blessing of Jacob in Jubilees 22 is the fullest pre-NT patriarchal-covenant-theology text — the covenant is transmitted from Abraham through Isaac to Jacob / sealed on the heavenly tablets / including the election of Jacob's seed as the holy people. **Cross-link: Romans 9:10–13 (*Rebekka ex henos koitēn echousa Isaak tou patros hēmōn... ho meizōn douleusei tō elassoni* — Rebekah conceived by one man Isaac / the older will serve the younger) = Paul reading the Jacob-election tradition through the Jubilees covenant-transmission framework.**

**cc. 23 — The generational decline and the eschatological generation:**

Chapter 23 is one of the most important eschatological passages in all of Jubilees — a compressed history of generational decline from the patriarchal era to the Maccabean period, followed by the eschatological restoration.

*23:11–25 — The generational decline:* Each generation shorter and more corrupt than the previous — the diminishing lifespans as a sign of increasing sin / the children abandoning the covenant / wars and famine and pestilence / children against parents / parents against children.

*23:26–31 — The eschatological restoration:* *And in those days the children shall begin to search the laws and to seek the commandments and to return to the path of righteousness. And the days shall begin to grow many and increase amongst those children of men — till their days draw nigh to one thousand years.* The eschatological generation characterized by: return to the Law / seeking righteousness / healing / peace / no adversary (*Mastema*) / joy and blessing / bones of the righteous rising.

**CAE Chain 15 (Eden-to-new-creation) Jubilees-eschatology node:**

*23:29 — the millennial restoration:* *And there shall be no Satan nor any evil destroyer — for all their days shall be days of blessing and healing.* The Jubilees millennium is the most explicit pre-NT text for the eschatological elimination of Mastema / the Satan — the direct background for Revelation 20:1–3 (*kai ebasen... ton Satanan kai edēsen auton chilia etē* — he seized the Satan and bound him for a thousand years). **Cross-link: 1 Enoch 10:4 (*bind Azâzêl hand and foot*) + Jubilees 23:29 (*no Satan... in those days*) + Revelation 20:1–3 (binding of Satan for the thousand years)** — three-witness millennial-Satan-binding confirmation.

*23:30–31 — The rising of bones / no old age:* *And their bones shall rest in the earth and their breath-of-life (*pneuma*) shall have much joy — and they shall know that it is the Lord who executes judgment and shows mercy (*eleos*) to hundreds and to thousands and to all that love him.* The resurrection-language of Jubilees 23:30–31 echoes Ezekiel 37:1–14 LXX (the valley of dry bones / *pneuma* entering the bones) and Daniel 12:2 LXX (*polloi tōn katheudontōn en tō platei tēs gēs anastēsontai* — many who sleep in the dust of the earth shall arise). **CAE resurrection-tradition note:** the Jubilees pneuma-joy-in-earth / bones-resting formulation is the pre-NT tradition's intermediate state described as *pneuma* rejoicing while bones await resurrection — the same two-stage anthropology (intermediate *pneuma*-joy / bodily resurrection) that Paul elaborates in 1 Corinthians 15 / 2 Corinthians 5 / 1 Thessalonians 4.

**cc. 27–45 — JACOB IN PADDAN-ARAM / THE SONS OF JACOB / JOSEPH**

**cc. 28–30 — Leah / Rachel / the birth of the twelve tribes / Dinah:**

*30:1–26 — The Dinah narrative and the prohibition of intermarriage:* The Dinah episode becomes in Jubilees the founding text for the prohibition of intermarriage between Israel and the nations — Levi and Simeon's violent response praised / Shechem's defilement condemned / the prohibition sealed on the heavenly tablets.

*30:18–20 — The Levitical priesthood grounded in Levi's zeal:* *And for this reason I have written for you in the words of the law all the deeds of the Shechemites which they wrought in Israel and how they have been judged / and the seed of Levi was chosen for the priesthood.* The Jubilees grounding of the Levitical priesthood in Levi's covenant-zeal (parallel to Phinehas's zeal in Numbers 25 LXX) is the background for: Hebrews 7:5–10 (Levi in the loins of Abraham paying tithes to Melchizedek — the Jubilees patriarchal-covenant framework presupposed) / Malachi 2:4–8 LXX (the covenant with Levi) / 1 Maccabees 2:54 (Phinehas's zeal as the Maccabean-Hasmonean legitimation).

**cc. 31–32 — The Bethel vision / the tithes / the Levitical election:**

*31:12–20 — Isaac's blessing of Levi:* The most important pre-NT text for the Levitical / Melchizedekian priesthood transition:

*31:13–17:* Isaac blesses Levi — *May the Lord give to you and to your seed greatness and great glory and cause you and your seed to approach him from all flesh to serve in his sanctuary as the angels of the Presence and as the holy ones.*

The Levi-blessing explicitly identifies the Levitical priesthood with the angelic liturgical service — Levi's sons serve *as the angels of the Presence* before the divine throne. **CAE Chain 9 (*skēnē / naos*) Levitical-angelic-service node:** the Jubilees identification of Levitical priestly service with angelic Presence-service is the background for Hebrews' entire high-priestly argument — the earthly tabernacle is a *copy and shadow* (*hypodeigma kai skia* — Hebrews 8:5) of the heavenly sanctuary in which the angelic *leitourgoi* (ministers) serve. The Jubilees Levi-as-angelic-service theology makes the Hebrews argument that the Anointed's heavenly high-priesthood supersedes the earthly Levitical service intelligible as a claim within the tradition's own terms.

*32:1–9 — The tithing and the Levitical inheritance:* Jacob tithes at Bethel — Levi's portion as the tithe — confirming Hebrews 7:9–10 (*di' Abraam kai Leui ho dekatous lambanōn dedekatōtai* — through Abraham even Levi who receives tithes paid tithes — for he was still in the loins of his ancestor when Melchizedek met him). The Jubilees tithing-at-Bethel is the specific patriarchal event that the Hebrews Melchizedek argument presupposes.

**cc. 34–46 — Joseph:**

*34:1–19 — The brothers' plot / Joseph sold:* The Jubilees Joseph narrative with Mastema's explicit involvement — *and the prince Mastema sent ravens and birds to devour the seed which was sown in the land.* Mastema is the adversarial power behind the brothers' hatred and the Joseph-sale — **the same adversary-behind-human-evil pattern that James 4:7 (*antistēte tō diabolō* — resist the devil) and 1 Peter 5:8 (*ho antidikos hymon diabolos* — your adversary the devil) address as the structural reality behind human conflict.**

*46:1–3 — Joseph's death and the future exodus:* Joseph makes Israel swear to take his bones to Canaan — the Genesis 50:25 LXX covenant-oath in Jubilees becomes the seed of the Exodus narrative / the covenant-continuity across the Egyptian sojourn. **Cross-link: Hebrews 11:22 (*pistei Iōsēph teleutōn peri tēs exodou tōn huiōn Israēl emnēmoneusen* — by faith Joseph when dying made mention of the exodus of the sons of Israel) = the Jubilees Joseph-bone-oath as the act of faith in the covenant's future fulfillment.**

**cc. 47–50 — MOSES / THE EXODUS / THE SINAI COVENANT / THE FINAL JUBILEE**

**cc. 47–49 — Moses / the plagues / Mastema / Passover:**

*48:1–19 — Mastema assists the Egyptians / the Lord overcomes:* The most detailed account in the pre-NT tradition of the adversary's activity during the Exodus — Mastema strengthens Pharaoh / Mastema assists the Egyptian sorcerers / Mastema is bound on the night of Passover so that he cannot accuse Israel. **CAE adversary-binding / Passover node:**

*48:15:* *And on that night — the beginning of the festival and the beginning of the joy — we bound him that he might not accuse the children of Israel on that night so that he might not smite them.*

The binding of Mastema on Passover night is the Jubilees tradition's most direct pre-figuration of the NT's *Christological Passover*: the Anointed's death on Passover (John 19:14 — the sixth hour on the *paraskeuē tou pascha* — preparation day of the Passover) is the eschatological fulfillment of the binding-of-the-adversary that Jubilees 48:15 describes as occurring on every Passover night. **Cross-link: John 12:31 (*nyn ho archōn tou kosmou toutou ekblēthēsetai exō* — now the ruler of this world will be cast out) / Colossians 2:15 (*apekdysamenos tas archas kai tas exousias edeigmatisen en parrēsia* — disarming the rulers and authorities he displayed them openly) = the eschatological fulfillment of Jubilees 48:15's annual Mastema-binding in the once-for-all Passover victory of the Anointed.**

*49:1–23 — The Passover statute and the 14th of Nisan:* The most detailed pre-NT Passover halakhah — the exact night / the moon's fullness / the eating within the sanctuary / the burning of bones. The Jubilees 364-day solar calendar's Passover always falls on the 14th of the first month on a Wednesday — creating the calendar-conflict with the lunar calendar that is the background for the Synoptic / Johannine chronological difference in the passion narrative. **CAE passion-chronology note:** the Synoptic Gospels present the Last Supper as a Passover meal (Matthew 26:17–19 / Mark 14:12–16 / Luke 22:7–15) while John presents the crucifixion occurring on the *preparation day* before Passover (John 18:28 / 19:14). The calendar-conflict is the Jubilees solar-calendar (in which Passover had already occurred before the lunar Passover) operating alongside the Temple's lunar calendar — both traditions are historically accurate within their respective calendrical systems.

**c.50 — The sabbatical theology and the final jubilee:**

*50:1–13 — The sabbath laws / the eschatological jubilee:* The final chapter summarizes the sabbath requirements and closes with the eschatological vision:

*50:4–5 — The jubilee as the eschatological telos:* *And the jubilee year shall be a jubilee to all of them — wherein they shall let the land lie fallow during it... and there shall be a jubilee for the land — a rest year.*

*50:12–13 — The eternal sabbath:* *The day which Moses was commanded — blessed and holy — the sabbath of the land of Canaan and the sabbath of its inhabitants — this is the sabbath of sabbbaths — and there are also fifty jubilees from the days of Adam until this day and one week and two years — and there are yet forty years to come for learning the commandments of the Lord until they pass over into the land of Canaan crossing the Jordan to the west.*

**CAE Chain 8 (*diathēkē aiōnios*) final-jubilee eschatology node:**

The fifty-jubilee count (50 × 49 = 2,450 years from Adam to the Sinai) with the 50th jubilee as the eschatological rest is the Jubilees tradition's compressed eschatology: the final jubilee is the creation's sabbath-rest — the *sabbatismos* that Hebrews 4:9 identifies as *still remaining for the people of God*. The Jubilees 50th jubilee is the *katapausis* (rest — Hebrews 4:1/3/5/10/11) of which every seventh year / every Yom Kippur / every jubilee year is the pattern and foretaste.

**The final-jubilee / Yom Kippur connection:** The 10th day of the 7th month of the jubilee year is Yom Kippur — the Day of Atonement / the *hilasmos* festival — which in Jubilees is simultaneously the day of covenant-renewal and the day that inaugurates the jubilee's liberation. **CAE Chain 1 (*hilastērion*) final-jubilee-as-atonement node:**

**Leviticus 25:8–10 LXX (*kai hagiaseis to etos to pentēkoston eniauton — kai aphesete dunamin en tē gē pasin tois oikousin autēn* — you shall sanctify the fiftieth year and proclaim liberty throughout the land to all its inhabitants) → Jubilees 50:12–13 (the fifty jubilees / the sabbath of sabbaths) → Isaiah 61:1–2 LXX (*kēryxai eniauton kyriou dekton* — to proclaim the acceptable year of the Lord) → Luke 4:18–21 (*kēryxai eniauton kyriou dekton... sēmeron peplērōtai hē graphē hautē* — to proclaim the acceptable year of the Lord / today this Scripture is fulfilled)**

The Anointed's Nazareth sermon (Luke 4:18–21) is the most explicit NT jubilee-proclamation — the *eniauton kyriou dekton* (acceptable year of the Lord) from Isaiah 61:1 LXX is the jubilee-year's liberation-proclamation. The Jubilees 50th-jubilee eschatology is the background that makes the Nazareth sermon's *today this Scripture is fulfilled* a jubilee-proclamation: the Anointed declares the eschatological jubilee — the final liberation / the permanent Yom Kippur atonement / the creation-sabbath rest — as inaugurated in his ministry. **This is the Jubilees tradition's single most important contribution to NT Christology:** the eschatological jubilee of Jubilees 50 is announced as *now* fulfilled in Luke 4:21 — the Anointed is the jubilee-herald / the one who proclaims liberty to the captives / who makes the final *hilasmos* / who inaugurates the *sabbatismos* that Hebrews 4 says still awaits its full entry.

The Pentecost tradition cannot be adequately explained without the Jubilees Shavuot-covenant-renewal framework — and that framework is LXX-calendar dependent.
