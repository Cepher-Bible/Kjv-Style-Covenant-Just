## 3. Major Translation Chains – Deeper Breakdown

### Chain 1: Logos / Chokhmah / Davar / Wisdom
- **John nodes:**  
  - 1:1–3 — In the beginning was the Word; all things through him  
  - 1:14 — Word became flesh and tabernacled among us  
  - 1:17 — grace and truth through Jesus Christ  
- **Cross-volume links:**  
  - → Prov 8:22–31 LXX (Wisdom beside God)  
  - → Wis 7:25–26 (breath of God’s power / emanation of glory)  
  - → Sir 24:3–8 (Wisdom from Most High / tabernacles in Jacob)  
  - → Acts 20:32 (word of his grace)  
  - → Heb 1:1–3 (final speech through Son / radiance of glory)  
  - → Col 1:15–17 (image of invisible God / all things through him)  
- **Red-team logic:** Not Stoic reason; pure LXX Wisdom/Davar convergence survives.

### Chain 2: I AM / Divine Name Disclosure
- **John nodes:**  
  - Absolute: 4:26 / 8:24,28,58 / 13:19 / 18:5–6 (soldiers fall)  
  - Predicate: 6:35 (bread of life) / 8:12 (light) / 10:7,9 (door) / 10:11,14 (good shepherd) / 11:25 (resurrection & life) / 14:6 (way/truth/life) / 15:1 (true vine)  
- **Cross-volume links:**  
  - → Ex 3:14 LXX (egō eimi ho ōn)  
  - → Isa 43:10 / 45:18 LXX (egō eimi)  
  - → Rev 1:17–18 (first and last / I am alive forever)  
- **Red-team logic:** Divine-name formula survives; no “I am he” softening allowed.

### Chain 3: Lamb / Sacrifice / Atonement
- **John nodes:**  
  - 1:29,36 — Lamb of God who takes away sin of world  
- **Cross-volume links:**  
  - → Isa 53:7 LXX (amnos before shearers)  
  - → Ex 12 (Passover lamb)  
  - → 1 Pet 1:19 (precious blood of Christ as lamb)  
  - → Rev 5:6 (arnion as though slain)  
- **Red-team logic:** Sacrificial Passover/Isaiah convergence survives; no diminutive drift.

### Chain 9: Tabernacle / Presence
- **John nodes:**  
  - 1:14 — Word tabernacled among us  
  - 2:19–21 — Destroy this sanctuary (his body)  
- **Cross-volume links:**  
  - → Sir 24:8 (Wisdom tabernacles in Jacob)  
  - → Acts 7:44 / 15:16 (tent of witness / rebuild David’s tent)  
  - → Heb 9:11 (true tent not made by hands)  
  - → Rev 21:3 (God tabernacles with men)  
- **Red-team logic:** Mobile divine presence survives; no fixed-temple drift.

### Chain 12: Feast-after-Reversal
- **John nodes:**  
  - Cana wedding (2:1–11) — first sign  
  - Passover / Hanukkah / Tabernacles settings  
- **Cross-volume links:**  
  - → Acts 2 (Pentecost outpouring on feast)  
  - → Tobit wedding feast  
  - → Rev 19 (marriage supper of Lamb)  
- **Red-team logic:** Reversal = death-to-life + feast survives.
