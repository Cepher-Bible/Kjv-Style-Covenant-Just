## 3. Major Translation Chains – Deeper Breakdown

### Chain 1: Ransom / Atonement / Substitution (*antipsychon*)
- **Mark nodes:**  
  - 10:45 — Son of Man gives his psychēn as antipsychon anti pollōn  
- **Cross-volume links:**  
  - → 4 Macc 6:29 / 17:21 (righteous death as antipsychon)  
  - → Matt 20:28 (parallel antipsychon formulation)  
  - → Rom 3:25 (hilastērion through faith in his blood)  
  - → Heb 2:17 (merciful high priest to make propitiation)  
- **Red-team logic:** Technical substitution term survives; no vague “ransom” softening. Chain 1 Gospel lock achieved.

### Chain 3: Pneuma / Sarx Tension
- **Mark nodes:**  
  - 1:12 (*ekballei* — Breath-of-life casts into wilderness)  
  - 14:38 (pneuma prothumon / sarx asthenēs — willing breath-of-life / weak flesh)  
- **Cross-volume links:**  
  - → Gen 2:7 LXX (breathed into nostrils)  
  - → Ezek 37:5–10 (pneuma enters bones → live)  
  - → John 3:6 (born of sarx is sarx / born of pneuma is pneuma)  
  - → Rom 8:5–13 (mind set on sarx vs mind set on pneuma)  
- **Red-team logic:** Not dualism; interim anthropological tension between renewal and weakness survives.

### Chain 9: Sanctuary / Presence (*naos / skēnē*)
- **Mark nodes:**  
  - 14:58 (destroy this naon cheiropoiēton / build acheiropoiēton)  
  - 15:38 (veil of naou torn top to bottom)  
- **Cross-volume links:**  
  - → Isa 66:1–2 LXX (heaven my throne / no house)  
  - → Acts 7:48 (not in cheiropoiētois)  
  - → Heb 9:11 (true naos not cheiropoiētos)  
  - → Rev 21:22 (no naos — Lord God and Lamb are its temple)  
- **Red-team logic:** Made/not-made-by-hands contrast survives; explicit Temple-supersession node.

### Chain 12: Feast-after-Reversal
- **Mark nodes:**  
  - Passover settings (14:1–16)  
  - Bread-breaking meals (6:41 / 8:6 / 14:22)  
- **Cross-volume links:**  
  - → Luke 2:41 (Passover) / 15:23 (fattened calf)  
  - → Acts 2 (Pentecost feast outpouring)  
  - → Rev 19 (marriage supper of Lamb)  
- **Red-team logic:** Reversal = death-to-life + meal survives.
