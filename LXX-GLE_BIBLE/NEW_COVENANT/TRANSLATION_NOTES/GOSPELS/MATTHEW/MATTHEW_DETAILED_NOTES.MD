## 3. Major Translation Chains – Deeper Breakdown

### Chain 1: Ransom / Atonement / Substitution (*antipsychon*)
- **Matthew nodes:**  
  - 20:28 — Son of Man gives psychēn as antipsychon anti pollōn  
- **Cross-volume links:**  
  - → 4 Macc 6:29 / 17:21 (martyrs’ deaths as antipsychon)  
  - → Mark 10:45 (parallel antipsychon)  
  - → Rom 3:25 (hilastērion through blood)  
  - → Heb 2:17 (merciful high priest / propitiation)  
- **Red-team logic:** Technical substitution term survives; Matthew locks Chain 1 Gospel node.

### Chain 8: Covenant Blood-Sealing (*diathēkē*)
- **Matthew nodes:**  
  - 26:28 — my blood of the covenant poured out for many  
- **Cross-volume links:**  
  - → Exod 24:8 LXX (blood of the covenant)  
  - → Zech 9:11 LXX (blood of covenant)  
  - → Mark 14:24 / Luke 22:20 (parallel institution)  
  - → Heb 13:20 (blood of eternal covenant)  
- **Red-team logic:** Direct Sinai echo survives; Chain 8 sealed.

### Chain 9: Sanctuary / Presence (*naos / skēnē*)
- **Matthew nodes:**  
  - 27:51 (veil of naou torn top to bottom)  
  - 27:40/43 (destroy naon / rebuild in three days — mockery)  
- **Cross-volume links:**  
  - → Exod 25:8 LXX (tabernacle)  
  - → Isa 66:1–2 LXX (heaven my throne)  
  - → Mark 14:58 (acheiropoiēton)  
  - → Heb 9:11 (true naos not cheiropoiētos)  
  - → Rev 21:22 (no naos — Lord and Lamb)  
- **Red-team logic:** Veil-tearing as fulfillment node survives.

### Chain 12: Feast-after-Reversal
- **Matthew nodes:**  
  - Wedding feast parable (22:1–14)  
  - Passover institution (26:17–29)  
- **Cross-volume links:**  
  - → Luke 14:16–24 (great banquet)  
  - → Rev 19:7–9 (marriage supper of Lamb)  
- **Red-team logic:** Reversal = invitation / judgment + meal survives.
