## 3. Major Translation Chains – Deeper Breakdown

### Chain 1: Redemption / Ransom / Atonement (*lytrōsis / apolytrōsis / hilaskomai*)
- **Luke nodes:**  
  - 1:68 — visited and *redeemed* his people (Benedictus)  
  - 2:38 — Anna speaks of *redemption* of Jerusalem  
  - 18:13 — tax collector: *hilasthēti moi* (be merciful / atone for me)  
  - 18:14 — goes home *dedikaiōmenos* (declared righteous)  
  - 21:28 — *apolytrōsis* drawing near  
  - 24:21 — Emmaus: hoped he would *redeem* Israel  
- **Cross-volume links:**  
  - → Acts 7:35 (Moses as *lytrōtēs* type)  
  - → Acts 20:28 (assembly obtained with his own blood)  
  - → Romans 3:24–25 (justified freely / *hilastērion*)  
  - → Hebrews 9:12 (eternal *apolutrōsis* through blood)  
- **Red-team logic:** *hilaskomai* (Lev 16 LXX) → *dikaioō* chain survives only if forensic atonement preserved; no softening to “mercy” allowed.

### Chain 3: Living to God (*zēn tō theō*)
- **Luke nodes:**  
  - 20:38 — *pantes gar autō zōsin* — "all live to him" (burning bush argument)  
- **Cross-volume links:**  
  - → 4 Macc 7:19 (patriarchs live to God)  
  - → Romans 6:10–11 (Christ lives to God / reckon yourselves alive to God)  
  - → Acts 17:28 (in him we live and move)  
- **Red-team logic:** Bodily resurrection (24:39 — not a *pneuma*) + present-tense life to God = no dualistic soul immortality drift.

### Chain 9: Tabernacle / Tent (*skēnē*)
- **Luke nodes:**  
  - Implicit in infancy (temple scenes 2:22–38)  
  - 24:53 — continually in the temple (*hieron*) blessing God (end of Gospel)  
- **Cross-volume links:**  
  - → Acts 7:44 (tent of witness)  
  - → Acts 15:16 (rebuild tent of David)  
  - → John 1:14 (Word *eskēnōsen* — tabernacled)  
  - → Hebrews 8:2 / 9:11 (true tent not made by hands)  
  - → Rev 21:3 (God tabernacles with men)  
- **Red-team logic:** Mobile presence (not fixed temple) survives; no sacral drift to “tabernacle” as object.

### Chain 12: Feast-after-Reversal
- **Luke nodes:**  
  - Passover (2:41)  
  - Prodigal Son fattened-calf feast (15:23–24 — dead and alive again)  
- **Cross-volume links:**  
  - → Acts 2 (Pentecost outpouring on feast day)  
  - → Tobit wedding feast  
  - → Rev 19 (marriage supper of the Lamb)  
- **Red-team logic:** Reversal = death-to-life + feast; no sentimental “family reunion” drift.
