

### Recommended RP2026 NT order:
Tier 1 — Four anchor books (maximum CAE payoff density from the OT corpus):
John — Logos/Chokhmah/Davar synthesis; every major LXX thread arrives; egō eimi fulfills the divine-name pattern; seven sign-structure
Hebrews — the most LXX-dependent NT book; cloud of witnesses closes the Maccabean arc; Melchizedek; the better covenant; the rest from Joshua
Romans — systematic OT-NT synthesis; hilastērion (4 Maccabees chain); dikaioō; huiothesia; eleos of the nations
Revelation — eschatological synthesis of the entire LXX: arnion, ōdē kainē, New Jerusalem (Tobit 13), seven angels (Tobit 12:15), martyrs under the altar (2–4 Maccabees)
Tier 2 — Synoptic-Acts complex (5–8)
Tier 3 — Pauline corpus by theological density (Galatians → Ephesians → Colossians → 1–2 Corinthians → Philippians → Thessalonians → Pastorals)
Tier 4 — General Epistles (James → 1–2 Peter → 1–2–3 John → Jude)
Beginning now with John.

\c 1
\p
\v 1 In the beginning was the Word (*Logos*) — and the Word was with God (*pros ton theon*) — and the Word was God (*theos ēn ho Logos*).
\v 2 He was in the beginning with God.
\v 3 All things came into being through him — and apart from him not one thing came into being that has come into being.
\v 4 In him was life (*zōē*) — and the life was the light of men.
\v 5 And the light shines in the darkness — and the darkness has not overcome it.
\p
\v 6 There was a man sent from God whose name was John.
\v 7 He came as a witness to testify about the light — so that all might believe through him.
\v 8 He was not the light — but came to testify about the light.
\v 9 The true light — who gives light to every man — was coming into the world.
\p
\v 10 He was in the world — and the world came into being through him — and the world did not know him.
\v 11 He came to his own — and his own people did not receive him.
\v 12 But as many as received him — to them he gave authority to become children of God (*tekna theou*) — to those who believe in his name —
\v 13 who were born not of blood nor of the will of the flesh (*sarkos*) nor of the will of man but of God.
\p
\v 14 And the Word became flesh (*sarx egeneto*) and tabernacled (*eskēnōsen*) among us — and we beheld his glory — glory as of the only-begotten (*monogenous*) from the Father — full of grace and truth (*charitos kai alētheias*).
\v 15 John testified about him and cried out saying: This was he of whom I said — He who comes after me has surpassed me — for he was before me.
\v 16 For of his fullness (*plērōmatos*) we all received — and grace upon grace (*charin anti charitos*).
\v 17 For the Law was given through Moses — grace and truth (*hē charis kai hē alētheia*) came through Jesus the Anointed (*Iēsou Christou*).
\v 18 No one has ever seen God — the only-begotten Son (*monogenēs huios*) who is in the bosom of the Father — he has made him known (*exēgēsato*).
\p
\v 19 This is the testimony of John when the Jews from Jerusalem sent priests and Levites to ask him: Who are you?
\v 20 He confessed and did not deny — he confessed: I am not the Anointed (*Christos*).
\v 21 They asked him: What then? Are you Elijah? He said: I am not. Are you the Prophet? He answered: No.
\v 22 So they said to him: Who are you? — so that we may give an answer to those who sent us. What do you say about yourself?
\v 23 He said: I am the voice of one crying in the wilderness: Make straight the way of the Lord — as Isaiah the prophet said.
\p
\v 24 Those who had been sent were from the Pharisees —
\v 25 and they asked him saying: Why then are you baptizing if you are not the Anointed nor Elijah nor the Prophet?
\v 26 John answered them saying: I baptize in water — but among you stands one whom you do not know —
\v 27 he who comes after me — the strap of whose sandal I am not worthy to untie.
\v 28 These things took place in Bethany beyond the Jordan — where John was baptizing.
\p
\v 29 The next day he saw Jesus coming toward him and said: Look — the Lamb of God who takes away the sin of the world (*ho amnos tou theou ho airōn tēn hamartian tou kosmou*).
\v 30 This is he of whom I said: After me comes a man who surpasses me — for he was before me.
\v 31 I myself did not know him — but so that he might be made manifest to Israel I came baptizing in water.
\v 32 And John testified saying: I beheld the breath-of-life (*pneuma*) descending like a dove out of heaven — and it remained on him.
\v 33 I myself did not know him — but the one who sent me to baptize in water — he said to me: On whom you see the breath-of-life descend and remain — this is the one who baptizes in the Holy Breath-of-life (*pneumati hagiō*).
\v 34 And I have seen and I testify that this is the Son of God.
\p
\v 35 The next day John was standing with two of his disciples —
\v 36 and he looked at Jesus walking and said: Look — the Lamb of God (*ho amnos tou theou*).
\v 37 The two disciples heard him say this and they followed Jesus.
\v 38 Jesus turned and seeing them following said to them: What are you seeking? They said to him: Rabbi — which means Teacher — where are you staying?
\v 39 He said to them: Come and see. So they came and saw where he was staying and they remained with him that day — for it was about the tenth hour.
\v 40 Andrew — the brother of Simon Peter — was one of the two who heard John and followed him.
\v 41 He first found his own brother Simon and said to him: We have found the Messiah — which is translated Anointed (*Christos*).
\v 42 He brought him to Jesus. Jesus looked at him and said: You are Simon the son of John — you shall be called Cephas — which means Rock (*Petros*).
\p
\v 43 The next day he decided to go out to Galilee and he found Philip. And Jesus said to him: Follow me.
\v 44 Now Philip was from Bethsaida — from the city of Andrew and Peter.
\v 45 Philip found Nathanael and said to him: We have found him about whom Moses in the Law and also the prophets wrote — Jesus of Nazareth the son of Joseph.
\v 46 Nathanael said to him: Can anything good come out of Nazareth? Philip said to him: Come and see.
\v 47 Jesus saw Nathanael coming toward him and said of him: Look — truly an Israelite in whom there is no deceit.
\v 48 Nathanael said to him: How do you know me? Jesus answered him: Before Philip called you — when you were under the fig tree — I saw you.
\v 49 Nathanael answered him: Rabbi — you are the Son of God — you are the King of Israel.
\v 50 Jesus answered him: Because I said to you that I saw you under the fig tree — do you believe? You will see greater things than these.
\v 51 And he said to him: Truly truly I say to you — you will see heaven opened and the angels of God ascending and descending upon the Son of Man (*huion tou anthrōpou*).

\p
\v 1 On the third day there was a wedding at Cana in Galilee and the mother of Jesus was there.
\v 2 Jesus also was invited to the wedding with his disciples.
\v 3 When the wine ran out the mother of Jesus said to him: They have no wine.
\v 4 And Jesus said to her: Woman — what does this have to do with me? My hour has not yet come.
\v 5 His mother said to the servants: Do whatever he tells you.
\p
\v 6 Now there were six stone water jars there for the Jewish rites of purification — each holding twenty or thirty gallons.
\v 7 Jesus said to them: Fill the jars with water. And they filled them up to the brim.
\v 8 And he said to them: Now draw some out and take it to the master of the feast. So they took it.
\v 9 When the master of the feast tasted the water now become wine and did not know where it came from — though the servants who had drawn the water knew — the master of the feast called the bridegroom
\v 10 and said to him: Everyone serves the good wine first and when people have drunk freely then the poor wine. But you have kept the good wine until now.
\v 11 This the beginning of signs (*archēn tōn sēmeiōn*) Jesus did at Cana in Galilee — and manifested his glory — and his disciples believed in him.
\p
\v 13 The Passover of the Jews was at hand and Jesus went up to Jerusalem.
\v 14 In the temple he found those who were selling oxen and sheep and pigeons and the money-changers sitting there.
\v 15 And making a whip of cords he drove them all out of the temple — both the sheep and the oxen — and he poured out the coins of the money-changers and overturned their tables.
\v 16 And he told those who sold the pigeons: Take these things away — do not make my Father's house a house of trade.
\v 17 His disciples remembered that it was written: Zeal for your house will consume me (*ho zēlos tou oikou sou kataphagētai me*).
\p
\v 18 So the Jews said to him: What sign do you show us for doing these things?
\v 19 Jesus answered them: Destroy this sanctuary (*naon*) and in three days I will raise it up (*egeirō*).
\v 20 The Jews then said: It has taken forty-six years to build this sanctuary and will you raise it up in three days?
\v 21 But he was speaking about the sanctuary (*naō*) of his body.
\v 22 When therefore he was raised from the dead his disciples remembered that he had said this and they believed the Scripture and the word that Jesus had spoken.

\c 3
\p
\v 1 Now there was a man of the Pharisees named Nicodemus — a ruler of the Jews.
\v 2 This man came to Jesus by night and said to him: Rabbi — we know that you are a teacher come from God — for no one can do these signs that you do unless God is with him.
\v 3 Jesus answered him: Truly truly I say to you — unless one is born from above (*anōthen*) he cannot see the kingdom of God.
\v 4 Nicodemus said to him: How can a man be born when he is old? Can he enter a second time into his mother's womb and be born?
\v 5 Jesus answered: Truly truly I say to you — unless one is born of water and breath-of-life (*pneumatos*) he cannot enter the kingdom of God.
\v 6 What is born of the flesh (*sarkos*) is flesh (*sarx*) — and what is born of the breath-of-life (*pneumatos*) is breath-of-life (*pneuma*).
\v 7 Do not marvel that I said to you: You must be born from above (*anōthen*).
\v 8 The breath-of-life (*pneuma*) blows where it wishes and you hear its sound — but you do not know where it comes from or where it goes. So it is with everyone who is born of the breath-of-life (*pneumatos*).
\p
\v 14 And as Moses lifted up the serpent in the wilderness — so must the Son of Man be lifted up (*hypsōthēnai*) —
\v 15 so that everyone who believes in him may have everlasting life (*zōēn aiōnion*).
\v 16 For God so loved the world (*houtōs gar ēgapēsen ho theos ton kosmon*) that he gave his only-begotten Son (*ton huion ton monogenē*) — so that everyone who believes in him should not perish but have everlasting life (*zōēn aiōnion*).
\v 17 For God did not send the Son into the world to judge the world — but so that the world might be saved (*sōthē*) through him.
\v 18 Whoever believes in him is not judged — but whoever does not believe is judged already because he has not believed in the name of the only-begotten Son of God.
\v 19 And this is the judgment: the light has come into the world and people loved the darkness rather than the light — for their deeds were evil.
\v 20 For everyone who does evil hates the light and does not come to the light lest his deeds should be exposed.
\v 21 But the one who does what is true comes to the light so that it may be clearly seen that his deeds have been carried out in God.
\p
\v 30 He must increase and I must decrease.
\v 31 He who comes from above is above all — he who is of the earth belongs to the earth and speaks in an earthly way. He who comes from heaven is above all.
\v 34 For he whom God has sent utters the words of God — for he gives the breath-of-life (*pneuma*) without measure.
\v 35 The Father loves the Son and has given all things into his hand.
\v 36 Whoever believes in the Son has everlasting life (*zōēn aiōnion*) — whoever does not obey the Son shall not see life (*zōēn*) — but the wrath of God remains on him.

\p

\v 5 So he came to a town of Samaria called Sychar — near the field that Jacob had given to his son Joseph.
\v 6 Jacob's well was there — so Jesus wearied as he was from his journey was sitting beside the well. It was about the sixth hour.
\v 7 A woman from Samaria came to draw water. Jesus said to her: Give me a drink.
\v 10 Jesus answered her: If you knew the gift of God and who it is that is saying to you — Give me a drink — you would have asked him and he would have given you living water (*hydōr zōn*).
\v 13 Jesus said to her: Everyone who drinks of this water will be thirsty again —
\v 14 but whoever drinks of the water that I will give him will never be thirsty again. The water that I will give him will become in him a spring of water welling up to everlasting life (*eis zōēn aiōnion*).
\p
\v 19 The woman said to him: Sir — I perceive that you are a prophet.
\v 20 Our fathers worshipped on this mountain — but you say that in Jerusalem is the place where people ought to worship.
\v 21 Jesus said to her: Woman believe me — the hour is coming when neither on this mountain nor in Jerusalem will you worship the Father.
\v 23 But the hour is coming and is now here when the true worshippers will worship the Father in breath-of-life and truth (*en pneumati kai alētheia*) — for the Father is seeking such people to worship him.
\v 24 God is breath-of-life (*pneuma ho theos*) — and those who worship him must worship in breath-of-life and truth (*en pneumati kai alētheia*).
\v 25 The woman said to him: I know that the Anointed One is coming — he who is called the Anointed (*Christos*). When he comes he will tell us all things.
\v 26 Jesus said to her: I AM — the one speaking to you (*Egō eimi — ho lalōn soi*).
\p
\v 42 They said to the woman: It is no longer because of what you said that we believe — for we have heard for ourselves and we know that this is truly the Savior of the world (*ho sōtēr tou kosmou*).

\p
\v 17 But Jesus answered them: My Father is working until now — and I am working.
\v 18 This was why the Jews were seeking all the more to kill him — because not only was he breaking the Sabbath but he was even calling God his own Father — making himself equal to God.
\p
\v 19 So Jesus said to them: Truly truly I say to you — the Son can do nothing on his own — only what he sees the Father doing. For whatever the Father does the Son does likewise.
\v 20 For the Father loves the Son and shows him all that he himself is doing.
\v 21 For as the Father raises the dead and gives them life (*zōopoiei*) so also the Son gives life to whom he will.
\v 22 For the Father judges no one but has given all judgment to the Son —
\v 23 so that all may honor the Son even as they honor the Father. Whoever does not honor the Son does not honor the Father who sent him.
\v 24 Truly truly I say to you — whoever hears my word and believes him who sent me has everlasting life (*zōēn aiōnion*) — and does not come into judgment but has passed from death into life.
\v 25 Truly truly I say to you — an hour is coming and is now here when the dead will hear the voice of the Son of God — and those who hear will live.
\v 26 For as the Father has life in himself (*zōēn en heautō*) so he has granted the Son also to have life in himself (*zōēn en heautō*).
\v 28 Do not marvel at this — for an hour is coming when all who are in the tombs will hear his voice
\v 29 and come out — those who have done good to the resurrection of life (*anastasin zōēs*) and those who have done evil to the resurrection of judgment (*anastasin kriseōs*).
\p
\v 39 You search the Scriptures because you think that in them you have everlasting life (*zōēn aiōnion*) — and it is they that testify about me.
\v 40 Yet you refuse to come to me that you may have life (*zōēn*).
\v 46 For if you believed Moses you would believe me — for he wrote of me.
\v 47 But if you do not believe his writings how will you believe my words?

\p
\v 27 Do not work for the food that perishes — but for the food that endures to everlasting life (*zōēn aiōnion*) which the Son of Man will give to you. For on him God the Father has set his seal.
\v 32 Jesus then said to them: Truly truly I say to you — it was not Moses who gave you the bread from heaven — but my Father gives you the true bread from heaven.
\v 33 For the bread of God is he who comes down from heaven and gives life (*zōēn*) to the world.
\v 35 Jesus said to them: I AM the bread of life (*Egō eimi ho artos tēs zōēs*) — whoever comes to me shall not hunger and whoever believes in me shall never thirst.
\p
\v 37 All that the Father gives me will come to me — and whoever comes to me I will never cast out.
\v 38 For I have come down from heaven not to do my own will but the will of him who sent me.
\v 39 And this is the will of him who sent me — that I should lose nothing of all that he has given me — but raise it up on the last day.
\v 40 For this is the will of my Father — that everyone who looks to the Son and believes in him should have everlasting life (*zōēn aiōnion*) — and I will raise him up on the last day.
\p
\v 44 No one can come to me unless the Father who sent me draws him — and I will raise him up on the last day.
\v 47 Truly truly I say to you — whoever believes has everlasting life (*zōēn aiōnion*).
\v 48 I AM the bread of life (*Egō eimi ho artos tēs zōēs*).
\v 51 I am the living bread that came down from heaven. If anyone eats of this bread he will live forever. And the bread that I will give for the life of the world is my flesh (*sarx*).
\p
\v 53 So Jesus said to them: Truly truly I say to you — unless you eat the flesh (*sarka*) of the Son of Man and drink his blood you have no life (*zōēn*) in you.
\v 54 Whoever feeds on my flesh and drinks my blood has everlasting life (*zōēn aiōnion*) and I will raise him up on the last day.
\v 55 For my flesh is true food and my blood is true drink.
\v 56 Whoever feeds on my flesh and drinks my blood abides in me and I in him.
\v 57 As the living Father sent me and I live because of the Father — so whoever feeds on me he also will live because of me.
\v 58 This is the bread that came down from heaven — not like the bread the fathers ate and died. Whoever feeds on this bread will live forever.
\p
\v 63 It is the breath-of-life (*pneuma*) that gives life — the flesh (*sarx*) is of no avail. The words that I have spoken to you are breath-of-life and life (*pneuma estin kai zōē estin*).
\v 68 Simon Peter answered him: Lord — to whom shall we go? You have the words of everlasting life (*rhēmata zōēs aiōniou*) —
\v 69 and we have believed and have come to know that you are the Holy One of God (*ho hagios tou theou*).

\p
\v 37 On the last day of the feast — the great day — Jesus stood up and cried out: If anyone thirsts let him come to me and drink.
\v 38 Whoever believes in me — as the Scripture has said — out of his heart (*koilias*) shall flow rivers of living water (*hydatos zōntos*).
\v 39 Now this he said about the breath-of-life (*pneumatos*) whom those who believed in him were to receive — for as yet the breath-of-life (*pneuma*) had not been given because Jesus was not yet glorified.

\p
\v 12 Again Jesus spoke to them saying: I AM the light of the world (*Egō eimi to phōs tou kosmou*). Whoever follows me will not walk in darkness but will have the light of life.
\p
\v 24 I told you that you would die in your sins — for unless you believe that I AM (*hoti egō eimi*) you will die in your sins.
\v 28 So Jesus said to them: When you have lifted up the Son of Man (*hypsōsēte ton huion tou anthrōpou*) then you will know that I AM (*hoti egō eimi*) — and that I do nothing on my own authority but speak just as the Father taught me.
\p
\v 31 So Jesus said to the Jews who had believed him: If you abide in my word you are truly my disciples —
\v 32 and you will know the truth (*alētheian*) and the truth will set you free.
\v 33 They answered him: We are offspring of Abraham and have never been enslaved to anyone. How is it that you say: You will become free?
\v 34 Jesus answered them: Truly truly I say to you — everyone who practices sin is a slave to sin.
\v 35 The slave does not remain in the house forever — the son remains forever.
\v 36 So if the Son sets you free you will be free indeed.
\p
\v 44 You are of your father the devil — and your will is to do your father's desires. He was a murderer from the beginning and does not stand in the truth (*alētheia*) — because there is no truth in him. When he lies he speaks out of his own character — for he is a liar and the father of lies.
\v 45 But because I tell the truth (*alētheian*) you do not believe me.
\p
\v 56 Your father Abraham rejoiced that he would see my day — he saw it and was glad.
\v 57 So the Jews said to him: You are not yet fifty years old and have you seen Abraham?
\v 58 Jesus said to them: Truly truly I say to you — before Abraham was I AM (*prin Abraam genesthai egō eimi*).

\v 39 Jesus said: For judgment I came into this world — so that those who do not see may see and those who see may become blind.

\p
\v 27 My sheep hear my voice and I know them and they follow me.
\v 28 I give them everlasting life (*zōēn aiōnion*) and they will never perish — and no one will snatch them out of my hand.
\v 29 My Father who has given them to me is greater than all — and no one is able to snatch them out of the Father's hand.
\v 30 I and the Father are one (*Egō kai ho Patēr hen esmen*).
\p
\v 33 The Jews answered him: It is not for a good work that we are going to stone you but for blasphemy — because you being a man make yourself God.
\v 36 Do you say of him whom the Father consecrated and sent into the world: You are blaspheming — because I said I am the Son of God?
\v 38 But if I do them even though you do not believe me — believe the works — so that you may know and understand that the Father is in me and I am in the Father.

## CRITICAL TEXTUAL NOTES — JOHN

**The LXX substratum:** John is the most LXX-saturated NT book after Hebrews. Its Prologue deploys LXX Wisdom-tradition vocabulary (*en archē* / *Logos* / *phōs* / *skotia* / *eskēnōsen*). Its *egō eimi* absolute declarations echo the LXX divine-name formula. Its citation of Scripture follows the LXX. Its *alētheia* theology echoes the LXX sapiential tradition and the Tobit/1 Esdras *alētheia* spine.

**The seven signs structure:** John's Gospel is organized around seven *sēmeia* = signs: water-to-wine (2:1–11) / healing of official's son (4:46–54) / healing at Bethesda (5:1–15) / feeding five thousand (6:1–14) / walking on water (6:16–21) / healing of blind man (9:1–41) / raising of Lazarus (11:1–44). The seventh sign (Lazarus) is the climactic sign before the Passion. The signs structure mirrors the seven-day creation structure of Genesis 1.

**The seven *egō eimi* declarations with predicate:**
1. *Egō eimi ho artos tēs zōēs* — I AM the bread of life (6:35, 48)
2. *Egō eimi to phōs tou kosmou* — I AM the light of the world (8:12)
3. *Egō eimi hē thyra* — I AM the door (10:7, 9)
4. *Egō eimi ho poimēn ho kalos* — I AM the good shepherd (10:11, 14)

6. *Egō eimi hē hodos kai hē alētheia kai hē zōē* — I AM the way and the truth and the life (14:6)
7. *Egō eimi hē ampelos hē alēthinē* — I AM the true vine (15:1, 5)

**The absolute *egō eimi* declarations** (without predicate — the divine-name formula):
- 4:26 — *egō eimi ho lalōn soi* = I AM — the one speaking to you

## CAE NODES — JOHN

### John 1:1–18 — the Prologue / *Logos* / *Chokhmah* / *Davar* synthesis — CAE CRITICAL NODE

**1:1–3** — "In the beginning was the Word (*Logos*) — and the Word was with God — and the Word was God. He was in the beginning with God. All things came into being through him and apart from him not one thing came into being."

**1:14** — "And the Word became flesh (*sarx egeneto*) and tabernacled (*eskēnōsen*) among us — and we beheld his glory — glory as of the only-begotten from the Father — full of grace and truth (*charitos kai alētheias*)."

**1:17–18** — "For the Law was given through Moses — grace and truth came through Jesus the Anointed. No one has ever seen God — the only-begotten God who is in the bosom of the Father — he has made him known (*exēgēsato*)."

***en archē ēn ho Logos kai ho Logos ēn pros ton theon kai theos ēn ho Logos***

= In the beginning was the Word — and the Word was toward God — and God was the Word.

The Prologue is the NT's supreme theological statement and the landing point of the entire OT-deuterocanonical Wisdom/Word tradition. The GLE reads it through the Covenant Anchor Engine v2 Trinitarian model:

**The *Logos* as *Chokhmah* + *Davar*:**

The *Logos* of John 1:1 is not the Stoic *logos spermatikos* = seminal reason of cosmic order. It is the convergence of two Hebrew/LXX streams:

1. ***Chokhmah* / *Sophia* = Wisdom as eternal semantic content**: Proverbs 8:22–31 (*I was beside him as a master craftsman / I was his delight daily*) / Wisdom of Solomon 7:25–26 (*she is a breath of the power of God / a pure emanation of the glory of the Almighty*) / Sirach 24:3 (*I came forth from the mouth of the Most High*). The eternal Wisdom who was *with* God in creation / who is the *amon* = craftsman/artisan beside the Father / who *tabernacles* in Israel (Sirach 24:8). The *Logos* is this Wisdom — eternal / personal / co-creator / dwelling with God before the world.

2. ***Davar* = Word as expressive act**: The Hebrew *davar* = spoken word as enacted reality (Genesis 1: *God said / and it was*). The spoken word of God does not merely describe — it enacts. The *Logos* is the *Davar* = the divine self-expression that is simultaneously the divine creative act. All things came into being *through him* (1:3) because the creation-by-*Davar* of Genesis 1 is the creation *through the Logos*.

The Prologue's *en archē* = in the beginning is the deliberate echo of Genesis 1:1 (*en archē epoiēsen ho theos* = in the beginning God made). The *Logos* who was *en archē* is the *Davar* of Genesis 1 now disclosed as the eternal Person who was with God — as *Chokhmah* was beside the Father — from before creation.

**The *eskēnōsen* = tabernacled** (1:14): The *Logos* *eskēnōsen* = pitched his tent / tabernacled among us — the same root as *skēnē* = tabernacle (the locked vocabulary of the GLE). The Logos-become-flesh is the divine *skēnē* = tabernacle enacted in human form. The cloud of glory that filled the Tabernacle (Exodus 40:34) / the Temple (2 Paralipomenon 5:13–14) / that departed in exile (Ezekiel 10) — now *eskēnōsen* = tabernacled in the flesh of the Son. The *doxa* = glory that Ezekiel tracked departing and returning has returned in the person of Jesus.

**The *charis kai alētheia*** (1:14, 17): The LXX renders the Hebrew *ḥesed we'emet* = steadfast love and faithfulness as *eleos kai alētheia* (Psalm 85:10 / Proverbs 3:3) and also as *charis kai alētheia*. John 1:14, 17 deploys *charis kai alētheia* = grace and truth as the Prologue's summary of what the *Logos-made-flesh* brings. This is the *ḥesed we'emet* = steadfast love and faithfulness of the covenant — the character of YHWH declared in Exodus 34:6 (*abundant in steadfast love and faithfulness*) — now incarnate in Jesus the Anointed. The covenant-character becomes a Person.

**Proverbs 8:22–31** — The eternal Wisdom present with God in creation is the primary LXX ground for the Prologue's *Logos* theology.

**Wisdom of Solomon 7:25–26** — "*She is a breath of the power of God and a pure emanation of the glory of the Almighty*" — the Wisdom-as-emanation-of-glory language feeds directly into the Prologue's *doxa monogenous para Patros* = glory as of the only-begotten from the Father.

**Sirach 24:8** — "*Then the Creator of all things gave me a commandment and my Creator chose the place for my tent. He said: Make your dwelling in Jacob and in Israel receive your inheritance.*" The Wisdom who tabernacles in Israel is the *Logos* who *eskēnōsen* among us. The Sirach *skēnē* = tent of Wisdom in Israel is fulfilled in the *eskēnōsen* of the Logos in flesh.

**Genesis 1:1–3** — *En archē* / *God said* / *and it was* — the *Davar*-creation is the OT ground for *all things came into being through him*.

**Colossians 1:15–17** — "He is the image of the invisible God the firstborn of all creation. For by him all things were created... He is before all things and in him all things hold together." Paul's Colossian Christology is the Pauline equivalent of John's Logos-Prologue: both deploy the Wisdom/Davar tradition to describe the Son as the agent of creation and the one in whom all things cohere.

**Hebrews 1:1–3** — "Long ago at many times and in many ways God spoke to our fathers by the prophets but in these last days he has spoken to us by his Son... He is the radiance of the glory of God and the exact imprint of his nature." The Hebrews opening is the third NT deployment of the Wisdom-Christology: *radiance of glory* echoes Wisdom of Solomon 7:26 / *exact imprint of his nature* = *charaktēr tēs hypostaseōs* echoes the *ikon* / *image* language. The Prologue / Colossians / Hebrews are the NT Wisdom-Christology triptych.

### John 1:29, 36 — *ho amnos tou theou* / the Lamb of God — CAE CRITICAL NODE

**1:29** — "Look — the Lamb of God who takes away the sin of the world (*ho amnos tou theou ho airōn tēn hamartian tou kosmou*)."

**1:36** — "Look — the Lamb of God (*ho amnos tou theou*)."

***ho amnos tou theou*** — the Lamb of God.

The Baptist's declaration is the NT's first explicit sacrificial-Christology statement. The *amnos* = lamb is the sacrificial animal of the Passover / the daily burnt offering / the Yom Kippur ritual. The theological weight:

**1. The Passover lamb** (Exodus 12): The *amnos* whose blood protected Israel from the destroyer on the night of the Exodus. *Christ our Passover has been sacrificed* (1 Corinthians 5:7) — the Passover-lamb typology fulfilled.

**2. The Isaiah 53 Servant**: The most direct OT source for John's *amnos* formula: "He was led like a sheep to the slaughter and like a lamb (*amnos*) before its shearers is silent" (Isaiah 53:7 LXX). The *amnos tou theou* = Lamb of God is the Baptist's identification of Jesus as the Isaiah 53 Servant — the one who bears the sin of many.

**3. The Yom Kippur scapegoat**: *ho airōn tēn hamartian tou kosmou* = who takes away the sin of the world — the *airōn* = taking away / lifting off / carrying away is the LXX language for the scapegoat that carried (*apopherei*) the sins into the wilderness (Leviticus 16:21–22).

The *amnos* / *airōn* combination is precisely the 4 Maccabees *hilastērion* + *antipsychos* theology applied to Jesus: not merely the Maccabean martyrs whose deaths covered Israel's sins but the one whose death covers the *sin of the world*.

**Note on *amnos* vs. *arnion***: John 1:29, 36 uses *amnos* = lamb (sacrificial). Revelation uses *arnion* = little lamb (the triumphant Lamb on the throne). The two forms carry the same person but different emphases: *amnos* = the sacrificial victim / *arnion* = the exalted victor. Both are vocabulary-locked in the GLE.

**Isaiah 53:7** — the primary LXX source for the *amnos* identification.

**1 Peter 1:19** — "the precious blood of Christ like that of a lamb (*amnos*) without blemish or spot." Peter's *amnos* citation confirms the Passover-lamb / Isaiah 53 convergence that the Baptist's declaration initiates.

**Revelation 5:6** — "And between the throne and the four living creatures and among the elders I saw a Lamb (*arnion*) standing as though it had been slain." The *arnion* of Revelation 5 is the exalted form of the *amnos* of John 1: the sacrificial Lamb who was slain now stands victorious at the throne. The Baptist's declaration in John 1:29 and the heavenly vision of Revelation 5:6 are the two termini of the Lamb-theology arc.

### John 2:19–21 — *Destroy this sanctuary / in three days I will raise it up* — CAE PRIMARY NODE

**2:19–21** — "Jesus answered them: Destroy this sanctuary (*naon*) and in three days I will raise it up. The Jews then said: It has taken forty-six years to build this sanctuary and will you raise it up in three days? But he was speaking about the sanctuary (*naō*) of his body."

The *naos* = sanctuary declaration is the GLE's vocabulary-lock for the Temple-theology at its NT fulfillment. The evangelist's interpretive gloss (*he was speaking about the naos of his body*) is the explicit statement of what the OT-deuterocanonical sequence prepares:

- **2 Maccabees 5:19** — *God did not choose the nation for the sake of the holy place but the place for the sake of the nation* — the Temple is instrumental / the people are primary.
- **Tobit 14:5** — the second Temple will be built *not like the first until the time is fulfilled* — the provisional Temple awaiting the final one.
- **Ezekiel 40–48** — the visionary Temple whose *glory returns* from the east.
- **Haggai 2:9** — *the latter glory of this house shall be greater than the former*.

All of these point to a Temple supersession that Jesus enacts in John 2:19–21: the stone sanctuary is provisional / the *naos* that matters is his body / destroyed at the cross / raised on the third day. The resurrection is the ultimate Temple-dedication — the *enkainismos* that the Hanukkah feast pointed to (John 10:22).

**Acts 7:48–50** — Stephen's citation of Isaiah 66:1 — "The Most High does not dwell in houses made by hands" — is the NT homiletical equivalent of John 2:21: the *naos* of Jesus's body supersedes every *naos* made of stone.

**Revelation 21:22** — "And I saw no temple in the city for its temple is the Lord God the Almighty and the Lamb." The ultimate fulfillment of John 2:21: the New Jerusalem has no stone *naos* because the Lord and the Lamb are themselves the Temple. The *naos* of his body raised at the resurrection is the seed of the eschatological Temple-reality of Revelation 21.

### John 3:3–8 — born from above / *pneuma* blows where it wills — CAE PRIMARY NODE

**3:5–8** — "Unless one is born of water and breath-of-life (*pneumatos*) he cannot enter the kingdom of God. What is born of the flesh (*sarkos*) is flesh — and what is born of the breath-of-life (*pneumatos*) is breath-of-life. Do not marvel that I said to you: You must be born from above (*anōthen*). The breath-of-life (*pneuma*) blows where it wishes and you hear its sound but you do not know where it comes from or where it goes."

The *pneuma* theology of John 3 is the NT enactment of the CAE v2 *pneuma* = breath-of-life principle. Three declarations:

**1. Birth of *pneuma***: The new creation is *pneumatic* — initiated by the divine *pneuma* that blows like wind. This is the Genesis 1:2 *pneuma* hovering over the waters enacted in individual human renewal: the same *pneuma* that brooded over the chaos of the first creation broods over the new creation of each person born from above.

**2. The *sarx* / *pneuma* antithesis**: What is born of *sarx* = flesh is *sarx* — and what is born of *pneuma* is *pneuma*. This is not Greek soul-body dualism but covenant-anthropology: *sarx* = the unaided human nature / *pneuma* = the divine breath-life that alone can produce the life of the kingdom. Paul's *sarx* / *pneuma* antithesis in Romans 8 is the sustained theological development of this Johannine principle.

**3. The *pneuma* blows where it wills**: The sovereignty of the *pneuma* — like wind (*anemos*) whose origin and destination are unknown — cannot be controlled or predicted by human religious systems. The *pneuma* of John 3:8 is the same sovereign *pneuma* that *rushed upon* (Judges) / *remained on* (John 1:33) / *breathed* (John 20:22) — always the divine initiative, never human manipulation.

**Ezekiel 37:1–14** — the valley of dry bones: *pneuma* commanded to enter the dead and they lived. The Ezekiel *pneuma*-restoration is the OT type for the John 3 *pneuma*-birth: both describe the divine *pneuma* as the sole source of life where death prevails.

**Romans 8:5–9** — "For those who live according to the flesh (*sarx*) set their minds on the things of the flesh — but those who live according to the breath-of-life (*pneuma*) set their minds on the things of the breath-of-life... You however are not in the flesh (*sarx*) but in the breath-of-life (*pneuma*) if in fact the breath-of-life of God dwells in you." Paul's sustained *sarx* / *pneuma* argument is the theological development of John 3:6.

### John 8:58 / 18:5–6 — *prin Abraam genesthai egō eimi* — CAE CRITICAL NODE

**8:58** — "Jesus said to them: Truly truly I say to you — before Abraham was I AM (*prin Abraam genesthai egō eimi*)."

**18:5–6** — "They answered him: Jesus of Nazareth. Jesus said to them: I AM (*egō eimi*). Judas who betrayed him was standing with them. When Jesus said to them: I AM — they drew back and fell to the ground."

***egō eimi*** — I AM.

The absolute *egō eimi* declarations are the Johannine divine-name disclosure. The background:

**LXX Exodus 3:14**: *egō eimi ho ōn* = I AM THE ONE WHO IS — God's self-identification to Moses at the burning bush. The *egō eimi* is the LXX's rendering of the divine name *ehyeh asher ehyeh*.

**LXX Isaiah 43:10**: *hina gnōte kai pisteusēte kai synēte hoti egō eimi* = that you may know and believe and understand that I AM. The *egō eimi* declarations of Deutero-Isaiah — where YHWH contrasts himself with the idols — use the absolute *egō eimi* as the divine self-identification formula. In Isaiah 43:10, 25 / 45:18 / 48:12 / 51:12 / 52:6 the *egō eimi* is the covenant-God's identity-claim.

Jesus's use of the absolute *egō eimi* in John is the deliberate deployment of both traditions: I AM = YHWH's self-identification = the one who was / who is / who is to come. The pre-Abraham existence (*prin Abraam genesthai egō eimi*) places Jesus before all of human history — in the position that Proverbs 8:22–25 / John 1:1–2 describe as the Wisdom/Logos who was with God from the beginning.

The **soldiers falling to the ground** (18:6) at the *egō eimi* declaration is the theophanic response: the same prostration that occurred before divine manifestations throughout the LXX (Genesis 15:12 / Ezekiel 1:28 / Daniel 10:9) occurs here at the sound of the divine name.

**Isaiah 43:10–13** — the *egō eimi* source in Deutero-Isaiah.
**Revelation 1:17** — "When I saw him I fell at his feet as though dead. And he laid his right hand on me saying: Fear not — I am the first and the last." The Risen Christ's self-identification as *the first and the last* is the Revelation equivalent of the Johannine *egō eimi*: both deploy the Isaiah divine-name formula.

---

### John 11:25 — *egō eimi hē anastasis kai hē zōē* — CAE CRITICAL NODE

**11:25** — "Jesus said to her: I AM the resurrection and the life (*Egō eimi hē anastasis kai hē zōē*). Whoever believes in me — though he die — yet shall he live. And everyone who lives and believes in me shall never die. Do you believe this?"

This is the climactic *egō eimi* declaration — the fifth of seven. Its theological weight:

Martha declares her resurrection-faith: *he will rise again in the resurrection on the last day* (11:24). Her theology is exactly the theology of 2 Maccabees 7 and 4 Maccabees 7:17–19: the resurrection at the last day / the *anastasōs aiōniou* = everlasting life-renewal. The Maccabean martyr-theology has been correctly received.

Jesus's response does not correct her theology but transforms it: *I AM the resurrection and the life* — the resurrection is not merely a future event at the last day but a Person present now. The *anastasis* = resurrection is not an eschatological event Martha waits for but the *egō eimi* who stands before her.

This transforms the entire Maccabean resurrection-theology: the brothers declared *the King of the universe will raise us up* (2 Maccabees 7:9) — Jesus declares *I AM the one who raises*. The resurrection is not a power the Father exercises independently — the resurrection is the Son himself. The *anastasis* and the *zōē* are not gifts Jesus gives but what Jesus is.

**2 Maccabees 7:9** — *The King of the universe will raise us up* — Jesus is that King.
**4 Maccabees 7:19** — *they live to God as Abraham and Isaac and Jacob live to God* — Jesus is the *egō eimi* who guarantees that living.
**Luke 20:38** — *for all live to him* — Jesus's own argument with the Sadducees echoes the 4 Maccabees *zōsin tō theō* formula.
**Romans 6:10** — *the life he lives he lives to God* — Paul's *zēn tō theō* is the Christological ground of the 4 Maccabees formula.

### John 14:6 — *egō eimi hē hodos kai hē alētheia kai hē zōē* — CAE CRITICAL NODE

**14:6** — "Jesus said to him: I AM the way and the truth and the life (*Egō eimi hē hodos kai hē alētheia kai hē zōē*). No one comes to the Father except through me."

The sixth *egō eimi* declaration — the most comprehensive. Three identity-claims:

***hodos*** = the way: The *hodos* = road/way is the covenant-path vocabulary throughout the LXX — the *way* of the Lord in which Israel was commanded to walk (Deuteronomy 5:33 / Psalms 1:6 / Isaiah 40:3). The *hodos* is also the early church's self-description (*the Way* / Acts 9:2 / 19:9). Jesus as *hē hodos* = the Way declares himself to be the covenant-path itself — not a guide to the path but the path.

***alētheia*** = truth: The *alētheia* spine runs through the entire GLE from 1 Esdras 4:41 (*megalē hē alētheia kai hyperischyei* = great is truth and it prevails) through Tobit 3:2 (*all your ways are mercy and truth*) through John's prologue (*full of grace and truth*) through Pilate's question (*what is truth?* / 18:38). Jesus as *hē alētheia* = the Truth declares himself to be the ontological ground of reality — not a true teacher but Truth itself. The *alētheia* that 1 Esdras 4:41 praised as the greatest of all things is a Person.

***zōē*** = life: The *zōē* that runs through the Prologue (*in him was life*) through every *zōē aiōnios* declaration reaches its summit: Jesus is not only the giver of *zōē* but *hē zōē* = Life itself. The source of life is not separable from the person of the Son.

The triple identity-claim replaces the Temple's triple function: the Temple was the *hodos* to God's presence / the place where divine *alētheia* was declared / the source of covenant *zōē* for the people. Jesus as *hodos / alētheia / zōē* is the Temple superseded — himself the access / the revelation / the life.

### John 14:16–17 / 15:26 / 16:13 — the *Paraklētos* / *pneuma tēs alētheias* — CAE PRIMARY NODE

**14:16–17** — "And I will ask the Father and he will give you another Advocate (*Paraklēton*) — to be with you forever — even the breath-of-life of truth (*to pneuma tēs alētheias*)."

**15:26** — "But when the Advocate (*Paraklētos*) comes — whom I will send to you from the Father — the breath-of-life of truth (*to pneuma tēs alētheias*) who proceeds from the Father (*ho para tou Patros ekporeuetai*) — he will testify about me."

**16:13** — "When the breath-of-life of truth (*to pneuma tēs alētheias*) comes he will guide you into all the truth."

***to pneuma tēs alētheias*** = the breath-of-life of truth.

The Farewell Discourse's *Paraklētos* = Advocate / Comforter theology is the NT's fullest statement of the *pneuma*'s personal identity and mission. Three aspects of the CAE v2 relevance:

**1. The *Paraklētos* as another Paraclete** (14:16): *allon Paraklēton* = another Advocate — implying that Jesus himself is the first Advocate (cf. 1 John 2:1 *paraklēton echomen pros ton patera*). The *Paraklētos* is a Person distinct from the Son but of the same category.

**2. The *pneuma tēs alētheias*** (14:17 / 15:26 / 16:13): The *Paraklētos* is identified as *the breath-of-life of truth* — the *pneuma* whose defining characteristic is *alētheia*. This connects to the entire *alētheia* spine of the GLE: the *megalē hē alētheia* of 1 Esdras 4:41 / the *alētheia* of the Prologue / Jesus as *hē alētheia* — the *pneuma* that guides into all truth is the one who bears the character of the one who *is* the truth.

**3. The procession formula** (15:26): *ho para tou Patros ekporeuetai* = who proceeds from the Father — this is the pneumatological procession statement that became central in later Trinitarian theology. The *pneuma* proceeds from the Father / is sent by the Son / bears the character of the truth that the Son embodies.

The GLE's CAE v2 Trinitarian model: *Ruach* (breath/spirit/wind) = *Pneuma* / *Chokhmah* (wisdom as eternal semantic content) = *Logos* / *Davar* (word as expressive act) = the Son's speech-act in creation and redemption — the Farewell Discourse's *Paraklētos* theology is the NT's most developed statement of this model.

### John 17:3 — the definition of everlasting life — CAE CRITICAL NODE

**17:3** — "And this is everlasting life (*aiōnios zōē*) — that they know you the only true God and Jesus the Anointed (*Iēsoun Christon*) whom you have sent."

***hautē de estin hē aiōnios zōē hina ginōskōsin se ton monon alēthinon theon kai hon apesteilas Iēsoun Christon***

= and this is the everlasting life — that they know you the only true God and Jesus the Anointed whom you sent.

This is the only explicit NT definition of *zōē aiōnios* = everlasting life. The definition is relational-cognitive (*ginōskōsin* = know in the covenant-knowledge sense — not merely intellectual knowledge but the *yada'* = experiential / covenantal knowing of the OT).

The definition has two objects:
1. *Ton monon alēthinon theon* = the only true God — the monotheism affirmation. The *monon alēthinon* = only true contrasts with all false gods — the anti-idolatry declaration that runs from the Shema through Daniel through the Maccabean martyrs through the Revelation *theos zōn* = living God.
2. *Iēsoun Christon hon apesteilas* = Jesus the Anointed whom you sent — the Christological identification. Knowing the only true God and knowing Jesus the Anointed are not two separate things but one knowing: the only true God is known through and in the one he sent.

The *zōē aiōnios* of the Maccabean martyrs (*2 Maccabees 7:9 / 4 Maccabees 18:23*) is now defined by Jesus himself: it is not merely an extended life-span or a post-mortem state but a quality of *knowing* — relationship with the Father through the Son. The *anastasōs aiōniou* that the brothers declared as a future hope is the present reality of those who *know* the only true God and Jesus the Anointed.

## STRUCTURAL NOTE — JOHN AS THE LOGOS-GOSPEL

John is the Cepher Hakam project's theological capstone for the NT, precisely because it is the most deliberately LXX-shaped of the four Gospels and the one in which the OT-deuterocanonical theological threads arrive at their explicit fulfillment.

**The seven structures of fulfillment in John:**

1. **The Prologue** fulfills the Wisdom/Davar tradition (Proverbs 8 / Wisdom 7 / Sirach 24 / Genesis 1)
2. **The *egō eimi* declarations** fulfill the divine-name pattern (Exodus 3:14 / Isaiah 43:10 LXX)
3. **The Lamb declaration** (1:29, 36) fulfills the Isaiah 53 Servant and the Passover typology
4. **The *naos* declaration** (2:19–21) fulfills the Temple-supersession trajectory (2 Maccabees 5:19 / Haggai 2 / Ezekiel 40–48)
5. **The *pneuma* teaching** (3:5–8 / 14:16–17 / 20:22) fulfills the Ezekiel 37 / Joel 2 / Zechariah 12 pneuma-outpouring
6. **The resurrection declaration** (11:25) fulfills the Maccabean resurrection-theology (2 Maccabees 7 / 4 Maccabees 7:17–19)
7. **The Hanukkah / Temple declaration** (10:22–30) fulfills the 1 Maccabees Dedication (4:59) — Jesus at Hanukkah declares *I and the Father are one*

The *alētheia* spine connecting 1 Esdras 4:41 (*megalē hē alētheia kai hyperischyei*) → John 14:6 (*egō eimi hē hodos kai hē alētheia kai hē zōē*) → John 18:38 (Pilate's *what is truth?*) is the most important single verbal thread in the GLE project: the great truth that overcomes all things (1 Esdras) is a Person (John 14:6) who stands before the Roman governor who does not recognize him (John 18:38). The *alētheia* wins not by human power but by the cross and resurrection — exactly as the *megalē hē alētheia* of 1 Esdras overcomes by the decree of the Persian king.
