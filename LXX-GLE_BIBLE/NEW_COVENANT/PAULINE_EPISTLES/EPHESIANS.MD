**EPHESIANS — GLE v2.2 CAE NOTES**

---

**TEXT BASE:** RP2026 Byzantine Majority Text

**VERSE COUNT:** 155 verses / 6 chapters

---

**EPHESIANS IN THE CORPUS ARCHITECTURE**

Ephesians is the most cosmologically elevated Pauline letter — the view from the heavenly places (*en tois epouraniois*) looking down at the entire sweep of redemptive history from before creation to the final age. Where Galatians is polemical and compressed, Ephesians is doxological and expansive. Where Romans builds the forensic argument, Ephesians builds the cosmic architecture. The letter's CAE function is threefold: it contains the densest concentration of vocabulary-lock terms in sustained theological prose outside Romans 8 — *apolytrōsis*, *huiothesia*, *klēronomia*, *pneuma*, *agapē*, *sophia*, *ekklēsia*, *diathēkē*, *alētheia*, and *dikaiosynē* all appearing in sustained argument — it provides the fullest ecclesiological development of the *naos* chain, and it anchors the armor-of-God sequence as the final Wisdom/Folly confrontation frame.

The *en tois epouraniois* phrase — "in the heavenly places" — occurs five times (1:3 / 1:20 / 2:6 / 3:10 / 6:12) and is unique to Ephesians in the NT. It is the spatial marker for a letter written from the perspective of accomplished redemption — the called-out assembly is already seated with the Anointed in the heavenly places (2:6) even while still engaging the spiritual forces of evil in those same heavenly places (6:12). The tension between accomplished position and ongoing combat is Ephesians' structural dynamic.

---

**KEY LEXICAL DECISIONS**

*apolytrōsis* — "ransom-release": 1:7 (ransom-release through his blood), 1:14 (Holy Breath-of-life as guarantee until the ransom-release of his possession), 4:30 (sealed for the day of ransom-release). Three occurrences — highest density outside Romans. The vocabulary lock holds throughout. *1:7 CAE NOTE:* *apolytrōsin dia tou haimatos autou* — "ransom-release through his blood." The *apolytrōsis* is specifically blood-mediated — not a general liberation but a ransom paid in blood. The connection to *hilastērion* (Romans 3:25) is direct: the blood that accomplishes ransom-release is the same blood that constitutes the atoning cover. CAE composite anchor: **Leviticus 25:47–55 LXX (*lytrōsis*) → Isaiah 53:10–12 LXX → Ephesians 1:7 → Romans 3:24–25 → Hebrews 9:12** — Chain 1 (*hilastērion*) receives its Ephesian ransom-release node.

*1:14 CAE NOTE:* The Holy Breath-of-life is described as *arrabōn* — guarantee / down-payment — of the inheritance until the *apolytrōsis tēs peripoiēseōs* — "ransom-release of his possession." The eschatological structure: the Breath-of-life is the present guarantee of a future full ransom-release. The possession (*peripoiēsis*) is the covenant community — echoing LXX Exodus 19:5 (*laos periousios* — treasured possession) and Malachi 3:17 LXX. CAE anchor: **Exodus 19:5 LXX → Malachi 3:17 LXX → Ephesians 1:14** — the *peripoiēsis* vocabulary connecting the Sinai covenant-people to the new covenant assembly.

*huiothesia* — "son-placement": 1:5 — *proorisas hēmas eis huiothesian dia Iēsou Christou* — "predestined us for son-placement through Jesus the Anointed." The son-placement is here grounded in predestination before the foundation of the world — it is not a response to human decision but the eternal purpose of the Father expressed in covenant-love (*agapē*). **Chain 4 (*huiothesia*) node:** The Ephesian occurrence adds the pre-temporal, elective dimension to the chain — the son-placement accomplished in Galatians 4:4–5 and applied by the Breath-of-life in Romans 8:15 is here traced to its eternal source in the Father's predestining will. Chain 4 fully confirmed: **Exodus 4:22 LXX → Psalm 2:7 LXX → Galatians 4:4–7 → Romans 8:15 → Ephesians 1:5 → Romans 8:23**.

*klēronomia / klēronomos* — "inheritance / heir": 1:11 (*eklērōthēmen* — obtained an inheritance), 1:14 (Holy Breath-of-life as guarantee of inheritance), 1:18 (riches of his glorious inheritance among the holy ones), 3:6 (Gentiles are *sygklēronomia* — fellow heirs), 5:5 (no inheritance in the kingdom for the immoral). The *sygklēronomia* at 3:6 is the cosmological capstone of the inheritance chain — the Gentiles are not secondary participants in Israel's inheritance but co-heirs of the same body and co-partakers of the promise. The mystery hidden for ages (3:5–6) is precisely this co-inheritance. CAE anchor: **Numbers 18:20 LXX / Psalm 16:5 LXX (Lord as inheritance) → Galatians 3:29 → Ephesians 3:6 → Hebrews 9:15** — the inheritance chain extended to cosmic co-heirship.

*pneuma* — "breath-of-life" throughout. Ephesians has the most architecturally structured *pneuma* usage in the corpus — organized around four distinct functions: the sealed guarantee (1:13–14), the *pneuma* of wisdom and revelation (1:17), the unifying breath-of-life (4:3–4), and the sword of the breath-of-life (6:17). Key instances beyond those already noted: 2:2 (ruler of the authority of the air — the *pneuma* at work in sons of disobedience), 2:18 (access in one *pneuma* to the Father), 2:22 (built together into a dwelling place of God by the *pneuma*), 3:5 (mystery revealed by the *pneuma*), 3:16 (strengthened with power through his *pneuma* in the inner being), 4:3 (unity of the *pneuma* in the bond of peace), 4:4 (one body and one *pneuma*), 4:23 (renewed in the *pneuma* of your minds), 4:30 (do not grieve the Holy Breath-of-life), 5:18 (be filled with the *pneuma*), 6:12 (*pneumatika* of evil in heavenly places), 6:17/18 (sword of the *pneuma* / praying in the *pneuma*).

*2:2 CAE NOTE:* *ton archonta tēs exousias tou aeros — to pneuma to nyn energoun en tois huiois tēs apeitheias* — "the ruler of the authority of the air — the breath-of-life that is now at work in the sons of disobedience." This is the only NT text that explicitly identifies Satan's domain as a *pneuma* — a counter-breath-of-life animating the sons of disobedience. CAE Wisdom/Folly axis: there are two pneumatic orientations — the Holy Breath-of-life filling the called-out assembly (5:18) and the counter-breath-of-life energizing disobedience (2:2). The 6:12 *pneumatika tēs ponērias* — "spiritual forces of evil" — extends this into the cosmic combat frame. The armor of God (6:14–17) is the response to a *pneumatic* threat, not merely a moral one.

*4:30 CAE NOTE:* *mē lypeite to pneuma to hagion tou theou en hō esphragisthēte eis hēmeran apolytrōseōs* — "do not grieve the Holy Breath-of-life of God by whom you were sealed for the day of ransom-release." The grieving of the Holy Breath-of-life is the Ephesian counterpart to Mark's blasphemy-against-the-Holy-Breath-of-life — not the same offense in severity but the same category of pneumatic violation. The seal (*sphragis*) established at 1:13 is the target of the grievoation — behaviors that contradict the Breath-of-life's character (bitterness, wrath, slander — 4:31) assault the seal itself. The *apolytrōsis* orientation of the seal connects 4:30 back to 1:7 and 1:14 — the ransom-release that was accomplished in blood (1:7) and guaranteed by the Breath-of-life (1:14) is the eschatological horizon toward which the sealed community lives.

*agapē* — "covenant-love": 1:4 (chosen in him in *agapē*), 1:15 (*agapēn* toward all the holy ones), 2:4 (God rich in *eleos* / great *agapēn* with which he loved us), 3:17 (rooted and grounded in *agapē*), 3:18/19 (breadth and length and height and depth / the *agapē* of the Anointed that surpasses knowledge), 4:2 (bearing with one another in *agapē*), 4:15 (speaking truth in *agapē*), 4:16 (builds itself up in *agapē*), 5:2 (walk in *agapē* as the Anointed loved us), 5:25 (*agapate* your wives as the Anointed loved the called-out assembly), 6:23/24 (*agapē* with faith / love incorruptible). Ephesians has the highest sustained *agapē* density in the Pauline corpus — it is not merely a command but the cosmic medium in which the called-out assembly exists and grows.

*3:18–19 CAE NOTE:* "the breadth and length and height and depth — and to know the *agapē* of the Anointed that surpasses knowledge." The four-dimensional spatial metaphor for the *agapē* of the Anointed is the Ephesian counterpart to Romans 8:38–39 (neither height nor depth shall separate us from the *agapē* of God). In Romans 8 the *agapē* is a force that cannot be severed; in Ephesians 3 the *agapē* is a space that cannot be fully measured. Both passages characterize covenant-love as exceeding cognitive categories — it is known experientially through being filled with the fullness of God (3:19) rather than through propositional comprehension.

*sophia* — "wisdom": 1:8 (lavished in all *sophia* and insight), 1:17 (*pneuma sophias* — breath-of-life of wisdom and revelation), 3:10 (manifold *sophia* of God made known through the called-out assembly), 5:15 (walk not as unwise but as *sophoi* — wise). The *sophia* vocabulary lock is concentrated in Ephesians more than any other Pauline letter except Colossians and 1 Corinthians. The manifold *sophia* of God (3:10) being made known *through the called-out assembly* to rulers and authorities in heavenly places is one of the most astonishing statements in the NT — the assembly is not merely the recipient of wisdom but its instrument of cosmic disclosure.

*naos* — "sanctuary": 2:21 — *en hō pasa oikodomē synarmologoumenē auxei eis naon hagion en kyriō* — "in whom the whole structure being joined together grows into a holy sanctuary in the Lord." And 2:22: "you also are being built together into a dwelling place of God by the breath-of-life." **PRIMARY CHAIN NODE — Chain 9 (*skēnē / naos*):**

**Exodus 25:8 LXX → Leviticus 26:12 LXX → Ezekiel 37:27 LXX → John 1:14 (*eskēnōsen*) → Mark 14:58 (*acheiropoiēton*) → Acts 7:44 (*skēnē tou martyriou*) → Ephesians 2:21–22 (*naos hagios / katoikētērion tou theou*) → Hebrews 8:2 / 9:11 → Revelation 21:3/22**

The Ephesian node is the most ecclesiologically developed *naos* statement in the chain. The dwelling place of God is not a building, a celestial tabernacle, or even the body of Christ individually — it is the whole structure of the called-out assembly being joined together and growing. The *pneuma* is the builder — "built together into a dwelling place of God by the breath-of-life." The *acheiropoiēton* of Mark 14:58 is here constructed — not made by hands but by the Breath-of-life joining living stones. CAE three-witness lock: John 1:14 (*eskēnōsen* — the Word tabernacled) / Ephesians 2:21–22 (the called-out assembly as *naos*) / Hebrews 9:11 (the true tabernacle not made by hands) — three independent witnesses to the *naos* theme.

*diathēkē* — "covenant": 2:12 — *xenoi tōn diathēkōn tēs epangelias* — "strangers to the covenants of promise." The plural *diathēkōn* is notable — not one covenant but multiple covenants of promise (Abrahamic / Mosaic / Davidic) from which the Gentiles were excluded. The Gentile condition before the Anointed: separated from the Anointed / alienated from the commonwealth of Israel / strangers to the covenants of promise / without hope / without God. The five-fold exclusion is reversed by the blood of the Anointed (2:13) — brought near, made one, reconciled, given access in one breath-of-life to the Father. CAE anchor: **Genesis 12:3 / 17:7 LXX → Ephesians 2:12–13** — the covenants of promise now opened to Gentile co-inheritance.

*alētheia* — "truth": 1:13 (word of truth / glad tidings of salvation), 4:15 (speaking truth in covenant-love), 4:21 (truth is in Jesus), 4:24 (new self created in true righteousness and holiness), 4:25 (speak truth with your neighbor), 5:9 (fruit of light in righteousness and *alētheias*), 6:14 (belt of *alētheias*). The armor-of-God sequence begins with *alētheia* as the belt — the foundational piece that holds everything else in place. CAE Wisdom/Folly axis: truth is the structural foundation of the Wisdom-register life; the Folly register is characterized by *pseudos* (falsehood — 4:25) and *apate* (deceit — 4:22 / 5:6). The *alētheia* belt is not merely intellectual honesty but covenantal orientation — the whole self girded by the reality of what God has accomplished in the Anointed.

*dikaiosynē* — "righteousness": 4:24 (new self created in true *dikaiosynē* and holiness), 5:9 (fruit of light in all that is good and right), 6:1 (obey parents — this is *dikaion*), 6:14 (breastplate of *dikaiosynēs*). The breastplate of righteousness in the armor sequence — following the belt of truth — places *dikaiosynē* as the chest-covering. In Isaiah 59:17 LXX the Lord himself puts on righteousness as a breastplate (*endysato dikaiosynēn hōs thōraka*) — the armor of God in Ephesians 6 is the Lord's own armor transferred to the called-out assembly. **LXX-primacy argument: the armor-of-God sequence in Ephesians 6:13–17 is a sustained adaptation of Isaiah 59:17 LXX and Isaiah 11:5 LXX.** CAE anchor: **Isaiah 11:5 LXX (righteousness as belt) + Isaiah 59:17 LXX (righteousness as breastplate / helmet of salvation) → Ephesians 6:14–17**.

*ekklēsia* — "called-out assembly": 1:22 (head over all things to the called-out assembly), 2:19 (members of the household of God), 3:10 (manifold wisdom made known through the called-out assembly), 3:21 (glory in the called-out assembly and in the Anointed Jesus), 5:23/24/25/27/29/32 (the Anointed as head of the called-out assembly / the marriage mystery). The marriage-mystery passage (5:22–32) is the ecclesiological climax of Ephesians — the called-out assembly as the bride of the Anointed, the marriage of Genesis 2:24 (*sarx mia* — one flesh) applied to the Anointed-and-assembly union. The *mysterion* of 5:32 — "this mystery is profound and I am saying that it refers to the Anointed and the called-out assembly" — retroactively makes Genesis 2:24 a *proeuangelisato* moment for the called-out assembly, parallel to Galatians 3:8's characterization of Genesis 12:3 as proto-*euangelion*. **LXX-primacy argument: the *sarx mia* citation at 5:31 follows LXX Genesis 2:24** — the one-flesh union is the LXX's *kai esontai hoi dyo eis sarka mian* applied to the Anointed-assembly mystery.

**STRUCTURAL CAE NODES**

**1:3–14 — The Berakah / Eulogy block:**
The opening *eulogētos* — "blessed be God" — launches one of the most structurally complex theological statements in the NT. It is a single Greek sentence in the original (1:3–14) — a sustained berakah (*eulogia* — blessing) modeled on Jewish liturgical blessing forms. The CAE structure of the berakah: election before creation (1:4) → predestination to son-placement in covenant-love (1:5) → ransom-release through blood / forgiveness (1:7) → mystery revealed / summing up all things in the Anointed (1:9–10) → inheritance obtained (1:11) → sealed with the Holy Breath-of-life as guarantee of inheritance until full ransom-release (1:13–14). The entire sweep of redemption — from eternal election to eschatological completion — is compressed into twelve verses of sustained doxology. CAE Wisdom-register: this is the most concentrated positive Wisdom-register statement in the Pauline corpus — every vocabulary-lock term appearing in its fullest redemptive-historical context.

**1:17 — pneuma sophias kai apokalypseos:**
*pneuma sophias kai apokalypseos en epignōsei autou* — "a breath-of-life of wisdom and of revelation in the knowledge of him." The *pneuma* here is the Breath-of-life functioning as the organ of *sophia* and *apokalypsis* — wisdom and revelation. The eyes of the heart (*kardias*) are enlightened by the *pneuma* to perceive three things: the hope of the calling, the riches of the inheritance, and the immeasurable greatness of God's power. CAE Wisdom-register: *sophia* received through the *pneuma* into the *kardia* — the full Wisdom-chain operative at the level of personal knowledge. Cross-link: **Proverbs 2:6 LXX (Lord gives wisdom / from his face come knowledge and understanding) → Ephesians 1:17** — the *pneuma sophias* as the NT form of the LXX *sophia* gift.

**2:4–6 — Dead / Made alive / Raised / Seated:**
"Even when we were dead in trespasses — made us alive together with the Anointed (*synezōopoiēsen*) — raised us up together (*synēgeiren*) — seated us together (*synekathisen*) in the heavenly places in the Anointed Jesus." The three *syn-* compounds (co-vivified / co-raised / co-seated) are Ephesians' most compressed atonement-and-application statement. The death/life binary of 2:1–6 is the Ephesian form of Romans 6:4–5 (buried with him / raised to walk in newness of life) — but Ephesians extends it to co-seating in the heavenly places. The position is accomplished and present, not merely future. CAE chain: **Ezekiel 37:1–14 LXX (dead bones made alive / breath-of-life entering) → Romans 6:4–5 → Ephesians 2:4–6** — the Ezekiel valley-of-bones resurrection as the OT type for the co-vivification of the assembly.

**2:14–18 — The dividing wall / One new man / Access in one pneuma:**
"He himself is our peace — who has made us both one and has broken down in his flesh the dividing wall of hostility — by abolishing the Law of commandments expressed in ordinances — that he might create in himself one new man in place of the two." The *to mesotoichon tou phragmou* — "dividing wall" — is widely understood as a reference to the Temple barrier (*soreg*) that excluded Gentiles from the inner courts under pain of death. The Anointed's flesh-and-cross abolishes this barrier — the *naos* chain's Temple-replacement theology is here stated in terms of human-reconciliation. The result: "through him we both have access in one breath-of-life (*pneumati*) to the Father." The one *pneuma* is the single access-point for Jew and Gentile alike — the trinitarianism is structural: access to the Father through the Son in the one Breath-of-life. **CAE trinitarian governance node: Ephesians 2:18** — the fullest NT statement of Trinitarian access-structure.

**2:19–22 — The ekklēsia as naos / katoikētērion:**
"Built on the foundation of the apostles and prophets — the Anointed Jesus himself being the cornerstone — in whom the whole structure being joined together grows into a holy sanctuary (*naon hagion*) in the Lord — in him you also are being built together into a dwelling place (*katoikētērion*) of God by the breath-of-life." Two terms for the assembly-as-dwelling-place: *naos hagios* (holy sanctuary) and *katoikētērion* (dwelling place). The *katoikētērion* vocabulary connects to LXX Exodus 15:17 (*topon eis katoikian*) — "a place for dwelling" — the goal of the Exodus as a prepared dwelling for God. **LXX-primacy argument: Ephesians 2:22's *katoikētērion* echoes LXX Exodus 15:17 and 1 Kings 8:13 LXX (*oikon katoikētēriou sou*) — Solomon's Temple dedication prayer applied to the called-out assembly.** Chain 9 primary node confirmed.

**3:10 — The ekklēsia as instrument of cosmic wisdom-disclosure:**
*hina gnōristhē nyn tais archais kai tais exousiais en tois epouraniois dia tēs ekklēsias hē polypoikilos sophia tou theou* — "so that through the called-out assembly the manifold wisdom of God might now be made known to the rulers and authorities in the heavenly places." The called-out assembly is not merely the recipient of redemption but the *instrument* of cosmic revelation — the *sophia* of God is disclosed to principalities and powers through the existence of the multi-ethnic, Jew-and-Gentile community. The mystery hidden for ages (3:9) — that Gentiles are co-heirs in one body — is itself the content of the wisdom being displayed. CAE structural note: the *sophia* disclosure through the called-out assembly is the ecclesiological form of the *naos* theology — the assembly as the place where God's presence and wisdom are made visible to the cosmos.

**4:1–6 — The seven unities:**
One body / one breath-of-life / one hope / one Lord / one faith / one baptism / one God and Father of all. The sevenfold unity statement is the pneumatological constitution of the called-out assembly. The unity is grounded in *pneuma* (4:3–4) and expressed through *agapē* (4:2). CAE structural note: the one *pneuma* is the unifying principle — the diversity of gifts (4:7–11) serves the unity of the one *pneuma*, not the reverse. The *poimenas kai didaskalous* — shepherds and teachers — at 4:11 receives the *poimēn* vocabulary lock in the context of the assembly's pneumatic architecture.

**4:22–24 — Old self / New self / Renewed in pneuma of minds:**
"Put off your old self (*palaion anthrōpon*) which belongs to your former manner of life and is corrupt through deceitful desires — and be renewed in the breath-of-life (*pneumati*) of your minds — and put on the new self (*kainon anthrōpon*) created after the likeness of God in true righteousness and holiness." The *ananeousthai tō pneumati tou noos* — "be renewed in the breath-of-life of your minds" — is the anthropological process corresponding to the positional statement of 2:4–6. The co-vivification (2:5) is applied through ongoing renewal of the mind-breath-of-life. CAE Wisdom/Folly axis: the old self is characterized by *apatē* (deceit) and *epithymia* (desire) — Folly-register markers. The new self is characterized by *alētheia* and *dikaiosynē* — Wisdom-register markers. The renewal is *pneumatic* — it happens in the *pneuma* of the mind, not by moral effort.

**5:18 — Be filled with the pneuma:**
*plērousthe en pneumati* — "be filled in the breath-of-life." The contrast with wine-drunkenness (*mē methyskesthe oinō*) is structural: drunkenness produces *asōtia* (debauchery / dissolution); *pneuma*-filling produces psalms and hymns and spiritual songs and thanksgiving and submission. The filling is not a one-time event but an ongoing state (present passive imperative). CAE pneumatological note: Ephesians 5:18 is the general-assembly form of Acts 2:4 (they were all filled with the Holy Breath-of-life) — Pentecost as paradigm for ongoing assembly life. The *plērousthe* connects to 3:19 (*plērōthēte eis pan to plērōma tou theou* — filled with all the fullness of God) — the *pneuma*-filling is the mode of the fullness-of-God indwelling.

**5:31–32 — Genesis 2:24 / sarx mia / the mystery:**
The citation of Genesis 2:24 LXX (*kai esontai hoi dyo eis sarka mian* — "and the two shall become one flesh") followed by "this mystery is profound — and I am saying that it refers to the Anointed and the called-out assembly" is the hermeneutical climax of Ephesians' ecclesiology. The one-flesh marriage of Genesis 2 was always pointing beyond itself to the Anointed-and-assembly union. **LXX-primacy argument: the *sarx mia* of Genesis 2:24 LXX is the textual basis for the mystery — the Greek formulation of the union makes the application to the called-out assembly as the Anointed's body (*sarx* — 5:29/30) structurally coherent.** CAE chain: **Genesis 2:24 LXX → Ephesians 5:31–32** — the Eden-to-new-creation chain (Chain 15) receives its marriage-mystery node.

**6:13–17 — The Armor of God / Isaiah LXX adaptation:**
The full armor sequence: belt of truth (*alētheia*) / breastplate of righteousness (*dikaiosynē*) / shoes of glad tidings of peace (*euangelion eirēnēs*) / shield of faith / helmet of salvation (*sōtēriou*) / sword of the breath-of-life (*pneumatos*) — the word (*rhēma*) of God. Each piece maps to Isaiah LXX: Isaiah 11:5 LXX (righteousness as belt / faithfulness as girdle) / Isaiah 59:17 LXX (righteousness as breastplate / helmet of salvation) / Isaiah 52:7 LXX (feet of those who bring glad tidings of peace) / Isaiah 49:2 LXX (mouth as sharp sword). The sword of the breath-of-life — *machairan tou pneumatos ho estin rhēma theou* — is the *pneuma* wielding the word of God as the offensive weapon. The entire armor is the Lord's own Isaiah-prophesied armor transferred to the assembly for cosmic combat. **LXX-primacy argument: the armor-of-God sequence requires five independent LXX Isaiah texts for its construction — compositionally impossible without LXX.**

**LXX-PRIMACY ARGUMENTS IN EPHESIANS**

Six independent LXX dependency points:

1. 1:14 — Exodus 19:5 LXX / Malachi 3:17 LXX (*peripoiēsis* — treasured possession)
2. 2:17 — Isaiah 57:19 LXX (*eirēnēn tois makran kai eirēnēn tois eggys* — peace to far and near)
3. 2:22 — Exodus 15:17 LXX + 1 Kings 8:13 LXX (*katoikētērion*)
4. 4:8 — Psalm 68:18 LXX (*anabas eis hypsos* — ascending on high / giving gifts to men; follows LXX against MT which has "receiving gifts")
5. 5:31 — Genesis 2:24 LXX (*sarx mia* — applied to Anointed-assembly mystery)
6. 6:14–17 — Isaiah 11:5 + 52:7 + 59:17 LXX — five-piece armor from Isaiah LXX

*4:8 CAE NOTE:* Psalm 68:18 MT reads "you received gifts among men" — LXX Psalm 67:18 reads "he gave gifts to men." Paul's citation at 4:8 follows the LXX reading (*edōken domata tois anthrōpois* — "he gave gifts to men") which makes the Pentecost-gift argument structurally coherent. **LXX-primacy argument: the entire gifts-of-the-Anointed argument in Ephesians 4:7–11 depends on the LXX Psalm 67:18 reading against MT.**

**CHAIN STATUS AFTER EPHESIANS**

All 15 chains remain closed. Ephesians adds:

- *apolytrōsis*: **Ephesians 1:7** — Chain 1 (*hilastērion*) receives its Ephesian ransom-release node. Blood-mediated ransom-release confirmed as the mechanism
- *huiothesia*: **Ephesians 1:5** — Chain 4 receives the pre-temporal election node. The son-placement is grounded in eternal predestination before creation
- *naos / katoikētērion*: **Ephesians 2:21–22** — Chain 9 receives its fullest ecclesiological node. The called-out assembly as *naos hagios* built by the Breath-of-life — the most developed *naos* statement in the NT corpus
- *sygklēronomia*: **Ephesians 3:6** — Gentile co-inheritance confirmed as the revealed mystery — inheritance chain extended
- *sarx mia / marriage mystery*: **Ephesians 5:31–32** — Chain 15 (Eden-to-new-creation) receives the marriage-mystery node connecting Genesis 2:24 to the Anointed-assembly union
- Trinitarian access-structure: **Ephesians 2:18** — through the Son / in one Breath-of-life / to the Father — fullest NT Trinitarian access statement
- *pneuma sophias*: **Ephesians 1:17** — Wisdom received through the Breath-of-life into the heart — the Wisdom-chain operative at the level of personal *epignōsis*
