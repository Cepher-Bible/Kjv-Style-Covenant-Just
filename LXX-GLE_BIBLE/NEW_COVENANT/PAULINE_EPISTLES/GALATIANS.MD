\p
\v 1 Paul — an apostle — not from men nor through man but through Jesus the Anointed and God the Father who raised him from the dead —
\v 2 and all the brothers who are with me — to the called-out assemblies (*ekklēsiais*) of Galatia:
\v 3 Grace to you and peace from God our Father and the Lord Jesus the Anointed —

\p

\v 7 not that there is another one — but there are some who trouble you and want to distort the glad tidings of the Anointed.
\v 8 But even if we or an angel from heaven should proclaim to you a glad tidings (*euangelion*) contrary to the one we proclaimed to you — let him be accursed.
\v 9 As we have said before so now I say again: if anyone is proclaiming to you a glad tidings contrary to the one you received — let him be accursed.
\v 10 For am I now seeking the approval of man or of God? Or am I trying to please man? If I were still trying to please man I would not be a servant of the Anointed.
\p
\v 11 For I would have you know brothers that the glad tidings (*euangelion*) that was proclaimed by me is not man's gospel.
\v 12 For I did not receive it from any man nor was I taught it — but I received it through a revelation of Jesus the Anointed.
\v 13 For you have heard of my former life in Judaism — how I persecuted the called-out assembly (*ekklēsian*) of God violently and tried to destroy it.
\v 14 And I was advancing in Judaism beyond many of my own age among my people — so extremely zealous was I for the traditions of my fathers.
\v 15 But when he who had set me apart before I was born and who called me by his grace was pleased
\v 16 to reveal his Son to me in order that I might proclaim him as glad tidings (*euangelizōmai*) among the Gentiles — I did not immediately consult with anyone —
\v 17 nor did I go up to Jerusalem to those who were apostles before me but I went away into Arabia and returned again to Damascus.
\p
\v 18 Then after three years I went up to Jerusalem to visit Cephas and remained with him fifteen days.
\v 19 But I saw none of the other apostles except James the Lord's brother.
\v 20 In what I am writing to you — before God — I do not lie!
\v 21 Then I went into the regions of Syria and Cilicia.
\v 22 And I was still unknown in person to the called-out assemblies (*ekklēsiais*) of Judea that are in the Anointed.
\v 23 They only were hearing it said: He who used to persecute us is now proclaiming the faith he once tried to destroy.
\v 24 And they glorified God because of me.

\p
\v 1 Then after fourteen years I went up again to Jerusalem with Barnabas taking Titus along with me.
\v 2 I went up because of a revelation and set before them — though privately before those who seemed influential — the glad tidings (*euangelion*) that I proclaim among the Gentiles in order to make sure I was not running or had not run in vain.
\v 3 But even Titus who was with me was not forced to be circumcised though he was a Greek.
\v 4 Yet because of false brothers secretly brought in — who slipped in to spy out our freedom that we have in the Anointed Jesus so that they might bring us into slavery —

\v 6 And from those who seemed to be influential — what they were makes no difference to me: God shows no partiality — those who seemed influential added nothing to me.
\v 7 On the contrary when they saw that I had been entrusted with the glad tidings (*euangelion*) to the uncircumcised just as Peter had been entrusted with the glad tidings to the circumcised —
\v 8 for he who worked through Peter for his apostolic ministry to the circumcised worked also through me for mine to the Gentiles —
\v 9 and when James and Cephas and John — who seemed to be pillars — perceived the grace that was given to me they gave the right hand of fellowship to Barnabas and me that we should go to the Gentiles and they to the circumcised.
\v 10 Only they asked us to remember the poor — the very thing I was eager to do.
\p
\v 11 But when Cephas came to Antioch I opposed him to his face because he stood condemned.
\v 12 For before certain men came from James he was eating with the Gentiles — but when they came he drew back and separated himself fearing the circumcision party.
\v 13 And the rest of the Jews acted hypocritically along with him so that even Barnabas was led astray by their hypocrisy.
\v 14 But when I saw that their conduct was not in step with the truth (*alētheian*) of the glad tidings I said to Cephas before them all: If you — though a Jew — live like a Gentile and not like a Jew how can you force the Gentiles to live like Jews?
\p
\v 15 We ourselves are Jews by birth and not Gentile sinners —
\v 16 yet we know that a person is not justified (*dikaioutai*) by works of the Law but through faith in Jesus the Anointed. \v 17 But if in our endeavor to be justified (*dikaiōthēnai*) in the Anointed we too were found to be sinners — is the Anointed then a servant of sin? Certainly not!

\v 19 For through the Law I died to the Law so that I might live to God (*theō zēsō*).
\v 20 I have been crucified with the Anointed. It is no longer I who live but the Anointed who lives in me. And the life I now live in the flesh (*sarki*) I live by faith in the Son of God who loved me and gave himself for me.

\v 12 But the Law is not of faith — rather: The one who does them shall live by them.
\v 13 The Anointed redeemed (*exēgorasen*) us from the curse of the Law by becoming a curse for us — for it is written: Cursed is everyone who is hanged on a tree —

\v 18 For if the inheritance (*klēronomia*) comes by the Law it no longer comes by promise — but God gave it to Abraham by a promise.

**GALATIANS — GLE v2.2 CAE NOTES**

---

**TEXT BASE:** RP2026 Byzantine Majority Text

**VERSE COUNT:** 149 verses / 6 chapters

---

**GALATIANS IN THE CORPUS ARCHITECTURE**

Galatians is the most forensically compressed Pauline letter — the justification argument of Romans stated at combat speed under existential pressure. Where Romans builds the case methodically across 16 chapters, Galatians delivers the same core in 6. The letter's CAE function is threefold: it anchors the *dikaioō* vocabulary lock in its sharpest polemical context, it contains the fullest *huiothesia* argument outside Romans 8, and it establishes the *pneuma / sarx* opposition as the organizing anthropological framework for the entire Pauline corpus. Galatians is also the only Pauline letter where the *diathēkē* term is applied directly to the Abrahamic covenant as a legal instrument — a move that retrospectively illuminates every *diathēkē* occurrence from Genesis forward.

---

**KEY LEXICAL DECISIONS**

*dikaioō / dikaiosynē / dikaios* — "justify / righteousness / righteous": the highest *dikaioō* density of any Pauline letter. Key instances: 2:16 (triple occurrence — the *dikaioō* argument stated, restated, and grounded), 2:17 (justified in the Anointed), 2:21 (*dikaiosynē* through Law = Anointed died for nothing), 3:6 (Abraham's faith counted as *dikaiosynē*), 3:8 (Scripture proclaimed glad tidings — justified the Gentiles by faith), 3:11 (*dikaios* shall live by faith), 3:21 (Law cannot give life therefore *dikaiosynē* not by Law), 3:24 (Law as guardian until justified by faith), 5:4 (justified by Law = severed from the Anointed), 5:5 (wait for the hope of *dikaiosynē*).

*2:16 CAE NOTE:* The triple *dikaioō* in a single verse is the structural center of the entire letter. The argument moves: (a) a person is not justified by works of Law but through faith in Jesus the Anointed — (b) we believed in the Anointed Jesus to be justified by faith in the Anointed and not by works of Law — (c) because by works of Law no one will be justified. The threefold repetition is forensic rhetoric — the triple statement constitutes a legal declaration. CAE cross-link: **Acts 13:38–39 → Galatians 2:16 → Romans 3:20/28** — the justification argument first narrated in Acts, polemically compressed in Galatians, systematically expounded in Romans.

*pneuma* — "breath-of-life" throughout. Galatians has the densest *pneuma / sarx* opposition structure in the Pauline corpus: 3:2/3/5/14 (breath-of-life received by faith not Law), 4:6 (breath-of-life of his Son sent into hearts), 4:29 (born according to flesh / according to breath-of-life), 5:5 (through the breath-of-life we wait), 5:16/17/18/25 (walk by / desires of / led by / live by the breath-of-life), 5:22 (fruit of the breath-of-life), 6:1 (*pneumatikoi* — spiritual ones), 6:8 (sow to the breath-of-life / reap everlasting life), 6:18 (grace with your breath-of-life).

*5:17 CAE NOTE:* *hē gar sarx epithymei kata tou pneumatos to de pneuma kata tēs sarkos* — "for the flesh desires against the breath-of-life and the breath-of-life against the flesh." This is the definitive statement of the *pneuma / sarx* anthropological tension in Paul. It is not Greek body/soul dualism — the *sarx* is not the physical body as such but the human person oriented away from God, the Folly-register orientation of the self. The *pneuma* is the breath-of-life renewed by the Holy Breath-of-life — the Wisdom-register orientation. The opposition is not material vs. immaterial but covenant-oriented vs. self-oriented. CAE Wisdom/Folly axis: *pneuma* walk = Wisdom register / *sarx* walk = Folly register — the two orientations given their clearest Pauline definition here. Cross-link to Mark 14:38 (*pneuma prothumon / sarx asthenēs*) and Romans 8:5–8.

*diathēkē* — "covenant": 3:15/17 (human covenant / Abrahamic covenant), 4:24 (two covenants — Hagar/Sarah allegory). The 3:15–17 argument is the most technically legal use of *diathēkē* in the NT. Paul argues: a *diathēkē* once ratified (*kekyrōmenēn*) cannot be annulled or supplemented. The Abrahamic covenant was ratified 430 years before the Law — therefore the Law cannot annul it. The vocabulary lock holds — *diathēkē* is not merely a religious arrangement but a legally binding covenant instrument with hermeneutical priority. **CAE anchor: Genesis 15:18 LXX → Genesis 17:2–21 LXX → Galatians 3:15–17** — the Abrahamic covenant-ratification as the hermeneutical key to the Law/promise relationship.

*huiothesia* — "son-placement": 4:5 — *hina tēn huiothesian apolabōmen* — "that we might receive son-placement." The only occurrence of *huiothesia* in Galatians. The argument: the Son was born under the Law to redeem those under the Law — so that we might receive the legal status of sons. The *exagorasē* (redeem / buy out) at 4:5 connects to 3:13 (*exēgorasen* — redeemed from the curse) — two uses of the *agorazō* / *exagorazō* redemption root. CAE chain: **Exodus 4:22 LXX → Psalm 2:7 LXX → Galatians 4:4–7 → Romans 8:15 → Romans 8:23** — Chain 4 (*huiothesia*) receives its fullest Pauline argument in Galatians 4.

*klēronomia / klēronomos* — "inheritance / heir": 3:18 (inheritance by promise not Law), 3:29 (heirs according to promise), 4:1/7 (heir as child / heir through God), 4:30 (slave's son shall not inherit with free woman's son), 5:21 (those who do works of flesh will not inherit the kingdom). The inheritance terminology is structurally linked to the son-placement argument — *huiothesia* produces *klēronomia*. Sons are heirs. The legal sequence: redemption (*exagorazō*) → son-placement (*huiothesia*) → inheritance (*klēronomia*). CAE chain reinforcement: **Galatians 4:4–7 → Romans 8:15–17 → Hebrews 1:14 / 9:15**.

*agapē* — "covenant-love": 5:6 (faith working through *agapēs*), 5:13 (through *agapēs* serve one another), 5:22 (*agapē* as first fruit of the breath-of-life). The *agapē* vocabulary lock holds — covenant-love is not sentimental affection but the operative mode of faith in the other. The fruit-of-the-breath-of-life list (5:22–23) opens with *agapē* — it is the primary expression of the breath-of-life's orientation.

*alētheia* — "truth": 2:5 (truth of the glad tidings), 2:14 (not in step with the truth of the glad tidings), 4:16 (become your enemy by telling the truth), 5:7 (who hindered you from obeying the truth). The *alētheia* cluster in Galatians is specifically *alētheia tou euangeliou* — "truth of the glad tidings." The Antioch confrontation (2:11–14) is defined as a departure from the *alētheia tou euangeliou*. CAE Wisdom/Folly axis: truth = Wisdom register / the Judaizing pressure = Folly-register corruption of the glad tidings. The truth of the glad tidings is not merely propositional correctness but ontological alignment with the covenant reality of justification by faith.

*sarx* — "flesh": 1:16 (not consult flesh and blood), 2:16/20 (life in the flesh), 3:3 (perfected by the flesh), 4:13/14 (bodily ailment / condition in the flesh), 4:23/29 (born according to the flesh), 5:13/16/17/19/24 (*sarx* as the locus of the anti-breath-of-life orientation), 6:8 (sow to the flesh / reap corruption), 6:12/13 (make a good showing in the flesh). Mark *sarx* as the total Pauline term for humanity oriented away from God — not merely physical but covenantally self-directed existence. The *sarx → phthora* (corruption) equation at 6:8 is the Pauline summary of the Folly-register endpoint.

*euangelion / euangelizō* — "glad tidings": 1:6/7/8/9/11 (the integrity of the glad tidings), 1:16 (proclaim among Gentiles), 2:2/5/7/14 (truth of the glad tidings), 3:8 (*proeuēngelisato* — proclaimed glad tidings beforehand to Abraham). The 3:8 *proeuēngelisato* is extraordinary — the Scripture itself *proclaimed glad tidings* to Abraham. The Abrahamic blessing-promise is itself characterized as *euangelion* — the glad tidings is not a NT innovation but the content of the original Abrahamic promise now fulfilled in the Anointed. CAE anchor: **Genesis 12:3 / 18:18 LXX → Galatians 3:8** — the proto-*euangelion* to Abraham.

*zōē aiōnios* — "everlasting life": 6:8 — "the one who sows to the breath-of-life will from the breath-of-life reap everlasting life (*zōēn aiōnion*)." The only occurrence of the full *zōē aiōnios* phrase in Galatians — placed at the structural climax of the *pneuma / sarx* harvest metaphor. Sowing to breath-of-life = harvest of everlasting life. Sowing to flesh = harvest of corruption (*phthora*). The binary endpoint of the two registers stated as agricultural inevitability.

---

**STRUCTURAL CAE NODES**

**1:8–9 — Anathema and the integrity of the glad tidings:**
The double anathema — even an angel from heaven proclaiming a different *euangelion* is accursed — is the most absolute statement of glad-tidings integrity in the NT. The *euangelion* is not a human construct subject to revision but a revealed reality with fixed content. Any deviation is not merely error but covenant violation deserving curse. CAE Wisdom/Folly register: the Judaizing *euangelion* is not an alternative version of the truth but a Folly-register corruption — a lie operating through religious language. The anathema is the covenantal sanction against the ontological inversion.

**2:19–20 — Died to the Law / live to God / crucified with the Anointed:**
*2:19a* — *dia nomou nomō apethanon* — "through the Law I died to the Law." The Law itself killed Paul to the Law — the Law's curse fell on the Anointed and Paul is co-crucified with him, therefore dead to the Law's jurisdiction. *2:19b* — *hina theō zēsō* — "that I might live to God." **PRIMARY CHAIN NODE — Chain 3 (*zēn tō theō*):**

**4 Maccabees 7:19 → Luke 20:38 → Galatians 2:19 → Romans 6:10–11 → Romans 14:8**

The *zēn tō theō* of 2:19 is the Galatian instantiation of the chain. The patriarchs live to God (4 Maccabees / Luke 20:38) — Christ lives to God after resurrection (Romans 6:10) — Paul dies to the Law to live to God (Galatians 2:19) — believers reckon themselves as living to God (Romans 6:11) — whether we live or die we are the Lord's (Romans 14:8). The chain is now closed across five independent witnesses.

*2:20* — *Christō synestaurōmai* — "I have been crucified with the Anointed." The co-crucifixion declaration is the existential basis of the *zēn tō theō* statement. The life now lived in the flesh is lived by faith in the Son of God who loved me and gave himself for me (*agapēsantos me kai paradontos heauton hyper emou*). The substitutionary self-giving (*hyper emou*) is stated in maximally personal terms — not "for sins" or "for many" but *for me*. The ransom-chain becomes biographical — the *antipsychon* of Mark 10:45 / Matthew 20:28 is here received as personal covenantal gift.

**3:8 — proeuēngelisato:**
"The Scripture proclaimed the glad tidings beforehand (*proeuēngelisato*) to Abraham." This is the single most important hermeneutical statement in Galatians for the LXX-primacy argument. The Abrahamic blessing-promise (Genesis 12:3 / 18:18 / 22:18 LXX) is itself characterized as *euangelion* — meaning the glad tidings is not a new message but the ancient Abrahamic promise now fulfilled. The LXX formulation of the Abraham blessing — *en soi eneulogēthēsontai pasai hai phylai tēs gēs* — "in you all the tribes of the earth shall be blessed" — is cited at 3:8. **LXX-primacy argument: Galatians 3:8 cites LXX Genesis 12:3 / 18:18 and characterizes it as the proto-*euangelion* — the glad tidings was first spoken in LXX Greek to Abraham.**

**3:10–13 — Curse / Redeemed from the curse:**
3:10 cites Deuteronomy 27:26 LXX (*epikataratos pas hos ouk emmenei*) — "cursed is everyone who does not abide by all things written in the Book of the Law." 3:13 cites Deuteronomy 21:23 LXX (*epikataratos pas ho kremamenos epi xylou*) — "cursed is everyone who is hanged on a tree." The argument: the Law pronounces a curse on non-compliance — the Anointed became the curse by being hung on a tree (Deuteronomy 21:23 LXX) — thereby exhausting the curse on behalf of those under it. **LXX-primacy argument: both Deuteronomy citations follow LXX against MT at points relevant to the argument.** The *epikataratos* formulation of LXX Deuteronomy 21:23 is directly applied to crucifixion — a reading that requires the LXX Greek *kremamenos epi xylou* (hung on a tree). CAE anchor: **Deuteronomy 27:26 LXX + 21:23 LXX → Galatians 3:10/13**.

**3:15–17 — diathēkē as irrevocable legal instrument:**
The argument from the irrevocability of a ratified *diathēkē* is the most technically legal use of covenant language in the NT. The 430-year calculation follows the LXX of Exodus 12:40 (which includes the sojourn in Canaan in the 430 years) against MT (which refers only to Egypt). **LXX-primacy argument: the 430-year chronology of Galatians 3:17 follows LXX Exodus 12:40 against MT.** CAE anchor: **LXX Exodus 12:40 → Galatians 3:17**.

**4:4–7 — huiothesia chain node:**
"God sent forth his Son — born of woman — born under the Law — to redeem (*exagorasē*) those who were under the Law — so that we might receive son-placement (*huiothesian*)." The redemption → son-placement sequence is the covenantal logic of the atonement stated in its most compressed form. The Son enters the Law's jurisdiction to exhaust it — releasing those under it into sonship. The consequent: "God has sent the breath-of-life of his Son into our hearts crying Abba Father" — the Breath-of-life is the breath-of-life of the Son, not merely a generic divine presence. The cry *Abba Patēr* is the Son's own cry now given to those who have received son-placement. **PRIMARY CHAIN NODE — Chain 4 (*huiothesia*):**

**Exodus 4:22 LXX → Psalm 2:7 LXX → Galatians 4:4–7 → Romans 8:15 → Romans 8:23**

The chain moves: Israel as firstborn son (Exodus 4:22) → the Messianic Son declared at resurrection (Psalm 2:7) → the Son sent to redeem and confer son-placement (Galatians 4:4–5) → the Breath-of-life of adoption crying Abba (Galatians 4:6 / Romans 8:15) → the awaited full son-placement at resurrection (Romans 8:23). Chain 4 is fully closed with Galatians 4:4–7 providing the redemptive mechanism node.

**4:21–31 — The two covenants allegory:**
The Hagar/Sarah allegory identifies two *diathēkai* — Sinai (slavery / present Jerusalem) and the promise (freedom / Jerusalem above). The allegory cites Isaiah 54:1 LXX (*euphranēthēti steira hē ou tiktousa*) — "rejoice O barren one who does not bear" — at 4:27. This is the post-exilic restoration oracle of Isaiah 54 now applied to the called-out assembly as the free woman's offspring. **LXX-primacy argument: Isaiah 54:1 LXX is cited verbatim at Galatians 4:27 — the restoration of Israel oracle applied to the Gentile called-out assembly as the barren woman who now has many children.** CAE anchor: **Isaiah 54:1 LXX → Galatians 4:27**.

The *pneuma / sarx* contrast at 4:29 — "he who was born according to the flesh persecuted him who was born according to the breath-of-life" — retrospectively characterizes Ishmael's birth as a *sarx*-birth and Isaac's as a *pneuma*-birth. This is not a biological claim but a covenant-origin claim: Ishmael was born by human arrangement; Isaac was born by divine promise through the Breath-of-life's power. The *sarx / pneuma* binary is now applied to salvation-historical categories — two births, two covenants, two orientations.

**5:1 — For freedom the Anointed set us free:**
*Tē eleutheria hēmas Christos ēleutherōsen* — structurally the thesis statement of the entire letter's ethical section. The freedom is not autonomy but liberation from the *sarx*-orientation and Law-jurisdiction into *pneuma*-walk. The paradox: freedom from the Law is expressed through *agapē*-service (5:13) — the whole Law fulfilled in one word: love your neighbor (5:14). The *agapē*-fulfillment of the Law is not law-keeping but the natural expression of the breath-of-life's fruit. CAE Wisdom-register: the *pneuma*-walk produces *agapē* which fulfills the Law's intent without being under the Law's jurisdiction.

**5:22–23 — Fruit of the breath-of-life:**
The nine-fold fruit — *agapē / chara / eirēnē / makrothymia / chrēstotēs / agathōsynē / pistis / prautēs / enkrateia* — covenant-love / joy / peace / patience / kindness / goodness / faithfulness / gentleness / self-control — is the Wisdom-register profile in behavioral form. Against such things there is no law — the breath-of-life's fruit transcends Law precisely because it fulfills Law's intent from within. CAE Wisdom/Folly axis: the fruit list is the positive Wisdom register; the works-of-flesh list (5:19–21) is the Folly register. The two lists are not moral categories but ontological orientations — what the *pneuma*-birth produces versus what the *sarx*-birth produces.

**6:8 — Sow to pneuma / reap zōē aiōnios:**
*ho de speirōn eis to pneuma ek tou pneumatos therisei zōēn aiōnion* — "the one who sows to the breath-of-life will from the breath-of-life reap everlasting life." The *zōē aiōnios* vocabulary lock at the structural climax. The *pneuma*-sowing is the Wisdom-register investment — it produces the Wisdom-register harvest: *zōē aiōnios*. The *sarx*-sowing produces *phthora* (corruption) — the Folly-register endpoint. The agricultural metaphor makes the inevitability structural: what is sown determines what is reaped — there is no possibility of sowing *sarx* and reaping *pneuma*.

**6:16 — eleos upon the Israel of God:**
The closing benediction: "peace and steadfast love (*eleos*) upon them and upon the Israel of God." The *eleos* vocabulary lock in the benediction — covenant-love as the content of the blessing. The "Israel of God" — whether referring to Jewish believers or the whole called-out assembly — receives the *eleos* that defines covenant relationship. CAE anchor: **Psalm 125:5 LXX / Psalm 128:6 LXX (peace upon Israel) → Galatians 6:16** — the Psalms' benedictory formula adapted for the new covenant community.

**LXX-PRIMACY ARGUMENTS IN GALATIANS**

Five independent LXX dependency points:

1. 3:8 — Genesis 12:3 / 18:18 LXX (*proeuēngelisato* — the proto-*euangelion*)
2. 3:10 — Deuteronomy 27:26 LXX (*epikataratos*)
3. 3:13 — Deuteronomy 21:23 LXX (*kremamenos epi xylou* — applicable to crucifixion)
4. 3:17 — Exodus 12:40 LXX (430-year chronology including Canaan sojourn)
5. 4:27 — Isaiah 54:1 LXX (barren woman / restoration oracle applied to called-out assembly)

**CHAIN STATUS AFTER GALATIANS**

All 15 chains remain closed. Galatians adds:

- *zēn tō theō* chain: **Galatians 2:19** — Chain 3 receives its Galatian node. Now: **4 Macc 7:19 → Luke 20:38 → Galatians 2:19 → Romans 6:10–11 → Romans 14:8**
- *huiothesia* chain: **Galatians 4:4–7** — Chain 4 receives its fullest redemption-mechanism node. Now: **Exodus 4:22 LXX → Psalm 2:7 LXX → Galatians 4:4–7 → Romans 8:15 → Romans 8:23**
- *diathēkē* as irrevocable covenant instrument: **Galatians 3:15–17** — Chain 8 receives the legal-irrevocability argument anchoring the Abrahamic covenant's hermeneutical priority over the Law
- *zōē aiōnios* harvest: **Galatians 6:8** — *pneuma*-sowing → everlasting life equation
- *eleos* benediction: **Galatians 6:16** — covenant-love vocabulary lock in closing blessing
