**COLOSSIANS — GLE v2.2 CAE NOTES**

---

**TEXT BASE:** RP2026 Byzantine Majority Text

**VERSE COUNT:** 95 verses / 4 chapters

---

**COLOSSIANS IN THE CORPUS ARCHITECTURE**

Colossians is Ephesians compressed into four chapters with the Christological argument elevated to maximum intensity. Where Ephesians develops ecclesiology from a cosmic vantage point, Colossians develops Christology from the same vantage point — the *prōtotokos* hymn of 1:15–20 is the most concentrated Christological statement in the Pauline corpus outside Philippians 2:6–11. The letter's CAE function is threefold: it contains the fullest *prōtotokos* chain node, it develops the *cheiropoiētos / acheiropoiētos* vocabulary lock in its most anthropological application (circumcision made without hands — 2:11), and it establishes the *eikōn* — image — Christology that retroactively illuminates Genesis 1:26–27 as the Christological anchor of creation.

The letter's polemical target — the "Colossian heresy" of elemental principles / angel worship / ascetic regulations (2:8/16–23) — is the precise Folly-register pathology that the *sophia* Christology of 1:15–20 and 2:3 is designed to refute. All the treasures of wisdom and knowledge are hidden in the Anointed (2:3) — the claim of any supplementary wisdom-system is therefore a Folly-register move, regardless of its religious sophistication.

---

**KEY LEXICAL DECISIONS**

*prōtotokos* — "firstborn": 1:15 (*prōtotokos pasēs ktiseōs* — firstborn of all creation), 1:18 (*prōtotokos ek tōn nekrōn* — firstborn from the dead). Two occurrences in two verses — the double *prōtotokos* is the structural axis of the hymn. **PRIMARY CHAIN NODE — Chain 14 (*prōtotokos*):**

**Psalm 89:27 LXX → Romans 8:29 → Colossians 1:15/18 → Revelation 1:5**

The chain moves: the Davidic king as *prōtotokos* / highest of the kings of the earth (Psalm 89:27 LXX) → the Anointed as firstborn among many brothers in the resurrection order (Romans 8:29) → the Anointed as firstborn of all creation and firstborn from the dead (Colossians 1:15/18) → the Anointed as firstborn from the dead and ruler of kings of the earth (Revelation 1:5). The Colossian double *prōtotokos* is the chain's fullest statement — creation-firstborn and resurrection-firstborn in a single verse pair.

*1:15 CAE NOTE:* *prōtotokos pasēs ktiseōs* — "firstborn of all creation." This does not mean the Anointed is the first created thing — the following verse (1:16) states explicitly that all things were created *by him* and *for him*, and 1:17 that he is *before all things*. The *prōtotokos* is a sovereignty title — the one who holds the rights of the firstborn over the entire created order. The LXX background: *prōtotokos* in Psalm 89:27 LXX is a relational status title, not a temporal birth sequence. The Anointed is firstborn of all creation in the sense that he holds the firstborn's covenantal sovereignty over it. **LXX-primacy argument: the *prōtotokos* title applied to the Anointed depends on Psalm 89:27 LXX's use of *prōtotokos* as a sovereignty-and-covenant title for the Davidic king.**

*1:18 CAE NOTE:* *prōtotokos ek tōn nekrōn* — "firstborn from the dead." The second *prōtotokos* is explicitly resurrection-based — the Anointed is the first to pass through death into the new creation order, and as firstborn holds sovereignty over all who will follow. The connection to Romans 8:29 (*prōtotokos en pollois adelphois* — firstborn among many brothers) is direct: the resurrection-firstborn leads a family of resurrection-participants. CAE chain: Colossians 1:18 → Revelation 1:5 (*prōtotokos tōn nekrōn* — exact parallel formulation) — two independent witnesses to the resurrection-*prōtotokos* title.

*eikōn* — "image": 1:15 (*eikōn tou theou tou aoratou* — image of the invisible God), 3:10 (new self renewed in knowledge after the *eikona* of its creator). The double *eikōn* creates a Christological-anthropological inclusio — the Anointed is the *eikōn* of God (1:15), and believers are being renewed into the *eikōn* of their creator (3:10). CAE chain: **Genesis 1:26–27 LXX (*kat' eikona* — according to the image) → Colossians 1:15 (the Anointed as the *eikōn*) → Colossians 3:10 (renewal into the *eikōn*) → 2 Corinthians 3:18 (transformed into the same *eikōn*)** — the image-chain from creation through incarnation to eschatological renewal. The Anointed is not merely created in God's image — he *is* the image, the original of which Genesis 1:26 is the creaturely echo.

*sophia* — "wisdom": 1:9 (filled with knowledge of his will in all *sophia*), 1:28 (teaching everyone with all *sophia*), 2:3 (all treasures of *sophia* and knowledge hidden in the Anointed), 2:23 (appearance of *sophia* in self-made religion — false *sophia*), 3:16 (word of the Anointed dwelling richly — teaching in all *sophia*), 4:5 (walk in *sophia* toward outsiders). The *sophia* vocabulary lock is maximally loaded in Colossians — the true/false *sophia* contrast is the letter's organizing polemic. *2:3* is the axial statement: *en hō eisin pantes hoi thēsauroi tēs sophias kai gnōseōs apokryphoi* — "in whom are hidden all the treasures of wisdom and knowledge." The Colossian heresy offered supplementary wisdom — angelic knowledge, ascetic discipline, visionary access. Paul's response: all treasures of wisdom are already hidden in the Anointed. Any supplementary wisdom-claim is by definition the Folly register dressed in religious clothing. CAE Wisdom/Folly axis: true *sophia* = hidden in the Anointed / false *sophia* = self-made religion (2:23 — *ethelothrēskia*) = Folly-register religiosity.

*pneuma* — "breath-of-life": 1:8 (your covenant-love in the breath-of-life), 2:5 (present with you in breath-of-life), 3:16 (spiritual songs — *pneumatikais*). Colossians has the lowest *pneuma* density of the Prison Epistles — the letter's focus is Christological rather than pneumatological. The breath-of-life functions as the medium of covenant-love (1:8) and the medium of Paul's spiritual presence (2:5) but does not receive extended treatment. The *pneumatikais* of 3:16 parallels Ephesians 5:19 — both letters conclude the household code section with the same pneumatic-worship formula.

*acheiropoiētos* — "not made by hands": 2:11 — *perietmēthēte peritomē acheiropoiētō* — "you were circumcised with a circumcision not made by hands." This is the anthropological application of the *acheiropoiētos* vocabulary lock established in Mark 14:58 (the sanctuary not made by hands). The circumcision of the Anointed is the *acheiropoiētos* surgical removal of the body of flesh — not the physical foreskin but the *sarx*-orientation of the old self. **CAE chain reinforcement — Chain 9 / *acheiropoiētos* node:**

**Isaiah 2:18 LXX (*cheiropoiēta* — idols made by hands) → Mark 14:58 (*acheiropoiēton* — sanctuary not made by hands) → Colossians 2:11 (*acheiropoiētō* — circumcision not made by hands) → Hebrews 9:11 (*naou ou cheiropoiētou* — tabernacle not made by hands)**

The *acheiropoiētos* term migrates across domains: sanctuary (Mark 14:58) → circumcision (Colossians 2:11) → tabernacle (Hebrews 9:11). In each case the contrast is between the human-constructed and the divinely-enacted — the Folly register produces *cheiropoiēta* (idols / physical rites / earthly structures) while the Wisdom register operates through the *acheiropoiēton* (the true sanctuary / the Breath-of-life's circumcision / the true tabernacle). Three independent witnesses — three-witness lock achieved.

*klēronomia* — "inheritance": 1:12 (qualified to share in the inheritance of the holy ones in light), 3:24 (from the Lord you will receive the inheritance as your reward). The inheritance vocabulary lock holds — the inheritance is received from the Lord as covenantal gift, not earned as wage. The 3:24 formulation is unusual — *apolabon tēn klēronomian* used in the context of bondservant instruction, implying that the covenantal inheritance transcends social status. Even bondservants serving earthly masters are serving the Lord the Anointed and will receive the inheritance.

*agapē* — "covenant-love": 1:4/8 (faith and covenant-love), 2:2 (knit together in *agapē*), 3:14 (*agapēn* — the bond of perfect harmony), 3:19 (husbands love your wives). The *agapē* as bond (*syndesmos*) of perfect harmony at 3:14 is the Colossian parallel to Ephesians 4:3 (unity of the breath-of-life in the bond of peace). In Colossians the *agapē* is the *syndesmos tēs teleiotētos* — "bond of perfection / completeness" — the binding agent that holds the virtues of 3:12–13 in unified expression.

*alētheia* — "truth": 1:5 (word of truth / glad tidings), 1:6 (grace of God in truth). The *alētheia* occurrences are concentrated in the opening thanksgiving — the glad tidings is the word of truth, and the grace of God is received *in truth* (not merely notionally but as an operative reality). The relatively sparse *alētheia* count in Colossians (compared to Ephesians) reflects the letter's Christological rather than ethical focus — the truth issue is addressed through the *sophia* polemic rather than the *alētheia* vocabulary lock.

*logos* — "word": 1:5 (word of truth), 1:25 (make the word of God fully known), 3:16 (let the word of the Anointed dwell in you richly), 4:3 (door for the word), 4:6 (speech seasoned with salt). The *logos tou Christou* — "word of the Anointed" at 3:16 — is the only place in the NT where this phrase appears (Ephesians 5:26 has *rhēmati* — utterance). The word dwelling richly (*enoikeitō plousiōs*) in the community produces teaching and admonishing in all wisdom and pneumatic song — it is the community-indwelling form of the Anointed himself (1:27 — "the Anointed in you").

**STRUCTURAL CAE NODES**

**1:13–14 — Domain of darkness / Kingdom of beloved Son / apolytrōsis:**
"He has delivered us from the domain (*exousias*) of darkness and transferred us to the kingdom of his beloved Son — in whom we have ransom-release (*apolytrōsin*) — the forgiveness of sins." The transfer metaphor (*metestēsen*) is a deportation/resettlement term — the entire community has been relocated from one sovereign domain to another. The darkness-domain is the realm of the *archonta tēs exousias tou aeros* (Ephesians 2:2) — the counter-breath-of-life's jurisdiction. The *apolytrōsis* here is the mechanism of the transfer — the ransom-release that effects the domain-change. CAE structural note: this is the narrative form of Romans 6:17–18 (slaves of sin / freed and become slaves of righteousness) — the domain transfer stated cosmologically rather than forensically.

**1:15–20 — The prōtotokos Hymn:**
The hymn divides into two strophes — creation (1:15–17) and new creation / reconciliation (1:18–20) — linked by the double *prōtotokos*. The structure is precise:

*Strophe A — Creation:* Image of the invisible God / firstborn of all creation / all things created by him and for him / before all things / in him all things hold together / head of the body the called-out assembly

*Strophe B — New Creation:* Beginning / firstborn from the dead / that in everything he might be preeminent / all fullness pleased to dwell in him / reconcile all things through the blood of his cross

The *ta panta* — "all things" — occurs six times across the two strophes, binding creation and reconciliation into a single cosmic movement. The Anointed is the agent of creation (*di' autou* — through him) and the goal of creation (*eis auton* — for him) and the reconciler of creation — the three prepositions of Romans 11:36 (from him / through him / to him) distributed across the hymn's structure.

**LXX-primacy argument — 1:15 / *eikōn* and Wisdom tradition:** The *eikōn tou theou tou aoratou* connects to Wisdom of Solomon 7:26 LXX (*apaugasma gar esti phōtos aidiou kai esoptron akēlidōton tēs tou theou energeias kai eikōn tēs agathōtētos autou* — "for she is a radiance of eternal light and a spotless mirror of the working of God and an image of his goodness"). The Wisdom of Solomon's personified *Sophia* as *eikōn* of God is the LXX-tradition template for the Colossian hymn's application of the same title to the Anointed. The hymn applies the *Sophia* attributes of Wisdom of Solomon 7–8 to the Anointed — creation-agent, image of God, dwelling in those who receive wisdom. **LXX-primacy argument: Colossians 1:15–20 is compositionally dependent on Wisdom of Solomon 7:22–8:1 LXX — a text absent from MT tradition entirely.** CAE anchor: **Wisdom of Solomon 7:26 LXX → Colossians 1:15 / Hebrews 1:3** — the *eikōn / apaugasma* (image / radiance) pair appearing in both.

**2:2–3 — The Anointed as the mystery / all treasures of sophia:**
*eis epignōsin tou mystēriou tou theou Christou en hō eisin pantes hoi thēsauroi tēs sophias kai gnōseōs apokryphoi* — "the knowledge of God's mystery — which is the Anointed — in whom are hidden all the treasures of wisdom and knowledge." The mystery-revelation structure of Colossians echoes Ephesians 3:3–6 — but where Ephesians identifies the mystery as the Gentile co-inheritance, Colossians identifies the mystery as the Anointed himself. The two are not contradictory — "the Anointed in you" (1:27) is the mystery's content, and its Gentile scope (1:27 — "among the Gentiles") is its reach. CAE structural note: the *apokryphoi* — "hidden" — treasures are hidden *in* the Anointed, not hidden *from* those in the Anointed. The wisdom-seeker who is in the Anointed has access to all treasures; the wisdom-seeker who supplements the Anointed with elemental principles has left the only location where the treasures exist.

**2:9–15 — Fullness / Circumcision / Burial-Resurrection / Cancellation of the record of debt:**
Four compressed theological nodes in seven verses: (a) the whole fullness (*plērōma*) of deity dwelling bodily in the Anointed (2:9) — the Ephesian fullness-language (1:23 / 3:19) now stated in maximally incarnational terms; (b) circumcision made without hands — *acheiropoiētō* — the *sarx*-body put off by the Anointed's circumcision (2:11); (c) buried with him in baptism / raised through faith in the powerful working of God (2:12); (d) the *cheirographon* — "record of debt" / "certificate of indebtedness" — canceled by being nailed to the cross (2:14).

*2:14 CAE NOTE:* *exaleipsas to kath' hēmōn cheirographon tois dogmasin ho ēn hypenantian hēmin* — "canceling the record of debt that stood against us with its legal demands." The *cheirographon* is a handwritten debt-certificate — the legal document recording humanity's covenantal indebtedness. Its cancellation is accomplished by nailing it to the cross — the cross is the place where the debt-document is publicly voided. The *tois dogmasin* — "legal demands" — echoes Ephesians 2:15 (*ton nomon tōn entolōn en dogmasin* — the Law of commandments expressed in ordinances). The cross simultaneously exhausts the curse (Galatians 3:13) and cancels the debt-document (Colossians 2:14) — two complementary legal metaphors for the same atonement-reality. CAE composite anchor: **Deuteronomy 27:26 LXX + Galatians 3:13 → Colossians 2:14 → Hebrews 9:12** — Chain 1 reinforced.

**2:15 — Disarming rulers and authorities:**
*apekdysamenos tas archas kai tas exousias edeigmatisen en parrēsia thriambeusas autous en autō* — "he disarmed the rulers and authorities and put them to open shame — triumphing over them in him." The cross is simultaneously the place of apparent defeat (the rulers and authorities apparently won) and actual triumph (they were publicly shamed and disarmed). The *thriambeusas* — "triumphing over" — is a Roman triumph metaphor — the victor leads the defeated in public procession. The rulers and authorities who crucified the Lord of glory (1 Corinthians 2:8) are themselves led in triumphal procession at the cross. CAE structural note: this is the Colossian form of the Mark 3:27 strong-man-binding — the cross is the binding event that makes the plundering possible.

**3:1–4 — Raised with the Anointed / life hidden in God / appeared in glory:**
"If then you have been raised with the Anointed seek the things that are above — where the Anointed is seated at the right hand of God." The co-resurrection of Ephesians 2:6 (seated with him in heavenly places) is here stated as the ethical imperative's ground. "Your life (*zōē*) is hidden with the Anointed in God" — the *zōē* of believers is not merely eschatologically promised but presently hidden in the most secure location possible: in the Anointed / in God. "When the Anointed who is your life (*zōē*) appears then you also will appear with him in glory" — the appearing is the unveiling of what is currently hidden. CAE *zōē* chain: the *zōē* hidden with the Anointed in God is the *zōē aiōnios* of John 17:3 (knowing the only true God and Jesus the Anointed whom he sent) in its present-but-hidden mode. The *zōē* that is the Anointed himself (John 14:6 / 11:25) is the content of the hiding.

**3:9–11 — Old self / New self / eikōn of creator / Anointed all in all:**
"Having put off the old self (*palaion anthrōpon*) with its practices and having put on the new self (*neon*) which is being renewed in knowledge after the image (*eikona*) of its creator." The Ephesian put-off/put-on structure (Ephesians 4:22–24) is here given its Christological-anthropological ground: the renewal is toward the *eikōn* of the creator — the same *eikōn* that the Anointed *is* (1:15). The renewal is a re-imaging — the imago Dei damaged in the Fall being restored through the new self's formation after the original image, which is the Anointed himself. **Chain 15 (Eden-to-new-creation) node:**

**Genesis 1:26–27 LXX (*kat' eikona*) → Colossians 1:15 (*eikōn tou theou*) → Colossians 3:10 (renewed after the *eikōn* of creator) → 2 Corinthians 3:18 (transformed into the same *eikōn*)**

The image-restoration arc: humanity created in the image (Genesis 1:26–27) → the Anointed as the original Image (Colossians 1:15) → believers renewed toward that Image (Colossians 3:10) → transformation into that Image by the Breath-of-life (2 Corinthians 3:18). Chain 15 receives its image-restoration node — the Eden-to-new-creation movement now traced through the Christological *eikōn*.

"Here there is not Greek and Jew — circumcised and uncircumcised — barbarian — Scythian — slave — free — but the Anointed is all and in all." The Colossian form of Galatians 3:28 (neither Jew nor Greek / slave nor free / male and female) — but expanded to include the most extreme ethnic boundary (barbarian / Scythian) and grounded not in co-inheritance but in the Anointed being *all and in all* (*panta kai en pasin Christos*). CAE structural note: the *panta kai en pasin* echoes 1:16–17 (*ta panta* / *en autō* / *ta panta en autō synestēken*) — the cosmic *ta panta* of the creation hymn is now the social-anthropological *panta* of the new humanity. What is true cosmically (the Anointed holds all things together) is true ecclesially (the Anointed is all and in all).

**LXX-PRIMACY ARGUMENTS IN COLOSSIANS**

Four independent LXX dependency points:

1. 1:15 — Wisdom of Solomon 7:26 LXX (*eikōn* / *apaugasma* — image / radiance of God) — a deuterocanonical text absent from MT tradition
2. 1:15 — Psalm 89:27 LXX (*prōtotokos* as sovereignty title for the Davidic king)
3. 2:11 — *acheiropoiētō* — circumcision not made by hands — applying LXX idol-polemic vocabulary (*cheiropoiēta* — Isaiah 2:18 LXX) to the *sarx*-body
4. 3:10 — Genesis 1:26–27 LXX (*kat' eikona*) as the creation-ground for the renewal toward the Anointed as *eikōn*

*Wisdom of Solomon LXX dependency* is uniquely important: the *prōtotokos* hymn of 1:15–20 draws on a text that exists only in Greek, having no Hebrew original. The Colossian hymn is therefore compositionally impossible without LXX tradition, and specifically without the deuterocanonical Wisdom tradition that is part of the LXX canon but absent from the MT. This is the strongest single-text LXX-primacy argument in the Pauline corpus — the foundational Christological hymn of Colossians depends on a deuterocanonical LXX source.
