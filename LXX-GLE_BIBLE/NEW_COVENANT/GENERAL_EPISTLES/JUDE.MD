**JUDE IN THE CORPUS ARCHITECTURE**

Jude is the most forensically intense letter in the Catholic Epistles — 25 verses constituting the NT's most concentrated false-teacher judgment-anatomy, drawing on a remarkable range of sources: canonical LXX (Exodus / Numbers / Genesis / Isaiah), deuterocanonical LXX (Wisdom of Solomon / 1 Enoch / Testament of Moses / Sirach), and direct prophetic citation of non-canonical tradition (1 Enoch 1:9 in vv.14–15). The letter's structural logic is prosecutorial throughout: every paragraph from v.4 to v.16 advances the case against the infiltrators through accumulating evidence — OT judgment-precedents / behavioral profiling / cosmic-scale analogies / prophetic citation — before pivoting in v.17 to the community's positive formation. Jude is the NT's fullest deployment of the deuterocanonical LXX tradition as theological warrant — more sustained than any other NT letter in its use of non-Protocanonical sources — making it the CAE's most important single LXX-canon affirmation document.

The letter's CAE function is concentrated in five nodes: the *hapax paradotheisē tois hagiois pistei* — "faith once for all delivered to the holy ones" — of v.3, which is the most compact statement of the apostolic deposit's finality and completeness in the NT; the three OT judgment-precedents of vv.5–7 — the Exodus generation / the fallen angels / Sodom and Gomorrah — which are the most concentrated single-passage judgment-typology sequence in the NT; the *psychikoi / pneuma mē echontes* — "worldly people / devoid of the breath-of-life" — of v.19, which is the most precise *psychikos / pneumatikos* register-diagnostic in the NT outside 1 Corinthians 2:14–15; the Enoch citation of vv.14–15 — the only direct citation of 1 Enoch in the NT canon, establishing the letter's explicit use of the deuterocanonical tradition as prophetic warrant; and the closing doxology of vv.24–25 — the most structurally complete doxology in the Catholic Epistles and one of the most theologically dense in the NT.

**KEY LEXICAL DECISIONS**

*hapax* — "once for all": v.3 (*hapax paradotheisē tois hagiois pistei* — the faith once for all delivered to the holy ones). Single occurrence — the *hapax* vocabulary lock at its most ecclesiologically significant. The *hapax paradotheisē* — "once-for-all delivered" — is the *ephapax* / *hapax* atonement vocabulary (Hebrews 7:27 / 9:12 / 10:10 / 1 Peter 3:18 / Romans 6:10) applied not to the atonement-event but to the apostolic deposit — the *pistis* (faith / the body of belief) was delivered *hapax* — once / unrepeatable / complete / not requiring supplementation. **CAE apostolic-deposit finality principle:** the *hapax paradotheisē pistis* is to the community's doctrinal life what the *ephapax* atonement is to the community's soteriological life — unrepeatable / sufficient / the criterion against which all subsequent teaching is measured. **Cross-link: 2 Peter 1:19–21 (the *prophētikos logos* confirmed) + 2 Timothy 3:16 (*theopneustos* — breathed-out Scripture) + Jude v.3 (*hapax paradotheisē pistis*)** — three-witness apostolic-deposit finality confirmed. **LXX-primacy argument:** the *paradidōmi* — "deliver / hand over / transmit" — vocabulary is the LXX tradition-transmission term: Deuteronomy 32:7 LXX (*eperōtēson ton patera sou kai ananggelei soi* — ask your father and he will tell you / tradition-transmission from generation to generation) and Sirach 4:15 LXX (*ho hypakouōn autēs krinei ethna* — whoever obeys wisdom judges nations / wisdom as the transmitted tradition). The *hapax paradotheisē pistis* is the NT form of the LXX's covenantal-tradition transmission — the apostolic deposit as the new covenant's definitive transmitted *pistis*.

*agapē* — "covenant-love": vv.2 (*agapē* — multiplied to you), 12 (*agapais* — love feasts), 21 (*agapē tou theou* — the covenant-love of God). Three occurrences — the *agapē* vocabulary lock structurally deployed. The three uses span the entire letter: the opening multiplication-wish (v.2 — *agapē* as the community's foundational gift) / the *agapais* of v.12 (the community's covenant-love feast infiltrated by the false teachers — the *agapē* corrupted from within) / the closing imperative (v.21 — keep yourselves in the *agapē of God* — the Wisdom-register response to infiltration). **CAE *agapē*-threat analysis:** the letter is uniquely structured around the *agapē*'s vulnerability — the *agapapai* (love feasts — v.12) are the community's most intimate expression of covenant-love / the false teachers have infiltrated precisely this space (*en tais agapais hymōn syneuōchoumenoi* — feasting with you at your love feasts). The Folly register's most dangerous institutional form penetrates the Wisdom register's most intimate communal expression.

*v.12 CAE NOTE:* *houtoi eisin en tais agapais hymōn spilades syneuōchoumenoi aphobōs — heautous poimainontes* — "these are hidden reefs at your love feasts — as they feast with you without fear — shepherds feeding themselves." The *spilades* — "hidden reefs / spots / stains" — in the *agapai* is the most concrete false-teacher-in-community image in the NT: invisible dangers beneath the surface of the community's most intimate gathering. The *heautous poimainontes* — "shepherds feeding themselves" — is the precise inversion of the Ezekiel 34 LXX false-shepherd indictment. **CAE Chain 11 (*poimēn*) false-shepherd node:**

**Ezekiel 34:2–3 LXX (*ouai hoi poimenes tou Israēl... to gala katesthiete kai to erion periballesthe — to ischuron ou katischyete* — woe to the shepherds of Israel who feed themselves / eat the fat / clothe themselves with the wool but do not strengthen the weak) → Jude v.12 (*heautous poimainontes* — shepherds feeding themselves)**

The Jude false-shepherd profile is a direct Ezekiel 34 LXX echo — the self-feeding shepherds who use the *agapē* community for their own nourishment while giving nothing. Chain 11 receives its false-shepherd inversion node — the contrast with the *Archipoimenos* (1 Peter 5:4) and the *Poimēn ho kalos* (John 10:11) who lays down his life for the sheep is maximally sharp.

*pneuma* — "breath-of-life": vv.19 (*pneuma mē echontes* — devoid of the breath-of-life), 20 (*pneumati hagiō* — Holy Breath-of-life). Two structurally opposed occurrences — the Folly register's *pneuma*-absence (v.19) and the Wisdom register's *pneuma*-ground (v.20).

*v.19 CAE NOTE:* *houtoi eisin hoi apodiorizontes — psychikoi — pneuma mē echontes* — "it is these who cause divisions — worldly people (*psychikoi*) — devoid of the breath-of-life." The *psychikoi pneuma mē echontes* — "soul-people / natural-people devoid of the breath-of-life" — is the most precise register-diagnostic formula in the NT. **CAE Wisdom/Folly register pneumatological analysis:**

The *psychikos* — soul-person / natural-person — is the person operating at the level of the *psychē* alone — the volitional / affective / cognitive faculty without the *pneuma*'s orientation toward God. This is the 1 Corinthians 2:14 *psychikos anthrōpos* (*psychikos de anthrōpos ou dechetai ta tou pneumatos tou theou* — the natural person does not receive the things of the Breath-of-life of God) applied as the diagnostic of the false teachers. The *pneuma mē echontes* — "not having the breath-of-life" — is the most direct statement of the Folly register's defining pneumatological characteristic: the absence of the *pneuma* that is the Wisdom register's animating principle. **Two-witness lock: 1 Corinthians 2:14 (*psychikos*) + Jude v.19 (*psychikoi / pneuma mē echontes*)** — the register-diagnostic confirmed across two independent witnesses.

The *apodiorizontes* — "causing divisions / making separations" — is also significant: the *psychikoi* are the ones who *divide* the community — the Folly register's community-function is *schismatic* / the Wisdom register's is *koinōnial*. The false teachers who claim deeper spirituality are in fact the ones who *lack* the *pneuma* and *divide* the community — the precise inversion of their self-presentation.

*v.20 CAE NOTE:* *hymeis de agapētoi — tē hagiōtatē hymōn pistei epoikodomountes heautous — en pneumati hagiō proseuchomenoi* — "but you beloved — building yourselves up in your most holy faith — praying in the Holy Breath-of-life." The four-fold Wisdom-register formation sequence of vv.20–21 is the letter's positive theological core: (1) *tē pistei epoikodomountes* — building up in the faith (the *hapax paradotheisē pistis* of v.3 as the foundation) / (2) *en pneumati hagiō proseuchomenoi* — praying in the Holy Breath-of-life (the *pneuma*-prayer as the counter to the *pneuma mē echontes* of v.19) / (3) *en agapē theou tēreīte* — keeping in the covenant-love of God (the *agapē* as the Wisdom-register dwelling-space) / (4) *eleos... prosdechomenoi* — waiting for the mercy of the Lord Jesus the Anointed leading to everlasting life (the eschatological telos). **CAE Wisdom-register formation sequence:** faith-building / pneumatic-prayer / *agapē*-dwelling / *eleos*-waiting — the four dimensions of the Wisdom-register life under Folly-register pressure. The *en pneumati hagiō proseuchomenoi* directly contrasts with the *pneuma mē echontes* of the false teachers — the community's pneumatic-prayer is the precise counter to the false teachers' *pneuma*-absence.

*eleos* — "mercy / steadfast love": vv.2 (*eleos* — mercy multiplied), 21 (*eleos tou kyriou* — mercy of our Lord), 22 (*eleāte* — show mercy), 23 (*eleāte* — show mercy). Four occurrences — the *eleos* vocabulary lock at its pastoral and eschatological maximum. The four uses constitute a complete *eleos* arc: divine *eleos* wished for the community (v.2) / the Lord's *eleos* as the eschatological telos (v.21) / the community's enacted *eleos* toward doubters (v.22) / the community's fearful *eleos* toward those near the fire (v.23). **CAE Chain 10 (*eleos*) community-pastoral node:** the *eleos* of God (received — v.21) and the *eleos* of the community (enacted — vv.22–23) are the same *eleos* in two directions — the divine *eleos* received grounds the human *eleos* expressed. **LXX-primacy argument:** the *eleos* of vv.21–23 draws on Zechariah 3:2 LXX (*epitimēsai kyrios en soi ho eklegomenos Ierousalēm — ouk idou touto darbon exēspasmenos ek pyros* — is this not a brand plucked from the fire? — the *arpage ek pyros* / snatching from fire as the *eleos* enacted in urgent rescue) and Amos 4:11 LXX (*hōs daron exespasmenos ek pyros* — like a brand snatched from the fire). The *sōzete ek pyros harpazentes* — "save by snatching from fire" (v.23) is drawn from the LXX fire-rescue tradition — **LXX-primacy argument confirmed: Jude v.23 is a Zechariah 3:2 LXX + Amos 4:11 LXX composite echo.**

*sōtēria / Sōtēr* — "salvation / Savior": v.3 (*sōtērias* — our common salvation), v.25 (*Sōtēri* — to the only God our Savior). The *sōtēria* of v.3 is the common possession of the community — *hēmōn koinēs sōtērias* — "our common salvation" — the shared eschatological inheritance that the *hapax paradotheisē pistis* guards. The *Sōtēr* of v.25 — *monō theō Sōtēri hēmōn* — "to the only God our Savior" — closes the letter with the highest divine-identity doxology in the Catholic Epistles. **CAE *Sōtēr* vocabulary lock — divine-identity confirmation:**

**Isaiah 43:11 LXX (*egō eimi ho theos — ouk estin parex emou sōzōn* — I am God / there is no Savior beside me) → Jude v.25 (*monō theō Sōtēri hēmōn* — to the only God our Savior)**

The *monos* — "only" — combined with *theos* and *Sōtēr* is the most direct citation of the Isaiah 43:11 LXX exclusive-Savior predicate in the NT: there is only one *theos* / he is *Sōtēr* / all other claimed saviors are excluded. The doxology is addressed *dia Iēsou Christou tou kyriou hēmōn* — "through Jesus the Anointed our Lord" — establishing the trinitarian structure: the *monos theos Sōtēr* (the Father) glorified *through* Jesus the Anointed (the Son). The divine-identity chain: the Father is the *monos theos Sōtēr* / the Son is the mediating Lord through whom the doxology ascends / the *eleos* of the Lord Jesus (v.21) is the *eleos* of the *monos theos Sōtēr* expressed through the Son. **Cross-link: 1 Timothy 1:17 (*monō theō* — to the only God) + 1 Timothy 6:15–16 (King of kings / Lord of lords / alone immortal)** — the Jude doxology and the Pastoral doxologies are structurally parallel monotheistic-doxology forms.

*dikaiosynē / aiōnios* — "righteousness / everlasting": v.7 (*dikēn aiōniou pyros* — punishment of eternal fire), v.21 (*zōēn aiōnion* — everlasting life). Two *aiōnios* occurrences at the poles: *aiōniou pyros* — the Folly register's eschatological endpoint (v.7) / *zōēn aiōnion* — the Wisdom register's eschatological telos (v.21). **CAE *aiōnios* binary:** the same *aiōnios* (everlasting / age-long) qualifies both the Folly register's judgment (eternal fire) and the Wisdom register's inheritance (everlasting life) — the eternal dimension runs in both directions from the eschatological judgment. **Cross-link: 2 Thessalonians 1:9 (*aiōnion olethron* — everlasting destruction) + Matthew 25:46 (*aiōnion kolasin / aiōnion zōēn* — everlasting punishment / everlasting life)** — the *aiōnios* binary confirmed across three witnesses.

*psychikoi* — "worldly / natural-people": v.19 (*psychikoi pneuma mē echontes* — worldly people devoid of the breath-of-life). Single occurrence — but the most diagnostically precise use of the *psychikos* vocabulary lock in the NT outside 1 Corinthians 2:14. **CAE register-diagnostic vocabulary cross-reference:**

**1 Corinthians 2:14 (*psychikos anthrōpos ou dechetai ta tou pneumatos* — the natural person does not receive the things of the Breath-of-life) = James 3:15 (*sophia... psychikē* — earthly / unspiritual wisdom) = Jude v.19 (*psychikoi pneuma mē echontes*)**

Three independent witnesses using the *psychikos* vocabulary as the register-diagnostic for the Folly register's pneumatological condition — the absence of the *pneuma* that animates the Wisdom register. The Jude formulation is the most explicit — adding *pneuma mē echontes* as the direct explanation of the *psychikos* condition.

**STRUCTURAL CAE NODES**

**vv.1–3 — The salutation / the *hapax* deposit / the contention-call:**
The letter's compressed opening establishes three theological foundations in three verses: (1) the community's identity — *klētois* (called) / *ēgapēmenois* (beloved in God the Father) / *tetērēmenois* (kept for Jesus the Anointed) — the three perfect-passive participles describing the community's constituted status; (2) the triple greeting — *eleos kai eirēnē kai agapē* — mercy / peace / covenant-love — the only NT greeting combining all three; (3) the *hapax paradotheisē pistis* — the reason for writing is the necessity of defending the once-for-all-delivered faith.

**The three perfect-passive participles (v.1):** *klētois* (called — divine calling as the community's origin) / *ēgapēmenois en theō patri* (beloved in God the Father — the community's identity grounded in the Father's *agapē*) / *tetērēmenois Iēsou Christō* (kept for / by Jesus the Anointed — the community's security as the Anointed's preserving-work). **CAE trinitarian-election structure:** the three participles distribute the election across the three persons: the Father's *agapē* grounds identity / the Anointed's keeping secures it / the calling (implied: through the Holy Breath-of-life) initiates it. Cross-link: 1 Peter 1:2 (the trinitarian election formula) — Jude v.1 and 1 Peter 1:2 are the two NT trinitarian-election statements in the Catholic Epistles — both using triadic structures.

**The *hapax paradotheisē pistis* (v.3):** *epagōnizesthai tē hapax paradotheisē tois hagiois pistei* — "contend earnestly for the faith once for all delivered to the holy ones." The *epagōnizomai* — "contend earnestly / agonize for" — is the athletic / combat metaphor applied to doctrinal defense: the *pistis* is a contested possession that requires active defense. The *hapax* — once for all — establishes the deposit's closure: the *pistis* was delivered *once* / it is complete / it does not require the additions the false teachers bring. **CAE apostolic-deposit architecture:** the *hapax paradotheisē pistis* is the Jude form of the *parathēkē* (deposit — 1 Timothy 6:20 / 2 Timothy 1:14) — the entrusted treasure that must be guarded. The community's calling in Jude is not doctrinal innovation but faithful preservation and defense of the *hapax* delivered deposit.

**vv.5–7 — The three OT judgment-precedents:**
The most concentrated single-passage judgment-typology sequence in the NT — three precedents in three verses establishing the divine consistency-of-judgment principle:

*v.5 — The Exodus generation:* *hypomnēsai de hymas boulomia — eidotas hymas hapax panta — hoti kyrios laon ek gēs Aigyptou sōsas to deuteron tous mē pisteusantas apōlesen* — "the Lord who saved a people out of the land of Egypt afterward destroyed those who did not believe." The *hapax* reappears — "once fully knowing" — the community knew *hapax* / the false teachers cause forgetting. The Exodus generation was saved (*sōsas* — the *sōtēria* vocabulary) and then destroyed (*apōlesen*) for unbelief — the pattern: initial salvation does not guarantee final perseverance / unbelief produces judgment even within the covenant community. **LXX-primacy argument:** Numbers 14:26–35 LXX (*en tē erēmō tautē pesountai ta kōla hymōn* — your bodies will fall in this wilderness) — the Exodus judgment-narrative is LXX-shaped / the *tous mē pisteusantas* (those who did not believe) echoes Numbers 14:11 LXX (*heōs tinos ou pisteuousin moi* — how long will they not believe me?).

*v.6 — The fallen angels:* *angelous te tous mē tērēsantas tēn heautōn archēn alla apolipontas to idion oikētērion — eis krisin megalēs hēmeras desmois aidiois hypo zophon tetērēken* — "the angels who did not stay within their own domain but abandoned their proper dwelling — he has kept in eternal chains under gloomy darkness until the judgment of the great day." The fallen angels as judgment-precedent — the creatures who abandoned their *archē* (domain / beginning / proper order) and *oikētērion* (dwelling) are kept in *desmoi aidioi* (eternal chains). **LXX-primacy argument:** the fallen angels tradition draws on Genesis 6:1–4 LXX (*hoi huioi tou theou* — sons of God / the Nephilim tradition) and more directly on 1 Enoch 6–16 (the Watchers who descended and abandoned their heavenly station). **CAE canonical note:** the use of 1 Enoch as authoritative tradition confirms the letter's deployment of the full deuterocanonical / Second Temple tradition as theological warrant — Jude is the NT's most explicit user of the non-Protocanonical tradition as prophetic evidence.

*v.7 — Sodom and Gomorrah:* *hōs Sodoma kai Gomorra kai hai peri autas poleis ton homoion toutois tropon ekporneusasai kai apelthousai opisō sarkos heteras — prokeintai deigma pyros aiōniou dikēn hypeichouasai* — "Sodom and Gomorrah and the surrounding cities serve as an example by undergoing a punishment of eternal fire." The Sodom and Gomorrah precedent appears in all three judgment-sequences in the Catholic Epistles (2 Peter 2:6 / Jude v.7) — confirming its standard position in the NT judgment-typology tradition. **LXX-primacy argument:** Genesis 19:1–29 LXX is the narrative base / Isaiah 1:9–10 LXX (*hōs Sodoma egenēthēmen* — we became like Sodom) and Ezekiel 16:46–50 LXX (Sodom's sins enumerated) provide the prophetic-interpretation tradition. The *pyros aiōniou* — eternal fire — connects to Isaiah 33:14 LXX (*tis hypomeneī pyros kaiomenon aiōnion* — who can endure everlasting burning fire) — the LXX eschatological fire-judgment tradition.

**vv.8–16 — The false-teacher anatomy / the three woe-types / the Enoch citation:**
The most sustained single-letter false-teacher profile in the NT — combining behavioral analysis / OT woe-pattern / nature-metaphor / prophetic citation. The structure:

*Three woe-types (v.11):* the most compressed false-teacher typology in the NT — three OT figures as the Folly-register's paradigmatic forms:

(1) *hodō tou Kain eporeuthesan* — "walked in the way of Cain" — Cain as the paradigmatic Folly-register figure: the one whose offering was rejected (Genesis 4:3–5 LXX — the Folly-register worship that does not correspond to the divine requirement) / who murdered his brother out of envy (Genesis 4:8 LXX — the *misos* of the Folly register enacted as fratricide). **Cross-link: 1 John 3:12 (*hōsper Kain ek tou ponērou ēn* — Cain was of the evil one)**

(2) *tē planē tou Balaam misthō exechythesan* — "abandoned themselves to Balaam's error for profit" — Balaam as the paradigmatic Folly-register false prophet: the one who prophesied for gain / who led Israel into idolatry and immorality at Baal Peor (Numbers 22–24 / 25:1–3 / 31:16 LXX). **LXX-primacy argument:** Numbers 22:7/21 LXX (*ta manteia en tais chersin autōn* — the divination fees in their hands) — Balaam's profit-motivated prophecy is the LXX background for the Jude false-prophet-for-gain profile.

(3) *tē antilogia tou Kore apōlonto* — "perished in Korah's rebellion" — Korah as the paradigmatic Folly-register authority-challenger: Numbers 16:1–35 LXX (*Kore hyios Isaar* — Korah's rebellion against Moses and Aaron / the earth swallowing them). The three woe-types are progressive: Cain (*misos* / murder) / Balaam (profit-motivated false prophecy) / Korah (authority-rebellion) — the Folly register's three institutional forms: relational destruction / doctrinal corruption / hierarchical inversion.

*The five nature-metaphors (vv.12–13):* the most concentrated nature-metaphor cluster in the NT — five images of the false teacher's emptiness and judgment:

*spilades* (hidden reefs — invisible dangers beneath the surface)
*nephelai anudroi* (waterless clouds — the promise of refreshment / the delivery of nothing)
*dendra phthinnopōrina akarpa dis apothanontas* (fruitless autumn trees — twice dead / uprooted — the Folly register's double-death: dead in itself / uprooted from the Wisdom-register community)
*kymata agria tēs thalassēs epaphrizonta tas heautōn aischynas* (wild waves of the sea foaming up their own shame — the Folly register's speech as shameful foam / the *logoi ponēroi* of Diotrephes in their natural-world form)
*asteres planētai* (wandering stars — the most cosmologically resonant image — stars without fixed orbit / without the order of the *Logos*-governed cosmos / destined for *zophos tou skotous* — the gloom of darkness)

**LXX-primacy argument:** the nature-metaphor cluster draws on Wisdom of Solomon 5:9–14 LXX (*hōs skia* — like a shadow / *hōs karabos* — like a ship passing through water / *hōs aetos* — like an eagle / the wicked's transience described through nature-metaphors) — the deuterocanonical Wisdom tradition's nature-analogy for Folly-register emptiness is the compositional background for the Jude metaphor-cluster.

*The Enoch citation (vv.14–15):* the only direct citation of 1 Enoch in the NT canon — *prophēteusen de kai toutois hebdomos apo Adam Enōch legōn: idou ēlthen kyrios en myriasin hagiais autou — poiēsai krisin kata pantōn* — "it was also about these that Enoch — the seventh from Adam — prophesied saying: Behold the Lord comes with ten thousands of his holy ones — to execute judgment on all." The citation is from 1 Enoch 1:9 — a non-Protocanonical text introduced with *prophēteusen* (prophesied) — the highest possible citation-authority marker.

**CAE canonical-authority note:** Jude's citation of 1 Enoch as *prophēteusen* — prophetic utterance — is the NT's strongest endorsement of the deuterocanonical / Second Temple tradition as carrying prophetic authority. This does not establish 1 Enoch as canonical in the same sense as the Protocanonical books — but it does establish that the Holy Breath-of-life operating through Jude recognized authentic prophetic content in the 1 Enoch tradition. **LXX-primacy argument:** 1 Enoch exists in multiple manuscript traditions but the Greek version (Codex Panopolitanus / Chester Beatty Papyri) represents the tradition most accessible to the NT-era Greek-speaking community — Jude's citation of 1 Enoch is part of the broader Greek-LXX canonical tradition that the CAE's *textual witness hierarchy* (DSS → LXX → Aramaic Targums → Peshitta → Vulgate → MT) accommodates through the LXX's broad reception of Second Temple literature.

**Cross-link: Zechariah 14:5 LXX (*hēxei kyrios ho theos mou kai pantes hoi hagioi met' autou* — the Lord my God will come and all the holy ones with him) + 1 Thessalonians 3:13 (*en tē parousia tou kyriou hēmōn Iēsou meta pantōn tōn hagiōn autou* — at the coming of our Lord Jesus with all his holy ones)** — the *parousia*-with-holy-ones tradition confirmed across: Zechariah 14:5 LXX / 1 Enoch 1:9 / 1 Thessalonians 3:13 / Jude vv.14–15 — four-witness convergence.

**vv.17–23 — The community's positive formation / the four-fold Wisdom-register sequence:**
The letter's pivot from prosecutorial (vv.4–16) to pastoral (vv.17–23) — the community's formation response to the Folly-register infiltration. The four-fold formation sequence of vv.20–21:

*epoikodomountes heautous* — building yourselves up (in the *hapax paradotheisē pistis* — the faith as the foundation / the community as the building rising on it)
*proseuchomenoi en pneumati hagiō* — praying in the Holy Breath-of-life (the pneumatic-prayer as the counter to the *pneuma mē echontes*)
*tērēsate en agapē theou* — keeping in the covenant-love of God (the *agapē* as the dwelling-space / the Wisdom-register's relational ground)
*prosdechomenoi eleos* — waiting for the mercy of the Lord Jesus the Anointed leading to *zōēn aiōnion* (the eschatological telos / the *eleos* as the bridge from present *agapē*-dwelling to eschatological *zōē aiōnios*)

**CAE Wisdom-register formation under pressure:** the four-fold sequence is the letter's answer to the *psychikoi pneuma mē echontes* (v.19) — the counter to the Folly register's pneuma-absence / divisiveness / self-promotion is the community's pneumatic prayer / faith-building / *agapē*-dwelling / *eleos*-expectation. Each element directly counters a characteristic of the *psychikoi*: faith-building (vs. the *hapax* deposit abandoned) / pneumatic-prayer (vs. *pneuma mē echontes*) / *agapē*-keeping (vs. *agapais* infiltrated) / *eleos*-waiting (vs. *eleos* perverted into sensuality / v.4).

The *eleos* pastoral protocol of vv.22–23 — the three-tier mercy-ministry:
(1) *tous men diakrinomenous eleāte* — "have mercy on those who doubt" — the doubters receive *eleos* / pastoral engagement / gentle restoration
(2) *hous de sōzete ek pyros harpazontes* — "save others by snatching from fire" — the near-fallen receive urgent rescue / the *arpage ek pyros* (fire-snatching) from Zechariah 3:2 LXX
(3) *hous de eleāte en phobō — misoūntes kai ton apo tēs sarkos espilōmenon chitōna* — "to others show mercy with fear — hating even the garment stained by the flesh" — the deeply entangled receive cautious *eleos* / the *chiton espilōmenos* (garment stained by the flesh) is the defilement-contamination imagery from Zechariah 3:3–4 LXX (Joshua the high priest's filthy garments replaced with clean ones — the defilement removed by divine action). **LXX-primacy argument: Jude vv.22–23 is a Zechariah 3:2–4 LXX composite — the fire-snatching / the stained garment / the cautious mercy are all drawn from the Zechariah 3 LXX Joshua-the-high-priest restoration narrative.**

**vv.24–25 — The closing doxology:**
The most structurally complete doxology in the Catholic Epistles and one of the most theologically dense in the NT — four elements:

**The ability-declaration (v.24a):** *tō de dynamenō phylaxai hymas aptaistous* — "to him who is able to keep you from stumbling" — the *dynamenos phylaxai* (able to keep) is the divine preservation-assurance: the community that must *tēreīte en agapē theou* (keep itself in God's love — v.21) is also *kept* by the *dynamenos* God — the human keeping and the divine keeping are not competing but complementary. **Cross-link: John 10:28–29 (*ou mē apolōntai eis ton aiōna kai ouch harpasei tis auta ek tēs cheiros mou* — they will never perish and no one will snatch them out of my hand)** — the same *tēreō* / keeping-assurance of the Good Shepherd.

**The presentation-declaration (v.24b):** *kai stēsai katenōpion tēs doxēs autou amōmous en agalliāsei* — "and to present you blameless before the presence of his glory with great joy." The *amōmous* — "without blemish / blameless" — is the sacrificial-purity vocabulary of the LXX (Leviticus 1:3/10 LXX — the *amōmon* offering / the same vocabulary as 1 Peter 1:19 *amōmos kai aspilos*). The community is presented to God as the perfect offering — *amōmous* — at the eschatological presentation. **CAE Chain 1 (*hilastērion*) eschatological-presentation node:** the *hilasmos* (1 John 2:2) accomplished by the *amnos amōmos* (1 Peter 1:19 — the blameless lamb) produces the community that is presented *amōmous* (blameless) before the divine glory. **LXX-primacy argument:** *stēsai... amōmous* — "present blameless" — echoes Ephesians 5:27 LXX-shaped (*hina parastēsē... mē echousan spillon ē rhytida... all' hina ē hagia kai amōmos* — to present the community without spot or wrinkle / holy and blameless) — the eschatological presentation vocabulary is LXX sacrificial-purity shaped.

**The monotheistic doxology (v.25):** *monō theō Sōtēri hēmōn dia Iēsou Christou tou kyriou hēmōn doxa — megalōsynē — kratos — kai exousia pro pantos tou aiōnos kai nyn kai eis pantas tous aiōnas — amēn* — "to the only God our Savior through Jesus the Anointed our Lord be glory — majesty — dominion — and authority — before all time and now and forever — amen." The four-attribute doxology: *doxa* (glory) / *megalōsynē* (majesty) / *kratos* (dominion / might) / *exousia* (authority) — the most comprehensive attribute-doxology in the Catholic Epistles. **LXX-primacy argument:** the *megalōsynē* — "majesty" — is the LXX divine-transcendence term: Deuteronomy 32:3 LXX (*dote megalōsynēn tō theō hēmōn* — ascribe greatness to our God) / Psalm 144:3 LXX (*tēs megalōsynēs autou ouk estin peras* — his greatness has no end) / 1 Chronicles 29:11 LXX (*soi kyrie hē megalōsynē kai hē dynamis* — yours Lord is the greatness and the power). The *kratos* and *exousia* are Revelation doxology-vocabulary (Revelation 1:6 / 5:13 / 7:12) — **cross-link: Jude v.25 doxology and Revelation doxologies are constructed from the same LXX divine-attribute tradition.**

The temporal frame: *pro pantos tou aiōnos kai nyn kai eis pantas tous aiōnas* — "before all time and now and forever" — the most complete temporal-scope doxology in the NT: past (*pro pantos tou aiōnos* — before all the ages) / present (*nyn* — now) / future (*eis pantas tous aiōnas* — into all the ages). The divine glory / majesty / dominion / authority is not contingent on the present age's events but extends across the entire temporal spectrum. **CAE eschatological-ground:** the doxology that closes Jude is the theological answer to the false teachers' infiltration — the *monos theos Sōtēr* whose glory spans all ages is the ground of the community's confidence against the Folly register's temporary intrusion.
