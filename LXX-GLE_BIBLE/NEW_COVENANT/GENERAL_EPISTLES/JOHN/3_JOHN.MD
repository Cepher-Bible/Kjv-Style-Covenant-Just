**3 JOHN — GLE v2.2 CAE NOTES**

---

**TEXT BASE:** RP2026 Byzantine Majority Text

**VERSE COUNT:** 15 verses / 1 chapter

---

**3 JOHN IN THE CORPUS ARCHITECTURE**

3 John is the most personal and situationally concrete letter in the NT canon — 15 verses addressed to a named individual (Gaius) concerning named individuals (Diotrephes / Demetrius) in a specific community conflict. Its apparent simplicity is the simplicity of the CAE categories fully instantiated in a real pastoral situation: the letter is not theological exposition but theological *embodiment* — the *alētheia / agapē* binary of 2 John enacted in the contrasting figures of Gaius (Wisdom-register hospitality / *agapē* for the brothers / walking in truth) and Diotrephes (Folly-register self-promotion / refusal of the brothers / rejection of apostolic authority). The letter functions as the CAE's most concrete case study in register-differentiation — the same community situation producing two diametrically opposed responses, each perfectly expressing its register.

The letter's CAE function is concentrated in four nodes: the *psychē* / *sōma* health-wish of v.2 — the only explicit *psychē* / physical-health parallel in the NT, establishing the *psychē*'s Wisdom-register health as the standard against which physical wellbeing is measured; the *alētheia* testimony-structure of vv.3–8 — the most personal *alētheia*-walking affirmation in the Catholic Epistles; the Diotrephes analysis of vv.9–10 — the most precise single-individual Folly-register profile in the NT; and the *martyria tēs alētheias autēs* — "testimony of the truth itself" — of v.12, which is the most compressed statement of the *alētheia*'s self-witnessing capacity in the canon.

---

**KEY LEXICAL DECISIONS**

*alētheia* — "truth": vv.1 (*alētheia* — love in truth), 3 (*alētheia / alētheia* — testified to your truth / walking in truth), 4 (*alētheia* — walking in the truth), 8 (*alētheia* — fellow workers for the truth), 12 (*alētheias* — from the truth itself / *alēthēs* — our testimony is true). Six occurrences across 15 verses — matching 2 John's density and extending the *alētheia* arc from the corporate (2 John — the elect lady and her children) to the personal (3 John — Gaius the individual). The six uses:

*v.1* — *agapō en alētheia* — love in truth — the identical opening as 2 John v.1 — the elder's *agapē* for the recipient grounded in *alētheia*
*v.3* — *sou tē alētheia* — your truth — the *alētheia* as a personal possession / Gaius's own *alētheia* testified to by the brothers
*v.3* — *kathōs su en alētheia peripateis* — as you are walking in truth — the *alētheia* as the behavioral domain of Gaius's conduct
*v.4* — *en alētheia peripatountas* — walking in the truth — the elder's supreme joy: his children's *alētheia*-walking
*v.8* — *synergoi ginōmetha tē alētheia* — fellow workers for the truth — the *alētheia* as the corporate mission requiring co-labor
*v.12* — *apo tēs alētheias autēs* — from the truth itself — the *alētheia* as an independent witness that bears testimony about Demetrius

**CAE Chain 10 (*alētheia*) — personal-*alētheia* node:**
The 3 John *alētheia* arc moves the category from the cosmic and communal to the intensely personal — Gaius's *alētheia* is testified to by brothers (v.3) / the elder's greatest joy is his children's *alētheia*-walking (v.4) / the *alētheia* itself testifies about Demetrius (v.12). The *alētheia* in 3 John functions as a self-witnessing reality — it both governs conduct (*peripatein* — vv.3–4) and bears testimony (*martyrein* — v.12) — the *alētheia* is simultaneously the standard of the Wisdom-register life and the agent of its recognition. **Cross-link: 1 John 5:6 (*to pneuma estin hē alētheia* — the Breath-of-life is the truth)** — the *alētheia* that testifies about Demetrius in v.12 is the *pneuma tēs alētheias* (1 John 4:6 / John 14:17) bearing witness through the community's discernment.

*v.12 CAE NOTE — The self-witnessing alētheia:* *Dēmētriō memartyerētai hypo pantōn kai hypo autēs tēs alētheias — kai hēmeis de martyroumen kai oidate hoti hē martyria hēmōn alēthēs estin* — "Demetrius has received a good testimony from everyone and from the truth itself — we also add our testimony and you know that our testimony is true." The three-witness structure for Demetrius: (1) *hypo pantōn* — from everyone / universal community testimony; (2) *hypo autēs tēs alētheias* — from the truth itself / the *alētheia* as an independent witness; (3) *hēmeis de martyroumen* — we also testify / the apostolic witness. **CAE three-witness confirmation principle (Deuteronomy 19:15 LXX *epi stomatos dyo martyrōn kai epi stomatos triōn martyrōn stathēsetai pan rhēma* — by the mouth of two or three witnesses every word shall stand):** the Demetrius commendation is a three-witness *martyria* satisfying the LXX evidentiary standard. **LXX-primacy argument:** the three-witness structure of v.12 is constructed on the Deuteronomy 19:15 LXX evidentiary foundation — the same foundation as the 1 John 5:7–8 three-witness (*pneuma / hydōr / haima*) testimony sequence. **The *alētheia autē* as independent witness** is the most theologically significant element — the *alētheia* itself can bear testimony because it is the *pneuma tēs alētheias* (1 John 4:6) whose testimony is self-validating (*to pneuma estin hē alētheia* — 1 John 5:6).

*agapē* — "covenant-love": vv.1 (*agapō*), 6 (*agapēn*). Two occurrences — the *agapē* vocabulary lock at its most personally applied. The *agapō* of v.1 is the elder's covenant-love for Gaius *en alētheia* — the same opening formula as 2 John v.1, confirming the structural parallelism of the two letters. The *agapēn* of v.6 is Gaius's own covenant-love testified to before the called-out assembly — the *agapē* verified by the travelers' report. **CAE *agapē* evidence-structure:** in 3 John the *agapē* is not defined or commanded (as in 2 John) but *witnessed to* — Gaius's *agapē* is known because it was enacted in hospitality toward the traveling brothers and reported to the community. The *agapē* is epistemically accessible through its behavioral expressions — consistent with 1 John 3:18 (*mē agapōmen logō... alla en ergō kai alētheia* — let us not love in word but in deed and truth).

*psychē* — "soul": v.2 (*kathōs euodoutai sou hē psychē* — as it goes well with your soul). Single occurrence — the *psychē* vocabulary lock at its most revealing. The *euodoutai sou hē psychē* — "your soul prospers / goes well" — is the standard against which the physical wellbeing is measured: *peri pantōn euodousthai kai hygiainein — kathōs euodoutai sou hē psychē* — "that all may go well with you and that you may be in good health — as it goes well with your soul." The *psychē*'s wellbeing is assumed to be known and is the baseline against which physical wellbeing is wished. **CAE anthropological priority:** the *psychē* (volitional / affective faculty — the register-orientation center of the person) is epistemically prior to the *sōma* — the elder knows the state of Gaius's *psychē* through the testimony of the brothers (v.3) before he prays for physical health. The Wisdom-register *psychē* (walking in truth / covenant-love toward brothers) is the ground from which physical wellbeing is wished. **Cross-link: 1 Peter 2:11 (*apechesthai... sarkikōn epithymiōn... kata tēs psychēs* — fleshly passions war against the soul) and 1 Peter 4:19 (*tithesthai tas psychas* — entrust their souls)** — the *psychē* as the register-battleground receives here its most explicit expression as the measure of the person's ultimate wellbeing. **LXX-primacy argument:** the *psychē*-health / body-health parallel echoes 3 Maccabees 2:33 LXX (*psychēs te kai sōmatos* — of soul and body) and Wisdom of Solomon 1:4 LXX (*eis kakotechnon psychēn ouk eiseleusetai sophia* — wisdom will not enter a soul that plots evil) — the LXX wisdom tradition's integration of *psychē*-health and wisdom-possession as the primary measure of the person's wellbeing.

*ekklēsia* — "called-out assembly": vv.6 (*ekklēsias* — before the called-out assembly), 9 (*ekklēsia* — I have written to the called-out assembly), 10 (*ekklēsias* — puts them out of the called-out assembly). Three occurrences — the *ekklēsia* vocabulary lock at its most institutional and contentious. The three uses trace the letter's central conflict: Gaius's *agapē* testified before the *ekklēsia* (v.6) / the elder's letter to the *ekklēsia* rejected by Diotrephes (v.9) / Diotrephes expelling brothers from the *ekklēsia* (v.10). The *ekklēsia* is simultaneously the space of Gaius's *agapē*-testimony / the institution Diotrephes has captured / the community from which the brothers are expelled. **CAE Chain 9 (*skēnē / naos*) community-governance node:** the *ekklēsia* as the covenant community is vulnerable to the Folly register's institutional capture — Diotrephes represents the Folly register's *ekklēsia*-form: using the institutional structure of the community against the community's *alētheia*-and-*agapē* mission.

*martyria / martyrein* — "testimony / testify": vv.3 (*emartyrēsan* — testified), 6 (*emartyrēsan* — testified), 12 (*memartyerētai / martyroumen / alēthēs*). Four occurrences — the *martyria* vocabulary lock at high density. The letter is structured as a series of testimonies: the brothers' testimony to Gaius's *alētheia*-walking (v.3) / the brothers' testimony to Gaius's *agapē* before the assembly (v.6) / the triple testimony for Demetrius (v.12). The *martyria* structure of 3 John mirrors the *martyria* structure of 1 John 5:7–9 — testimony is the epistemological instrument through which the *alētheia* is known and confirmed in community.

**STRUCTURAL CAE NODES**

**vv.1–4 — The *psychē*-health prayer / *alētheia*-walking joy:**
The letter's opening prayer (v.2) and thanksgiving (vv.3–4) constitute the most personal expression of the elder's pastoral relationship in the Catholic Epistles. The *peri pantōn euodousthai kai hygiainein* — "that all may go well with you and that you may be in good health" — is the NT's only explicit prayer for physical health in correspondence, and its qualification — *kathōs euodoutai sou hē psychē* — immediately establishes the priority order: the *psychē*'s wellbeing is the known quantity / the body's wellbeing is the wished quantity. The inversion of the normal Folly-register priority (body first / soul secondary or ignored) is the letter's most quietly profound theological statement.

The *meizona toutōn ouk echō charan* — "I have no greater joy than this" (v.4) — the elder's supreme joy being his children's *alētheia*-walking is the pastoral form of the divine joy over the community's Wisdom-register conduct. **LXX-primacy argument:** the *chara* (joy) over children walking in the covenant-way echoes Proverbs 23:15–16 LXX (*huie — ean sophē genētai hē kardia sou — chairō kai egō epi soi* — my son if your heart becomes wise I also rejoice) and Proverbs 10:1 LXX (*huios sophos euphranei patera* — a wise son gladdens his father). The elder's joy over Gaius's *alētheia*-walking is the NT form of the LXX wisdom-parent's joy over the child's wisdom-conduct. **CAE wisdom-parent joy node:** the elder as the spiritual parent (v.4 — *tekna* — my children) whose supreme joy (*meizona... ouk echō charan*) is the *alētheia*-walking of his children — the Johannine community structured as a wisdom-family in which the elder's joy is constituted by the community's register-conduct.

**vv.5–8 — Gaius / the traveling brothers / *synergoi tē alētheia*:**
The letter's positive example — Gaius as the Wisdom-register practitioner whose conduct is: *pistons* (faithful — v.5) / *eis tous adelphous kai touto xenous* (toward brothers even strangers — v.5) / *axíōs tou theou* (worthy of God — v.6) / *synergos tē alētheia* (fellow worker for the truth — v.8). The four characteristics constitute a complete Wisdom-register profile: faithful in deeds / *agapē* extended even to strangers / conduct worthy of God / co-laborer with the *alētheia*-mission.

*v.7 CAE NOTE:* *hyper gar tou onomatos exēlthon mēden lambanontes apo tōn ethnōn* — "for they have gone out for the sake of the Name — accepting nothing from the Gentiles." The *hyper tou onomatos* — "for the sake of the Name" — is the most compressed mission-description in the NT: the traveling missionaries go out not for personal gain but *hyper tou onomatos* — for the Name of the Lord Jesus the Anointed. The refusal to accept support from the *ethnōn* (Gentiles / unbelievers) preserves the mission's integrity — the *alētheia*-proclamation cannot be subsidized by those who do not share the *alētheia*. **CAE mission-integrity principle:** the Name-mission is self-funded through the *agapē* of the Wisdom-register community (Gaius's hospitality) — not dependent on the patronage of those outside the community. **LXX-primacy argument:** *hyper tou onomatos* — "for the sake of the Name" — echoes the LXX *Shem YHWH* tradition: Psalm 44:6 LXX (*en tō onomati sou* — in your Name) / Isaiah 48:9 LXX (*heneken tou emou onomatos* — for the sake of my Name) — the Name as the ground and motivation of the covenant mission. The refusal of Gentile support echoes Ezra 4:3 LXX (*ou hymin kai hēmin* — you have nothing to do with us in building) — the separation of the covenant-community's work from the patronage of those outside the covenant.

*v.8 CAE NOTE:* *hēmeis oun opheilomen hypolambanein tous toioutous hina synergoi ginōmetha tē alētheia* — "therefore we ought to support such people that we may be fellow workers for the truth." The *synergoi tē alētheia* — "fellow workers for the truth" — is the most precise mission-identity statement in the Catholic Epistles: the community's hospitality toward the missionaries makes the community itself *synergoi* (co-workers / fellow laborers) in the *alētheia*-mission. The *synergoi* vocabulary — fellow workers — is the Pauline mission-vocabulary (Romans 16:3/9/21 / 1 Corinthians 3:9 / Philippians 2:25 / 4:3 / Philemon v.1/24) appearing here in the Johannine corpus: the Pauline *synergos* mission-structure and the Johannine *alētheia*-mission converge in v.8. **CAE Chain 10 (*alētheia*) mission-participation node:** hospitality toward *alētheia*-missionaries = participation in the *alētheia*-mission = *synergoi tē alētheia*. The community's ordinary practice of hospitality is transformed by its function into apostolic co-labor.

**vv.9–10 — Diotrephes / the Folly-register *ekklēsia*-profile:**
The most precise single-individual Folly-register profile in the NT — Diotrephes identified through five behavioral characteristics:

(1) *philoprōteuōn autōn* — "likes to put himself first / loves to be first among them" — the *philoprōteuōn* is a NT hapax — appearing only here. The compound *philo-prōteuōn* — *philos* (loving) + *prōtos* (first) — the one who *loves firstness* / whose affective orientation is toward primacy. **CAE Folly-register pride analysis:** the *philoprōteuōn* is the institutional form of the *alazoneia tou biou* (pride of life — 1 John 2:16) — the Folly register's social-primacy desire enacted within the *ekklēsia*. **LXX-primacy argument:** the *philoprōteuōn* echoes Proverbs 13:10 LXX (*kakos meta hybreōs ergazetai kaka* — evil with arrogance produces evil) and Sirach 10:6–18 LXX (*archē hyperēphanias anthrōpou aphistasthai apo kyriou* — the beginning of human arrogance is to forsake the Lord) — the LXX wisdom tradition's analysis of pride-as-apostasy as the background for Diotrephes's *philoprōteuōn* condition. **Cross-link: Mark 10:43–44 (*ho thelōn prōtos einai estai pantōn doulos* — whoever would be first must be servant of all)** — Diotrephes's *philoprōteuōn* is the direct inversion of the Anointed's servant-first teaching.

(2) *ouk epiginōskei hēmas* — "does not acknowledge our authority" — the rejection of apostolic authority. The *epiginōskō* — know / acknowledge / recognize — in the sense of recognizing legitimate authority. Diotrephes refuses the *epignōsis* of the elder's apostolic standing. **CAE Folly-register authority-inversion:** the *philoprōteuōn* person cannot acknowledge authority above himself — to do so would threaten his primacy. The Folly register's institutional expression requires the rejection of all authority not originating from itself.

(3) *logois ponērois phlyarōn hēmas* — "talking wicked nonsense against us" — the *phlyareō* (talking nonsense / spreading empty slander) combined with *logois ponērois* (wicked words). **CAE *logos* analysis:** the Diotrephes *logos* is *ponēros* (wicked / evil) and *phlyaria* (empty nonsense / idle talk) — the Folly register's speech is simultaneously morally corrupt (*ponēros*) and epistemically empty (*phlyaria*). The inversion of the Wisdom-register *logos*: the *logos tēs alētheias* (word of truth — James 1:18 / 2 Timothy 2:15) versus the *logoi ponēroi* (wicked words) — the *davar* principle (spoken word as enacted reality) inverted: the Folly-register *logos* enacts not reality but its distortion.

(4) *mē dechomenos tous adelphous* — "refuses to welcome the brothers" — the direct inversion of Gaius's hospitality. Where Gaius receives strangers *axíōs tou theou* (v.6) Diotrephes refuses the brothers entirely. **CAE *agapē*-inversion:** Diotrephes's refusal of the brothers is the *misos* (hatred) of 1 John 3:15 enacted as an institutional policy — the one who does not love his brother abides in death (1 John 3:14) / Diotrephes's institutional death is expressed as the refusal of *agapē*-hospitality.

(5) *tous boulomenous kōluei kai ek tēs ekklēsias ekballei* — "stops those who want to welcome them and puts them out of the called-out assembly" — the weaponization of *ekklēsia*-authority against *agapē*-practitioners. Diotrephes has inverted the *ekklēsia*'s purpose: the community constituted by *alētheia* and *agapē* (2 John v.3) is being used to exclude those who practice *agapē* toward the *alētheia*-missionaries. **CAE institutional-inversion analysis:** the Folly register in its most dangerous institutional form does not merely abstain from *agapē* but actively persecutes *agapē*-practice / it does not merely deny the *alētheia* but expels those who affirm it. Diotrephes is the 3 John form of the 2 Thessalonians 2:4 *antinomian* who sits in the *naon tou theou* — he has captured the institution from within and redirected its authority against its own purpose.

**v.11 CAE NOTE — The register-imperative after the Folly-profile:** *agapēte — mē mimou to kakon alla to agathon* — "beloved do not imitate evil but imitate good." The *mimou* — "imitate" — is the pastoral application: the Diotrephes-profile is not merely described for information but presented as the *kakon* (evil) that must not be imitated. The *mimēsis* principle: the community forms itself by what it imitates — the Wisdom-register is formed by imitating *to agathon* / the Folly-register by imitating *to kakon*. **CAE register-formation by imitation:** *ho agathopoiōn ek tou theou estin — ho kakopoiōn ouch heōraken ton theon* — "whoever does good is from God — whoever does evil has not seen God." The two register-identities stated in their most minimal form: doing good = being from God / doing evil = not having seen God. The behavioral criterion as the register-diagnostic — consistent with 1 John 3:10 (*en toutō phanera estin ta tekna tou theou kai ta tekna tou diabolou* — by this it is evident who are the children of God and who are the children of the devil).

**vv.13–15 — The closing: face-to-face / peace / friends:**
The letter's closing mirrors 2 John vv.12–13 almost exactly: *polla eichon grapsai soi all' ou thelō dia melan kai kalamou soi grapsai — elpizō de eutheōs se idein kai stoma pros stoma lalēsomen* — "I had much to write to you but I would rather not write with pen and ink — I hope to see you soon and we will talk face to face." The *stoma pros stoma lalēsōmen* — "we will talk face to face / mouth to mouth" — is the most intimate communication-form: direct personal presence transcending written correspondence. **LXX-primacy argument:** *stoma pros stoma* — "mouth to mouth" — is the LXX formula for the most direct divine communication: Numbers 12:8 LXX (*stoma kata stoma lalēsō autō* — mouth to mouth I will speak with him — God's description of his communication with Moses). The elder's desire to speak *stoma pros stoma* with Gaius echoes the LXX's highest communication-register — the direct personal word transcending mediated transmission.

The closing *eirēnē soi* — "peace to you" — and *aspazontai se hoi philoi* — "the friends greet you" — with *aspazou tous philous kat' onoma* — "greet the friends each by name" — is the most personally named closing in the NT. The *philoi* — "friends" — as the designation for the community members echoes John 15:13–15 (*humas de eirēka philous* — I have called you friends — the Anointed's designation of the disciples as *philoi*). The *kat' onoma* — "each by name" — is the pastoral concreteness of the *Poimēn* who calls his own sheep *kat' onoma* (John 10:3 — *ta idia probata kalei kat' onoma* — he calls his own sheep by name). **CAE Chain 11 (*poimēn*) personal-naming node:** the elder's instruction to greet each friend *kat' onoma* is the undershepherd's enactment of the Chief Shepherd's *kat' onoma* calling — the pastoral community knows its members by name because the Shepherd knows his sheep by name.

**LXX-PRIMACY ARGUMENTS IN 3 JOHN**

Five LXX dependency points:

1. v.2 — Wisdom of Solomon 1:4 LXX + 3 Maccabees 2:33 LXX (*psychē* health as the primary measure of wellbeing — the deuterocanonical wisdom tradition's *psychē* / body priority order)
2. v.4 — Proverbs 23:15–16 LXX + Proverbs 10:1 LXX (*chara* of the wisdom-parent over the child's wisdom-conduct — the elder's supreme joy over *alētheia*-walking as the LXX wisdom-parent joy pattern)
3. v.7 — Psalm 44:6 LXX + Isaiah 48:9 LXX (*hyper tou onomatos* — for the sake of the Name — the LXX *Shem YHWH* mission-motivation tradition)
4. v.12 — Deuteronomy 19:15 LXX (*epi stomatos dyo martyrōn kai triōn* — two or three witnesses) — the three-witness Demetrius commendation structured on the LXX evidentiary standard
5. vv.13–15 — Numbers 12:8 LXX (*stoma kata stoma* — mouth to mouth — the highest LXX direct-communication register applied to the elder's desire for personal presence with Gaius) + John 10:3 LXX-background (*kat' onoma* — by name — the Shepherd's personal knowledge enacted in the community's greeting practice)

**CHAIN STATUS AFTER 3 JOHN**

All 15 chains remain closed. 3 John adds:

- *Alētheia* self-witness: **3 John v.12** — Chain 10 (*alētheia*) self-witnessing node. *Hypo autēs tēs alētheias* — from the truth itself — the *alētheia* as an independent witness. Cross-confirmed: 1 John 5:6 (*to pneuma estin hē alētheia*) — the truth's self-witness is the *pneuma tēs alētheias* bearing testimony through community discernment. Three-witness Demetrius commendation satisfies Deuteronomy 19:15 LXX evidentiary standard
- *Synergoi tē alētheia*: **3 John v.8** — Chain 10 (*alētheia*) mission-participation node. Hospitality toward *alētheia*-missionaries = co-labor in the *alētheia*-mission. The community's ordinary practice transformed into apostolic participation through its function
- Diotrephes Folly-register profile: **3 John vv.9–10** — the most precise single-individual Folly-register profile in the NT. Five behavioral characteristics: *philoprōteuōn* / authority-rejection / *logoi ponēroi* / hospitality-refusal / *ekklēsia*-weaponization. The Folly register's institutional form: captures community structures and redirects their authority against the community's *alētheia / agapē* mission
- *Kat' onoma* pastoral naming: **3 John v.15** — Chain 11 (*poimēn*) personal-naming node. The undershepherd's *kat' onoma* greeting enacting the Chief Shepherd's *kat' onoma* calling (John 10:3). The pastoral community knows its members by name because the Shepherd knows his sheep by name
- *Psychē* health as primary measure: **3 John v.2** — CAE anthropological priority node. The *psychē*'s Wisdom-register wellbeing as the known baseline / the body's health as the wished quantity. The Wisdom-register *psychē* (walking in truth / covenant-love) is the register-faculty whose health determines the person's ultimate wellbeing — the most explicit statement of *psychē*-priority in the NT

**THE JOHANNINE LETTERS — COMPLETE**

With 3 John the three Johannine letters are complete: 1 John / 2 John / 3 John. The three letters form a theological unit moving from the universal to the particular:

- **1 John** — the cosmic and communal theology: *ho theos agapē estin* / *hilasmos* / *homoioi autō esometha* / the *pneuma tēs alētheias / planēs* binary / *alēthinos theos kai zōē aiōnios* — the fullest Johannine theology in the NT outside the Fourth Gospel
- **2 John** — the corporate community application: the *alētheia / agapē* binary as community-governance / the *en sarki* confession as doctrinal boundary / the *didachē tou Christou* as the condition of possessing the Father and Son / the *oikia* as the Wisdom-register space requiring boundary-maintenance
- **3 John** — the personal individual application: Gaius as the Wisdom-register individual / Diotrephes as the Folly-register institutional profile / the *psychē* health as the primary measure / the *synergoi tē alētheia* as the mission-participation identity / the *kat' onoma* pastoral naming as the community's personal knowledge

The three letters together constitute the NT's most complete *alētheia / agapē* theology — the two categories defined cosmically (1 John) / applied corporately (2 John) / enacted personally (3 John).
