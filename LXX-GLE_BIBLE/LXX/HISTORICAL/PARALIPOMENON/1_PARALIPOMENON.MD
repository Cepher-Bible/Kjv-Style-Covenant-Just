## CRITICAL TEXTUAL NOTES — LXX 1 PARALIPOMENON

**The title:** LXX calls this book *Paraleipomenōn* = of the things left over / of the omitted things. The Greek title accurately describes the book's function: it supplements and reframes what the Kingdoms books recorded, with particular emphasis on the cultic and Levitical arrangements and the Davidic covenant. The MT title *Divrei HaYamim* = words/matters of the days is the annalistic framing; the LXX title is the theological framing.

**Genealogies (chapters 1–9):** The GLE renders the genealogical chains in the naming-only format standard in the LXX. The genealogies are not inert — they are the theological architecture of the book: tracing the covenant-line from Adam through Abraham through Judah through David to the post-exilic community. The genealogy is the narrative of election at the level of bare names.

**Chapter 21 vs. 2 Samuel 24:** The critical divergence: 1 Paralipomenon 21:1 reads *ho satanas* = Satan stood against Israel and incited David. The parallel in 2 Kingdoms 24:1 reads: the Lord incited David. The LXX 1 Paralipomenon uses the *satanas* reading throughout — consistent with the later OT theology in which *ha-satan* becomes an independent adversarial agent (cf. Job 1–2 / Zech 3:1–2). The GLE follows the LXX text in both places without harmonization.

## CAE NODES — 1 PARALIPOMENON

### 1 Paralipomenon 3:17–19 — Jeconiah / Zerubbabel — the exile-line — CAE NODE

**3:17–19** — "And the sons of Jeconiah the captive: Shealtiel his son... And the sons of Pedaiah: Zerubbabel and Shimei."

The genealogy traces from Jeconiah = Jehoiachin (the king lifted from prison at the close of 4 Kingdoms) through the Babylonian exile to Zerubbabel the post-exilic governor. This is the Davidic covenant-line running *through* the exile — not around it.

**Matthew 1:12–13** — "And after the deportation to Babylon: Jeconiah was the father of Shealtiel and Shealtiel the father of Zerubbabel." Matthew's Messianic genealogy reproduces the 1 Paralipomenon 3:17–19 chain exactly — the exile-line is the Messianic line. The covenant survives Babylon through the captive king's descendants.

**Haggai 2:23** — "On that day declares the Lord of hosts I will take you O Zerubbabel my servant the son of Shealtiel declares the Lord and make you like a signet ring — for I have chosen you." Zerubbabel (the post-exilic heir of the Davidic line) is the signet ring — the covenant-seal of the dynasty. The 1 Paralipomenon genealogy provides the chain that makes Haggai 2:23 coherent.

---

### 1 Paralipomenon 16:8–36 — David's song / *diathēkē aiōnios* — CAE PRIMARY NODE

**16:15–17** — "Remember his covenant forever — the word that he commanded for a thousand generations — the covenant that he made with Abraham — his sworn promise to Isaac — which he confirmed to Jacob as a statute — to Israel as an everlasting covenant (*diathēkēn aiōnion*)."

**16:34** — "Oh give thanks to the Lord for he is good — for his steadfast love (*eleos*) endures forever."

The Ark-arrival song (drawn from Psalms 105, 96, and 106 in the MT but here presented as a unified composition by the Chronicler) is the first full deployment of the *diathēkē aiōnios* = everlasting covenant formula in the Paralipomenon.

The covenant chain: Abraham → Isaac → Jacob → the *diathēkē aiōnios* declared to Israel. The covenant is not a historical arrangement susceptible to historical cancellation — it is *aiōnios* = belonging to the Everlasting. The *eleos* = steadfast love refrain (16:34, 41) is the covenant's characteristic attribute: the love that persists beyond all human failure.

**Psalm 105:8–10** — the direct source-text: "He remembers his covenant forever — the word that he commanded for a thousand generations — the covenant he made with Abraham — his sworn promise to Isaac — which he confirmed to Jacob as a statute — to Israel as an everlasting covenant." The Chronicler's use of the Psalm as liturgical song at the Ark's arrival makes the *diathēkē aiōnios* vocabulary a cultic declaration, not merely a historical fact.

**Luke 1:72–73** — Zechariah's Benedictus: "to show the mercy promised to our fathers and to remember his holy covenant — the oath that he swore to our father Abraham." The *diathēkē aiōnios* of 1 Paralipomenon 16:17 / Psalm 105:10 is what Zechariah declares fulfilled in the birth of John: God has *remembered* the covenant sworn to Abraham — the *aiōnios* covenant has now reached its appointed moment.

**Hebrews 13:20** — "Now may the God of peace who brought again from the dead our Lord Jesus — the great shepherd of the sheep — by the blood of the eternal covenant (*diathēkēs aiōniou*)." The *diathēkē aiōnios* of 16:17 / Psalm 105 / 2 Sam 23:5 is the covenant sealed in Christ's blood and confirmed by his resurrection. The Hebrews formula is the culmination of the *diathēkē aiōnios* chain running from the Patriarchs through David through the exile to the resurrection.

---

### 1 Paralipomenon 17:11–14 — The Davidic covenant / the Son — CAE PRIMARY NODE

**17:11–14** — "When your days are fulfilled to walk with your fathers I will raise up your offspring after you — one of your own sons — and I will establish his kingdom. He shall build a house for me and I will establish his throne forever. I will be to him a father and he shall be to me a son — I will not take my steadfast love from him as I took it from him who was before you. But I will confirm him in my house and in my kingdom forever and his throne shall be established forever."

***egō esomai autō eis patera kai autos estai moi eis huion***

= I will be to him for a father and he will be to me for a son.

The 1 Paralipomenon 17 Davidic covenant is the parallel to 2 Kingdoms 7 with one critical difference: the 1 Paralipomenon version reads *in my kingdom* (not *in his kingdom*) — the throne is the Lord's throne, not merely a human royal dynasty. The 1 Paralipomenon reading is theologically sharper: Solomon sits on the throne of the Lord (29:23 — *Solomon sat on the throne of the Lord as king*).

**Psalm 2:7** — "You are my Son — today I have begotten you." The Psalm 2 *huios mou sy* = my son are you citation activates the Father-Son declaration of 1 Paralipomenon 17:13: God declares the Davidic king to be his son.

**2 Samuel 7:14 / 1 Paralipomenon 17:13** — the parallel texts. The 1 Paralipomenon version drops the discipline-clause of 2 Samuel 7:14 (*if he commits iniquity I will discipline him*) — the Chronicler's omission is theologically pointed: the 1 Paralipomenon version of the Davidic covenant focuses on the permanent faithfulness without the discipline-condition. The Chronicler is presenting the covenant in its ideal/fulfillment register.

**Hebrews 1:5** — "For to which of the angels did God ever say: You are my Son — today I have begotten you? Or again: I will be to him a father and he shall be to me a son?" Hebrews 1:5 cites both Psalm 2:7 and 2 Samuel 7:14 (= 1 Paralipomenon 17:13) as proof that the Son is superior to angels. The *egō esomai autō eis patera* formula of the Davidic covenant is cited as the definitive divine declaration that the Messiah is the Son, not merely a servant.

**Revelation 21:7** — "The one who conquers will have this heritage and I will be his God and he will be my son." The Davidic covenant Father-Son formula is applied in Revelation 21 to *all* who conquer — the Messianic inheritance extends to the whole redeemed community. The 1 Paralipomenon 17:13 formula becomes the eschatological adoption formula.

### 1 Paralipomenon 21:1 — *ho satanas* — CAE CRITICAL TEXTUAL NODE

**21:1** — "Then Satan (*ho satanas*) stood against Israel and incited David to number Israel."

***kai estē ho satanas en tō Israēl kai epesesen ton Dauid tou arithmēsai ton Israēl***

= and Satan stood in Israel and incited David to number Israel.

The divergence from 2 Kingdoms 24:1 (*the Lord incited David*) represents the development of the *ha-satan* theology across the OT tradition. In Job 1–2 and Zechariah 3:1–2 *ha-satan* = the adversary functions as an agent within the divine council — an adversarial accuser/tester operating under divine permission. The Chronicler's use of *ho satanas* in 21:1 reflects this developed theology: the incitement of David comes through an adversarial agent permitted by God, not directly from God.

The GLE does not harmonize these two texts. Both are rendered from the LXX. The theological resolution: both are true at different levels — ultimately the Lord permits what the adversary incites (the Job 1–2 model). Neither text is in error; they address different levels of causation.

**Job 1:12** — "The Lord said to Satan: Behold all that he has is in your hand — only against him do not stretch out your hand." The divine-permission model of Satan's activity is the hermeneutical key to the 1 Paralipomenon / 2 Kingdoms divergence.

**1 Peter 5:8** — "Your adversary the devil (*diabolos*) prowls around like a roaring lion seeking someone to devour." The NT *diabolos* = devil / slanderer is the Greek equivalent of *ho satanas* = the adversary. The 1 Paralipomenon 21:1 use of *ho satanas* is part of the OT chain that leads to the fully developed NT adversarial-agent theology.

### 1 Paralipomenon 22:8–9 — David the man of blood / Solomon the man of rest — CAE NODE

**22:8–9** — "The word of the Lord came to me saying: You have shed much blood and have waged great wars. You shall not build a house to my name because you have shed so much blood before me on the earth. Look — a son shall be born to you who shall be a man of rest (*anēr anapauseōs*). I will give him rest from all his surrounding enemies. For his name shall be Solomon and I will give peace and quiet to Israel in his days."

***anēr anapauseōs*** = a man of rest. The *anapausis* = rest vocabulary applied to Solomon is the Paralipomenon's deployment of the Sabbath-rest theology at the royal level: Solomon = the one in whom the long-sought rest is provisionally achieved. The Temple is built in a period of *anapausis* = rest — which is the condition for the divine dwelling.

**Hebrews 4:1–11** — the extended treatment of *katapausis* = rest in Hebrews draws on the Joshua-rest (Josh 21:44) and the Psalm 95 rest-warning but the Solomon-rest is the historical embodiment of the Joshua-rest typology: the king of peace who builds the house of God in a land at rest. Hebrews argues that neither Joshua nor Solomon achieved the *final* rest — the true rest remains.

**Matthew 11:28–29** — "Come to me all who labor and are heavy laden and I will give you rest (*anapausin*). Take my yoke upon you and learn from me for I am gentle and lowly in heart and you will find rest (*anapausin*) for your souls." Jesus offers the *anapausis* that Solomon's reign provisionally enacted: the true man of rest who gives rest to the heavy-laden. The *anēr anapauseōs* of 1 Paralipomenon 22:9 is the OT type of the *anapausin* Jesus offers.

### 1 Paralipomenon 28:9 — *the Lord searches all hearts* — CAE PRIMARY NODE

**28:9** — "And you Solomon my son know the God of your father and serve him with a whole heart and with a willing soul — for the Lord searches all hearts (*kardiōn exeraunā*) and understands every plan and thought. If you seek him he will be found by you — but if you forsake him he will cast you off forever."

***hoti pasas kardias exeraunā Kyrios kai pasan enthymēsin ginōskei***

= for the Lord searches all hearts and knows every intention. The *exeraunaō* = searches-thoroughly is the comprehensive divine knowledge of the *kardia* = heart. This is the Paralipomenon's most concentrated statement of divine omniscience of the interior person.

**Jeremiah 17:10** — "I the Lord search the heart (*kardia*) and test the mind to give every man according to his ways." Jeremiah's *eraunaō kardian* = searching of the heart is the parallel formulation — both texts establish the foundational principle of divine cardiac knowledge.

**Psalm 139:1–4** — "O Lord you have searched me (*exēraunēsas*) and known me... you discern my thoughts from afar... even before a word is on my tongue you know it altogether." The Psalm 139 divine-search language uses the same *exeraunaō* root as 1 Paralipomenon 28:9 — the omniscient searching of the divine gaze into the human interior.

**Revelation 2:23** — "And all the called-out assemblies will know that I am he who searches mind and heart (*eraunōn nephrous kai kardias*)." The risen Christ's self-declaration to the assembly at Thyatira uses the *eraunaō kardias* formula of 1 Paralipomenon 28:9 / Jeremiah 17:10 applied to Christ himself: the divine prerogative of heart-searching is claimed by Jesus. The OT *Lord searches all hearts* becomes the NT *I search hearts* — Christological declaration.

### 1 Paralipomenon 29:10–16 — David's final prayer / all comes from you — CAE PRIMARY NODE

**29:11** — "Yours O Lord is the greatness and the power and the glory and the victory and the majesty — for all that is in the heavens and in the earth is yours. Yours is the kingdom O Lord and you are exalted as head above all."

**29:14** — "But who am I and what is my people that we should be able thus to offer willingly? For all things come from you and of your own have we given you."

**29:15** — "For we are strangers before you and sojourners as all our fathers were. Our days on the earth are like a shadow and there is no abiding."

***hoti sou ta panta kai ek tōn sōn dedōkamen soi***

= for all things are yours and from what is yours we have given to you.

David's final doxology before his death is the theological summit of 1 Paralipomenon. The *sou ta panta* = all things are yours is the most concentrated OT statement of the divine ownership of all things — and therefore of the impossibility of human giving that is not first receiving. The offering is not a human gift to God; it is the return of what was always God's.

**Matthew 6:13** — "For yours is the kingdom and the power and the glory forever." The doxological close of the Lord's Prayer (in the liturgical tradition) is the direct citation of 1 Paralipomenon 29:11 — *sou estin hē basileia kai hē dynamis kai hē doxa eis tous aiōnas*. David's prayer before the Temple offering becomes the close of Jesus's prayer for the disciples. The doxology of 1 Paralipomenon 29:11 is the ground of the Lord's Prayer's close.

**Romans 11:36** — "For from him and through him and to him are all things. To him be glory forever. Amen." Paul's *ex autou kai di' autou kai eis auton ta panta* = from him and through him and to him are all things is the Trinitarian expansion of David's *sou ta panta kai ek tōn sōn dedōkamen soi*. The 1 Paralipomenon 29:14 all-from-God principle is Paul's ground for the doxology closing the theological argument of Romans 9–11.

**The sojourner-shadow theology** (29:15): David's *paroikoi kai parepidēmoi* = strangers and sojourners language is the Patriarchal theology applied to kingship. David the king uses the vocabulary of Abraham the wandering Aramean (Deut 26:5) — the greatest king in Israel's history declares himself a sojourner before God.

**Hebrews 11:13** — "These all died in faith not having received the things promised but having seen them and greeted them from afar and having acknowledged that they were strangers and exiles (*xenoi kai parepidēmoi*) on the earth." The Hebrews 11 cloud-of-witnesses theology uses the *parepidēmoi* = sojourner vocabulary of 1 Paralipomenon 29:15 — David's prayer becomes the articulation of the Patriarchal sojourner-faith that Hebrews celebrates.

### 1 Paralipomenon 29:23 — Solomon sat on the throne of the Lord — CAE CRITICAL NODE

**29:23** — "Then Solomon sat on the throne of the Lord (*epi thronou Kyriou*) as king in place of David his father."

***kai ekathisen Salōmōn epi thronou Kyriou eis basilea***

= and Solomon sat on the throne of the Lord as king. The 1 Paralipomenon reading *throne of the Lord* (not merely *throne of David*) is unique to the Paralipomenon and is the most theologically elevated description of the Davidic kingship in the entire OT. The king of Israel sits not on a human throne but on the throne of the Lord — the Davidic covenant is a theocratic arrangement in which human kingship is the expression of divine kingship.

**Psalm 2:6** — "As for me I have set my King on Zion my holy hill." The Lord's declaration that his king is installed on Zion is the Psalm 2 equivalent of the Paralipomenon throne-of-the-Lord formula.

**Revelation 3:21** — "The one who conquers I will grant him to sit with me on my throne as I also conquered and sat down with my Father on his throne." The eschatological throne-sharing of Revelation 3:21 is the ultimate development of the 1 Paralipomenon 29:23 throne-of-the-Lord formula: Solomon sat on the throne of the Lord as the provisional Davidic fulfillment; the conquering believer will sit on the throne of the Son who sits on the throne of the Father. The *thronos Kyriou* of 1 Paralipomenon reaches its eschatological form in the Revelation throne-promise.

**Matthew 19:28** — "Truly I say to you — in the new world when the Son of Man will sit on his glorious throne you who have followed me will also sit on twelve thrones judging the twelve tribes of Israel." Jesus's promise of throne-participation to the Twelve activates the 1 Paralipomenon throne-of-the-Lord theology: the Davidic thrones extend to the community of the Messiah.

## STRUCTURAL NOTE — 1 PARALIPOMENON AS THEOLOGICAL GENEALOGY AND DAVIDIC ARCHITECTURE

1 Paralipomenon has two movements:

**Movement 1 — The genealogies and the establishment of David (1–12):**
From Adam to Israel / the twelve tribes / the Davidic line through exile (3:17–19) / Saul's death and its theological verdict (10:13–14) / David's installation as king.

**Movement 2 — David's preparation of the Temple and its worship (13–29):**
The Ark brought to Jerusalem / the Davidic covenant given to Nathan / David's organization of the Levites and musicians / the Temple-plan delivered to Solomon / David's final doxology and death.

**The theological program of the Chronicler** is consistently Davidic-covenantal and cultic: the question is not *what happened* (the Kingdoms books record that) but *what it means* — specifically: (a) the covenant with David is permanent and theocratic (the throne is the Lord's throne); (b) the proper worship of Israel is the Levitical-musical pattern established by David; (c) the Temple is not Solomon's achievement but David's preparation completed by Solomon.

**The Exile is not the end** — the genealogy runs through Jeconiah the captive (3:17) to Zerubbabel (3:19). The covenant-line survives Babylon. 1 Paralipomenon opens with Adam and traces the line that will eventually reach Jesus (Matthew 1). The genealogies are not antiquarian lists — they are the architecture of the promise.

**Romans 11:29** — "For the gifts and the calling of God are irrevocable." Paul's irrevocability-declaration is the NT theological equivalent of the Paralipomenon's entire project: the covenant made with the Patriarchs / confirmed to David / surviving the exile / is irrevocable because God is its guarantor. The *diathēkē aiōnios* = everlasting covenant of 16:17 and the *thronos Kyriou* = throne of the Lord of 29:23 together declare: this covenant does not end.
