## STRUCTURAL NOTE — WISDOM OF SOLOMON

Wisdom of Solomon is the most theologically sophisticated book in the LXX deuterocanon — written in Greek by a Hellenistic Jewish author, probably in Alexandria, in the first century BC. It shows no Hebrew original. The book falls into three movements:

- **Chapters 1–6:** The fate of the righteous and the impious — the eschatology of the two ways
- **Chapters 7–9:** Solomon's praise of Wisdom and his prayer for her
- **Chapters 10–19:** Wisdom in history — a retelling of the Exodus plagues in theological depth

The book is the primary LXX source for NT Christology's Wisdom vocabulary — the *hypostasis* of Wisdom in chapter 7 directly shapes the Christological hymns of Colossians 1, Hebrews 1, and John 1.

## CAE NODES — WISDOM OF SOLOMON

### Wisdom 1:13–15 — *ho theos thanaton ouk epoiēsen* / God did not make death — CAE NODE

**1:13–15** — "For God did not make death — nor does he delight in the destruction of the living. For he created all things that they might exist... for righteousness is immortal."

***ho theos thanaton ouk epoiēsen*** = God did not make death. The theological statement that death is not part of the original creation — it is an intrusion. **Romans 5:12** — "Therefore just as through one man sin entered the world and death through sin — and so death spread to all men because all sinned." Death as the consequence of sin — not a divine creation.

**1:14** — "he created all things that they might exist" — *ektisen gar eis to einai ta panta* = he created all things for existence. **John 1:3** — "all things through him came to be — and without him not one thing came to be that has come to be." Creation as fundamentally oriented toward existence/being.

---

### Wisdom 2:12–20 — The Righteous Sufferer — CAE PRIMARY NODE

**2:12–20** — "Let us lie in wait for the righteous man — for he is inconvenient to us... He professes to have knowledge of God — and names himself child of the Lord... For if the righteous man is the son of God — he will help him — and will deliver him from the hand of his adversaries. Let us test him with insult and torture... Let us condemn him to a shameful death — for according to his own words he will be protected."

This is the single most direct OT parallel to the Passion narrative in all of Scripture — predating the NT by roughly a century:

**Matthew 27:40–43** — "If you are the Son of God — come down from the cross... He trusted in God — let God deliver him now if he wants him — for he said: I am the Son of God." The mockers at the cross reproduce the program of Wisdom 2 almost verbatim. The *pais theou* = child/servant of God (2:13), the *huios theou* = son of God (2:18), the shameful death, the test of whether God will rescue — all fire in the Passion narrative.

**2:23–24** — "For God created man for incorruption — and made him an image of his own eternity. But through the envy of the devil death entered the world — and those of his portion experience it."

***di' ou phthonon diabolou thanatos eisēlthen eis ton kosmon*** = through the envy of the devil death entered the world. The *diabolos* = devil as the agent of death's entry — the first explicit statement of this theology in Jewish literature. **Hebrews 2:14** — "so that through death he might destroy the one who has the power of death — that is the devil (*ton... kyrieūonta tou thanatou toutestin ton diabolon*)." The engine rendered "through the envy of the devil death entered the world" — the *diabolos* vocabulary preserved.

---

### Wisdom 3:1–9 — *psychai de dikaiōn en cheiri theou* / the souls of the righteous are in the hand of God — CAE PRIMARY NODE

**3:1–9** — "But the souls of the righteous are in the hand of God — and no torment will touch them. In the eyes of the foolish they seemed to have died — and their departure was reckoned as affliction... but they are in peace. For even if in the sight of men they were punished — their hope is full of immortality. And having been chastened a little they will be greatly rewarded — for God tested them and found them worthy of himself. As gold in the furnace he proved them — and as a whole burnt offering he accepted them."

**Romans 8:18** — "For I consider that the sufferings of the present time are not worth comparing with the glory that is to be revealed to us." The Wisdom 3 theology: apparent punishment is testing — and the reward exceeds the suffering.

**1 Peter 1:7** — "so that the tested genuineness of your faith — more precious than gold that perishes though tested by fire — may be found to result in praise and glory and honor at the revelation of Jesus Christ." The gold-in-furnace metaphor of Wisdom 3:6.

**3:7** — "And in the time of their visitation they will shine out — and will run as sparks through stubble." ***en kairō episkopēs autōn analampsousi*** = in the time of their visitation they will shine out. *Episkopē* = visitation/oversight — the divine coming to act. **Matthew 13:43** — "Then the righteous will shine like the sun in the kingdom of their Father." The shining-of-the-righteous eschatology.

### Wisdom 5:17–22 — The Armor of God — CAE PRIMARY NODE

**5:17–20** — "He will take his zeal as his whole armor — and will arm all creation to repel his enemies — he will put on righteousness as a breastplate — and will wear impartial judgment as a helmet — he will take holiness as an invincible shield — and will sharpen his dire wrath as a sword."

**Ephesians 6:13–17** — "Therefore take up the whole armor of God... Stand therefore — having girded your loins with truth — and having put on the breastplate of righteousness... take up the shield of faith... take the helmet of salvation — and the sword of the breath-of-life." Paul's *panoplia theou* = full armor of God in Ephesians 6 is built directly on Wisdom 5:17–20. The *thorax dikaiosynēs* = breastplate of righteousness, the *perikepapalaia* = helmet, the *aspis* = shield — all in Wisdom before Paul.

**Isaiah 59:17** is the other OT source for the armor imagery — but Wisdom 5 is closer to the Ephesians 6 elaboration.

The engine rendered each element at precision: "righteousness as a breastplate," "judgment as a helmet," "holiness as an invincible shield," "wrath as a sword."

### Wisdom 7:22–8:1 — The Attributes of Wisdom — CAE PRIMARY NODE

**7:22–26** — "For in her is a breath-of-life intelligent — holy — unique — manifold — subtle — mobile — clear — unpolluted — distinct — beyond harm — loving good — keen — irresistible — beneficent — philanthropic — steadfast — sure — free from care — all-powerful — overseeing all — and penetrating through all breath-of-life however intelligent — pure — and most subtle. For wisdom is more mobile than any motion — and because of her purity she pervades and penetrates all things. For she is the breath of the power of God — and a pure emanation of the glory of the Almighty — therefore nothing defiled gains entrance into her. For she is a reflection of eternal light — and a spotless mirror of the working of God — and an image of his goodness."

This is the most concentrated theological description of personified Wisdom in all ancient literature. Its NT import:

**7:25** — *atmis gar estin tēs tou theou dynameōs* = she is the breath of the power of God — *aporroia... tēs tou Pantokratoros doxēs eilikrinēs* = a pure emanation of the glory of the Almighty.

**Hebrews 1:3** — "who being the radiance of his glory (*apaugasma tēs doxēs*) and the exact representation of his nature (*charaktēr tēs hypostaseōs autou*)." The *apaugasma* = radiance/reflection vocabulary of Hebrews 1:3 draws directly on Wisdom 7:25–26. The author of Hebrews applies the Wisdom *aporroia/apaugasma* = emanation/reflection terminology directly to the Son.

**7:26** — *apaugasma gar estin phōtos aidiou* = she is a reflection/radiance of eternal light — *esoptron akēlidōton tēs tou theou energeias* = a spotless mirror of the working of God — *eikōn tēs agathōtētos autou* = an image of his goodness. The *eikōn* = image vocabulary. **Colossians 1:15** — "who is the *eikōn* of the invisible God." The Colossians application of the Wisdom *eikōn* to the Son.

**7:27** — "Though she is one — she can do all things — and while remaining in herself she renews all things — and in every generation passing into holy souls — she makes them friends of God and prophets." The *mone en heautē* = remaining in herself while pervading all — the divine *apatheia* and immanence together. **John 1:14** — "the *Logos* became flesh and dwelt among us." The Wisdom who passes into holy souls → the Logos who takes flesh.

The twenty-one attributes of Wisdom's *pneuma* (7:22–23) are the most elaborate pneumatological description in the LXX — the engine rendered all twenty-one in sequence.

### Wisdom 9:1–2 — *ho poiēsas ta panta en logō sou* / who made all things by your word — CAE NODE

**9:1–2** — "O God of the fathers and Lord of mercy — who made all things by your word — and by your wisdom formed man."

***ho poiēsas ta panta en logō sou*** = who made all things by your word. The *logos* = word as the instrument of creation. **John 1:1–3** — "In the beginning was the *Logos*... all things through him came to be." For in this tent we groan — longing to put on our heavenly dwelling." The *skēnos* = tent vocabulary. **2 Corinthians 5:4** — "for we who are in this tent groan under our burden." Paul's *skēnos barountai* = burdened in the tent echoes Wisdom 9:15's *sōma barynei*.

### Wisdom 11:20 — *panta metrō kai arithmō kai stathmō diataxas* / you arranged all things by measure and number and weight — CAE NODE

**11:20** — "But you have arranged all things by measure and number and weight."

***alla panta metrō kai arithmō kai stathmō diataxas*** = but all things you arranged by measure and number and weight. This is one of the most cited verses in the history of natural theology and Christian philosophy — Augustine, Aquinas, and the entire medieval tradition of mathematical cosmology cite it. The *metron kai arithmos kai stathmos* = measure, number, and weight as the structure of creation — the rational-mathematical order of reality as a gift of divine wisdom.

### Wisdom 18:14–16 — *omnipotent logos* leaping from heaven — CAE PRIMARY NODE

**18:14–16** — "For while gentle silence enveloped all things — and night in its swift course was now half gone — your all-powerful word leaped from heaven — from the royal throne — a stern warrior — into the midst of the land that was doomed — carrying your unambiguous commandment as a sharp sword — and stood and filled all things with death — and reached to heaven while standing on the earth."

***ho pantodynamos sou logos ap' ouranou... epēdēsen*** = your all-powerful word leaped from heaven. The *logos* = word as divine warrior leaping from the heavenly throne — the Passover night/midnight coming of the word that fills all things with death and reaches from earth to heaven.

**John 1:14** — "and the *Logos* became flesh and dwelt among us." The *logos* who leaps from heaven in Wisdom 18 → the *Logos* who becomes flesh in John 1. The divine warrior-word who fills all things becomes the incarnate Son.

**Hebrews 4:12** — "For the word (*logos*) of God is living and active and sharper than any two-edged sword." The *logos* carrying "a sharp sword" (*rhomphaia*) of Wisdom 18:15 → the *logos* sharper than a two-edged sword of Hebrews 4:12.

**Revelation 19:11–15** — the rider on the white horse — "his name is called the Word (*ho Logos*) of God... and from his mouth goes a sharp sword (*rhomphaia*)." The Wisdom 18 imagery of the divine word as warrior with a sword fires directly in Revelation 19.

The engine rendered "your all-powerful word leaped from heaven — from the royal throne — a stern warrior" — the full military imagery preserved.

## VOCABULARY DECISIONS — WISDOM OF SOLOMON SYSTEMATIC

### *Athanasia* — immortality

The dominant eschatological word of Wisdom — *athanasia* = immortality — the deathlessness that is the righteous person's hope (1:15, 3:4, 4:1, 6:18–19, 8:13, 8:17, 15:3). **1 Corinthians 15:53–54** — "this mortal must put on immortality (*athanasian*) — then will come to pass the saying that is written: Death was swallowed up in victory." The engine rendered "immortality" throughout — the *athanasia* vocabulary preserved.

### *Pais theou* / *huios theou* — child of God / son of God

**2:13** — *paidon kyriou heauton einai legei* = he names himself child of the Lord. **2:18** — *ei gar estin ho dikaios huios theou* = if the righteous man is the son of God. The *pais* = child/servant and *huios* = son vocabulary for the righteous sufferer — both rendered at full precision. The *pais theou* = child of God / servant of God vocabulary connects to Isaiah 52–53 (*pais* as the Servant).

### *Pneuma* — breath-of-life

The *pneuma sophias* = breath-of-life of wisdom (7:7) — the *pneuma* as the animating divine gift pervading creation (1:7: "the breath-of-life of the Lord has filled the world"). The engine rendered "breath-of-life" consistently — the twenty-one attributes of Wisdom's *pneuma* in 7:22–23 all rendered with the "breath-of-life" register.

### *Logos* — word

The *logos* as creative instrument (9:1), as divine warrior (18:14–16). Rendered "word" throughout — the *logos* vocabulary maintained at precision for the Johannine resonance.

### *Eikōn* — image

**7:26** — *eikōn tēs agathōtētos autou* = image of his goodness — Wisdom as *eikōn* of God. Rendered "image" — the *eikōn* = likeness/representation vocabulary that Colossians 1:15 applies to the Son.

### *Apaugasma* — reflection / radiance

**7:26** — *apaugasma gar estin phōtos aidiou* = she is a reflection of eternal light. *Apaugasma* = radiance, outshining, reflection — the light-from-light vocabulary. Rendered "reflection" here — with note that **Hebrews 1:3** uses the same word for the Son as *apaugasma tēs doxēs* = the radiance of the glory.

### *Diabolos* — devil

**2:24** — *di' phthonon diabolou thanatos eisēlthen eis ton kosmon* = through the envy of the devil death entered the world. Rendered "devil" — the *diabolos* = accuser/slanderer as the agent of death's entry into creation. The engine maintained "devil" — not "adversary" or "satan" — the LXX *diabolos* vocabulary preserved.
