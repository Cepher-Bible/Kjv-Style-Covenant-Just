\v 1 In the eighth month in the second year of Darius the word of the Lord came to Zechariah the son of Berechiah the son of Iddo the prophet saying:
\v 2 The Lord was exceedingly angry with your fathers.
\v 3 And you will say to them: Thus says the Lord Almighty — return to me says the Lord Almighty and I will return to you says the Lord Almighty.
\v 4 And do not be as your fathers to whom the former prophets proclaimed saying: Thus says the Lord Almighty — return now from your evil ways and from your evil practices. And they did not hear and they did not give heed to me says the Lord.
\v 5 Your fathers — where are they? And the prophets — will they live into the age?
\v 6 But my words and my ordinances — as many as I commanded my servants the prophets — did they not overtake your fathers? And they returned and said: As the Lord Almighty purposed to do to us according to our ways and according to our practices so he has done to us.
\p
\v 7 On the twenty-fourth day of the eleventh month — this is the month of Shebat — in the second year of Darius the word of the Lord came to Zechariah the son of Berechiah the son of Iddo the prophet saying:
\v 8 I saw in the night and look — a man riding upon a red horse and he was standing among the shaded mountains — and behind him red horses and dappled and white and variegated.
\v 9 And I said: What are these my lord? And the angel who was speaking in me said to me: I will show you what these are.
\v 10 And the man who was standing among the shaded mountains answered and said: These are those whom the Lord sent out to pass through the earth.
\v 11 And they answered the angel of the Lord who was standing among the shaded mountains and said: We passed through all the earth and look — all the earth is inhabited and at rest.
\v 12 And the angel of the Lord answered and said: O Lord Almighty — until when will you not have mercy upon Jerusalem and the cities of Judah which you overlooked these seventy years?
\v 13 And the Lord answered the angel who was speaking in me with good words and comforting words.
\v 14 And the angel who was speaking in me said to me: Cry out saying: Thus says the Lord Almighty — I was zealous for Jerusalem and for Zion with great zeal.
\v 15 And with great wrath I am angry at the nations that are at ease — because I was angry a little and they added to the evil.
\v 16 Therefore thus says the Lord — I will return to Jerusalem with compassion and my house will be built in it says the Lord Almighty and a measuring-cord will be stretched out over Jerusalem.
\v 17 And yet cry out saying: Thus says the Lord Almighty — yet the cities will overflow with good things and the Lord will yet have mercy upon Zion and will yet choose Jerusalem.
\p
\v 18 And I lifted up my eyes and saw and look — four horns.
\v 19 And I said to the angel who was speaking in me: What are these? And he said to me: These are the horns that scattered Judah and Israel and Jerusalem.
\v 20 And the Lord showed me four smiths.
\v 21 And I said: What are these coming to do? And he said: These are the horns that scattered Judah and Israel and no man lifted his head — and these came to file them down — and the four horns that lifted themselves up against the land of Judah to scatter it.

\v 13 Let all flesh be in awe before the face of the Lord — because he has been stirred up out of his holy cloud.

\v 2 And the Lord said to the Accuser: The Lord rebuke you O Accuser — and the Lord who chose Jerusalem rebuke you. Is this one not a brand snatched from fire?
\v 3 Now Joshua was clothed in filthy garments and was standing before the face of the angel.

\v 9 Because look — the stone which I set before the face of Joshua — upon one stone are seven eyes — look I am cutting its engraving says the Lord Almighty and I will remove the lawless deed of that land in one day.
\v 10 In that day says the Lord Almighty each of you will invite his neighbor under the vine and under the fig tree.

\v 4 And I answered and said to the angel who was speaking in me saying: What are these things my lord?
\v 5 And the angel who was speaking in me answered and said to me: Do you not know what these are? And I said: No my lord.

\v 7 Who are you O great mountain before Zerubbabel — for a level-place? And he will bring out the top-stone with shouts of grace grace to it.
\v 8 And the word of the Lord came to me saying:
\v 9 The hands of Zerubbabel laid the foundation of this house and his hands will complete it — and you will know that the Lord Almighty has sent me to you.
\v 10 Because who has despised the day of small things? And they will rejoice and see the plumb-stone in the hand of Zerubbabel — these seven are the eyes of the Lord — they are looking over all the earth.
\v 11 And I answered and said to him: What are these two olive trees on the right of the lampstand and on its left?
\v 12 And I answered a second time and said to him: What are the two olive branches — the ones in the hands of the two golden pipes that pour out the gold from themselves?
\v 13 And he said to me: Do you not know what these are? And I said: No my lord.
\v 14 And he said: These are the two sons of fatness who stand beside the Lord of all the earth.

\v 4 And I will bring it out says the Lord Almighty and it will enter into the house of the thief and into the house of the one who swears falsely by my name — and it will lodge in the middle of his house and will consume it and its timbers and its stones.

\v 6 And I said: What is it? And he said: This is the measuring-vessel going out. And he said: This is their lawless deed in all the earth.
\v 7 And look — a leaden cover was lifted up — and one woman was sitting in the middle of the measuring-vessel.
\v 8 And he said: This is Lawlessness. And he threw her into the middle of the measuring-vessel and threw the leaden weight upon its mouth.
\v 9 And I lifted up my eyes and saw and look — two women coming out and wind in their wings — and they had wings like the wings of a hoopoe — and they lifted up the measuring-vessel between the earth and the heaven.
\v 10 And I said to the angel who was speaking in me: Where are they carrying the measuring-vessel?
\v 11 And he said to me: To build for it a house in the land of Babylon — and to prepare it — and they will set it there upon its own base.

\v 9 And the word of the Lord came to me saying:
\v 10 Take from the captivity — from the chiefs and from those who are useful to it — and you will enter on that day into the house of Josiah the son of Zephaniah who came from Babylon.

\v 12 And you will say to him: Thus says the Lord Almighty — look a man — the Branch is his name — and he will spring up from below and he will build the house of the Lord.
\v 13 And he will receive power and will sit and rule upon his throne — and he will be a priest upon his throne — and the counsel of peace will be between the two of them.
\v 14 And the crown will be for those who endure and for the useful ones and for those who have known it and for the grace of the son of Zephaniah — as a memorial in the house of the Lord.
\v 15 And those who are far off will come and build in the house of the Lord — and you will know that the Lord Almighty has sent me to you — and this will come to pass if you hear attentively the voice of the Lord your God.

\v 1 And it came to pass in the fourth year of Darius the king — the word of the Lord came to Zechariah on the fourth day of the ninth month which is Chislev.
\v 2 And Sharezer and Arbeseer the king and his men sent to Bethel and to entreat the face of the Lord —
\v 3 saying to the priests who were in the house of the Lord Almighty and to the prophets: Shall I weep in the fifth month of sanctification as I have done these many years?
\v 4 And the word of the Lord Almighty came to me saying:
\v 5 Speak to all the people of the land and to the priests saying: When you fasted and wailed in the fifth and the seventh month — and this seventy years — did you fast at all a fast to me?
\v 6 And when you ate and drank — were you not eating for yourselves and drinking for yourselves?
\v 7 Are not these the words which the Lord proclaimed by the hand of the former prophets when Jerusalem was inhabited and prospering and her cities round about her and the Negev and the lowland were inhabited?
\p
\v 8 And the word of the Lord came to Zechariah saying:
\v 9 Thus says the Lord Almighty — judge true judgment and deal with each other in mercy and compassion.
\v 10 And do not oppress widow and orphan and sojourner and poor man — and let none of you plot evil against his brother in your heart.
\v 11 But they refused to give heed and they turned a stubborn shoulder and they made their ears heavy so as not to hear.
\v 12 And they made their heart as adamant so as not to hear the law and the words which the Lord Almighty sent out in his breath-of-life by the hand of the former prophets — and great wrath came from the Lord Almighty.
\v 13 And it will be as he called and they did not hear — so they will call and I will not hear says the Lord Almighty.
\v 14 And I scattered them into all the nations whom they had not known — and the land was made desolate from those who passed through and from those who returned — and they laid the desirable land waste.

\v 1 And the word of the Lord Almighty came saying:
\v 2 Thus says the Lord Almighty — I was zealous for Zion with great zeal and with great wrath I was zealous for her.
\v 3 Thus says the Lord — I have returned to Zion and I will dwell in the midst of Jerusalem — and Jerusalem will be called a true city and the mountain of the Lord Almighty a holy mountain.
\v 4 Thus says the Lord Almighty — old men and old women will yet sit in the open-ways of Jerusalem — each having his staff in his hand because of the multitude of days.
\v 5 And the open-ways of the city will be filled with boys and girls playing in her open-ways.
\v 6 Thus says the Lord Almighty — if it is impossible before the remnant of this people in those days — will it also be impossible before me? says the Lord Almighty.
\v 7 Thus says the Lord Almighty — look — I am saving my people from the land of the east and from the land of the going-down of the sun.
\v 8 And I will bring them and they will dwell in the midst of Jerusalem — and they will be my people and I will be their God in truth and in righteousness.
\p
\v 9 Thus says the Lord Almighty — let your hands be strong — you who hear in these days these words from the mouth of the prophets — from the day the house of the Lord Almighty was founded — the temple — so that it might be built.
\v 10 Because before those days the wages of men were not for profit and there was no wage for cattle — and to the one going out and to the one coming in there was no peace from the affliction — and I sent out all men each against his neighbor.
\v 11 And now I will not deal with the remnant of this people as in the former days says the Lord Almighty.
\v 12 But I will show them peace — the vine will yield its fruit and the land will yield its produce and the heaven will give its dew — and I will give to the remnant of my people all these things as an inheritance.
\v 13 And it will be as you were a curse among the nations O house of Judah and house of Israel — so I will save you and you will be a blessing. Take courage and let your hands be strong.
\v 14 Because thus says the Lord Almighty — as I purposed to afflict you when your fathers provoked me says the Lord Almighty and I did not repent —
\v 15 so I have prepared and purposed in these days to do good to Jerusalem and to the house of Judah. Do not fear.
\v 16 These are the things which you will do — speak truth each with his neighbor — judge true judgment and judgment of peace in your gates.
\v 17 And let none of you plot evil in his heart against his neighbor — and do not love a false oath — because all these things I hate says the Lord Almighty.
\p
\v 18 And the word of the Lord Almighty came to me saying:
\v 19 Thus says the Lord Almighty — the fast of the fourth and the fast of the fifth and the fast of the seventh and the fast of the tenth will be for the house of Judah into joy and into gladness and into good appointed-times — and you will love truth and peace.
\v 20 Thus says the Lord Almighty — yet peoples will come and the inhabitants of many cities —
\v 21 and the inhabitants of five cities will go to one city saying: Let us go to entreat the face of the Lord and to seek the face of the Lord Almighty — I will also go.
\v 22 And many peoples and many nations will come to seek the face of the Lord Almighty in Jerusalem and to entreat the face of the Lord.
\v 23 Thus says the Lord Almighty — in those days ten men out of all the languages of the nations will take hold — and they will take hold of the fringe of a Jewish man saying: We will go with you — because we have heard that God is with you.

\v 2 And Hamath on her boundary and Tyre and Sidon — because they were exceedingly wise.
\v 3 And Tyre built herself a fortification and heaped up silver as dust and gold as mud of the open-ways.
\v 4 And therefore the Lord will inherit her and strike her power into the sea — and she will be devoured with fire.
\v 5 Ashkelon will see and fear and Gaza will be greatly pained and Ekron — because she was put to shame at her hope. And a king will perish from Gaza and Ashkelon will not be inhabited.
\v 6 And foreigners will dwell in Ashdod and I will bring down the pride of the Philistines.
\v 7 And I will take away their blood from their mouth and their abominations from between their teeth — and they also will be left for our God and will be as a ruler of thousands in Judah and Ekron as the Jebusite.
\v 8 And I will encamp as a guard for my house — from those passing through and from those returning — and no taskmaster will pass through them any longer — because now I have seen with my eyes.
\p
\v 9 Rejoice greatly O daughter of Zion — cry aloud O daughter of Jerusalem — look your king is coming to you — righteous and saving — he is lowly and riding upon a beast of burden and upon a young foal.
\v 10 And he will destroy the chariots from Ephraim and the horse from Jerusalem — and the battle-bow will be destroyed — and abundance and peace from the nations — and he will rule from sea to sea and from the rivers to the ends of the earth.
\v 11 And you — by the blood of your covenant I sent out your captives from a waterless pit.
\v 12 You will dwell in the fortresses O prisoners of the assembly — and instead of one day of your captivity I will repay you double.
\v 13 Because I bent Judah for myself as a bow — I filled Ephraim — and I will stir up your sons O Zion against your sons O Greece — and I will handle you as the sword of a warrior.
\v 14 And the Lord will be over them and his arrow will go out as lightning — and the Lord Almighty will blow a trumpet and will go in the earthquake of the south wind.
\v 15 The Lord Almighty will shield them — and they will devour them and subdue them with sling-stones — and they will drink their blood as wine and they will fill the bowls as an altar.
\v 16 And the Lord their God will save them in that day as the flock of his people — because holy stones are rolling in his land.
\v 17 Because if there is anything good of his — and if there is anything beautiful of his — grain for young men and fragrant wine for virgins.

\v 1 Ask rain from the Lord in season — the early rain and the late rain — the Lord made flashing-visions and rain of abundance will be given to them — to each one grass in the open-field.
\v 2 Because the household-gods spoke empty things and the diviners saw false visions and the dreamers spoke false dreams and gave empty comfort — therefore they were led away as sheep and they were afflicted because there was no healing.
\v 3 My wrath was kindled against the shepherds and I will visit upon the he-goats — because the Lord Almighty visited his flock the house of Judah and will appoint them as his horse of glory in battle.
\v 4 From him came the cornerstone and from him the stake and from him the battle-bow and from him every exactor together.
\v 5 And they will be as warriors trampling mud in the open-ways in battle — and they will set in order because the Lord is with them — and those riding upon horses will be put to shame.
\v 6 And I will strengthen the house of Judah and the house of Joseph I will save — and I will settle them because I have mercy upon them — and they will be as if I had not cast them away — because I am the Lord their God and I will hear them.
\v 7 And Ephraim will be as a warrior and their heart will rejoice as in wine — and their children will see and rejoice and their heart will be glad in the Lord.
\v 8 I will signal to them and gather them because I have ransomed-released them — and they will be multiplied as they were multiplied.
\v 9 And I will sow them among the peoples — and those who are far off will remember me — they will rear their children and will return.
\v 10 And I will return them from the land of Egypt and I will gather them from the Assyrians — and I will bring them into the land of Gilead and into Lebanon — and there will not be enough room for them.
\v 11 And they will pass through a narrow sea and will strike the waves in the sea — and all the depths of the rivers will be dried up — and all the arrogance of the Assyrians will be removed and the scepter of Egypt will be taken away.
\v 12 And I will strengthen them in the Lord their God and they will boast in his name says the Lord.

\v 9 And I said: I will not shepherd you — the dying let die and the being cut off let be cut off and the remaining let devour each the flesh of his neighbor.
\v 10 And I will take my staff Beauty and cast it away to make void my covenant which I made with all the peoples.

\v 13 And the Lord said to me: Drop it into the smelting-furnace and see if it is tested as I was tested by them. And I took the thirty silver pieces and dropped them into the smelting-furnace in the house of the Lord.
\v 14 And I cut off my second staff Cord to break the brotherhood between Judah and Israel.

\v 15 And the Lord said to me: Yet take for yourself the instruments of a foolish shepherd.
\v 16 Because look — I am raising up a shepherd upon the land — the missing ones he will not visit and the scattered he will not seek and the broken he will not heal and the whole he will not sustain — and the flesh of the choice ones he will eat and their hooves he will tear off.
\v 17 O worthless shepherds abandoning the sheep — a sword upon his arms and upon his right eye — his arm will be utterly withered and his right eye will be utterly darkened.

\v 8 And in that day the Lord will shield the inhabitants of Jerusalem — and the one who stumbles among them will be as David in that day and the house of David will be as God — as the angel of the Lord before their face.

\v 10 And I will pour out upon the house of David and upon the inhabitants of Jerusalem a breath-of-life of grace and of compassion — and they will look upon me in return for those over whom they danced in triumph — and they will mourn over him as one mourns over a beloved and they will be in pain over him as one is in pain over a firstborn.
\v 11 In that day the mourning will be great in Jerusalem as the mourning of the pomegranate grove being cut down in the plain.
\v 12 And the land will mourn — family by family apart — the family of the house of David apart and their wives apart — the family of the house of Nathan apart and their wives apart —

\v 3 And the Lord will go out and wage war against those nations as the day he waged war in the day of battle.
\v 4 And his feet will stand in that day upon the Mount of Olives which is opposite Jerusalem from the east — and the Mount of Olives will be split from the east and from the west — a very great valley — and half the mountain will be moved to the north and half of it to the south.
\v 5 And the valley of my mountains will be blocked — and the valley of the mountains will be joined to Azal — and it will be blocked as it was blocked in the days of the earthquake in the days of Uzziah king of Judah. And the Lord my God will come and all the holy ones with him.
\v 6 And it will be in that day — there will be no light and there will be cold and frost.
\v 7 And it will be one day — that day is known to the Lord — not day and not night — and toward evening it will be light.

**Quick notes on how it lands**

- **"the Accuser"** (ὁ διάβολος / *diabolus*) — 3:1–2 — Thayer: *diabolos* = the slanderer, the false accuser — from *diaballō* = to throw across, to bring charges across, to accuse by casting allegations. LSJ: one who slanders, a false accuser, one who throws accusations. The LXX uses *diabolos* consistently for the adversarial accuser in the divine council — the same word that becomes "the devil" in NT usage. In Zechariah 3 the accuser stands in the divine council proceeding against Joshua the great priest. This is not a general evil force — it is a named institutional role in the council proceeding: the one whose function is to bring charges. The Lord's rebuke is not merely moral disapproval — it is a judicial ruling that overturns the accusation and restores the accused to standing.

- **"the Branch"** (Ἀνατολή / *Oriens*) — 3:8, 6:12 — Thayer: *anatole* = a rising up, a springing forth — used for the rising of the sun and for the sprouting of a plant from below ground. LSJ: a rising, an emergence from beneath — the upward motion of something breaking through the surface. Vulgate *Oriens* = the rising, the east, the dawn-direction — Jerome preserved the rising-emergence register. The Branch who springs up from below in 6:12 carries both registers simultaneously: a plant pushing up through soil and a sun rising over the horizon. The same word appears in Luke 1:78 — "the rising from on high will visit us" — Zechariah's prophecy at John's birth uses *anatole* directly. The dawn-rising of the Branch is not merely a plant metaphor — it is a cosmic emergence from below into full light.

- **"sons of fatness"** (οἱ δύο υἱοὶ τῆς πιότητος / *filii pinguedinis*) — 4:14 — Thayer: *piotes* = fatness, richness, the condition of being saturated with oil; *huioi* = sons — with full lineage and status weight. LSJ: fatness, richness, the quality of full saturation. The two olive trees beside the lampstand are named "sons of fatness" — those saturated with and constituted by the oil-richness they pour out. These are the two anointed ones who stand beside the Lord of all the earth — the royal and priestly offices flanking the divine throne. The *huioi* construction is not decorative — it means they are defined by and constituted from the oil-substance. They do not merely pour out anointing — they are sons of it, born from it, their identity is its fullness.

- **"they will look upon me in return for those over whom they danced in triumph"** (ἐπιβλέψονται πρός με ἀνθ' ὧν κατωρχήσαντο) — 12:10 — **CAE CONTAMINATION NODE — Vulgate suspended.** Thayer: *epiblepō* = to look upon, to fix the gaze upon with attention; *anti* = in return for, in exchange for, against; *katorcheōmai* = to dance in triumph over, to celebrate victory over someone with dancing — a specific verb for the triumph-dance performed over a defeated enemy. LSJ: *katorcheōmai* = to dance over someone in mockery or triumph — the compound *kata* intensifies the direction downward, dancing over the one below. The LXX Rahlfs-Hanhart reading is: they will look upon me in return for — or because of — those over whom they danced in triumph. This is a corporate mourning triggered by the recognition of what was done to those who were mocked and defeated. The MT reads "they will look on me whom they have pierced" — *dāqārû* = pierced. The LXX and MT diverge completely at the key verb: *katorcheōmai* (triumph-danced over) vs *dāqārû* (pierced). John 19:37 quotes the MT reading at the crucifixion. The LXX reading is not wrong — it is a different text tradition reading the corporate guilt differently. Both traditions arrive at a moment of recognition and mourning. Rendered from LXX base as specified. The divergence is itself forensically significant — it shows two textual streams processing the same theological event through different lexical choices.

- **"feast of the tent-pitching"** (ἑορτὴ τῆς σκηνοπηγίας / *festum tabernaculorum*) — 14:16–19 — Thayer: *skēnopēgia* = the feast of tent-pitching, the construction of booths — from *skēnē* (tent) + *pēgnymi* (to fix, to fasten, to pitch). LSJ: the festival of tent-erection, the Feast of Booths/Tabernacles — the annual covenant-renewal feast commemorating the wilderness sojourn. The closing chapter of Zechariah — and of the entire prophetic sequence — ends with all surviving nations making annual pilgrimage to Jerusalem to keep the tent-pitching feast. The cosmic re-ordering of chapters 12–14 resolves not into an abstract eternal state but into a recurring liturgical act. The nations who survive the final battle come year after year to pitch tents and commemorate the wilderness. The tent — temporary, portable, dwelling-in-transit — becomes the permanent mark of covenant membership for all nations.

- **"Holy to the Lord Almighty on the bridle of the horse"** (Ἅγιον τῷ κυρίῳ παντοκράτορι) — 14:20 — Thayer: *hagion* = holy, set apart, consecrated — the same word inscribed on the high priest's golden plate worn on the turban; *pantokratōr* = the all-ruling one, the one who holds all things in his power. LSJ: *hagion* = the consecrated thing, the set-apart; *pantokratōr* = the omnipotent ruler. The inscription on the high priest's turban — "Holy to the Lord" — stamped onto the war-horse's bridle. The most intimate priestly consecration-mark moved to military equipment. Then the cooking pots of Jerusalem declared equivalent to the sacred bowls before the altar. The entire final vision is a collapse of the sacred/profane distinction — not its abolition but its expansion: the whole city becomes temple, the war-horse becomes priest, the kitchen pot becomes altar-ware. The prophetic canon closes on total consecration of the ordinary.
