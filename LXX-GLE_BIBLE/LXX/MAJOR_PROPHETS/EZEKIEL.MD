\v 1 And it came to pass in the thirtieth year — in the fourth month — on the fifth day of the month — as I was among the exiles by the river Chebar — the heavens were opened and I saw visions of God.
\v 2 On the fifth day of the month — it was the fifth year of the exile of King Jehoiachin —
\v 3 the word of the Lord came to Ezekiel the priest son of Buzi — in the land of the Chaldeans by the river Chebar — and the hand of the Lord was upon him there.
\p
\v 4 As I looked — look — a stormy wind came out of the north — a great cloud with brightness around it and fire flashing forth continually — and in the midst of the fire as it were gleaming metal.
\v 5 And from the midst of it came the likeness of four living creatures — and this was their appearance: they had the likeness of a man —
\v 6 and each had four faces and each of them had four wings.
\v 7 Their legs were straight and the soles of their feet were like the sole of a calf's foot — and they sparkled like burnished bronze.
\v 8 Under their wings on their four sides they had human hands — and the four had their faces and their wings thus:
\v 9 their wings touched one another — they did not turn when they went but each went straight forward.
\v 10 As for the likeness of their faces — each had a human face — the four had the face of a lion on the right side — the four had the face of an ox on the left side — and the four had the face of an eagle.
\v 11 Such were their faces — and their wings were spread out above — each creature had two wings — each of which touched the wing of another — while two covered their bodies.
\v 12 And each went straight forward — wherever the breath-of-life would go they went — without turning as they went.
\v 13 As for the likeness of the living creatures — their appearance was like burning coals of fire — like the appearance of torches moving to and fro among the living creatures — and the fire was bright and out of the fire went forth lightning.
\v 14 And the living creatures darted to and fro like the appearance of a flash of lightning.
\p
\v 15 Now as I looked at the living creatures — I saw a wheel on the earth beside the living creatures — one for each of the four of them.
\v 16 As for the appearance of the wheels and their construction — their appearance was like the gleaming of beryl — and the four had the same likeness — their appearance and construction being as it were a wheel within a wheel.
\v 17 When they went they went in any of their four directions without turning as they went.
\v 18 And their rims were tall and awesome and the rims of all four were full of eyes all around.
\v 19 And when the living creatures went the wheels went beside them — and when the living creatures were lifted up from the earth the wheels were lifted up.
\v 20 Wherever the breath-of-life wanted to go they went — and the wheels were lifted up alongside them — for the breath-of-life of the living creatures was in the wheels.
\v 21 When those went these went — and when those stood these stood — and when those were lifted up from the earth the wheels were lifted up alongside them — for the breath-of-life of the living creatures was in the wheels.
\p
\v 22 Over the heads of the living creatures there was the likeness of an expanse — shining like awesome crystal spread out above their heads.
\v 23 And under the expanse their wings were stretched out straight — one toward another — and each creature had two wings covering its body.
\v 24 And when they went I heard the sound of their wings like the sound of many waters — like the sound of the Almighty — a sound of tumult like the sound of an army — when they stood they let down their wings.
\v 25 And there came a voice from above the expanse over their heads — when they stood and let down their wings.
\p
\v 26 And above the expanse over their heads there was the likeness of a throne — in appearance like sapphire — and seated above the likeness of a throne was a likeness with a human appearance.
\v 27 And upward from what had the appearance of his waist I saw as it were gleaming metal — like the appearance of fire enclosed all around — and downward from what had the appearance of his waist I saw as it were the appearance of fire — and there was brightness around him.
\v 28 Like the appearance of the bow that is in the cloud on the day of rain — so was the appearance of the brightness all around. Such was the appearance of the likeness of the glory of the Lord — and when I saw it I fell on my face and I heard the voice of one speaking.

\c 2
\p
\v 1 And he said to me: Son of man — stand on your feet and I will speak with you.
\v 2 And as he spoke to me the breath-of-life entered into me and set me on my feet — and I heard him speaking to me.
\v 3 And he said to me: Son of man — I send you to the sons of Israel — to nations of rebels who have rebelled against me — they and their fathers have transgressed against me to this very day.
\v 4 The descendants also are impudent and stubborn — I send you to them and you shall say to them: Thus says the Lord God.
\v 5 And whether they hear or refuse to hear — for they are a rebellious house — they will know that a prophet has been among them.
\v 6 And you son of man — be not afraid of them nor be afraid of their words — though briers and thorns are with you and you sit on scorpions — be not afraid of their words nor be dismayed at their looks — for they are a rebellious house.
\v 7 And you shall speak my words to them whether they hear or refuse to hear — for they are a rebellious house.
\p
\v 8 But you son of man — hear what I say to you — be not rebellious like that rebellious house — open your mouth and eat what I give you.
\v 9 And when I looked — look — a hand was stretched out to me and look — a scroll of a book was in it.
\v 10 And he spread it before me — and it had writing on the front and on the back — and there were written on it words of lamentation and mourning and woe.

\c 3
\p
\v 1 And he said to me: Son of man — eat what is before you — eat this scroll and go speak to the house of Israel.
\v 2 So I opened my mouth and he gave me this scroll to eat.
\v 3 And he said to me: Son of man — feed your belly with this scroll that I give you and fill your stomach with it. Then I ate it and it was in my mouth sweet as honey.
\p
\v 4 And he said to me: Son of man — go to the house of Israel and speak with my words to them.
\v 5 For you are not sent to a people of foreign speech and a hard language — but to the house of Israel —
\v 6 not to many peoples of foreign speech and a hard language — whose words you cannot understand — surely if I sent you to such — they would listen to you.
\v 7 But the house of Israel will not be willing to listen to you — for they are not willing to listen to me — because all the house of Israel have a hard forehead and a stubborn heart.
\v 8 Look — I have made your face as hard as their faces and your forehead as hard as their foreheads.
\v 9 Like diamond harder than flint have I made your forehead — fear them not nor be dismayed at their looks — for they are a rebellious house.
\p
\v 10 Moreover he said to me: Son of man — all my words that I shall speak to you receive in your heart and hear with your ears.
\v 11 And go to the exiles — to your people and speak to them and say to them: Thus says the Lord God — whether they hear or refuse to hear.
\p
\v 12 Then the breath-of-life lifted me up and I heard behind me the sound of a great earthquake — blessed be the glory of the Lord from its place —
\v 13 and the sound of the wings of the living creatures as they touched one another — and the sound of the wheels beside them — and the sound of a great earthquake.
\v 14 The breath-of-life lifted me up and took me away — and I went in bitterness in the heat of my breath-of-life — the hand of the Lord being strong upon me.
\v 15 And I came to the exiles at Tel-abib who were dwelling by the river Chebar — and I sat there overwhelmed among them seven days.
\p
\v 16 And at the end of seven days the word of the Lord came to me:
\v 17 Son of man — I have made you a watchman for the house of Israel — whenever you hear a word from my mouth you shall give them warning from me.
\v 18 If I say to the impious: You shall surely die — and you give him no warning nor speak to warn the impious from his impious way in order to save his life — that impious man shall die in his iniquity but his blood I will require at your hand.
\v 19 But if you warn the impious and he does not turn from his impiety and from his impious way — he shall die in his iniquity but you will have delivered your soul.
\v 20 Again — if a righteous person turns from his righteousness and commits injustice and I lay a stumbling block before him — he shall die — because you have not warned him he shall die in his sin and his righteous deeds that he has done shall not be remembered — but his blood I will require at your hand.
\v 21 But if you warn the righteous person not to sin and he does not sin — he shall surely live because he took warning — and you will have delivered your soul.
\p
\v 22 And the hand of the Lord was upon me there — and he said to me: Arise — go out into the valley and there I will speak with you.
\v 23 So I arose and went out into the valley and look — the glory of the Lord stood there — like the glory that I had seen by the river Chebar — and I fell on my face.
\v 24 But the breath-of-life entered into me and set me on my feet and he spoke with me and said to me: Go shut yourself within your house.
\v 25 And you son of man — look — cords will be placed upon you and you will be bound with them so that you cannot go out among the people.
\v 26 And I will make your tongue cling to the roof of your mouth so that you shall be mute and unable to reprove them — for they are a rebellious house.
\v 27 But when I speak with you I will open your mouth and you shall say to them: Thus says the Lord God — let him who will hear hear — and let him who will refuse to hear refuse — for they are a rebellious house.

\v 1 And you son of man — take a brick and lay it before you and engrave on it a city — even Jerusalem.
\v 2 And put the siege against it — build a siege wall against it — cast up a mound against it — set camps against it and plant battering rams against it all around.
\v 3 And you — take an iron plate and place it as an iron wall between you and the city — and set your face toward it and let it be in a state of siege and you shall press the siege against it — this is a sign for the house of Israel.
\p
\v 4 Then lie on your left side and place the punishment of the house of Israel upon it — for the number of the days that you lie on it you shall bear their punishment.
\v 5 For I assign to you a number of days — three hundred and ninety days — equal to the number of the years of their punishment — so long shall you bear the punishment of the house of Israel.
\v 6 And when you have completed these you shall lie down a second time — on your right side — and bear the punishment of the house of Judah — forty days — I assign you one day for each year.
\v 7 And you shall set your face toward the siege of Jerusalem with your arm bared and you shall prophesy against it.
\v 8 And look — I will place cords upon you so that you cannot turn from one side to the other until you have completed the days of your siege.
\p
\v 9 And you — take wheat and barley — beans and lentils — millet and emmer — and put them into a single vessel and make your bread from them — during the number of days that you lie on your side — three hundred and ninety days — you shall eat it.
\v 10 And your food that you eat shall be by weight — twenty shekels a day — from day to day you shall eat it.
\v 11 And water you shall drink by measure — the sixth part of a hin — from day to day you shall drink.
\v 12 And you shall eat it as a barley cake — baking it in their sight on human dung.
\v 13 And the Lord said: Thus shall the sons of Israel eat their bread unclean among the nations where I will drive them.
\v 14 Then I said: Ah Lord God — look — I have never defiled myself — from my youth up until now I have never eaten what died of itself or was torn by beasts — nor has tainted meat come into my mouth.
\v 15 Then he said to me: See — I will let you have cow's dung instead of human dung and you shall prepare your bread over it.
\p
\v 16 Moreover he said to me: Son of man — look — I will break the supply of bread in Jerusalem — they shall eat bread by weight and with anxiety and they shall drink water by measure and in dismay.
\v 17 I will do this that they may lack bread and water and look at one another in dismay and rot away because of their punishment.

\p
\v 1 And you son of man — take a sharp sword — use it as a barber's razor and pass it over your head and your beard — then take balances for weighing and divide the hair.
\v 2 A third part you shall burn in the fire in the midst of the city when the days of the siege are completed — and a third part you shall take and strike with the sword all around the city — and a third part you shall scatter to the wind and I will unsheathe the sword after them.
\v 3 And you shall take from these a small number and bind them in the skirts of your robe.
\v 4 And of these again you shall take some and cast them into the midst of the fire and burn them in the fire — from there a fire will come out against all the house of Israel.
\p
\v 5 Thus says the Lord God: This is Jerusalem — I have set her in the center of the nations with countries all around her.
\v 6 And she has rebelled against my rules by doing wickedness more than the nations and against my statutes more than the countries all around her — for they have rejected my rules and have not walked in my statutes.
\v 7 Therefore thus says the Lord God: Because you are more turbulent than the nations that are all around you and have not walked in my statutes or obeyed my rules and have not even acted according to the rules of the nations that are all around you —
\v 8 therefore thus says the Lord God: Look — I — even I — am against you and I will execute judgments in your midst in the sight of the nations.
\v 9 And because of all your abominations I will do with you what I have never yet done and the like of which I will never do again.
\v 10 Therefore fathers shall eat their sons in your midst and sons shall eat their fathers — and I will execute judgments on you and any of you who survive I will scatter to all the winds.
\p
\v 11 Therefore as I live — says the Lord God — surely because you have defiled my sanctuary with all your detestable things and with all your abominations — therefore I will withdraw — my eye will not spare and I will have no pity.
\v 12 A third part of you shall die of pestilence and be consumed with famine in your midst — a third part shall fall by the sword all around you — and a third part I will scatter to all the winds and will unsheathe the sword after them.
\p
\v 13 Thus shall my anger spend itself and I will vent my fury upon them and satisfy myself — and they shall know that I am the Lord — that I have spoken in my jealousy — when I spend my fury upon them.
\v 14 Moreover I will make you a desolation and an object of reproach among the nations all around you and in the sight of all who pass by.
\v 15 You shall be a reproach and a taunt — a warning and a horror — to the nations all around you when I execute judgments on you in anger and fury and with furious rebukes — I am the Lord who has spoken —
\v 16 when I send against you the deadly arrows of famine — arrows for destruction which I will send to destroy you — and when I bring more and more famine upon you and break your supply of bread.
\v 17 I will send famine and wild beasts against you and they will rob you of your children — pestilence and blood shall pass through you and I will bring the sword upon you — I am the Lord who has spoken.

\v 2 Son of man — set your face toward the mountains of Israel and prophesy against them
\v 3 and say: You mountains of Israel — hear the word of the Lord God — thus says the Lord God to the mountains and the hills — to the ravines and the valleys: Look — I — even I — will bring a sword upon you and I will destroy your high places.
\v 4 Your altars shall become desolate and your incense altars shall be broken — and I will cast down your slain before your idols.
\v 5 And I will lay the dead bodies of the sons of Israel before their idols and I will scatter your bones around your altars.
\v 6 Wherever you dwell the cities shall be laid waste and the high places shall be desolate — so that your altars will be laid waste and made desolate and your idols broken and destroyed and your incense altars cut down and your works wiped out.
\v 7 And the slain shall fall in your midst and you shall know that I am the Lord.
\p
\v 8 Yet I will leave some of you alive — when you have among the nations some who escape the sword when you are scattered through the countries.
\v 9 Then those of you who escape will remember me among the nations where they are carried captive — how I have been broken over their whoring heart that has departed from me and over their eyes that go whoring after their idols — and they will be loathsome in their own sight for the evils that they have committed for all their abominations.
\v 10 And they shall know that I am the Lord — I have not said in vain that I would do this evil to them.
\p
\v 11 Thus says the Lord God: Clap your hands and stamp your foot and say: Alas — because of all the evil abominations of the house of Israel — for they shall fall by the sword — by famine — and by pestilence.
\v 12 He who is far off shall die of pestilence and he who is near shall fall by the sword and he who is left and is preserved shall die of famine — thus I will spend my fury upon them.
\v 13 And you shall know that I am the Lord — when their slain lie among their idols around their altars on every high hill — on all the mountaintops — and under every green tree and under every leafy oak — wherever they offered pleasing aroma to all their idols.
\v 14 And I will stretch out my hand against them and make the land desolate and waste in all their dwelling places from the wilderness to Riblah — then they will know that I am the Lord.

\v 2 And you son of man — thus says the Lord God to the land of Israel: An end — the end has come upon the four corners of the land.
\v 3 Now the end is upon you and I will send my anger upon you — I will judge you according to your ways and I will punish you for all your abominations.
\v 4 And my eye will not spare you nor will I have pity — but I will punish you for your ways while your abominations are in your midst — then you will know that I am the Lord.
\p
\v 5 Thus says the Lord God: Disaster after disaster — look — it comes.
\v 6 An end has come — the end has come — it has awakened against you — look — it comes.
\v 7 Your doom has come to you O inhabitant of the land — the time has come — the day is near — a day of tumult and not of joyful shouting on the mountains.
\v 8 Now I will soon pour out my wrath upon you and spend my anger against you and judge you according to your ways and punish you for all your abominations.
\v 9 And my eye will not spare nor will I have pity — I will punish you according to your ways while your abominations are in your midst — then you will know that I am the Lord who strikes.
\p
\v 10 Look — the day — look — it comes — your doom has gone out — the rod has blossomed — pride has budded.
\v 11 Violence has grown up into a rod of wickedness — none of them shall remain — none of their abundance — none of their wealth — neither shall there be preeminence among them.
\v 12 The time has come — the day has arrived — let not the buyer rejoice nor the seller mourn — for wrath is upon all their multitude.
\v 13 For the seller shall not return to what he has sold while they live — for the vision concerns all their multitude — it shall not turn back — and because of his iniquity no one can maintain his life.
\p
\v 14 They have blown the trumpet and made everything ready but none goes to battle — for my wrath is upon all their multitude.
\v 15 The sword is without — pestilence and famine are within — he who is in the field dies by the sword — and him who is in the city famine and pestilence devour.
\v 16 And if any survivors escape — they will be on the mountains like doves of the valleys — all of them moaning — each one over his iniquity.
\v 17 All hands are feeble and all knees turn to water.
\v 18 They put on sackcloth and horror covers them — shame is on all faces and baldness on all their heads.
\v 19 They cast their silver into the streets and their gold is like an unclean thing — their silver and gold are not able to deliver them in the day of the wrath of the Lord — they cannot satisfy their hunger or fill their stomachs with it — for it was the stumbling block of their iniquity.
\v 20 His beautiful ornament they used for pride and they made their abominable images and their detestable things of it — therefore I make it an unclean thing to them.
\v 21 And I will give it into the hands of foreigners for prey and to the wicked of the earth for spoil and they shall profane it.
\v 22 I will turn my face from them and they shall profane my treasured place — robbers shall enter it and profane it.
\p
\v 23 Make a chain — for the land is full of bloody crimes and the city is full of violence.
\v 24 I will bring the worst of the nations to take possession of their houses — I will put an end to the pride of the strong and their holy places shall be profaned.
\v 25 When anguish comes they will seek peace but there shall be none.
\v 26 Disaster comes upon disaster and rumor follows rumor — they seek a vision from the prophet but the law perishes from the priest and counsel from the elders.
\v 27 The king mourns and the prince is wrapped in despair and the hands of the people of the land are paralyzed by terror — according to their way I will do to them and according to their judgments I will judge them and they shall know that I am the Lord.

\v 1 In the sixth year — in the sixth month — on the fifth day of the month — as I sat in my house with the elders of Judah sitting before me — the hand of the Lord God fell upon me there.
\v 2 Then I looked and look — a likeness with the appearance of fire — below what appeared to be his waist was fire and above his waist was something like the appearance of brightness — like gleaming metal.
\v 3 He put out the form of a hand and took me by a lock of my head — and the breath-of-life lifted me up between earth and heaven and brought me in visions of God to Jerusalem — to the entrance of the gateway of the inner court that faces north where was the seat of the image of jealousy which provokes to jealousy.
\v 4 And look — the glory of the God of Israel was there like the vision that I had seen in the valley.
\p
\v 5 Then he said to me: Son of man — lift up your eyes now toward the north. So I lifted up my eyes toward the north and look — north of the altar gate in the entrance was this image of jealousy.
\v 6 And he said to me: Son of man — do you see what they are doing — the great abominations that the house of Israel are committing here — to drive me far from my sanctuary? — but you will see still greater abominations.
\p
\v 7 And he brought me to the entrance of the court and when I looked look — there was a hole in the wall.
\v 8 Then he said to me: Son of man — dig in the wall — and when I dug in the wall look — there was an entrance.
\v 9 And he said to me: Go in and see the vile abominations that they are committing here.
\v 10 So I went in and looked and look — every form of creeping things and loathsome beasts and all the idols of the house of Israel — carved all around on the wall.
\v 11 And before them stood seventy men of the elders of the house of Israel — with Jaazaniah son of Shaphan standing among them — each with his censer in his hand and the smoke of the cloud of incense going up.
\v 12 Then he said to me: Son of man — have you seen what the elders of the house of Israel are doing in the dark — each in his room of pictures? — for they say: The Lord does not see us — the Lord has forsaken the land.
\v 13 He said also to me: You will see still greater abominations that they are committing.
\p
\v 14 Then he brought me to the entrance of the north gate of the house of the Lord and look — there sat women weeping for Tammuz.
\v 15 Then he said to me: Have you seen this O son of man? — you will see still greater abominations than these.
\p
\v 16 And he brought me into the inner court of the house of the Lord — and look — at the entrance of the temple of the Lord between the porch and the altar were about twenty-five men with their backs to the temple of the Lord and their faces toward the east — worshipping the sun toward the east.
\v 17 Then he said to me: Have you seen this O son of man? — is it too light a thing for the house of Judah to commit the abominations that they commit here — that they should fill the land with violence and provoke me still further to anger? — look — they put the branch to their nose.
\v 18 Therefore I will act in wrath — my eye will not spare nor will I have pity — and though they cry in my ears with a loud voice I will not hear them.

\v 8 And while they were striking and I was left alone — I fell upon my face and cried: Ah Lord God — will you destroy all the remnant of Israel in the outpouring of your wrath on Jerusalem?
\v 9 Then he said to me: The guilt of the house of Israel and Judah is exceedingly great — the land is full of blood and the city full of injustice — for they say: The Lord has forsaken the land and the Lord does not see.
\v 10 As for me — my eye will not spare nor will I have pity — I will bring their deeds upon their heads.
\v 11 And look — the man clothed in linen with the writing case at his waist brought back word saying: I have done as you commanded me.

\v 5 And the sound of the wings of the cherubim was heard as far as the outer court — like the voice of God Almighty when he speaks.

\v 7 And a cherub stretched out his hand from between the cherubim to the fire that was between the cherubim and took some of it and put it into the hands of the man clothed in linen — who took it and went out.
\v 8 The cherubim appeared to have the form of a human hand under their wings.
\p

\v 10 And as for their appearance the four had the same likeness — as if a wheel were within a wheel.
\v 11 When they went they went in any of their four directions without turning as they went — but in whatever direction the front wheel faced the others followed without turning as they went.
\v 12 And their whole body — their backs — their hands — their wings — and the wheels were full of eyes all around — the wheels of all four of them.
\v 13 As for the wheels they were called in my hearing the whirling wheels.
\v 14 And each of the cherubim had four faces — the first face was the face of the cherub — the second face was a human face — the third the face of a lion — and the fourth the face of an eagle.
\v 15 And the cherubim mounted up — these were the living creatures that I saw by the river Chebar.
\v 16 And when the cherubim went the wheels went beside them — and when the cherubim lifted up their wings to mount up from the earth the wheels did not turn from beside them.
\v 17 When they stood still these stood still and when they mounted up these mounted up with them — for the breath-of-life of the living creatures was in them.
\p
\v 18 Then the glory of the Lord went out from the threshold of the house and stood over the cherubim.
\v 19 And the cherubim lifted up their wings and mounted up from the earth before my eyes as they went out — and the wheels were beside them — and they stood at the entrance of the east gate of the house of the Lord — and the glory of the God of Israel was over them.
\v 20 These were the living creatures that I saw underneath the God of Israel by the river Chebar — and I knew that they were cherubim.
\v 21 Each had four faces and each four wings and underneath their wings the likeness of human hands.
\v 22 And the likeness of their faces was the same as the faces that I had seen by the river Chebar — their appearance and themselves — each went straight forward.

\v 1 The breath-of-life lifted me up and brought me to the east gate of the house of the Lord which faces east — and look — at the entrance of the gateway there were twenty-five men — and I saw among them Jaazaniah son of Azzur and Pelatiah son of Benaiah — princes of the people.
\v 2 And he said to me: Son of man — these are the men who devise iniquity and who give wicked counsel in this city.
\v 3 They say: The time is not near to build houses — this city is the pot and we are the meat.
\v 4 Therefore prophesy against them — prophesy O son of man.
\p

## CRITICAL TEXTUAL NOTE — LXX EZEKIEL

The LXX Ezekiel is approximately one-eighth shorter than the MT — similar in scale to the LXX Jeremiah divergence — and contains numerous rearrangements and expansions not present in the MT. The LXX Ezekiel is substantially preserved in Papyrus 967 (Chester Beatty / Cologne Papyrus) — a 3rd century AD Greek manuscript that also presents the chapters in a different order (chapters 36–39 after chapter 11, placing the valley of dry bones before the Tyre oracles). ## CAE NODES — EZEKIEL

### Ezekiel 1:4–28 — *merkabah* / the chariot-throne — CAE PRIMARY NODE

**1:26–28** — "Above the expanse over their heads there was the likeness of a throne — in appearance like sapphire — and seated above the likeness of a throne was a likeness with a human appearance... like the appearance of the bow that is in the cloud on the day of rain — so was the appearance of the brightness all around — such was the appearance of the likeness of the glory of the Lord."

***doxa Kyriou*** = glory of the Lord. The *merkabah* = chariot-throne vision — four living creatures (*zōa*) with four faces (human / lion / ox / eagle) — wheels within wheels full of eyes — crystalline expanse — sapphire throne — the enthroned *doxa Kyriou* in human form surrounded by rainbow.

**Revelation 4:2–8** — "a throne stood in heaven with one seated on the throne... around the throne and on each side of the throne are four living creatures (*tessara zōa*) full of eyes in front and behind — the first living creature like a lion — the second like an ox — the third with a human face — the fourth like an eagle in flight." John's throne vision is a direct expansion of the Ezekiel 1 *merkabah*. The four *zōa* faces are identical.

**Revelation 4:6** — "a sea of glass like crystal" → Ezekiel 1:22 "awesome crystal spread out above their heads." **Revelation 4:3** — "like a jasper stone" with a rainbow → Ezekiel 1:28 "appearance of the bow in the cloud." **Revelation 5:11** — "ten thousand times ten thousand" → Ezekiel *doxa* throne language.

**Ezekiel 1:10** — the four faces: *anthrōpos / leōn / moschos / aetos* = human / lion / ox / eagle. The four Gospel symbols in the patristic tradition draw on these four faces — Matthew (human) / Mark (lion) / Luke (ox) / John (eagle). The *tetramorphon* = four-form iconographic tradition of the evangelists is grounded in Ezekiel 1.

**Isaiah 6:1–3** parallel throne-vision (seraphim / holy holy holy). **Daniel 7:9–10** parallel (Ancient of Days / fiery river). All three converge in Revelation 4.

### Ezekiel 2:8–3:3 — *biblidarion* / scroll eaten — CAE NODE

**3:3** — "Feed your belly with this scroll that I give you and fill your stomach with it — then I ate it and it was in my mouth sweet as honey."

***glykys hōs meli*** = sweet as honey. The prophet eats the divine word — it is sweet but contains lamentation and woe.

**Revelation 10:9–10** — "Take and eat it — it will make your stomach bitter but in your mouth it will be sweet as honey (*glykys hōs meli*)." John eats the little scroll — the Ezekiel 3:3 vocabulary reproduced exactly. The prophet-as-scroll-eater typology: internalizing the divine word so completely that the prophet becomes its embodiment.

**Jeremiah 15:16** — "Your words were found and I ate them and your words became to me joy and delight of my heart." The scroll-eating tradition.

### Ezekiel 9:4 — *tav* / mark on foreheads — CAE PRIMARY NODE

**9:4** — "Pass through the city — through Jerusalem — and put a mark (*sēmeion*) on the foreheads of the men who sigh and groan over all the abominations that are committed in it."

***dos sēmeion epi ta metōpa*** = put a mark/sign on the foreheads. The LXX renders the Hebrew *tav* (the last letter of the Hebrew alphabet — in ancient script written as X or +) with *sēmeion* = sign/mark. The righteous are sealed on the forehead — the executioners are commanded not to touch those bearing the mark.

**Revelation 7:3** — "Do not harm the earth or the sea or the trees until we have sealed the servants of our God on their foreheads." **Revelation 9:4** — "only those people who do not have the seal of God on their foreheads." **Revelation 14:1** — "the Lamb standing on Mount Zion and with him 144,000 who had his name and his Father's name written on their foreheads." The Revelation forehead-sealing of the righteous is the direct eschatological extension of Ezekiel 9:4.

**Revelation 13:16** — the mark of the beast on the forehead or hand is the anti-*tav* — the counterfeit seal. The entire mark-of-the-beast / seal-of-God binary in Revelation is structured as the inversion of Ezekiel 9:4.

The *tav* sign is also the last letter = completion/fullness — the alpha-and-omega logic in embryo.

### Ezekiel 11:19 / 36:26 — *kardia kainē* / new heart — CAE PRIMARY NODE

**11:19** — "I will give them one heart and a new breath-of-life I will put within them — I will remove the heart of stone from their flesh and give them a heart of flesh."

**36:26** — "I will give you a new heart and a new breath-of-life I will put within you — and I will remove the heart of stone from your flesh and give you a heart of flesh — and I will put my breath-of-life within you."

***kardia kainē... pneuma kainon*** = new heart... new breath-of-life. The double promise — new heart replacing the stone heart and new *pneuma* = breath-of-life — is the Ezekiel foundation for the new covenant theology of the NT.

**Jeremiah 31:33** — "I will put my law within them and I will write it on their hearts" — the Jeremiah new covenant and the Ezekiel new heart are the two OT pillars of NT new-creation anthropology.

**2 Corinthians 3:3** — "written not with ink but with the Breath-of-Life of the living God — not on tablets of stone but on tablets of human hearts." Paul fuses Jeremiah 31:33 (law on hearts) with Ezekiel 36:26 (heart of flesh not stone) — the stone tablets of Moses replaced by the heart-of-flesh through the divine *pneuma*.

**John 3:5** — "unless one is born of water and the Breath-of-Life he cannot enter the kingdom of God." **Ezekiel 36:25–27** — "I will sprinkle clean water on you... I will give you a new heart... I will put my *pneuma* within you." The water + *pneuma* = new birth sequence in John 3 follows the Ezekiel 36 water-sprinkling + new *pneuma* sequence.

**Acts 2:17–18** — "I will pour out my *pneuma* on all flesh" → Ezekiel 36:27 / 39:29 "I will pour out my *pneuma* upon the house of Israel."

### Ezekiel 17:22–24 — *anatolē* / tender sprig from cedar top — CAE NODE

**17:22–23** — "I myself will take a sprig from the lofty top of the cedar and will set it out — I will break off from the topmost of its young twigs a tender one and I myself will plant it on a high and lofty mountain... and under it will dwell every kind of bird — in the shade of its branches birds of every sort will nest."

***anatolē mikra*** = small/tender shoot. The Davidic branch planted by God that becomes a great cedar sheltering all birds = the universal kingdom from the Davidic sprig.

**Mark 4:30–32** — "What shall we compare the kingdom of God to?... it is like a grain of mustard seed... it grows and becomes larger than all the garden plants and puts out large branches so that the birds of the air can make nests in its shade." Jesus's mustard-seed parable draws on Ezekiel 17:23 — the small planted thing that becomes a great shelter for birds. The kingdom-as-sheltering-tree typology.

**Daniel 4:12** — "the birds of the heavens lived in its branches" — Nebuchadnezzar's tree-dream uses the same imagery. Both Ezekiel 17 and Daniel 4 feed into the mustard-seed parable's tree-kingdom structure.

### Ezekiel 18:4 / 18:20 — *hē psychē hē hamartanousa* / the soul that sins — CAE NODE

**18:4 / 18:20** — "The soul who sins shall die — the son shall not suffer for the iniquity of the father nor the father suffer for the iniquity of the son — the righteousness of the righteous shall be upon himself and the wickedness of the impious shall be upon himself."

***hē psychē hē hamartanousa autē apothaneitai*** = the soul that sins — it shall die. Individual moral accountability — displacing inherited generational guilt as the primary judicial framework.

**Romans 2:6** — "He will render to each one according to his works." **2 Corinthians 5:10** — "We must all appear before the judgment seat of Christ so that each one may receive what is due for what he has done in the body." The individual accountability theology of Ezekiel 18 undergirds Paul's judgment-according-to-works passages.

**Ezekiel 18:23 / 18:32** — "Have I any pleasure in the death of the impious... and not rather that he should turn from his way and live?... I have no pleasure in the death of anyone — so turn and live."

**2 Peter 3:9** — "not wishing that any should perish but that all should reach repentance." **1 Timothy 2:4** — "who desires all people to be saved and to come to the knowledge of the truth." The divine will-for-life-not-death of Ezekiel 18 is the OT foundation for the NT declarations of God's universal salvific will.

### Ezekiel 28:12–17 — *kepos Eden* / garden of Eden fall — CAE PRIMARY NODE

**28:13–15** — "You were in Eden the garden of God — every precious stone was your covering... you were an anointed guardian cherub — I placed you — you were on the holy mountain of God — in the midst of the stones of fire you walked. You were blameless in your ways from the day you were created until unrighteousness was found in you."

***en tō paradeisō tou theou en Edem ēs*** = you were in the paradise of God in Eden. The king of Tyre oracle contains the most developed pre-Adamic cosmic-fall narrative in the OT — a cherub/guardian figure of perfection cast down from the holy mountain of God because of pride.

**Isaiah 14:12–15** — "How you are fallen from heaven O Day Star son of Dawn... you said in your heart: I will ascend to heaven... I will be like the Most High — but you are brought down to Hades." The two passages together — Ezekiel 28 and Isaiah 14 — form the OT Satan-fall tradition that the NT draws on.

**Luke 10:18** — "I saw Satan fall like lightning from heaven." **Revelation 12:7–9** — "Michael and his angels fighting against the dragon... the great dragon was thrown down — that ancient serpent who is called the devil and Satan." **John 8:44** — "He was a murderer from the beginning and does not stand in the truth — because there is no truth in him." These NT references draw on the Ezekiel 28 / Isaiah 14 cherub-fall pattern.

The engine rendered "guardian cherub" for *cherub hēggiasmenon* — the *anointed* guardian who covers the throne.

### Ezekiel 34:11–16 / 34:23 — *poimainō* / I myself will shepherd — CAE PRIMARY NODE

**34:11–12** — "Look — I myself will search for my sheep and will seek them out. As a shepherd seeks out his flock when he is among his sheep that have been scattered — so will I seek out my sheep."

**34:16** — "I will seek the lost and I will bring back the strayed and I will bind up the injured and I will strengthen the weak."

**34:23–24** — "And I will set up over them one shepherd — my servant David — and he shall feed them — he shall feed them and be their shepherd. And I the Lord will be their God and my servant David shall be prince among them."

***egō autous poimanō... David doulos mou poimēn*** = I myself will shepherd them... David my servant as shepherd. The direct identification of God as the true shepherd who replaces the false shepherds, fulfilled through the Davidic shepherd-prince.

**John 10:11** — "I am the good shepherd (*ho poimēn ho kalos*) — the good shepherd lays down his life for the sheep." **John 10:14** — "I am the good shepherd — I know my own and my own know me." Jesus's *egō eimi ho poimēn* = I am the shepherd is the direct fulfillment of Ezekiel 34:11 *egō pastō* = I myself will shepherd.

**John 10:16** — "I have other sheep that are not of this fold — I must bring them also... so there will be one flock and one shepherd (*mia poimnē heis poimēn*)." Ezekiel 34:23 "one shepherd" (*poimēn heis*) → John 10:16 *heis poimēn*.

**Luke 15:4–6** — "What man of you having a hundred sheep if he has lost one of them does not leave the ninety-nine in the open country and go after the lost one until he finds it?" The lost-sheep parable draws on Ezekiel 34:16 "I will seek the lost."

**Matthew 9:36** — "When he saw the crowds he had compassion on them because they were harassed and helpless like sheep without a shepherd (*hōsei probata mē echonta poimena*)." Ezekiel 34:5 "they were scattered because there was no shepherd" → Matthew 9:36.

**Hebrews 13:20** — "the God of peace who brought again from the dead our Lord Jesus — the great shepherd of the sheep (*ton poimena ton megan*)." **1 Peter 2:25** — "you were straying like sheep but have now returned to the Shepherd and Overseer of your souls."

### Ezekiel 36:25–27 — *hydōr katharon / pneuma kainon* / clean water and new breath — CAE PRIMARY NODE

**36:25–27** — "I will sprinkle clean water on you and you shall be clean from all your uncleannesses and from all your idols I will cleanse you. And I will give you a new heart and a new breath-of-life I will put within you — and I will remove the heart of stone from your flesh and give you a heart of flesh. And I will put my breath-of-life within you and cause you to walk in my statutes."

The triple promise: water-cleansing / new heart / divine *pneuma* indwelling. This is the single most comprehensive OT prophecy of what the NT calls regeneration / new birth / baptism of the Spirit.

**John 3:5** — "unless one is born of water and the Breath-of-Life (*hydatos kai pneumatos*)" — the explicit water + *pneuma* pair of Ezekiel 36:25–27.

**Titus 3:5** — "he saved us through the washing of regeneration and renewing of the Holy Breath-of-Life (*dia loutrou palingenesias kai anakainōseōs pneumatos hagiou*)." The *loutron* = washing and *anakainōsis* = renewal are the Ezekiel 36:25 water-sprinkling and 36:26 new-heart.

**Acts 2:38** — "Repent and be baptized every one of you in the name of Jesus Christ for the forgiveness of your sins and you will receive the gift of the Holy Breath-of-Life." The Peter Pentecost formula follows the Ezekiel 36 sequence: cleansing from sin (36:25) + gift of *pneuma* (36:27).

---

### Ezekiel 37:1–14 — *anastasis nekrōn* / resurrection of dry bones — CAE PRIMARY NODE

**37:3–6** — "Son of man — can these bones live?... Look — I will cause breath to enter you and you shall live... and I will put breath in you and you shall live and you shall know that I am the Lord."

**37:12** — "Look — I will open your graves and raise you from your graves O my people and I will bring you into the land of Israel."

***anoixō ta mnēmata hymōn kai anaxō hymas ek tōn mnēmatōn*** = I will open your graves and bring you up from your graves. The dry-bones vision — national restoration framed in resurrection language.

**John 5:25** — "the hour is coming and is now here when the dead will hear the voice of the Son of God and those who hear will live." **John 5:28–29** — "all who are in the tombs will hear his voice and come out." The Johannine resurrection discourse draws on Ezekiel 37:12–14 *grave-opening* vocabulary.

**Romans 8:11** — "he who raised Christ Jesus from the dead will also give life to your mortal bodies through his Breath-of-Life who dwells in you." The *pneuma* that gives life to the dead bones (Ezekiel 37:5–6) = the *pneuma* that raises mortal bodies (Romans 8:11).

**Revelation 20:13** — "the sea gave up the dead who were in it — Death and Hades gave up the dead who were in them." The opened graves of Ezekiel 37:12 → the eschatological grave-opening of Revelation 20.

**37:9** — "Come from the four winds O breath (*pneuma*) and breathe on these slain that they may live." The four-winds breath-command → **John 20:22** — "he breathed on them and said: Receive the Holy Breath-of-Life (*Labete pneuma hagion*)." Jesus's post-resurrection breath-giving to the disciples echoes Ezekiel 37:9 — the *empneō* = breathe-into of the Creator/Restorer.

### Ezekiel 37:24–27 — *diathēkē eirēnēs aiōnios* / everlasting covenant of peace — CAE NODE

**37:26–27** — "I will make a covenant of peace with them — it shall be an everlasting covenant with them — and I will set them in their land and multiply them and will set my sanctuary in their midst forevermore. My dwelling place shall be with them and I will be their God and they shall be my people."

***diathēkē eirēnēs aiōnios... hē kataskēnōsis mou en autois*** = everlasting covenant of peace... my dwelling among them.

**Revelation 21:3** — "Look — the dwelling place of God is with man (*hē skēnē tou theou meta tōn anthrōpōn*) and he will dwell with them (*kataskēnōsei met' autōn*) and they will be his people and God himself will be with them as their God." The Revelation 21 *new Jerusalem* consummation draws directly on Ezekiel 37:27 *kataskēnōsis* = dwelling/tabernacle vocabulary. The *skēnē* = tabernacle/tent of the new creation is the fulfillment of the Ezekiel everlasting covenant of peace.

**John 1:14** — "*eskenōsen en hēmin*" = he tabernacled among us — the Incarnation as the first fulfillment of Ezekiel 37:27.

### Ezekiel 40–48 — The Temple Vision — CAE NODE

The Temple Vision is the most architecturally detailed prophetic text in the OT — a new Temple, a restored land-allotment, a new city with twelve gates.

**Revelation 21:12–21** — the New Jerusalem with twelve gates named for the twelve tribes of Israel and twelve foundations named for the twelve apostles — draws on Ezekiel 48:30–35 (the twelve city gates named for the twelve tribes). The Ezekiel 48:35 closing verse — *"The Lord is there"* = ***Yhwh Shammah*** — is the name-theology fulfilled in Revelation 21:3 "God himself will be with them."

**Ezekiel 47:1–12 — the river from the temple** — CAE PRIMARY NODE:

**47:1** — "Water was issuing from below the threshold of the temple toward the east."

**47:9** — "Wherever the river goes every living creature that swarms will live and there will be very many fish — for this water goes there that the waters of the sea may become fresh — so everything will live where the river goes."

**47:12** — "On the banks on both sides of the river there will grow all kinds of trees for food — their leaves will not wither nor their fruit fail but they will bear fresh fruit every month because the water for them flows from the sanctuary — their fruit will be for food and their leaves for healing."

**Revelation 22:1–2** — "Then the angel showed me the river of the water of life (*potamon hydatos zōēs*) — bright as crystal — flowing from the throne of God and of the Lamb through the middle of the street of the city — also on either side of the river the tree of life with its twelve kinds of fruit — yielding its fruit each month — the leaves of the tree were for the healing (*therapeia*) of the nations." The Revelation 22 river-of-life and healing-tree are the direct eschatological fulfillment of Ezekiel 47:1–12. Every element is present: river from the sanctuary / living water / fruit every month / leaves for healing.

**John 7:37–38** — "If anyone thirsts let him come to me and drink — he who believes in me as the Scripture said: out of his belly shall flow rivers of living water." Jesus's living-water declaration at the Feast of Tabernacles draws on the Ezekiel 47 temple-river. **Zechariah 14:8** parallel — "living waters shall flow out from Jerusalem."

**Ezekiel 47:12** — *ta phylla autōn eis hygieian* = the leaves of them for healing. **Revelation 22:2** — *ta phylla tou xylou eis therapeian tōn ethnōn* = the leaves of the tree for healing of the nations. The *phylla eis therapeian* = leaves for healing vocabulary is exact.

---

### Ezekiel 48:35 — *Kyrios ekei* / The Lord is there — CAE NODE

**48:35** — "The name of the city from that time on shall be: The Lord is there."

***Kyrios ekei*** = the Lord is there. The entire 48-chapter book closes with a city-name: *Yhwh Shammah* = The Lord is present. Not "the Lord is worshipped here" or "the Lord is remembered here" — but the Lord *is there* — his permanent presence as the city's identity.

**Revelation 21:22–23** — "I saw no temple in the city for its temple is the Lord God the Almighty and the Lamb — and the city has no need of sun or moon to shine on it for the glory of God gives it light and its lamp is the Lamb." The eschatological city requires no temple because God himself is its temple — Ezekiel 48:35 *Kyrios ekei* fulfilled and surpassed: not merely *the Lord is there* but *the Lord is the temple*.

**Revelation 21:3** — "Look — the dwelling place of God is with man." The Ezekiel *Kyrios ekei* → Revelation *hē skēnē tou theou meta tōn anthrōpōn*.

## VOCABULARY DECISIONS — EZEKIEL

### *Pneuma* — breath-of-life throughout
Rendered "breath-of-life" consistently — never "spirit" or "wind" alone where the *pneuma* clearly carries the life-breath register. Ezekiel's *pneuma* vocabulary is central: the *pneuma* lifts the prophet (1:12 / 2:2 / 3:12 / 8:3 / 11:1 / 37:1) — the *pneuma* enters the dry bones (37:5) — the *pneuma* is poured out in the new covenant promise (39:29 / 36:27). Consistent rendering preserves the through-line.

### *Doxa Kyriou* — glory of the Lord
Rendered "glory of the Lord" throughout — never "presence" or "majesty." The *doxa Kyriou* in Ezekiel has a specific narrative arc: arrives in the chariot vision (1:28) → departs the Temple eastward (10:18–19 / 11:22–23) → returns through the east gate in the new Temple vision (43:2–5) → fills the new Temple (43:5). This narrative of departure and return is the theological spine of the book. The *doxa* return = the eschatological promise fulfilled.

### *Huios anthrōpou* — son of man
Rendered "son of man" consistently as Ezekiel's address form throughout — the 93 occurrences of *huios anthrōpou* as the divine address to Ezekiel. This is the same phrase Jesus claims from Daniel 7:13 — the Ezekiel usage establishes the prophetic-human register; the Daniel usage establishes the cloud-coming-sovereignty register. Both are present in Jesus's self-designation.

### *Tav* mark (9:4) — sēmeion
Rendered "mark" — the LXX *sēmeion* = sign/mark. The Hebrew *tav* (X / +) preserved in the Greek as *sēmeion* = sign — the same word for Jesus's miracles in John. The forehead-sealing sign that the righteous bear.

### *Poimēn / poimainō* — shepherd / to shepherd
Rendered "shepherd" / "feed as shepherd" — preserving the full *poimēn* vocabulary that John 10 requires. Never softened to "care for" or "tend."

## STRUCTURAL NOTE — THE THREE MOVEMENTS OF EZEKIEL

Ezekiel has the clearest three-part theological structure of any prophetic book:

**Part 1 — Judgment on Israel (1–24):** The *doxa Kyriou* departs from the defiled Temple (chapters 8–11). Jerusalem's judgment is decreed and enacted (chapters 4–7 / 21–24). Ezekiel is the watchman whose ministry is silent during Jerusalem's siege (24:27 / 33:22).

**Part 2 — Oracles against the Nations (25–32):** Ammon / Moab / Edom / Philistia / Tyre / Egypt. The surrounding nations that gloated at Israel's fall are themselves judged. Chapter 28 (king of Tyre / Eden-fall) and chapters 29–32 (Egypt / Pharaoh as dragon) are the theological apex.

**Part 3 — Restoration of Israel (33–48):** The *doxa Kyriou* returns (43:2–5). New heart / new *pneuma* / dry bones raised (36:26 / 37:1–14). One shepherd-David (34:23 / 37:24). Gog/Magog defeated (38–39). New Temple and river of life (40–47). City named *Kyrios ekei* (48:35).

The structural arc is: **Glory departs → Nations judged → Glory returns.** This is the same arc as Revelation: **Seals/trumpets/bowls = judgment → Babylon falls → New Jerusalem descends with the glory of God.**
