## LXX-Aligned Restoration Table for Biblical Translation

By identifying these "chords" and applying a consistent English translation across both testaments, the fragmented reading experience created by modern translations is repaired. The Old Testament and New Testament share a single Greek lexical inheritance through the Septuagint (LXX). When that vocabulary is rendered consistently, the Bible reads as one continuous divine administration from Eden to the New Jerusalem.

This table is organized into twenty theological categories. For each chord, the table shows how standard translations break the lexical flow between testaments, and how the LXX-Aligned Restored English locks the text back together.
