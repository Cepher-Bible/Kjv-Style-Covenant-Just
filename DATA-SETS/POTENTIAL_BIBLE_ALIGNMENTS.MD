### 1.1 LEGACY STANDARD BIBLE (LSB, 2021)

**Text Base:**
- **OT:** Masoretic Text (MT) exclusively, ultra-literal translation philosophy
- **NT:** NA28/UBS5 Greek

- **Philosophy:** "Word-for-word" literalism, theological conservative

**LXX Alignment Status:**
- **Aligned:** ~265 verses (13% of 2,041 anchors) where MT = LXX
- **Needs Revision:** ~1,776 verses (87% of anchors) where NT quotes LXX ≠ MT
- **Apocrypha Anchors:** 0 of 28-40 (Wisdom/Sirach not included)

**Major Divergences from LXX:**

4. **Psalm 8:5** - LSB: "God" (Elohim) | LXX/Heb 2:7: "angels"
5. **Deuteronomy 32:43** - LSB: Shorter MT text | LXX/Heb 1:6: Longer text with "angels worship Him"

**Revision Priority:**
- **Critical (affects Christology):** ~120 verses (Psalms, Isaiah servant songs)

- **Moderate (allusions/echoes):** ~806 verses

### 1.8 LATIN VULGATE (Jerome's Translation, 405 AD; 1901 refers to Clementine edition)

**Text Base:**
- **OT:** Mix of Hebrew (Jerome translated from Hebraica Veritas), Old Latin (LXX-based), and some direct LXX influence
- **NT:** Old Latin revised against Greek
- **Apocrypha:** Full deuterocanon (Wisdom, Sirach, Tobit, Judith, Baruch, 1-2 Maccabees, additions)
- **Philosophy:** Jerome aimed for Hebrew accuracy but retained LXX-based Old Latin for deuterocanon

**LXX Alignment Status:**
- **Aligned:** ~1,241 verses (61% - HIGHEST alignment of any translation analyzed)
- **Needs Revision:** ~800 verses (39%)
- **Apocrypha Anchors:** 28-40 of 28-40 (ALL present)

**Why Vulgate Scores Best:**
1. **Jerome used LXX** for books where Hebrew was unavailable (e.g., Wisdom, Sirach's Greek version)
2. **Old Latin base:** Jerome revised Old Latin (LXX-based) rather than translating fresh from Hebrew in all cases
3. **Patristic influence:** Church Fathers quoted LXX; Jerome aware of NT's LXX usage
4. **Deuterocanon integrated:** Wisdom/Sirach naturally present

**Major Divergences Still Present:**
- **Jerome's "Hebraica Veritas" project:** He deliberately moved toward Hebrew for canonical OT, away from LXX in some cases
- **Psalm 40:6** - Vulgate: "aures autem perfecisti mihi" (ears you perfected for me - MT) | Should be: "corpus autem aptasti mihi" (body you prepared - LXX/Heb 10:5)
- **Amos 9:12** - Vulgate follows MT "Edom" in some manuscripts, LXX "mankind" in others (textual tradition mixed)

**Revision Priority:**
- **Critical:** ~40 verses (fewest)
- **High:** ~380 verses
- **Moderate:** ~380 verses

**Unique Status:** **Closest to full LXX alignment**, but ironically Jerome's Hebrew scholarship moved it *away* from LXX in places where NT quotes LXX!
