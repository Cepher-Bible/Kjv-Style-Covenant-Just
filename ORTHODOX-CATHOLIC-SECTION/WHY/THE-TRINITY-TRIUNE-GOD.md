**# Biblical Justification for the Triune God — Invisible Father, Eternal Son (Word & Image), Holy Spirit (Spirit-Breath)**  
**The Covenant Continuity of the One God in Three Persons: From Hint to Revelation**

**Purpose of this Document**  
This is the foundational pillar of the Covenant Toolkit. It traces the single, unbroken revelation of the one God in three Persons — the Invisible Father, the Eternal Son (Word & Image), and the Holy Spirit (Spirit-Breath) — from the first hint in creation to the full New Testament revelation. This reality undergirds every previous pillar: the Incarnation is the Son (Word & Image) becoming flesh, the Heart Law is the Spirit (Spirit-Breath) writing the law on hearts of flesh, the sacraments are visible signs of Covenant-Favor from the Triune God, and all signs, seals, and honor point back to the one God who is eternally three Persons.

**CRITICAL HONESTY DISCLAIMER**  
The precise terminology “one God in three Persons” and “one substance, three hypostases” is the Church’s faithful summary (Nicene Creed 325, Chalcedon 451). The **biblical reality** itself — the Father is God, the Son is God, the Spirit (Spirit-Breath) is God, yet there is only one God — is explicit and repeated in Scripture. We defend the biblical truth; the creedal language protects that truth against heresy.

**What this document defends:**  
- The Triune God as revealed in the Bible: Invisible Father, Eternal Son (Word & Image), Holy Spirit (Spirit-Breath) who proceeds from the Father and the Son.  

**What this document explicitly does NOT defend:**  
- Any view that divides God into three gods (tritheism), confuses the Persons (modalism), or subordinates the Son or Spirit (Spirit-Breath) (Arianism/Subordinationism).

## INTRODUCTION: The Triune God Story Begins with God’s Design

**The covenant story begins with the One God who is three Persons.**

**Genesis 1:1-3:**  
> "In the beginning God created the heavens and the earth. Now the earth was formless and empty… and the Spirit (Spirit-Breath) of God was hovering over the waters. And God said, 'Let there be light,' and there was light."

**From the very first verses, God (the Father) creates by His Word (the Son) and by His Spirit (Spirit-Breath).** The plurality is already present in the act of creation.

**The revelation of the Triune God does not create three gods; it guards and patterns the unity of the one God.**

**Deuteronomy 6:4 (Shema):**  
> "Hear, O Israel: The LORD our God, the LORD is one."

**The issue is never the number of Persons but covenant relationship.** The one God reveals Himself as Father, Son (Word & Image), and Spirit (Spirit-Breath) — with the Spirit (Spirit-Breath) proceeding from the Father and the Son — so that we may know Him truly and be brought into communion with Him.

## PART ONE: THE OLD COVENANT PATTERN — God Hints at His Triune Nature

**If the Trinity meant “three gods,” God contradicts Himself by repeatedly revealing plurality within strict monotheism.**

### A. Plurality in Creation (Genesis 1:26-27; 3:22; 11:7)

"Let us make mankind in our image… Let us go down and confuse their language." The one God speaks in plural while remaining one.

### B. The Angel of the Lord as Distinct yet Divine (Genesis 16:7-13; Exodus 3:2-6; Judges 13:18-22)

The Angel of the Lord speaks as God, is worshipped as God, and is distinguished from God. This is the pre-incarnate Son (Word & Image) appearing in human form.

### C. Wisdom as Personified (Proverbs 8:22-31)

Wisdom is with God at creation, “brought forth” yet eternal, delighting in the sons of men. This personified Wisdom points to the Eternal Son (Word & Image), the Wisdom of God (1 Cor 1:24, 30).

**Expanded Table: Legitimate vs Illegitimate Views of God’s Plurality**

| Aspect                  | Legitimate View (Old Covenant Hint)            | Illegitimate View                              |
|-------------------------|------------------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------|
| Origin                  | God reveals Himself as one yet plural          | Human invention of multiple gods              |
| Function                | Points to the coming full revelation           | Becomes polytheism or modalism                |
| Posture of Heart        | Awe and faith in the one God                   | Fear or denial of the plurality               |
| Theological Role        | Signpost to the Triune God                     | Replacement of the one God                    |
| New Covenant Parallel   | Hints → Full revelation in Father, Son (Word & Image), Spirit (Spirit-Breath) | Tritheism or modalism                         |

**Governing Safeguard:** Hints of plurality are legitimate; they become Nehushtan when turned into multiple gods or denied as impossible.

## PART TWO: THE PROPHETIC PROMISE — The Triune God Announced

**Isaiah 48:16; 63:9-10; Zechariah 2:10-11.** The Lord God and His Spirit (Spirit-Breath) send the Lord God. The Angel of His Presence saves them. God promises to live among His people. The promise moves from hint to clearer plurality within the one God.

**Reasoning Context:** The prophets never abandon monotheism. They deepen the revelation of the one God who is three Persons.

---

## PART THREE: THE FULFILLMENT IN CHRIST — The Decisive Turning Point

**Reasoning Context:** The invisible God becomes fully revealed in the three Persons.

### A. The Invisible Father
**John 1:18; 6:46; 1 Timothy 6:16:** No one has seen the Father. He is the invisible source who sends the Son and the Spirit (Spirit-Breath).

### B. The Eternal Son — Word & Image
**John 1:1,14:** The Word was with God and was God; the Word became flesh.  
**Colossians 1:15:** The Son is the Image of the invisible God.  
**Hebrews 1:3:** The Son is the exact representation of God’s being.

### C. The Holy Spirit (Spirit-Breath) — Proceeds from the Father and the Son
**John 15:26:** The Spirit (Spirit-Breath) proceeds from the Father and is sent by the Son.  
**Romans 8:9; Galatians 4:6:** The Spirit (Spirit-Breath) of the Son is sent into our hearts.  
**The Spirit (Spirit-Breath) is fully God (Acts 5:3-4) and proceeds from both the Father and the Son (filioque), bringing the life and presence of the Triune God.**

**Biblical Imagery of the Spirit (Spirit-Breath):**  
- **Breath / Wind:** John 3:8; Acts 2:2 (invisible yet powerful movement).  
- **Water:** John 7:37-39 (living water flowing from the believer).  
- **Fire:** Matthew 3:11; Acts 2:3 (purifying, empowering presence).

**Matthew 28:19 (Baptismal Formula):**  
> "Go and make disciples of all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit (Spirit-Breath)."

**The one name, three Persons.**

### D. Addressing the Core Objections

**“The Trinity is not in the Bible”:** The word is not, but the reality is: one God (Deut 6:4), three Persons who are each called God and who interact as distinct (Matt 3:16-17; John 14-16; 2 Cor 13:14).

**“Modalism” (one God in three modes):** The Persons interact with each other (the Father sends the Son; the Son prays to the Father; the Spirit (Spirit-Breath) intercedes).

**“Tritheism” (three gods):** Scripture repeatedly affirms one God while revealing three Persons.

**“Subordinationism” (Son/Spirit less than Father):** The Son is eternally begotten, the Spirit (Spirit-Breath) proceeds from the Father and the Son, yet they are equal in nature (Phil 2:6; John 1:1; Acts 5:3-4).

**Practical Abuse:** Where the Persons are confused or one is neglected, the Trinity is distorted.

## PART SIX: THE CONTROLLING PRINCIPLE — Safeguards Against Heresy & Neglect

### A. The Distinctions That Matter
**Worship belongs to the Triune God alone.**  
**Honor of the Son (Word & Image) and the work of the Spirit (Spirit-Breath) is permissible without confusion.**

### B. The Functional Tests
**Test 1:** Does this increase faith in the Triune God?  
**Test 2:** Is the heart trusting the Spirit (Spirit-Breath) or a distorted view?  
**Test 3: Nehushtan Threshold** — The moment one Person is treated as less than fully God or the Persons are confused.  
**Test 4: Supplication Test** — Does prayer flow to the Father through the Son by the Spirit (Spirit-Breath)?  
**Test 5: Covenant-Favor Test** — Are you resting in the grace (Covenant-Favor) of the Triune God?  
**Test 6: The Pharisee Test** — Does it make you merciful or judgmental?  
**Test 7: The Fruit Test** — Does it produce the fruit of the Spirit (Spirit-Breath)?


## PART EIGHT: Historical Development and Christian Liberty

The early Church confessed the Triune God immediately (Matt 28:19; 2 Cor 13:14). The precise formulation was clarified at Nicaea (325) and Constantinople (381) to protect the biblical truth against heresy. This is theological maturation, not innovation. Affirmation of the Triune God remains essential for all Christians.

## CONCLUSION: The Framework Complete

**The covenant story is the Triune God from beginning to end:**

- **Genesis 1:1-3:** One God creates by Word and Spirit (Spirit-Breath)  
- **Genesis 3:15:** The woman & her Seed promised  
- **Isaiah 7:14 / 9:6:** Immanuel, Mighty God foretold  
- **John 1:1,14:** The Word (Son) was God and became flesh  
- **Colossians 1:15; Hebrews 1:3:** Son (Word & Image) is the exact representation  
- **Matthew 28:19:** Baptize in the name of the Father, Son, and Holy Spirit (Spirit-Breath)  
- **2 Corinthians 13:14:** Grace (Covenant-Favor) of Christ, love of the Father, fellowship of the Spirit (Spirit-Breath)  
- **Revelation 21-22:** Face-to-face with the Triune God forever  

**The Triune God stands as the foundation** of the entire Toolkit. The Invisible Father sends the Son (Word & Image) who becomes flesh, and the Spirit (Spirit-Breath) — who proceeds from the Father and the Son — applies the work to us.

It becomes problematic only when the Persons are divided, confused, or one is neglected.

**The old hints pointed to the day when the one God would be fully revealed as three Persons; the New Testament is the celebration of that promise fulfilled. We worship the one God who is eternally Father, Son (Word & Image), and Holy Spirit (Spirit-Breath).**

**While the New Testament focuses on the internal Spirit (Spirit-Breath), it does not do so by discarding the revelation of the Triune God, but by deepening it. Because the Body is the Temple and the Triune God is the foundation, what we do with images, sacraments, and honor matters to the Heart.**

**The covenant logic stands stronger than ever:**

**Signposts are legitimate. Substitutes are not.**  
**Covenant-Favor and the Spirit (Spirit-Breath) may serve faith without becoming its object.**  
**All reality participates in the one God who is three Persons — the center of the entire Covenant Toolkit.**

**The structure holds. The safeguards function. The biblical arc — from hint to revelation — supports the entire Covenant Toolkit.**
