### **APPENDIX A: The Covenant Anchor Engine**  
**Translation Methodology & Terminology Framework for the Covenant Toolkit**

**Purpose of this Appendix**  
This appendix explains the translation and terminology framework (the “Covenant Anchor Engine”) used consistently across all **eight** main theological documents in the Toolkit. It is **not** a theological pillar itself — it is the operating system that ensures every pillar speaks with the same precise, covenant-rooted language.

The engine enforces one-way rails (OT: Hebrew → Greek → Latin → English; NT: Greek → Hebrew covenant-map → Latin → English), preserves sacred polysemy, never splits chesed, treats agape as distinct from chesed, reserves kinsman for goel/apolutrósis/geulah only, and anchors everything in the Covenant Spine before the nations.

**Cadence Flexible Terms (Context Determines Choice)**

- **Hell**: Hades (Realm-of-Dead), Gehenna (Judgment-Fire), Sheol (Grave/Underworld), Tartarus (Prison-Deep for angels), Abyss (Bottomless for demons) — selected by context.
- **Servant**: Ebed / doulos (legal ownership, not employment).
- **Submit**: Hypotassō (arranged-under, voluntary covenant-ordering) — distinct from kabash (subdue).
- **Fear of the Lord**: Yir'ah / phobos (Holy-Dread).
- **Love**: Ahavah / agapē (Covenant-Love) vs. philia (friendship) vs. storgē (family-bond).
- **Son**: Ben / huios (heir) vs. monogenēs (Only-Begotten, Trinitarian).

**Wisdom Stack Enforcement**

- Preserve sacred polysemy (tension is holy voltage).  
- OT flow: Hebrew → Greek → Latin → English.

### **APPENDIX B: Terminology Guide**  
**Quick Reference for Readers**

**CORE COVENANT TERMS**  
- **Loyal-Trust (faith)** — Faithful reliance on God’s promises (Hebrew *emunah*, Greek *pistis*)  
- **Victory-Proclamation (gospel)** — Good news of God’s kingdom reign (Hebrew *besorah*, Greek *euangelion*)  
- **Covenant-Assembly (church)** — God’s people gathered under covenant (Hebrew *qahal*, Greek *ekklesia*)  
- **Kinsman-Purchase (redemption)** — God buying back His people as family redeemer (Hebrew *geulah*, Greek *apolutrósis*)  
- **Covenant-Favor (grace)** — God’s unearned kindness within covenant relationship (Hebrew *chen*, Greek *charis*)  

**GOD’S NATURE & WORK**  
- **Spirit (Spirit-Breath)** — God’s living presence as breath, wind, fire, or water (Hebrew *ruach*, Greek *pneuma*)  
- **Glory-Weight** — The heavy, majestic presence of God (Hebrew *kabod*, Greek *doxa*)  
- **Covenant-Justice** — Right relationship under covenant (Hebrew *tzedakah* and *mishpat*, Greek *dikaiosynē*)  
- **Covering-Compassion (mercy)** — God’s tender compassion that covers sin (Hebrew *rachamim*, Greek *eleos*)  
- **Unfailing-Loyalty** — God’s steadfast covenant love that never fails (Hebrew *chesed*)  

**HUMAN RESPONSE**  
- **Turning (repentance)** — Returning to covenant relationship (Hebrew *teshuvah*, Greek *metanoia*)  
- **Holy-Dread (fear of the Lord)** — Awe-filled reverence for God (Hebrew *yir'ah*, Greek *phobos*)  
- **Living-Self (soul)** — The whole person, not just an immortal spirit (Hebrew *nephesh*, Greek *psychē*)
