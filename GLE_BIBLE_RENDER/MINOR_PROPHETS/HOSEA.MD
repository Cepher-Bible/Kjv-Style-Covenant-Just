**Quick notes on how it lands**

- **"legal-case"** (κρίσις / *iudicium*) — 4:1, 12:2 — Thayer: *krisis* = a separating, a formal legal decision, a judicial proceeding with named parties, charges, and verdict. LSJ: a judgment, a trial, a decision reached by formal process. The Lord does not merely rebuke Israel — he opens a formal covenant lawsuit. The structure of chapters 4 and 12 is forensic: plaintiff states charges, evidence is presented, verdict declared. The same covenant-lawsuit architecture as Micah 6 and Habakkuk. The prophetic book is a legal transcript.

- **"I will betroth you to myself"** (μνηστεύσομαί σε ἐμαυτῷ / *desponsabo te mihi*) — 2:19–20 — Thayer: *mnēsteuō* = to woo, to betroth, to enter into a formal covenant of marriage — the legal betrothal transaction that precedes the wedding. LSJ: to court, to betroth with full legal intention. The verb fires three times in two verses — in righteousness, in judgment, in mercy and compassions, in faithfulness. Not a renewal of feeling — a threefold covenant transaction restated with full legal weight. Each prepositional phrase names a term of the covenant. Vulgate *desponsabo* confirms — Jerome read the legal weight correctly here.

- **"I will ransom-release them from the hand of Hades"** (ἐκ χειρὸς ᾅδου ῥύσομαι αὐτούς / *de manu mortis liberabo eos*) — 13:14 — Thayer: *rhyomai* = to draw to oneself, to rescue by pulling out of danger — an active physical extraction; *hadēs* = the realm of the dead. LSJ: to drag out of, to rescue from a power that holds. Paul quotes this verse directly in 1 Corinthians 15:55 — "where O death is your victory, where O death is your sting?" The LXX render is the text Paul was reading. The verse is not a question of comfort in context — it is a ransom-extraction declaration followed immediately by a taunt thrown at the power being stripped. The sting and penalty of death are named as things being taken away in the act of ransom-release.

- **"out of Egypt I called my son"** (ἐξ Αἰγύπτου μετεκάλεσα τὰ τέκνα αὐτοῦ / *ex Aegypto vocavi filium meum*) — 11:1 — Thayer: *metakaleō* = to call away from, to summon out of — a deliberate calling-out from one location to another. LSJ: to call from one place to another, to summon away. Matthew 2:15 quotes this verse as fulfilled in the flight from Egypt and return — "out of Egypt I called my son." The LXX has *tekna autou* — his children. Matthew quotes it as *huion mou* — my son, singular. Matthew is reading the corporate-to-individual typological movement: what was true of Israel-as-son is now concentrated in the one true Son. The LXX plural preserves the original referent; Matthew's singular makes the typological application. Both are correct readings of different layers of the same text.

- **"where is your penalty O death — where is your sting O Hades"** (ποῦ ἡ δίκη σου θάνατε / ποῦ τὸ κέντρον σου ᾅδη) — 13:14 — Thayer: *dikē* = a legal penalty, a judicial sentence carried out; *kentron* = a sting, a goad, a sharp point that drives — used for the sting of scorpions and the goad that drives cattle. LSJ: *dikē* = justice, the penalty exacted by law; *kentron* = a sharp point, a sting, the driving instrument. These are not rhetorical questions of comfort — they are taunts thrown at defeated powers. *Dikē* is the legal penalty death holds over all living things — the sentence. *Kentron* is the instrument by which Hades drives souls into itself. The ransom-release in the previous clause strips both: the sentence is cancelled, the driving instrument is removed. Paul reads this correctly in 1 Cor 15 — the resurrection is the ransom-release event that makes the taunt possible.

**Drift eliminated:** Check 3 fired on 13:14 — "where O death is your victory" pew-register from KJV/Paul echo suppressed the legal-penalty weight of *dikē*; restored as "penalty" — the judicial sentence death holds. Check 1 fired on 2:19–20 — "I will betroth" almost became "I will be married to" losing the legal-transaction precision of *mnēsteuō*; restored as formal betrothal covenant. Check 6 fired on 11:1 — Matthew's singular *huion mou* quotation almost absorbed the LXX plural *tekna*; both preserved with typological note. Check 5 fired on 4:6 — "my people are destroyed for lack of knowledge" too pew-ready from KJV register; rewritten from Greek — "my people were made like those having no knowledge" — the passive construction is the LXX reading, not an active destruction. Check 3 fired on 13:14 — *kentron* as "sting" preserved but goad/driving-instrument meaning added to restore Hades as active power being stripped of its instrument, not merely painful.
