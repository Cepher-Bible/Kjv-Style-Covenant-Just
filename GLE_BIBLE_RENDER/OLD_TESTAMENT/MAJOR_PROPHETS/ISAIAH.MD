\v 1 The vision which Isaiah the son of Amoz saw concerning Judah and Jerusalem in the days of Uzziah and Jotham and Ahaz and Hezekiah who were kings of Judah.
\p
\v 2 Hear O heaven and give ear O earth — because the Lord has spoken: I reared sons and lifted them up but they rejected me.
\v 3 An ox knows its owner and a donkey the manger of its lord — but Israel did not know me and the people did not understand me.
\v 4 Woe to the sinful nation — the people full of sins — the evil seed — the lawless sons. You abandoned the Lord and provoked the Holy One of Israel to anger — you turned away backward.
\v 5 Why will you be struck any longer — adding lawlessness? Every head is in pain and every heart in grief.
\v 6 From feet to head there is no wholeness in it — neither wound nor bruise nor festering sore — they have not been bound up nor treated with oil.
\v 7 Your land is desolate — your cities burned with fire — your land — strangers devour it before you and it is made desolate having been overthrown by alien peoples.
\v 8 The daughter of Zion will be abandoned as a shelter in a vineyard and as a storehouse in a cucumber-field and as a city being besieged.
\v 9 And if the Lord Almighty had not left us a seed we would have become as Sodom and we would have been made like Gomorrah.
\p
\v 10 Hear the word of the Lord O rulers of Sodom — give ear to the law of our God O people of Gomorrah.
\v 11 What is the multitude of your sacrifices to me? says the Lord. I am full of ascending-offerings of rams and the fat of lambs and the blood of bulls and goats I do not want — not even if you come to appear before me.
\v 12 For who required these things from your hands? You will no longer trample my court.
\v 13 If you bring fine flour it is in vain — incense is an abomination to me — your new moons and your sabbaths and your great day I cannot endure — fasting and rest and your new moons and your feasts my soul hates.
\v 14 You have become a satiation to me — I will no longer forgive your sins.
\v 15 When you stretch out your hands I will turn away my eyes from you — and even if you multiply your petition I will not hear — for your hands are full of blood.
\v 16 Wash — become clean — remove the evil of your practices from before my eyes — cease from your evil deeds —
\v 17 learn to do good — seek judgment — rescue the wronged one — judge the orphan — justify the widow.
\p
\v 18 Come now and let us reason together says the Lord — and if your sins are as scarlet I will make them white as snow — and if they are red as crimson I will make them white as wool.
\v 19 And if you are willing and hear me you will eat the good of the land —
\v 20 but if you are unwilling and do not hear me a sword will devour you — for the mouth of the Lord has spoken these things.
\p
\v 21 How did the faithful city Zion become a prostitute — full of judgment in which righteousness lodged — but now murderers.
\v 22 Your silver has become worthless — your wine-dealers mix wine with water.
\v 23 Your rulers are disobedient — partners of thieves — loving gifts — pursuing recompense — not judging the orphan and the legal-case of the widow not coming before them.
\p
\v 24 Therefore thus says the Lord God Almighty — the Mighty One of Israel: Woe — I will be comforted over my adversaries and I will exact vengeance from my enemies.
\v 25 And I will bring my hand upon you and purify you with fire unto cleanness — and I will destroy those who disobey and remove all the lawless ones from you —
\v 26 and I will establish your judges as before and your counselors as from the beginning — and after these things you will be called a city of righteousness a faithful mother-city Zion.
\v 27 For her captives will be saved with judgment and her captivity with mercy.
\v 28 And the lawless ones and the sinners will be crushed together — and those who abandon the Lord will be utterly consumed.
\p
\v 29 Because they will be ashamed of their idols which they desired — and they were ashamed of the gardens which they coveted.
\v 30 Because they will be as a terebinth that has cast its leaves and as a garden that has no water.
\v 31 And their strength will be as a thread of tow and their works as sparks — and the lawless ones and the sinners will be burned together and there will be no one to extinguish.

\p
\v 1 The word that came to Isaiah the son of Amoz concerning Judah and concerning Jerusalem.
\p
\v 2 And it will be in the last days the mountain of the Lord will be made visible and the house of God will be upon the tops of the mountains and will be lifted up above the hills — and all the nations will come to it.
\v 3 And many peoples will go and say: Come let us go up to the mountain of the Lord and to the house of the God of Jacob — and he will announce to us his way and we will walk in his paths. For out of Zion the law will go forth and the word of the Lord out of Jerusalem.
\v 4 And he will judge between the nations and reprove many peoples — and they will beat their swords into plowshares and their spears into pruning-hooks — and nation will not take up sword against nation and they will no longer learn war.
\p
\v 5 And now O house of Jacob come and let us walk in the light of the Lord.
\v 6 For he abandoned his people the house of Israel — because their land was filled as at the beginning with divinations as the land of the Philistines and many foreign children were born to them.
\v 7 And their land was filled with silver and gold — and there was no number of their treasures — and their land was filled with horses and there was no number of their chariots.
\v 8 And their land was filled with abominations — the works of their hands — and they bowed down to what their fingers made.
\v 9 And the small man bowed down and the great man was humbled — and I will not forgive them.
\p
\v 10 And now enter into the rocks and hide in the earth from the face of the fear of the Lord and from the glory of his strength — when he rises to terrify the earth.
\v 11 For the eyes of the Lord are high — but man is lowly — and the height of men will be humbled and the Lord alone will be exalted in that day.
\v 12 For the day of the Lord Almighty is upon every one who is arrogant and proud and upon every one who is lifted up — and they will be brought low —
\v 13 and upon every cedar of Lebanon that is high and lifted up and upon every oak of Bashan —
\v 14 and upon every high mountain and upon every high hill —
\v 15 and upon every high tower and upon every high wall —
\v 16 and upon every ship of Tarshish and upon every conspicuous sight of beauty.
\v 17 And every man will be humbled and the height of men will fall — and the Lord alone will be exalted in that day.
\v 18 And all the made-things they will hide with their hands —
\v 19 bringing them into the caves and into the clefts of the rocks and into the hollows of the earth from the face of the fear of the Lord and from the glory of his strength when he rises to terrify the earth.
\v 20 For in that day a man will cast away his silver abominations and his gold abominations which they made as useless and worthless things —
\v 21 to enter into the clefts of the solid rocks and into the hollows of the crags from the face of the fear of the Lord and from the glory of his strength when he rises to terrify the earth.

\v 1 For look — the Lord God Almighty will remove from Jerusalem and from Judah the mighty one and the mighty woman — the strength of bread and the strength of water —
\v 2 the giant and the strong man and the warrior and the judge and the prophet and the counselor and the elder —
\v 3 the captain of fifty and the honored counselor and the wise architect and the prudent hearer.
\v 4 And I will set up young men as their rulers — and mockers will rule over them.
\v 5 And the people will fall — man upon man and each upon his neighbor — the young man will be insolent against the elder and the dishonorable against the honorable.
\v 6 Because a man will take hold of his brother in the house of his father saying: You have a garment — you be our ruler and let this fallen thing be under your hand.
\v 7 And he will answer in that day and say: I will not be your healer — for there is no bread nor garment in my house — you will not make me ruler of the people.
\v 8 For Jerusalem has fallen and Judah has fallen — and their tongues spoke with lawlessness — disobedient toward the Lord.
\v 9 Therefore now their glory has been humbled and the shame of their face has withstood them — and they proclaimed their sin as Sodom and made it visible — woe to their soul for they have devised an evil counsel against themselves.
\v 10 Say to the righteous: It is good — for they will eat the fruit of their works.
\v 11 Woe to the lawless one — evil things — for the recompenses of his hands will be rendered to him.
\v 12 O my people — your exactors strip you and mockers rule over you. O my people — those who call you blessed lead you astray and pervert the path of your feet.
\p
\v 13 But now the Lord will stand for judgment and will set his people to be judged.
\v 14 The Lord himself will enter into judgment with the elders of the people and with the rulers — but why did you set my vineyard on fire and the plunder of the poor is in your houses?
\v 15 Why do you wrong my people and shame the face of the poor?
\p
\v 16 Thus says the Lord: Because the daughters of Zion were lifted up and walked with a high neck and with winking of eyes — sweeping their garments and playing with their feet together —
\v 17 the Lord will humble the leading daughters of Zion and the Lord will uncover their form in that day.
\v 18 And in that day the Lord will take away the adornment of their garments and the braids and the fringe and the crescents —
\v 19 and the necklace and the ornaments and the bracelets and the hair-covering —
\v 20 and the fine head-covering and the ankle-chains and the arm-chains and the woven garments and the transparent garments —
\v 21 and the rings and the earrings —
\v 22 and the fine garments and the purple ones and the linen garments and the purple head-covering and the lightweight mantles —
\v 23 and the transparent summer garments and those of white linen — the fine linens and the blue ones —
\v 24 and instead of a sweet smell there will be dust — and instead of a sash you will gird yourself with a rope — and instead of a golden ornament upon your head you will have baldness because of your works — and instead of a purple robe you will gird yourself with sackcloth.
\v 25 And your most beautiful son whom you love will fall by the sword — and your strong men will fall by the sword —
\v 26 and the storehouses will mourn and be emptied — and she will be left alone and will be struck to the ground.

\p
\v 2 And in that day God will shine in counsel with glory upon the earth — to lift up and to glorify the remnant of Israel.
\v 3 And it will be that those who are left in Zion and those who remain in Jerusalem will be called holy — all those who are written for life in Jerusalem.

\c 5
\p
\v 1 Now I will sing to my beloved a song of my beloved concerning his vineyard. My beloved had a vineyard on a horn on a fat hill.
\v 2 And I made a wall round it and fenced it in and planted a choice vine and built a tower in the midst of it and dug a pit in it — and I waited for it to produce grapes and it produced thorns.
\v 3 And now O inhabitants of Jerusalem and O man of Judah — judge between me and my vineyard.
\v 4 What shall I do yet to my vineyard that I have not done to it? I waited for it to produce grapes — why did it produce thorns?
\v 5 And now I will tell you what I will do to my vineyard — I will remove its wall and it will be for plundering — and I will tear down its fence and it will be for trampling.
\v 6 And I will leave my vineyard and it will not be pruned or dug — and thorns will come up upon it as upon barren ground — and I will command the clouds not to rain rain upon it.
\v 7 For the vineyard of the Lord Almighty is the house of Israel and the man of Judah is a new beloved shoot — I waited for him to do judgment and he did lawlessness — and not righteousness but a cry.
\p
\v 8 Woe to those who join house to house and who add field to field — so as to take something from their neighbor. Will you dwell alone upon the land?
\v 9 For these things were heard in the ears of the Lord Almighty — for even if many houses become great and good there will be no inhabitants in them.
\v 10 For where ten yoke of oxen work the ground will produce one jar and he who sows six homers will produce three measures.
\p
\v 11 Woe to those who rise up in the morning and pursue strong drink — who remain until late in the evening for wine inflames them.
\v 12 For they drink wine with harp and lyre and drum and flute — but they do not look upon the works of the Lord and they do not consider the works of his hands.
\v 13 Therefore my people were taken captive because they did not have knowledge — and their dead became a great multitude from hunger and thirst.
\v 14 And Hades enlarged its soul and opened its mouth without ceasing — and the glorious ones and the great and the rich and the pestilent ones will go down into it.
\v 15 And the man will be humbled and the great man will be dishonored and the proud eye will be brought low —
\v 16 and the Lord Almighty will be exalted in judgment and the holy God will be glorified in righteousness.
\v 17 And those who were scattered will be fed as bulls and lambs will feed upon the waste places of the rich.
\p
\v 18 Woe to those who draw sins toward themselves as with a long rope and lawless deeds as with a strap of a heifer's yoke —
\v 19 who say: Let him hasten quickly what he will do so that we may see — and let the counsel of the Holy One of Israel come so that we may know.
\p
\v 20 Woe to those who call evil good and good evil — who make darkness light and light darkness — who make bitter sweet and sweet bitter.
\p
\v 21 Woe to those who are wise in their own sight and knowing before themselves.
\p
\v 22 Woe to those who are strong in wine — the mighty ones who mix strong drink —
\v 23 who justify the impious for gifts and take away the righteousness of the righteous.
\p
\v 24 Therefore as straw is burned by a coal of fire and as dry grass is scorched by a flame — their root will be as chaff and their flower will go up as dust — for they rejected the law of the Lord Almighty and they provoked the word of the Holy One of Israel to anger.
\v 25 And the Lord Almighty was made angry with wrath against his people and he stretched out his hand against them and struck them — and the mountains were troubled and their corpses were as dung in the middle of the road. In all these things his wrath was not turned away but his hand was still lifted up.
\p
\v 26 Therefore he will lift up a signal to the nations from far off and will whistle for them from the end of the earth — and look they come swiftly with lightness.
\v 27 They will not hunger nor be weary nor will they slumber nor sleep — neither will the girdle of their loins be loosened nor will the strap of their sandals be broken.
\v 28 Whose arrows are sharp and their bows bent — the hooves of their horses as solid rock — their wheels as a whirlwind.
\v 29 They rush as lions and stand near as lion-cubs — and he will seize and roar as a wild-animal and cast out and no one will rescue.
\v 30 And he will roar over them in that day as the sound of the swelling sea — and they will look to the land and look — darkness of distress and the light has grown dark in her mist.

\v 1 And it came to pass in the year that king Uzziah died I saw the Lord sitting upon a throne high and lifted up — and the house was full of his glory.
\v 2 And seraphim stood round about him — each had six wings — with two they covered their face and with two they covered their feet and with two they flew.
\v 3 And they cried out one to another and said: Holy holy holy is the Lord Almighty — the whole earth is full of his glory.
\v 4 And the lintel was lifted from the voice of those who cried out and the house was filled with smoke.
\v 5 And I said: Woe is me — I am undone — because I am a man of unclean lips and I dwell in the midst of a people of unclean lips — and my eyes saw the king the Lord Almighty.
\v 6 And one of the seraphim was sent to me and in his hand was a live coal which he had taken with tongs from the altar.
\v 7 And he touched my mouth and said: Look — this has touched your lips and will take away your lawless deeds and will purify your sins.
\p
\v 8 And I heard the voice of the Lord saying: Whom shall I send and who will go to this people? And I said: Look — I am here — send me.
\v 9 And he said: Go and say to this people — hearing you will hear and will not understand — and looking you will look and will not see.
\v 10 For the heart of this people was made fat and their ears were made heavy and they closed their eyes — lest they see with their eyes and hear with their ears and understand with their heart and turn and I heal them.
\p
\v 11 And I said: Until when O Lord? And he said: Until the cities are utterly destroyed without inhabitant and the houses without men and the land is left desolate.
\v 12 And after these things God will lengthen the men and those who are left upon the land will be multiplied.
\v 13 And yet upon it is a tenth — and again it will be for plundering as a terebinth and as an acorn when it falls from its husk.

\p
\v 1 And it came to pass in the days of Ahaz the son of Jotham the son of Uzziah king of Judah — Rezin king of Aram and Pekah the son of Remaliah king of Israel went up against Jerusalem to make war against it and were not able to take it.
\v 2 And the house of David was told saying: Aram has camped in Ephraim. And his soul was shaken and the soul of his people as a tree of the forest is shaken by wind.
\v 3 And the Lord said to Isaiah: Go out now to meet Ahaz — you and your son Jasub who is left — to the pool of the upper road of the fuller's field.
\v 4 And you will say to him: Take heed to be quiet and do not fear — and let not your soul be weakened because of these two smoking firebrands — for when my fierce anger turns away I will heal again.
\v 5 And the son of Aram and the son of Remaliah — because they devised an evil counsel against you saying:
\v 6 We will go up against Judah and speak to them and turn them to our side — and we will make the son of Tabeel king over it.
\v 7 Thus says the Lord Almighty: This counsel will not stand nor come to pass.
\v 8 But the head of Aram is Damascus and the head of Damascus is Rezin — and yet sixty-five years and the kingdom of Ephraim will be removed from being a people.
\v 9 And the head of Ephraim is Samaria and the head of Samaria is the son of Remaliah — and if you do not trust you will not at all understand.
\p
\v 10 And the Lord spoke again to Ahaz saying:
\v 11 Ask for yourself a sign from the Lord your God — into the depth or into the height.
\v 12 And Ahaz said: I will not ask nor will I test the Lord.
\v 13 And Isaiah said: Hear now O house of David — is it a small thing to you to contend with men that you also contend with the Lord?
\v 14 Therefore the Lord himself will give you a sign — look the virgin will conceive in the womb and will bear a son and you will call his name Emmanuel.
\v 15 Butter and honey he will eat before he knows or chooses evil — he will choose the good.
\v 16 For before the child knows good or evil he disobeys evil to choose the good — and the land will be abandoned which you dread because of the two kings.
\v 17 But God will bring upon you and upon your people and upon the house of your father days which have not come since the day Ephraim took itself away from Judah — the king of the Assyrians.
\p
\v 18 And it will be in that day the Lord will whistle for the flies that rule over part of the river of Egypt and for the bee that is in the land of the Assyrians.
\v 19 And they will all come and will rest in the ravines of the land and in the clefts of the rocks and in the caves and in every ravine and in every wood.
\v 20 In that day the Lord will shave with the razor hired from the other side of the river — the king of the Assyrians — the head and the hairs of the feet — and he will remove the beard.
\v 21 And it will be in that day a man will rear a young cow and two sheep —
\v 22 and from the abundance of the milk produced he will eat butter — for everyone who is left upon the land will eat butter and honey.
\v 23 And it will be in that day every place where there were a thousand vines worth a thousand silver pieces — they will be for thorns and briers.
\v 24 With arrow and bow they will enter there — for all the land will be thorns and briers.
\v 25 And every mountain that is tilled with a hoe — fear of thorns and briers will not come upon it — and it will be for the sending out of oxen and for the trampling of sheep.

\v 21 And it will pass through the land hard pressed and hungry — and it will come to pass when they are hungry they will be grieved and will curse their king and their gods and will look upward —
\v 22 and they will look to the earth and look — affliction and distress and darkness and constraint and darkness — unable to see — and he will not be able to fly out of his distress.

\p
\v 1 Woe to those who write wickedness — for when they write they write wickedness —
\v 2 turning aside the judgment of the poor and snatching the decision from the lowly of my people — so that the widow becomes their plunder and the orphan their prey.
\v 3 And what will you do in the day of the visitation — for the affliction will come to you from far off — and to whom will you flee for help? And where will you leave your glory?
\v 4 So as not to fall into captivity — they will fall among the slain. In all these things his anger was not turned away but his hand was still lifted up.
\p
\v 5 Woe to the Assyrian — the rod of my wrath — and anger is in their hands.
\v 6 I will send him to the lawless nation and I will direct him against my people — to plunder their plunder and to take their spoil — and to trample the cities and to make them as dust of the road.
\v 7 But he does not purpose thus and does not think thus in his soul — but his mind will be to destroy and to cut off nations not a few.
\v 8 And if they say to him: You alone are the ruler —
\v 9 and he will say: Did I not take the land above Babylon and Chalanes where the tower was built — and I took Arabia and Damascus and Samaria —
\v 10 as I took them so I will also take all the kingdoms — wail you carved-images in Jerusalem and in Samaria.
\v 11 For as I did to Samaria and to her made-things so I will also do to Jerusalem and to her idols.
\p
\v 12 And it will come to pass when the Lord finishes all that he does upon mount Zion and upon Jerusalem — I will visit upon the arrogant heart of the king of Assyria and upon the pride of the height of his eyes.
\v 13 For he said: With the strength of my hand I will act and with the wisdom of my understanding I will remove the boundaries of nations and plunder their strength.
\v 14 And I will shake the inhabited world with my hand as a nest and as abandoned eggs I will pick them up — and there is no one who will escape or speak against me.
\v 15 Shall the axe be glorified apart from the one who cuts with it — or shall the saw be lifted up apart from the one who drags it? As if someone raised a rod or a staff — and it is not wood.
\v 16 And the Lord Almighty will send dishonor upon your honor — and upon your glory a burning fire will be kindled.
\v 17 And the light of Israel will be for a fire and it will sanctify him with a burning flame — and it will devour the forest as hay on that day.
\v 18 The forest will be consumed and the orchard and the soul together — and he will be as the one who flees from a burning flame.
\v 19 And the remaining trees of his forest will be in number and a child will be able to write them.

\v 20 And it will be in that day the remnant of Israel will no longer join with the one who struck them but they will trust upon the Holy One of Israel in truth.
\v 21 And the remnant of Jacob will trust upon the mighty God.
\v 22 And if the people of Israel become as the sand of the sea — the remnant of them will be saved — for he will bring to completion and cut short a word in righteousness.
\v 23 Because the Lord will make a completed word upon all the inhabited world.
\p
\v 24 Therefore thus says the Lord God Almighty: Do not fear O my people who dwell in Zion because of the Assyrians — for he will strike you with a rod and he will lift up his staff against you on the way of Egypt.
\v 25 For yet a little while and the wrath will cease — and my wrath upon their counsel.
\v 26 And God will stir up upon them a scourge — according to the blow of Midian in the place of affliction — and his wrath will be on the road of the sea on the way of Egypt.
\v 27 And it will be in that day his burden will be removed from your shoulder and his yoke from your neck — and the yoke will be rotted from your shoulders.
\p
\v 28 For he will arrive at the city of Aggai and will pass into Makkedah.
\v 29 And he will carry his vessels through and will pass through the ford — and fear will seize Ramah the city of Saul.
\v 30 The daughter of Gallim will flee — Laish will hear — Anathoth will hear.
\v 31 Madmenah was shaken — the inhabitants of Gibeah fled.
\v 32 Exhort one another today to remain in the road — exhort the mountain the daughter of Zion and the hills in Jerusalem.
\v 33 Look — the Lord God Almighty will shake the glorious ones with force and the arrogant in pride will be crushed and the lofty ones will be humbled.
\v 34 And the lofty ones will fall by the sword and Lebanon will fall with its lofty ones.

\c 11
\p
\v 1 And a rod will come out of the root of Jesse and a flower will come up from his root.
\v 2 And the breath-of-life of God will rest upon him — the breath-of-life of wisdom and understanding — the breath-of-life of counsel and strength — the breath-of-life of knowledge and godliness.
\v 3 The breath-of-life of the fear of God will fill him — he will not judge according to appearance nor reprove according to report.
\v 4 But he will judge the lowly with righteousness and reprove the lowly of the earth — and he will strike the earth with the word of his mouth and with the breath of his lips he will destroy the impious.
\v 5 And he will be girded with righteousness around his loins and with truth wrapped around his sides.
\v 6 And a wolf will feed together with a lamb and a leopard will lie down together with a young goat — and a calf and a young bull and a lion will feed together — and a small child will lead them.
\v 7 And an ox and a bear will feed together and their young will be together — and a lion will eat straw as an ox.
\v 8 And an infant will put his hand on the hole of an asp and upon the nest of young asps — and the child who has been weaned from the breast will put his hand.
\v 9 And they will not harm nor will they be able to destroy anyone upon my holy mountain — for the whole earth will be filled with the knowledge of the Lord as much water covering the seas.
\p
\v 10 And it will be in that day the root of Jesse will rise up and he who rises up to rule the nations — in him the nations will hope and his rest will be honor.
\v 11 And it will be in that day the Lord will again show his hand to be zealous for the remnant that is left of the people — who were left from the Assyrians and from Egypt and from Babylon and from Ethiopia and from the Elamites and from the rising of the sun and from Arabia.
\v 12 And he will lift up a signal to the nations and will gather the lost ones of Israel and the scattered ones of Judah will be gathered from the four wings of the earth.
\v 13 And the jealousy of Ephraim will be removed and the enemies of Judah will perish — Ephraim will not be jealous of Judah and Judah will not afflict Ephraim.
\v 14 And they will fly in the ships of foreigners — they will plunder the sea together — and those from the rising of the sun and from Arabia — and Edom and Moab will be the first to put their hands on it.
\v 15 And the Lord will make the sea of Egypt desolate and he will lay his hand upon the river with a mighty wind and will strike the seven gullies of it and will cause men to cross with sandals.
\v 16 And there will be a passable road for the remnant of my people who were left from the Assyrians — as when there was a road for Israel in the day they came up from the land of Egypt.

\c 12
\p
\v 1 And in that day you will say: I will bless you O Lord — for you were angry with me and turned away your wrath and had mercy upon me.
\v 2 Look — God is my savior — I will trust and not be afraid — for my glory and my praise is the Lord — and he became my salvation.
\p
\v 3 And you will draw water with joy from the springs of salvation.
\p
\v 4 And in that day you will say: Sing to the Lord and call upon his name — declare his glorious things among the nations — remember that his name has been lifted up.
\v 5 Sing the name of the Lord — for he has done lofty things — declare these things in all the earth.
\v 6 Exult and rejoice O dwelling of Zion — for the Holy One of Israel has been magnified in your midst.
