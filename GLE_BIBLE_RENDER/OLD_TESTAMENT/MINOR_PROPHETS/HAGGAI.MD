**Quick notes on how it lands**

- **"set your hearts upon your ways"** (θέσθε δὴ τὰς καρδίας ὑμῶν / *ponite corda vestra*) — 1:5, 1:7, 2:15, 2:18 — Thayer: *tithēmi* = to place, to set down with intention, to position deliberately; *kardia* = the heart as the seat of thought, will, and purpose — not mere emotion but the directing center of the whole person. LSJ: to set, to place in position; *kardia* = the inner life, the seat of intelligence and decision. The phrase fires four times — a structural refrain. Not "reflect on your situation" — set your directing-center upon your ways. The command is a posture-realignment instruction. The Lord is not asking for feeling. He is commanding a repositioning of the whole decision-making apparatus toward a specific object.

- **"breath-of-life stirred up"** (ἐξήγειρεν τὸ πνεῦμα / *suscitavit Dominus spiritum*) — 1:14 — Thayer: *exegeirō* = to rouse out of sleep, to raise up from inactivity, to stir from a dormant state; *pneuma* = the breath, the animating life-force. LSJ: to wake thoroughly, to raise up completely. The Lord stirs the *pneuma* of Zerubbabel, Joshua, and the remnant simultaneously — three separate breath-of-life activations in one verse. Not inspiration in the modern softened sense — a rousing from dormancy into activity. The same verb used for resurrection rousing. The people were not ignorant. They were inert. The Lord reactivates what was present but not moving.

- **"yet once more — a little while"** (ἔτι ἅπαξ — αὐτὸς μικρὸν / *adhuc unum modicum*) — 2:6 — Thayer: *hapax* = once, one single time, the singular unrepeatable occurrence; *mikron* = small, brief, a little while. LSJ: *hapax* = once and once only; *mikron* = a small interval of time. The Hebrews author quotes this verse directly (Heb 12:26-27) — "yet once more I will shake." The *hapax* is the theological load-bearer: the coming shaking is the final singular event, the one-time cosmic disruption that ends the present order. The "little while" is not comfort — it is compression. The final shaking is imminent in prophetic time. Vulgate *adhuc unum modicum* confirms the singular-final weight — Jerome read *hapax* correctly here.

- **"seal-ring"** (σφραγίς / *signaculum*) — 2:23 — Thayer: *sphragis* = a seal, a signet ring, the instrument of personal authentication and legal authority — what a king uses to authorize documents and transfer authority. LSJ: a seal, a stamp of ownership, the mark that validates and secures. L&S: *signaculum* = a small seal, a mark of ownership. The closing word of the book is a signet-ring appointment. Zerubbabel — the Davidic heir, the governor under Persian authority — is placed as the Lord's personal seal-ring. The signet ring was the king's authority-transfer instrument. This is a Davidic covenant re-commissioning delivered to the man standing in the rubble of the returned exile. The Messianic resonance is structural — the Davidic line is not dead, it has been placed as a seal.

- **"chosen things of all the nations"** (τὰ ἐκλεκτὰ πάντων τῶν ἐθνῶν / *desideratum cunctis gentibus*) — 2:7 — Thayer: *eklektos* = chosen, selected, the picked-out ones — those specifically selected from among a larger group. LSJ: chosen, select, the best of a class. The Vulgate diverges here — Jerome renders *desideratum* = the desired thing, the longed-for one — which became the basis for the traditional "desire of all nations" messianic reading in Western Christianity. The LXX Rahlfs-Hanhart reads *eklekta* = the chosen things, plural — the selected valuables of the nations flowing into the temple. Two legitimate readings from the same Hebrew — Jerome's Latin pulled toward singular messianic desire, LXX pulled toward plural covenant ingathering. Both traditions are downstream of genuine textual ambiguity. Rendered from LXX base as plural chosen things — Jerome's reading noted as divergence not contamination.

**Drift eliminated:** Check 1 fired on *pneuma* — "spirit" smoothed; restored to "breath-of-life" with rousing-from-dormancy weight throughout. Check 3 fired on 2:23 — "signet ring" rendered too decoratively in first pass; restored *sphragis* as legal authority-transfer instrument with full Davidic covenant weight. Check 2 fired on 2:7 — Vulgate divergence on *eklekta/desideratum* is legitimate tradition not contamination; both readings noted, LXX base rendered, Jerome's messianic pull flagged in notes. Check 6 fired on 1:13 — "messenger of the Lord among the messengers" — *aggelos* double construction almost compressed; restored both instances to preserve the deliberate identification of Haggai as a divine-messenger-class prophet.
