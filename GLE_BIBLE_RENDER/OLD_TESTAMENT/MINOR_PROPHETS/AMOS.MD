**Quick notes on how it lands**

- **"hidden-counsel"** (μυστήριον / *sacramentum*) — 3:7 — Thayer: a secret once hidden now disclosed to the initiated; L&S: a sacred oath, a binding secret. The Lord reveals his *mysterion* to his servants the prophets before acting. This is not casual disclosure — it is covenantal intelligence-sharing with those bound to the oath. The prophets are not reporters; they are cleared insiders.

- **"adamant"** (ἀδάμας / *adamas*) — 7:7–8 — Thayer: the hardest metal, unconquerable, diamond-hard; L&S: the hardest substance known, used for the most unyielding standard. The LXX uses *adamas* where the MT has a disputed word — Rahlfs-Hanhart preserves the hardest-substance reading. The Lord sets the hardest possible standard in the midst of Israel — not a plumb line of mercy but a diamond-hard measure of judgment. The standard is unconquerable and unyielding.

- **"famine of hearing the word"** (λιμὸς τοῦ ἀκοῦσαι / *fames audiendi*) — 8:11 — Thayer: *limos* = hunger, famine, destitution; *akouō* = to hear with understanding, to give ear to. L&S: *fames* = hunger, craving; *audiendi* = of the hearing, of the receiving. The coming famine is not silence — it is searching and not finding. The word will be sought but unavailable. This is the most terrifying judgment in the book: not destruction but withdrawal of access to the voice.

- **"tent of David"** (σκηνὴν Δαυίδ / *tabernaculum David*) — 9:11 — Thayer: *skēnē* = a tent, a temporary dwelling, a booth; L&S: *tabernaculum* = a tent, a temporary military encampment. The fallen tent is the Davidic dynasty — collapsed, temporary-looking, not a permanent structure. The promise is its reconstruction. James quotes this verse in Acts 15:16 to argue that Gentile inclusion fulfills Amos — the rebuilt tent of David is the community through which all nations seek the Lord. The LXX render "remnant of men and all the nations" is broader than the MT here — the Rahlfs-Hanhart base preserves the universal reach James was reading from.

- **"ascending-offerings"** (ὁλοκαυτώματα / *holocausta*) — 5:22 — Thayer: whole-burnt offerings, the offerings that ascend entirely; L&S: *holocaustum* = total consumption by fire, nothing retained. The Lord refuses even these — the most complete form of offering, where nothing is held back. Total sacrifice rejected because the social order is corrupt. The offering that ascends means nothing while judgment rolls down like water in the next verse.

**Drift eliminated:** Check 3 fired on 3:7 — "secret" smoothed over the covenant-intelligence weight of *mysterion*; restored to hidden-counsel with oath-binding precision. Check 5 fired on 7:7 — "plumb line" (standard MT/modern render) replaced with "adamant" per Rahlfs-Hanhart LXX — the hardest-substance reading is the harder and older render. Check 1 fired on 5:24 — "let justice roll down like waters" too pew-ready from King/KJV register; rewritten from the Greek base. Check 3 fired on 9:11–12 — Gentile-inclusion reach of LXX "remnant of men" broader than MT; preserved per Rahlfs-Hanhart as the base James was reading in Acts 15.
