"We're using Hebrew **context** to understand what the Greek NT **means**. The NT authors were Jews who thought in Hebrew categories and quoted the LXX. Understanding the Hebrew semantic field behind Greek words helps us read the NT more accurately.

This does NOT impose Torah observance. Paul explicitly rejects that in Galatians. We are under the New Covenant, not the Old. But the New Covenant is incomprehensible without understanding the Old."

### **Objection 2: "Why not just use normal English words?"**

**Response:**
"Because 'normal English' often loses biblical meaning. Example:

- 'Soul' implies Greek dualism (immortal essence separate from body)
- But נֶפֶשׁ (nephesh) means 'whole living person'
- Understanding nephesh helps us see why resurrection is BODILY (1 Cor 15)

This corrects Greek philosophy, not imposes Hebrew law. We're being MORE faithful to what the NT actually teaches."
